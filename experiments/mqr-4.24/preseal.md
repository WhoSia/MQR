# MQR-4.24 — Authority-Minimality Theorem, C–E–P Component Ablation, Necessary-vs-Sufficient Governance Basis, Redundant Credential Elimination & Whether Generation-IV Has Reached a Minimal Realist-Authority Constitution

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEAL

## Objective
Determine whether Generation-IV has reached a minimal authority basis.

Candidate basis:
C = independent constitutional authority
E = admissible execution/world-contact receipt
P = independently auditable provenance/time authority

Candidate promotion law:
PROMOTE := C AND E AND P

## Minimality criteria
A basis B={C,E,P} is minimal iff:

M1 Sufficiency:
C=E=P=PASS -> PROMOTE.

M2 Necessity by single ablation:
For each x in {C,E,P}, setting x=FAIL while the other two remain PASS -> HOLD.

M3 No proper subset suffices:
Every proper subset of {C,E,P} fails to authorize promotion.

M4 Redundant-credential elimination:
Adding an auxiliary credential R that does not restore any failed C/E/P condition and does not change promotion when C/E/P already pass contributes zero marginal scientific authority.

M5 Monotonicity:
Adding valid evidence cannot demote an already valid C∧E∧P state unless it introduces a direct contradiction or invalidates one of C/E/P.

M6 Non-substitutability:
No auxiliary lane may substitute for a failed C, E, or P.

## Auxiliary credentials under ablation
Test as candidate redundant lanes:
- persistent author-key / authorship continuity K;
- keyless OIDC principal continuity I;
- public display/visibility V;
- provider multiplicity X absent constitutional independence.

These may retain attribution, audit convenience, or operational robustness value without scientific-authority value.

## Theorem target
If M1–M6 pass over the full Boolean state space and no auxiliary lane rescues a failed C/E/P state:

MINIMAL REALIST-AUTHORITY CONSTITUTION = {C,E,P}

Scope:
Minimality is relative to the current Generation-IV claim class and frozen authority semantics.
It is not asserted as a universal theorem for all sciences, institutions, or future MQR generations.
