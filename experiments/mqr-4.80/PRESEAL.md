## Explanandum lock

Given two locally warranted coverage constitutions `K_A` and `K_B` whose target families overlap only partially, what conditions allow a composed constitution `K_*` to inherit local authority without:

- erasing A-only obligations;
- erasing B-only obligations;
- manufacturing unsupported joint obligations;
- treating overlap agreement as global authority;
- treating nonoverlap silence as irrelevance;
- hiding unresolved conflict behind a common denominator;
- losing provenance of transported obligations?

The target is not generic data fusion, generic scope transport, generic institutional conflict resolution, or generic mathematical gluing.

## Relative positive rule candidate

A joint constitution may carry authority only for obligations whose authority can be traced to a locally warranted source, whose translation preserves the claim-relevant distinction, and whose conflicts/nonoverlap debts remain explicit.

Composition may preserve authority; it cannot create authority ex nihilo.
