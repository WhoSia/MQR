## 1. The first novelty kill: history dependence is not enough

4.69 may not infer a new scientific ontology from the observation that two identical-looking current states can react differently because of their histories.

A standard response is state augmentation:

```text
PATH DEPENDENCE
→
ADD A SUFFICIENT HISTORY SUMMARY TO STATE
→
MARKOVIZE
```

POMDP belief states, predictive state representations, and computational-mechanics causal states are direct comparators.

The important baseline question is therefore not:

> does history matter?

but:

> what is the coarsest function of history that preserves all authority-relevant future transitions under the declared intervention family?

This is the central 4.69 burden.

## 3. POMDP / belief-state baseline

Partially observed decision processes replace full observation history with a belief state when that belief is sufficient for future control.

Therefore a difference between raw histories is not a scientific residue if both histories map to the same defensible belief state and all future authority transitions are the same.

4.69 includes an explicit BELIEF_STATE baseline-win control.

## 7. Prespecification and authorization-path baseline

FDA adaptive-design guidance and ICH adaptive-design/statistical guidance treat prospectively planned modifications differently from unplanned/post-hoc modifications and require explicit documentation of design/adaptation rules.

This provides a real scientific-governance example where:
- a current numerical rule does not carry its whole governance status extensionally;
- authorization path can affect what future analysis/adaptation is legitimate.

But ordinary statistical-design governance is a strong explanation.

Hence any 4.69 authorization-pair separation must be labeled:

**PRESPECIFICATION_GOVERNANCE_BASELINE_LIVE**

until a stronger residue is shown.

## 10. Candidate MQR residue

The current candidate is not:
