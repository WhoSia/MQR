## Three levels

- **A — relation-theoretic extendability:** existence and structure of ordering extensions after a declared mathematical repair.
- **B — constitutional admissibility:** every added comparison, deleted comparison, scope transition, debt operation, provenance relation and selector satisfies the frozen MQR constitution.
- **C — scientific authority promotion:** the admissible transformation is allowed to modify the live authority state.

A does not automatically imply B; B does not automatically imply C.

## Parent object

The parent is MQR-4.84's maximal warranted comparison structure. The executable representation uses:

```text
N = (V, R, Sigma, W, D, T)
```

with authority states V, comparison relation R, scope Sigma, warrant/provenance W, residual-debt state D and debt/obligation transport T.

## Pure extension versus repair-then-extension

Pure extension:

```text
R -> R+
R subseteq R+
```

Repair-then-extension:

```text
R -> R \ Delta -> R*
```

Deleting an edge is a scientific operation only if the deleted edge has a defeat/revocation warrant and its residual obligations are discharged or rerouted. Graph-theoretic cycle removal alone is not a scientific repair.

## Candidate extension certificate

C_E = (R*, A*, Delta*, Lambda+, Lambda-, Theta, Pi, rho)

- R*: final comparison relation
