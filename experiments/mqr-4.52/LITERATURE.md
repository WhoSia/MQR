## Novelty kill 1 — escaping theory-test / falsification primacy is not new

Steinle's exploratory experimentation explicitly studies experiments not driven by a specific theory or even by an available conceptual framework. Experimental goals, instruments and theory relations can be reorganized by exploration itself.

Hacking/Galison-style autonomy of experiment is already part of the literature Steinle engages.

**Killed:** MQR cannot claim novelty for saying that science is more than hypotheses awaiting falsification.

**4.52 consequence:** falsification becomes one defeat route among a larger algebra of measurement, calibration, exploration, intervention, representation and positive convergence.

## Novelty kill 2 — active / pragmatic realism is not new

Chang's active realism treats realism as a policy of learning as much as possible about reality, places empirical objects in an operable realm, and stresses coherent epistemic activity rather than a binary final declaration of real/unreal. The account is explicitly scoped and defeasible.

**Killed:** MQR cannot claim novelty for operational/pragmatic realism, graded/scoped realist commitment, or reality learned through successful activity.

**Pressure:** MQR must specify what its executable authority objects add beyond a philosophical policy of active learning.

## Novelty kill 3 — perspectival and local realism are not new

Massimi's perspectival realism gives scientific representations and data-to-phenomena inferences a perspectival, plural role.

Menon's 2026 normative-pragmatist proposal explicitly reframes realism in terms of normativity/authority and argues that implementation is domain-specific and agent-first.

Mayne's 2026 operationalization argument explicitly rejects a single global ontological principle in favour of radically local, question/domain-relative principles when that better serves empirical inquiry.

**Killed:** authority language, perspectival representation, domain-sensitive realism and local pragmatist ontological policy are not MQR inventions.

**4.52 pressure:** a single universal MQR stop threshold would repeat precisely the globalism these views warn against.

## Novelty kill 4 — robustness is not automatic realism

Stegenga emphasizes that evidential robustness can mislead under discordance.

Stegenga & Menon distinguish ontic diversity from the stronger independence conditions needed for warranted robustness inference; multiple techniques do not automatically yield the hoped-for epistemic “oomph.”

**Killed:** MQR cannot treat multiple instruments, replications or methods as an automatic reality oracle.

**4.52 donor:** ancestry, common-mode error, conditional independence and discordance remain load-bearing even under positive convergence.

## Novelty kill 5 — empirical models are open systems

Oreskes, Shrader-Frechette & Belitz reject verification/validation as demonstrations of truth for open natural systems, stressing nonuniqueness, auxiliary assumptions and incomplete access. Confirmation can remain partial.

**Killed:** MQR cannot claim novelty for open-system anti-finality or partial confirmation.

**4.52 donor:** naturalistic stop windows should be partially identified; successful model/data agreement cannot manufacture final closure.

## Naturalistic case pressure on the literature

The seven PRIMARY episodes add a different kind of constraint.

### OPERA
The decisive problem was not abstract theory falsification. It was a concrete timing/instrument control failure exposed through targeted checks and independent measurements.

### BICEP2
The measured B-mode excess and its primordial-tensor interpretation separated. New foreground information changed one authority coordinate without erasing the observational programme.

### GFAJ-1
Independent laboratories inherited specific chemical/growth obligations. Handoff and replication were epistemic state transitions, not just “another falsification.”

### Higgs
Independent positive convergence supported a new-particle claim; later work continued on identity and properties. A falsifier was not required for local claim closure.

### GW150914
Detector characterization, calibration, environmental channels and independent analysis pipelines collectively supported claim closure. The observatory did not stop doing science when the discovery claim closed.

### H. pylori
Association, culture, pathogenicity and intervention accumulated different kinds of authority. Therapeutic/action authority became strong while broader mechanistic inquiry remained open.

### Venus phosphine
Calibration and reanalysis produced durable discordance. A principled framework must be able to remain right-censored rather than forcing a victory/defeat story.

## 4.51 doctrine defeated in naturalistic representation

The generated benchmark found a calibration-only `lambda=1`.

Naturalistic traces show that “one additional transition” has no invariant scientific unit:
- a transition may be a publication, instrument check, independent replication, clinical trial, public claim review, or years of accumulated evidence;
- inserting an observationally inert checkpoint changes the event count while leaving the world state fixed.

Therefore:

~~~text
ONE MORE EVENT
!=
ONE MORE UNIT OF WORLD CONTACT

EVENT-COUNT CALIBRATION
IS REPRESENTATION-DEPENDENT
~~~

This is a representation failure before it is an empirical performance failure.
