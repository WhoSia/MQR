## Governing question

MQR-4.51 showed that generated-world stopping eligibility and calibrated stopping time are distinct.

MQR-4.52 asks whether that distinction survives contact with investigations that arose independently of MQR:

~~~text
DOES REALSTOP-0.18
TRANSPORT TO NATURALISTIC SCIENTIFIC INQUIRY

OR

DOES THE WORLD FORCE
A DIFFERENT ONTOLOGY
OF STOPPING ITSELF?
~~~

The target is not to find historical anecdotes that resemble MQR.

The target is to let independently arising inquiry histories attack MQR's stopping constitution.

## Anti-Popper firewall

MQR-4.52 does not take falsification as the master semantics of science.

A naturalistic episode may change authority because of:
- positive measurement convergence;
- calibration failure;
- representation change;
- instrument drift;
- model underdetermination;
- background-assumption revision;
- new phenomenon construction;
- intervention success/failure;
- cross-instrument transport;
- decision/action urgency;
- resource exhaustion;
- external replication;
- institutional handoff;
- discovery of a new question that changes what the old claim means.

Therefore:

~~~text
CONTINUE != SEEK FALSIFIER
STOP != ACCEPT UNFALSIFIED THEORY
REOPEN != THEORY FALSIFIED
WORLD CONTACT != NEGATIVE TEST ONLY
~~~

Falsification is one possible defeat route among many.

## Naturalistic unit: inquiry episode

A case is not a paper, theory, person or historical controversy.

A case is a bounded inquiry episode with:
1. a declared claim/use target;
2. a reconstructable state before a later outcome;
3. independently arising inquiry actions;
4. timestamped public evidence;
5. at least one consequential continue/stop/freeze/archive/reopen decision;
6. later world contact capable of adjudicating whether additional already-live inquiry mattered.

Case identity is frozen before scoring.

## Admission before outcome scoring

A candidate investigation is admitted only if all A-criteria pass.

### A1 — Timestamped source spine

At least three distinct chronological checkpoints must be supported by date-bearing primary or near-primary public records.

Preferred order:
- original paper/preprint/data release;
- collaboration/institutional statement;
- follow-up measurement or replication;
- later technical adjudication.

A secondary chronology may bridge gaps but cannot be the sole spine.

### A2 — Ex-ante state reconstructability

At each scored checkpoint we must reconstruct, from material available no later than that checkpoint:
- live claim;
- admitted rival/alternative explanation surface;
- known measurement/instrument limitations;
- explicit pending tests or obligations;
- current action/use contract where observable;
- current inquiry action actually taken.

No later evidence may be used to manufacture an earlier obligation unless contemporaneous records show that obligation was already live/reachable.

### A3 — Independently arising decision

At least one inquiry transition must have occurred without MQR:
- CONTINUE targeted inquiry;
- FREEZE/limit claim;
- ACT provisionally;
- STOP active investigation;
- ARCHIVE/deprioritize;
- REOPEN.

The historical actors' decision is descriptive evidence, not automatically correct gold.

### A4 — Deferred outcome layer

The case packet must be split into:
- EX_ANTE: sources at or before checkpoint cutoffs;
- REVEAL: later sources used only after the ex-ante state table is frozen.

The reconstruction ledger records both dates.

### A5 — Material later contact

There must be later evidence that changes, confirms, narrows, or leaves unresolved the authority/use state in a way relevant to the frozen claim contract.

Purely sociological interest without adjudicative contact is insufficient for the primary calibration set.

### A6 — Nontrivial stopping geometry

The case must contain at least one of:
- already-live obligation whose execution later matters;
- long low-yield continuation;
- legitimate provisional closure followed by novel reopening;
- decision/use shift that changes the value of continued inquiry;
- multiple authority modes that binary STOP/CONTINUE would conflate.

### A7 — Trace completeness threshold

A case is PRIMARY only if at least 70% of required checkpoint fields are source-supported and no load-bearing field is inferred solely from later hindsight.

Cases below this threshold may enter SENSITIVITY or REJECTED, never the primary verdict.

## Candidate discovery and selection freeze

Before detailed outcome scoring:

1. discover a broad candidate pool using neutral search terms about scientific retraction, replication, calibration, unresolved anomalies, confirmation campaigns, instrument faults, and terminated investigations;
2. apply A1–A7 using source availability, not expected MQR outcome;
3. admit the first qualifying set needed to achieve domain diversity;
4. freeze admission/rejection reasons;
5. only then build REVEAL layers and score policy transport.

Target:
- at least 6 admitted investigations if evidence permits;
- at least 4 distinct science/instrument regimes;
- at least one case dominated by positive convergence rather than falsification;
- at least one instrumentation/calibration failure;
- at least one legitimate post-stop reopening;
- at least one likely over-inquiry/low-yield continuation;
- at least one case where binary stop semantics visibly fail.

No case is included because it is famous, philosophically convenient, or expected to vindicate MQR.

## Stopping is not initially assumed binary

Naturalistic scientific authority may occupy these modes:

- CONTINUE_PROBING — active evidence acquisition;
- CLAIM_FREEZE — stop expanding the claim while analysis continues;
- PROVISIONAL_USE — act under a declared decision contract while epistemic inquiry remains open;
- ARCHIVE — cease active resource allocation without asserting resolved truth;
- HANDOFF — transfer unresolved inquiry to another group/instrument/program;
- REOPEN — reactivate due to qualifying new contact.

REALSTOP-0.18's STOP is evaluated as a projection onto this richer state space.

If no faithful projection exists, v0.18 transport fails even if a binary scorer could be forced.

## Naturalistic OQSC mapping

4.52 does not silently assume historical records contain all v0.17 fields.

Each checkpoint gets a mapping status:

- EXACT — explicit contemporaneous evidence for the required v0.18 coordinate;
- BOUNDED — coordinate lies in a source-supported interval/set;
- PROXY — justified operational proxy with stated loss;
- UNKNOWN — not reconstructable.

A stop prediction is AUTHORITATIVE for the primary court only when all load-bearing coordinates are EXACT or BOUNDED.

PROXY-bearing predictions enter sensitivity analysis.

UNKNOWN-bearing predictions are CENSORED.

## Stop window, not gold stop point

Naturalistic cases generally do not identify a unique true stopping instant.

For each inquiry episode, scorer-only post-reveal adjudication may establish:

~~~text
W_stop = [t_earliest_safe, t_latest_nonwasteful]
~~~

where:
- before t_earliest_safe, stopping would leave at least one already-live material obligation unresolved;
- after t_latest_nonwasteful, additional inquiry produces no material change for the frozen claim/use contract relative to its defensible cost;
- if either boundary is underidentified, it remains open/interval-valued.

Possible states:
- IDENTIFIED_WINDOW;
- LEFT_CENSORED;
- RIGHT_CENSORED;
- INTERVAL_ONLY;
- NONIDENTIFIABLE.

No point tau-star is fabricated.

## Naturalistic error semantics

For a policy stop prediction t_p:

### N-PSE

Premature if t_p is demonstrably earlier than every admissible t_earliest_safe.

### N-OIW

Over-inquiry if t_p is demonstrably later than every admissible t_latest_nonwasteful.

### INDETERMINATE

If t_p overlaps the partially identified admissible window.

This is a feature, not a scoring failure.

## Reopening

A legitimate local closure followed by genuinely novel evidence is not retroactively premature.

Record:
- closure timestamp;
- first qualifying novel break;
- first actual or policy REOPEN;
- latency interval.

If historical granularity only supports a date range, use an interval.

The historical action is not gold truth.

- POLICY vs HISTORICAL ACTION.

This allows MQR to disagree with scientists without declaring scientists irrational merely because they did not follow MQR.

## Strong failure conditions for v0.18

REALSTOP-0.18 transport FAILS if any of the following occurs systematically:

1. OQSC coordinates cannot be reconstructed without hindsight in most naturalistic cases.
2. Binary STOP/CONTINUE projection collapses materially distinct authority modes.
3. Lag-1 generates definite premature stops across independent regimes.
4. Lag-1 generates definite over-inquiry where current-use contracts make continuation materially wasteful.
5. Naturalistic reopening requires semantics not expressible by v0.18.
6. The generated-benchmark confirmation lag has no stable meaning under irregular historical event spacing.
7. Case results depend on source granularity rather than scientific state.
8. Policy verdict reverses under equally defensible ex-ante reconstructions.

If these occur, v0.19 must be a revision, not merely a wrapper.

## Broader realist ambition

MQR-4.52 permits a stronger theoretical shift if the cases demand it:

Scientific realism need not be represented as a sequence of theories waiting to be falsified.

A scientific community may earn local authority by building and maintaining a **world-coupled control structure** over:
- what can be measured;
- what remains distinguishable;
- what interventions can be trusted;
- what representations preserve action-relevant structure;
- which failures remain live;
- when resources should move;
- when a claim can be frozen without declaring reality exhausted;
- when new contact should reopen what was provisionally closed.

Candidate thesis under attack:

~~~text
REALIST AUTHORITY
MAY LIVE IN THE
DISCIPLINED DYNAMICS
OF CONTACT, CONTROL,
TRANSPORT, ACTION,
AND REOPENING

RATHER THAN IN
A SINGLE FINAL THEORY
OR A PURE
FALSIFICATION RELATION.
~~~

This thesis is not granted by preseal. Naturalistic cases must earn or defeat it.
