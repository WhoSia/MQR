## External pressure

### Partial orders / Pareto / non-domination

Recent synthesis of dominance-based evaluation explicitly treats Pareto dominance as a partial order and recognizes that non-dominated alternatives can remain incomparable. This supports the frozen MQR distinction:

~~~text
PARETO FRONTIER
!=
DECISION PROCEDURE
~~~

Donor:
- “International university rankings: composite indicators and methodological approaches,” Frontiers in Research Metrics and Analytics (2026), DOI 10.3389/frma.2026.1828850.

### MCDA / incomparability

A comprehensive MCDA taxonomy distinguishes score-based recommendations from binary-relation approaches and notes that incomparability allows partial rather than complete orders.

Donor:
- Cinelli et al., “How to support the application of multiple criteria decision analysis? Let us start with a comprehensive taxonomy,” Omega / PMC (2021), PMCID PMC7970504.

MQR novelty kill:
- incomparability itself is established;
- non-compensatory/binary-relation reasoning is established;
- MQR must earn only its narrower presealed constitutional use.

### Scientific theory choice / value plurality

Kuhn’s “Objectivity, Value Judgment, and Theory Choice” (1977) treats scientific theory choice as structured by values without reducing those values to an algorithmic rule that mechanically determines one choice.

Donor:
- Thomas S. Kuhn, The Essential Tension, pp. 320–339, DOI 10.7208/9780226217239-014.

MQR pressure:
- scientific reasons can be legitimate without jointly forming one universal total decision procedure.

### Robust decision making

Robust Decision Making stresses strategies across many plausible futures rather than requiring a single predictive probability model.

Donor:
- Robert J. Lempert, “Robust Decision Making,” in Decision Making under Deep Uncertainty (2019).
- Lempert, Popper & Bankes, “Confronting Surprise” (2002), DOI 10.1177/089443902237320.

MQR pressure:
- horizon-sensitive decisions and reopening are legitimate;
- a single point estimate can hide future-regime sensitivity.

### Option value

Real-options work on R&D explicitly values retaining future opportunities and staged decisions under uncertainty.

Donors:
- Pennings & Lint, “The option value of advanced R&D,” European Journal of Operational Research 103(1), 1997, DOI 10.1016/S0377-2217(96)00283-4.
- Ross, Fisch & Varga, “Unlocking the value of real options,” Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal, DOI 10.1002/sej.1275.

MQR novelty kill:
- option value is not an MQR invention.
- MQR-4.60 uses option value only as one typed reason whose force may conflict with other reasons.
