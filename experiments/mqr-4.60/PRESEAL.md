## Explanandum lock

MQR-4.60 asks how a reflexive scientific constitution should behave when individually admissible promotion reasons conflict.

The stage does **not** assume a scalar utility, total ordering, global priority list, or unique winner.

## Primary hypotheses

1. Pairwise dominance need not induce a total order.
2. Pareto non-domination is not by itself a decision procedure.
3. A local veto need not imply universal priority.
4. Lexicographic priority can become criterion dictatorship.
5. Weighted sums can be normalization- and duplication-sensitive.
6. Duplicated/common-mode reasons must not gain force by repetition.
7. Horizon changes can reverse local admissibility without contradiction.
8. Debt can defeat a locally positive reason only under an explicit scope/horizon rule.
9. Incomparability is a legitimate output, not a parser failure.
10. No universal promotion meta-utility is earned.
