## Selection-induced circularity

Let `Q` be a separator generator and `S_Q` the family it can produce.

The forbidden inference is:

```text
Q -> S_Q
S_Q fails to distinguish G1 and G2
therefore G1 and G2 are dependency-equivalent
therefore S_Q was adequate
```

This is **SEPARATOR-CONSTITUTION CIRCULARITY**.

A generator may not use the indistinguishability induced by its own representational/search restrictions as evidence of its own completeness.
