# MQR-4.83 — PRESEAL

Status: **PRESEALED / OUTCOME-BLIND / CROSS-TRUNCATION-AUTHORITY-COMPARISON-UNDER-ATTACK**

## Explanandum

Let `C_A` and `C_B` be independently warranted finite coherence certificates, each valid under MQR-4.82.

MQR-4.83 asks whether and when one certificate may dominate, subsume, transport to, or be treated as authority-equivalent to the other.

## Frozen certificate coordinates

For each certificate:
- claim scope;
- truncation depth;
- coherence basis;
- basis-authority source;
- higher-order relevance rule;
- residual debt set;
- reopening rule;
- version/provenance.

For a comparison:
- scope correspondence;
- relevance-rule correspondence;
- basis-authority transport;
- certificate refinement map;
- residual-debt migration;
- direction of claimed authority transport;
- later cross-level escape.

## Frozen nonidentities

```text
DEEPER_TRUNCATION != STRONGER_AUTHORITY
MORE_CHECKED_LEVELS != WARRANT_DOMINANCE
TRUNCATION_MAP != AUTHORITY_TRANSPORT
CERTIFICATE_REFINEMENT != SCIENTIFIC_SUBSUMPTION
SCOPE_INCLUSION != AUTHORITY_INCLUSION
BASIS_INCLUSION != BASIS_AUTHORITY_INHERITANCE
LOWER_RESIDUAL_DEBT != HIGHER_GLOBAL_AUTHORITY
COMMON_DEPTH != COMMON_RELEVANCE_RULE
CROSS_LEVEL_EMBEDDING != WARRANT_EMBEDDING
BIDIRECTIONAL_TRANSLATION != AUTHORITY_EQUIVALENCE
```

## Forbidden moves

- rank certificates by depth alone;
- infer authority transport from a mathematical truncation/refinement map alone;
- treat a superset claim scope as automatically stronger;
- erase debt that is not represented in the target certificate;
- infer basis authority from basis inclusion;
- convert one-way transport into equivalence;
- use same numerical depth as evidence of common relevance constitution;
- deny all comparison merely because open-world completeness is unavailable.

## Candidate relation states

- `A_AUTHORITY_DOMINATES_B`
- `B_AUTHORITY_DOMINATES_A`
- `AUTHORITY_EQUIVALENT_AT_SHARED_SCOPE`
- `AUTHORITY_INCOMPARABLE`
- `DEPTH_LAUNDERING`
- `REFINEMENT_WITHOUT_AUTHORITY_TRANSPORT`
- `BASIS_AUTHORITY_NONINHERITANCE`
- `RESIDUAL_DEBT_MIGRATION_FAILURE`
- `REOPEN_CROSS_LEVEL_ESCAPE`

## Candidate positive rule

A directional authority comparison is licensed only if:
1. the relevant claim scopes admit an explicit direction-preserving correspondence;
2. the target relevance rule preserves every source claim-relevant higher-order distinction;
3. basis authority, not merely basis content, transports;
4. source residual debt is preserved, discharged by an independently warranted procedure, or explicitly mapped to target debt;
5. the comparison does not rely on depth alone;
6. later cross-level escape reopens the relation.

Authority equivalence requires the corresponding conditions in both directions.

Absence of those conditions may yield legitimate incomparability rather than failure.
