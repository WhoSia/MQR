## 4.72 theorem

For a claim phi, scope S, predecessor practice P and successor practice P', authority transport is not licensed by label/procedure continuity alone.

A necessary MQR condition is: for each still-live claim-relevant defeat obligation d in D_P(phi,S), there must be either a preserved route, an explicit transport to a successor obligation, or an independently warranted replacement that preserves or strengthens exposure to the relevant error/counterexample class.

This criterion is deliberately weaker than route identity and stronger than procedure continuity.

## Natural science / mathematics bridge

The common structure is not that experiment and proof are the same epistemic activity.

The common structure is: **inherited authority requires preserved or reconstructed vulnerability to claim-relevant defeat.**

Natural science realizes defeat through world-facing measurement, intervention, calibration and replication routes. Mathematics realizes defeat through counterexample, derivational failure, model/semantic mismatch, or framework-explicit proof obligations.
