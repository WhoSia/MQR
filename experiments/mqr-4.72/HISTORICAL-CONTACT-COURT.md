# MQR-4.72 — Historical Contact Court

Status: **ADJUDICATED / STRONG-DEFEAT-ROUTE-IDENTITY-REJECTED / DEFEAT-CAPABILITY-TRANSPORT-SURVIVES**

## Historical pressure

Chang’s thermometry case does not support a fixed-route inheritance rule. Measurement procedures, scale realizations and conceptual interpretations can change while the practice remains corrigible through comparison, calibration and epistemic iteration.

Result: SAME DEFEAT ROUTE IS NOT NECESSARY FOR CONTINUED SCIENTIFIC AUTHORITY.

Tal’s model-based account of calibration supports the stronger nonidentity: indication continuity is not semantic or authority continuity.

Steinle’s exploratory experimentation defeats full target ontology as a necessary condition. Structured directedness, reproducible contact and an available anomaly/failure route are sufficient for the narrower MQR contact condition.

Schindler’s theory-driven reliability cases show that theory guidance is not automatically circular, but sole self-gating by the tested theory is insufficient for authority. Independent support or calibration can break the self-confirming loop.

Millikan–Ehrenhaft and the 1919 eclipse are retained as contested pressure cases, not retrospective truth oracles. Their MQR use is procedural: agreement with the tested claim cannot itself certify which data are reliable.

In mathematics, Lakatosian counterexamples can change definitions, theorem domains and proof obligations. Computer-assisted proof can preserve framework-relative proof authority when a certificate or kernel checks the derivation under an explicitly fixed framework.

## Rejected strong theorem

Authority inheritance requires continuity of the same defeat route.

This is too strong because route replacement can be epistemically legitimate.

## Surviving theorem candidate

Let D_old(phi,S) be the live failure/counterexample obligations relevant to claim phi on declared scope S.

A successor practice may inherit authority only if every still-relevant obligation is either:

1. exposed by a preserved route;
2. transported by an explicit semantic/contact map; or
3. covered by an independently warranted replacement route that exposes the same failure class or a stronger one.

Therefore:

**AUTHORITY INHERITANCE TRACKS CLAIM-RELEVANT DEFEAT CAPABILITY, NOT LABEL, PROCEDURE OR ROUTE IDENTITY.**

This is a scope-relative authority result, not a universal theory of truth.
