# MQR-4.72 — Literature Collision I

Status: **CORE-LITERATURE-READ / INTENTIONALITY-NARROWED / MEASUREMENT-SEMANTICS-BASELINE-STRONG / EXPLORATORY-EXPERIMENT-COUNTERPRESSURE / MATHEMATICAL-PRACTICE-BRIDGE-OPEN**

## 1. Tal 2017 — calibration and measurement semantics

Calibration is a modelling activity that connects instrument indications to properties/quantities of the object being measured.

A pointer position or display value is not automatically the measurement outcome.

Measurement outcome authority depends on inferential structure linking:
- indication;
- measurement-process model;
- target quantity;
- uncertainty;
- predictive/model coherence.

Collision:

```text
SAME INDICATION
!=
SAME MEASUREMENT OUTCOME
```

and:

```text
RAW INSTRUMENT STATE
!=
TARGET-QUANTITY CLAIM
```

This strongly absorbs any naive MQR claim that intentional target / measurement semantics separation is new.

## 2. Chang 2004 — measurement, operationalization and epistemic iteration

Inventing Temperature documents how temperature measurement was not grounded by one indubitable foundation.

Relevant structures include:
- fixing and revising fixed points;
- comparison across thermometric substances;
- extension beyond original domains;
- operationalization linking abstract concepts to concrete procedures;
- epistemic iteration in which an inherited knowledge system is used to improve and correct itself.

Collision:

```text
MEASUREMENT AUTHORITY CAN BE HISTORICALLY INHERITED
WITHOUT AN ABSOLUTE FOUNDATION
```

provided the inherited system remains open to correction through further contact.

This is a major baseline for MQR-4.72 scope inheritance.

## 3. Steinle 1997 — exploratory experimentation

Exploratory experimentation can proceed when no specific theory, or even no mature conceptual framework, is available.

It follows systematic experimental guidelines and can have major epistemic significance.

Collision with strong intentionality:

```text
FULLY SPECIFIED THEORY-TARGET INTENTION
IS NOT NECESSARY FOR
EPISTEMICALLY PRODUCTIVE EXPERIMENT
```

Therefore MQR-4.72 must not require every legitimate scientific contact to be directed at a fully articulated theory/object.

Candidate replacement:

**structured directedness** rather than full target specification.

An exploratory inquiry can be directed toward variation, regularity, stability, anomaly or phenomenological mapping without a mature ontology of the eventual phenomenon.

## 4. Theory-ladenness and theory-driven reliability

Schindler's theory-laden experimentation analysis identifies theory-driven data-reliability judgments: the theory under test can help determine which data are judged reliable.

Historical examples include:
- Franck–Hertz;
- 1919 eclipse measurements;
- Millikan–Ehrenhaft;
- parity-violation experiments.

Collision:

```text
INTENTION / THEORY CAN SHAPE
NOT ONLY WHICH CONTACTS ARE MADE
BUT WHICH CONTACTS ARE TRUSTED
```

This creates a circularity risk if the tested theory is the sole authority over data reliability.

Independent calibration, independent background support, cross-instrument agreement or exterior checks are possible anti-circularity routes.

## 5. Dubova, Moskvichev & Zollman 2026

Their formal multi-agent model reports that theory-motivated experimental choice can generate an illusion of epistemic success by sampling less diverse / less representative parts of the ground-truth domain.

Random experimental choice performs better in their modeled setting.

Collision:

```text
INTENTIONALLY THEORY-DIRECTED SEARCH
CAN BE TRUTH-ANTI-CONDUCIVE
IN SOME SEARCH ECOLOGIES
```

Therefore intentionality cannot itself confer epistemic authority.

## 6. Worrall 1989 / structural continuity

Structural realism supplies a major baseline for continuity across theory change.

MQR-4.72 gets no novelty credit merely for claiming that some invariant mathematical structure can survive replacement of theoretical ontology.

The live question is narrower:

> Which contact/defeat obligations must transport together with structure before inherited scientific authority survives?

## 7. Oreskes, Shrader-Frechette & Belitz 1994

Verification/validation of numerical models in open natural systems does not amount to proof of truth in the strong logical sense.

Collision:

```text
MODEL VALIDATION
!=
TRUTH BY PROCEDURE
```

This is a strong baseline for the MQR truth-authority ceiling.

## 8. Mathematical practice

Lakatos-style proofs and refutations, Rav's distinction between ordinary proof and formal derivation, and the history of computer-assisted proof all pressure a static view of mathematical authority.

Relevant boundary:

```text
THEOREM LABEL
!=
FULL EPISTEMIC CONTENT OF PROOF
```

and:

```text
DERIVABILITY UNDER FRAMEWORK F
!=
UNQUALIFIED TRUTH OUTSIDE F
```

These are major baselines, not MQR discoveries.

## 9. Strong novelty kill

MQR-4.72 gets zero novelty credit for:
- theory-ladenness;
- calibration-model dependence;
- exploratory experimentation;
- historical evolution of measurement concepts;
- structural continuity across theory change;
- fallibility of mathematical practice;
- computer-assisted-proof epistemology;
- the distinction between model validation and truth.

## 10. Surviving candidate

The candidate residue is a cross-domain authority discipline:

1. **target/contact separation** — what an inquiry is about is not identical to how it contacts it;
2. **directedness without full ontology** — exploratory science may be legitimate before mature target concepts exist;
3. **defeat-route inheritance** — inherited authority survives only if relevant error/counterexample routes survive or are replaced by independently stronger ones;
4. **truth-contact ceiling** — procedure, consensus and inherited scope remain weaker than truth;
5. **framework-relative mathematical authority** — proof authority transports only with explicit framework/semantic conditions;
6. **anti-circularity** — the object/theory being tested cannot be the sole authority over which contacts count as reliable.

Whether these six reduce to existing measurement / experiment / mathematical-practice theory remains OPEN.
