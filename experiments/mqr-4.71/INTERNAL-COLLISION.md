The main 4.62 object was a prospective profile over **candidate evidence-generating actions** and the problem of choosing among them.

- target relation;
- probe type;
- outcome partition;
- identifiability contribution;
- option effects;
- cost;

MQR-4.71 moves one level upstream.

```text

```

1. explicit separation between **current admitted envelope** and **current generative constitution**;
2. present-envelope insufficiency for future contact dynamics;
