# MQR-4.71 — Naturalistic Near-Match: Adaptive Platform Trial Governance

Status: **NATURALISTIC-STRUCTURAL-DONOR / SAME-CURRENT-ARMS-FUTURE-CAPABILITY-DISTINCTION / NOT-A-PROSPECTIVE-MATCHED-PAIR-VALIDATION**

## Domain

Adaptive platform / master-protocol clinical trials.

## Relevant governance structure

FDA master-protocol guidance treats platform trials as ongoing structures in which products may enter or leave over time.

Master protocols are expected to specify conditions under which treatment arms may be:
- added;
- expanded;
- discontinued.

Those changes may depend on prespecified interim analyses or external new data.

RECOVERY is an explicit adaptive platform trial whose protocol archive spans many versions. Its protocol synopsis states that new treatment comparisons may be added in future and that the platform has no fixed overall end date.

## MQR-4.71 mapping

At a given time, the currently active treatment-arm set is analogous to the current executable contact envelope X_t.

The master protocol / platform governance is analogous to part of the generative constitution that determines which future treatment comparisons may enter or leave.

Therefore two trial designs can be extensionally similar at the level of a current active-arm set while differing in the future transition rules authorized by their protocols.
