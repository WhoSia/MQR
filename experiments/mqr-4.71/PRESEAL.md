## Explanandum lock

MQR-4.70 showed that state minimality is only as strong as the warranted closure of the intervention/measurement family relative to which it was proved.

MQR-4.71 does not ask how to optimize experiments inside a fixed family.

It asks:

> What constitutes the scientifically admissible contact envelope itself, and what authorizes its generation, exclusion, expansion, revision and transport?

## Dynamic contact-envelope object

At time t define:

- `G_t`: candidate-contact generator;
- `C_t = G_t(K_t, T_t, X_t)`: generated candidate contacts under knowledge K, technology T and context X;
- `A_t subseteq C_t`: admitted scientific-contact envelope;
- `Q_t`: admissibility rule / gate;
- `J_t`: justification ledger for inclusion and exclusion;
- `R_t`: revision rule for Q, G and A;
- `D_t`: defeater ledger;
- `H_t`: horizon/scope.

Admission is:

    a in A_t iff Q_t(a ; K_t, T_t, X_t, J_t, D_t) = ADMIT.

No universal scalar utility is primitive.

## Generator authority question

A candidate-contact generator may be:

1. **theory-generated** — proposed because a live theory predicts a discriminating contact;
2. **rival-generated** — proposed by an explicit competitor;
3. **instrument-generated** — newly enabled by technology;
4. **anomaly-generated** — triggered by unexplained residuals;
5. **domain-grammar-generated** — enumerated from a scientific intervention/measurement grammar;
6. **external/adversarial** — introduced independently of the current model family.

No generator class is privileged in advance.

## Frozen constitutional conditions

### C1 — Feasibility is local and revisable

Current infeasibility does not imply scientific inadmissibility forever.

The constitution must distinguish:

    IMPOSSIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE
    CURRENTLY_UNREALIZABLE
    CURRENTLY_UNAVAILABLE
    AVAILABLE

Technology change may move a contact between the latter three statuses.

### C2 — Ethics/safety/legal constraints are not epistemic falsifiers

A contact can be scientifically discriminating yet ethically, legally or operationally inadmissible.

The Court must preserve:

    SCIENTIFIC_DISCRIMINATION_VALUE
    !=
    PERMISSION_TO_EXECUTE

No conclusion may treat prohibited execution as evidence that the underlying distinction is scientifically meaningless.

### C3 — Cost/resource constraints are context-indexed

Budget, time, sample, instrument and computation constraints may restrict A_t without proving closure of the larger scientifically meaningful candidate space.

### C4 — Novel-contact entry must be possible

If Q_t only admits contacts representable in the current state's vocabulary, the constitution is self-sealing.

A valid constitution must contain a route by which a contact not expressible under the current preferred model can enter for evaluation.

### C5 — Generator plurality

The current theory cannot be the sole authority over which contacts are admitted against itself.

At least one generator route must be independently sourced from the current favored model.

### C6 — Exclusion receipts

Every scientifically material exclusion must carry a typed reason:
- physical impossibility;
- current technology limitation;
- ethics/safety/legal restriction;
- resource limit;
- scope boundary;
- redundancy under an independently certified closure;
- unresolved identity/semantics;
- other explicit defeater.

“Not currently selected” is not a closure proof.

### C7 — Revision is defeasible

A_t, Q_t and G_t may change when:
- technology changes;
- new instruments appear;
- new ethical/legal governance applies;
- rival theories arise;
- anomalies appear;
- new domains become transport-relevant;
- prior closure certificates fail.

### C8 — Closure certificates are defeasible upstream

Any MQR-4.70 closure certificate C0–C5 is conditional on the admissible envelope used to formulate it.

If A_t expands, the prior certificate may be downgraded without contradiction.

### C9 — Cross-domain transport requires generator/admissibility transport

A contact label cannot be transported merely by name.

Transport requires preserving enough of:
- realizability;
- semantics;
- causal/measurement role;
- relevant constraints;
- distinction obligations;
- execution provenance.

### C10 — Open-world meta-closure is not primitive

No finite generator, governance rule or current ontology may declare:

    ALL FUTURE SCIENTIFICALLY LEGITIMATE CONTACTS ARE NOW ENUMERATED

without an independently warranted scope theorem.
