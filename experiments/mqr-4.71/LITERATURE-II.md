# MQR-4.71 — Literature Collision II: Dynamic Constraint-State Absorption

Status: **STATE-AUGMENTATION-BASELINE-ABSORBED / AUTHORITY-RESIDUE-NARROWED**

## Strong baseline

Guo et al. (2026), *Constrained Bayesian Experimental Design via Online Planning*, explicitly models a dynamic constraint state.

Examples include:
- previous design / actuator or smoothness constraints;
- remaining budget;
- design- and state-dependent cost;
- time-varying feasible design set.

The admissible experiment set is therefore already a function of an evolving state.

Huan & Marzouk (2016) and Shen & Huan (2023) likewise place sequential experimental design inside dynamic decision processes.

## Consequence for MQR-4.71

The following is **not** MQR novelty:

```text
CURRENT FEASIBLE ACTION SET
MAY BE INSUFFICIENT TO PREDICT
FUTURE FEASIBLE ACTION SET

AND A LARGER CONSTRAINT STATE
CAN RESTORE MARKOV SUFFICIENCY
```

This is standard state augmentation / constrained dynamic programming.

Therefore even if the phase-II predictive quotient identifies:

`generators + technology + budget + ethics`

as a minimal state on the frozen Court, MQR must not present that as a new mathematical discovery.

## Additional baseline

Safe control / viability / constrained MDP / POMDP literature already treats:

- reachability under constraints.

Again, generic dynamic admissibility is prior art.

## Surviving candidate residue after this collision

### 1. Dual scientific-vs-execution envelope

Optimization normally defines feasibility relative to a task/constraint contract.

MQR-4.71's remaining governance question is whether scientific closure authority must distinguish:

- what contacts are scientifically relevant/discriminating;
- what contacts are currently executable/permitted.

This is substantially supported by research ethics/governance prior art and may itself be baseline-level.

### 2. Closure-certificate scope typing

A closure certificate should not silently move from:

`complete among currently executable contacts`

to:

`complete among scientifically relevant contacts`.

Potential contribution is audit discipline, not new dynamics.

### 3. Meta-closure ceiling

A visible generator output cannot certify that no admissible contact exists outside the generator without an added scope/completeness assumption.

This is likely classical indistinguishability/open-world reasoning.

Its MQR value is doctrinal integration, not mathematical novelty.

## Current novelty pressure

At this point, the strongest defensible MQR-4.71 claim may be:

> Scientific-contact closure claims require explicit scope separation and cannot derive completeness of their own contact generator from the generator's visible output.

This is narrower than the original 4.71 title and is acceptable.
