## Domain A — Human-subject research governance

NIH ethical-research guidance distinguishes:
- social/clinical value;
- scientific validity;
- feasibility;
- favorable risk-benefit ratio;
- independent review;
- informed consent;
- respect for participants.

This is a direct real-world governance separation between:
- whether a study can answer a scientifically valuable question;
- whether executing that study is ethically authorized.

- substantially improved detector sensitivity relative to Initial LIGO;
- opened a much larger observable volume;
