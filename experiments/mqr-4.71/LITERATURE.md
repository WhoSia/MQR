## 7. Surviving candidate residue

The remaining candidate is narrower:

1. **same-current-envelope nonidentity**  
   Two systems may admit exactly the same contacts now but differ in future expansion because their generator/gate/revision state differs.

2. **generator plurality as anti-self-sealing structure**  
   A theory-only generator can preserve a coarse contact vocabulary while an independently authorized rival/instrument/anomaly generator introduces a future separator.

3. **closure-certificate defeasibility**  
   A certificate valid relative to A_t may be downgraded when G/Q/T expand A.

4. **compressed generative state**  
   Full generator ancestry is not primitive if current generator capabilities + gate state + revision state suffice for future transitions.

5. **transport of contact authority**  
   Same labels across domains are insufficient if feasibility/semantics/gating differ.

6. **meta-closure ceiling**  
   The output of a current generator cannot prove the generator complete.
