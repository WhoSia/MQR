# MQR-4.71 — Naturalistic Generator-Scope Audit

Status: **GENERATOR-SCOPE-REALITY-CONTACT / COMPLETENESS-NOT-OBSERVED / META-CLOSURE-RELEVANCE-SUPPORTED**

## Robot Scientist Adam

King et al. (2004, 2009) demonstrates autonomous hypothesis generation and experiment selection.

The system was configured with:
- detailed biological background knowledge;
- a logical model / ontology;
- abduction for hypothesis generation;
- deduction / experiment selection;
- automated assay execution.

Adam was scientifically productive, but its generator operated through a declared formal representation and domain model.

Naturalistic lesson:

    AUTONOMOUS HYPOTHESIS GENERATION
    !=
    ONTOLOGY-INDEPENDENT GENERATION

## Mobile robotic chemist

Burger et al. (2020) reports:
- autonomous operation over eight days;
- 688 experiments;
- a batched Bayesian search algorithm;
- search **within a ten-variable experimental space**.

Naturalistic lesson:

    EFFECTIVE AUTONOMOUS SEARCH
    CAN BE CONDITIONED ON
    A PREDECLARED EXPERIMENTAL SPACE

The result validates search performance inside that space.

It does not establish completeness of the space relative to all scientifically meaningful material interventions.

## Connection to MQR-4.71

These systems support the practical relevance of separating:

1. generator/search performance inside a declared space;
2. authority for the declared candidate space itself.

They do not establish the full finite meta-closure no-go as an empirical law.

They do support the doctrinal boundary:

    AUTONOMOUS / OPTIMAL SEARCH INSIDE C
    !=
    EVIDENCE THAT C IS COMPLETE

## Baseline warning
