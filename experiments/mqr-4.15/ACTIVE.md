# MQR-4.15 — Governance-Independent Source/Join Compilation, Sealed Rival Packet Separation, Fresh Blind Replay & Whether Executable Transport Can Cross the Final Independence Boundary

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEALED

Architecture:
- source/join compiler packet cannot see rival thresholds;
- rival/scorer packet cannot see source identity;
- fresh outcome is retrieved only after sealing;
- external compute lanes execute compiler and scorer separately;
- governance independence is adjudicated separately from runtime/provider separation.

No claim of governance independence is authorized merely because different external runtimes are used.
