## 1. The first correction: progress state is not continuation value

MQR-4.51 and 4.52 were framed around when inquiry may stop.

MQR-4.53 must now separate two objects that are often conflated:

~~~text
ACHIEVED PROGRESS
!=
EXPECTED VALUE OF CONTINUING INQUIRY
~~~

A history may have accumulated substantial knowledge, understanding, discrimination or intervention capacity while still making one further experiment highly valuable.

Conversely, an inquiry may have made little global progress yet have no remaining action-relevant continuation worth its opportunity cost for the declared use contract.

Therefore no constitutive measure of past progress, by itself, yields a stopping rule.

The stop problem is prospective.

The progress problem is state-comparative.

They may interact but are not identical.

## 2. The second correction: more authority is not necessarily more progress

Naturalistic cases already supply a decisive counterexample.

In OPERA, scientific progress included **removing** authority from the superluminal-neutrino interpretation after timing-system diagnosis.

In BICEP2, progress included narrowing the primordial-tensor interpretation while preserving the underlying polarization observation.

Thus:

~~~text
MORE CLAIM AUTHORITY
!=
MORE SCIENTIFIC PROGRESS
~~~

A progress relation must allow a correction transition that lowers one authority coordinate while improving calibration to world contact.

This defeats a naive componentwise monotone order over REALTRACE authority levels.

## 3. The third correction: no-go must not be too strong

It would be a mistake to conclude:

> representation-invariant scientific geometry is impossible.

There are local counterexamples to that no-go.

Within statistical-model manifolds, Fisher-type geometry can be characterized by invariance/monotonicity under sufficient-statistic or Markov transformations.

Within experiment comparison, Blackwell informativeness supplies a representation-insensitive partial order based on decision dominance/garbling.

Therefore the live conjecture is not “no invariant geometry.”

It is:

~~~text
NO UNLICENSED GLOBAL
SCIENTIFIC-PROGRESS METRIC

BUT

LOCAL INVARIANT GEOMETRIES
MAY EXIST
UNDER DECLARED TRANSFORMATION
AND TARGET STRUCTURES.
~~~

## 4. World-Contact Progress Atlas

Candidate synthesis:

A scientific domain does not inherit one global progress ruler.

It receives a local chart:

~~~text
C = (
  S,
  G_inert,
  Q = S / G_inert,
  K,
  R,
  P
)
~~~

where:

- `S` = inquiry-state space;
- `G_inert` = admissible representation transformations declared scientifically inert;
- `Q` = quotient state space;
- `K` = claim/use/decision contract;
- `R` = reopening and world-contact transition structure;
- `P` = local progress relation, preorder, partial order, metric or admissible-region structure if earned.

Different charts may admit different `P`.

Examples:
- Fisher–Rao metric inside a statistical model family;
- Blackwell order over experiments;
- obligation-discharge preorder over constituted burden ancestry;
- causal-identifiability order under interventions;
- decision-relative value-of-information ordering;
- mode-relative admissible closure region.

The atlas is global only in its **meta-constitution**:
every local progress claim must expose its transformation group, target, contract and transport map.

The numerical geometry itself need not be global.

## 6. Candidate theorem: independent-rescaling obstruction

Suppose two load-bearing progress axes x and y are independently meaningful only up to positive rescaling.

Consider crossing states:

~~~text
A = (1,0)
B = (0,1)
~~~

Any weighted scalar:

~~~text
F(x,y) = a x + b y
~~~

can rank A above B or B above A solely by an admissible change in relative units.

The same issue generalizes to many scalar aggregators.

Therefore a cross-axis scalar ranking requires an additional calibration relation tying the scales together.

Without such calibration, the invariant order is at most partial on crossing profiles.

This recovers MQR-4.28's Pareto lesson at the progress-measure level.

## 7. Event count, time and cost

### Event count

Fails inert checkpoint refinement/coarsening.

### Elapsed time

May matter causally in some domains, but is not a universal progress coordinate.

A laboratory waiting three months and actively running a three-month intervention programme have equal elapsed time and different world contact.

### Cost

Cost is crucial for stopping and allocation but not constitutive of epistemic progress.

Spending more can produce no new discrimination.

Changing accounting units cannot change progress.

Therefore:

~~~text
TIME / COST
CAN ENTER POLICY
WITHOUT BECOMING
EPISTEMIC DISTANCE.
~~~

## 8. Information is local, not universal

Information-theoretic criteria can support experimental design.

But the information quantity depends on:
- prior/model family;
- target variable;
- parameterization or statistical experiment;
- utility if information is valued through action.

High information about an irrelevant distinction need not discharge a live scientific obligation.

More strongly, causal-identification and intervention reach can differ from passive descriptive information.

Hence:

~~~text
DESCRIPTIVE INFORMATION GAIN
!=
INTERVENTION REACH
!=
ACTION VALUE
~~~

No one of the three can silently stand in for the others.

## 9. Blackwell order as a positive donor

Blackwell comparison is unusually important for MQR-4.53 because it demonstrates that experiment comparison need not be scalar.

One experiment can dominate another because the latter can be obtained by garbling the former / because it is no better in any decision problem of the relevant class.

This yields a **partial order**.

Two experiments can remain incomparable.

MQR consequence:

A useful progress object may be an order over world-contact opportunities, not a distance.

But Blackwell order still does not encode:
- causal intervention identity;
- obligation ancestry;
- claim/probe/use mode;
- open-world rival discovery;
- reopening.

So it is a local chart, not the global answer.

## 10. Fisher geometry as a positive donor

The Fisher metric / Amari–Chentsov structures are important for the opposite reason.

They show that, after specifying a statistical-model category and the transformations that should count as information-preserving, a nonarbitrary local geometry can be characterized.

MQR consequence:

~~~text
REPRESENTATION INVARIANCE
CAN CONSTITUTE
A LOCAL GEOMETRY.
~~~

But the geometry measures statistical distinguishability, not:
- intervention competence;
- problem-solving progress;
- understanding;
- obligation discharge;
- decision value;
- scientific authority as a whole.

Again: chart, not atlas-wide ruler.

## 11. Obligation-discharge geometry

Raw obligation count is rejected by MQR-4.44.

The candidate replacement operates on burden ancestry rather than labels.

Let:

~~~text
B_t = currently constituted material burden ancestry
D_t = burden ancestry discharged with valid world-contact receipts
L_t = live unresolved burden ancestry
~~~

Under a valid split/merge transport, two representations are quotient-equivalent when they preserve the same:
- burden ancestry;
- discharge witnesses;
- materiality;
- claim/use dependencies;
- reopening conditions.

Candidate relation:

~~~text
q1 <=_O q2
~~~

when q2 preserves every valid discharge in q1 and adds at least one valid discharge, **unless** a qualifying reopening event has invalidated a predecessor discharge.

The reopening exception is load-bearing.

Discovery of a calibration flaw may correctly reduce D_t.

That reduction can itself be progressive because it corrects over-authorized structure.

Therefore the final progress relation cannot simply equal monotone growth of D_t.

It must distinguish:
- epistemic correction;
- authority accumulation;
- debt discharge.

## 12. Correction-sensitive progress

Introduce a provisional distinction:

~~~text

  new warranted distinction/control/use

CORRECTIVE PROGRESS:
  removal or reopening of previously over-authorized structure
~~~

A correct progress structure must assign:
- positive acquisition as non-null;
- corrective progress as non-null;
- inert recoding as null.

This already rules out both “more authority” and raw path length as universal measures.

## 13. Authority-mode path length

A cumulative metric over REALTRACE coordinates fails immediately under an inert loop:

~~~text
A -> B -> A
~~~

If B adds no durable world-contact distinction and leaves no changed obligation, use contract, ancestry or reopening status, cumulative path length is positive while net scientific state is quotient-identical.

Therefore:

~~~text
PATH LENGTH != NET PROGRESS.
~~~

However not every loop may be quotiented.

If A -> B -> A reveals a new failure mode or changes reopening rights, it is materially different.

The quotient criterion must be world-contact semantic, not purely topological.

## 14. Path noncommutativity

The order of operations may matter:

~~~text
CALIBRATE ; INTERVENE
!=
INTERVENE ; CALIBRATE
~~~

when the uncalibrated intervention cannot support the same inference or use authority.

Thus the quotient must remove **inert** path differences while preserving noncommuting world-facing sequences.

This blocks a simple endpoint-only progress measure.

## 19. The atlas is categorical before it is metric

The transformation object should not be idealized as a single group acting on one homogeneous state space.

Scientific representations admit different types of maps:

- bijective coordinate recodings;
- sufficient-statistic maps that may be many-to-one in raw sample space while preserving a declared statistical target;
- Blackwell garblings that deliberately lose experiment informativeness;
- causal abstraction / transport maps;
- obligation split/merge maps preserving burden ancestry;
- authority-mode refinements.

Therefore the more faithful mathematical object is a **typed category of inquiry representations and world-contact morphisms**.

A local quotient equivalence may be generated only by morphisms certified to preserve the declared load-bearing target in both relevant directions.

One-way lossy morphisms should induce an order, not an equivalence.

This yields:

~~~text
EQUIVALENCE / QUOTIENT
for target-preserving null transformations

PREORDER / PARTIAL ORDER
for one-way information or authority loss/gain

NONCOMMUTING PATH STRUCTURE
for materially order-sensitive interventions
~~~

A single metric cannot represent these three relations without additional constitution.

## 20. Blackwell and Fisher occupy different atlas roles

Blackwell and Fisher are not two implementations of the same quantity.

### Fisher-type geometry

Within a statistical-model chart, it supplies local differential distinguishability and can be canonical under the relevant sufficient-statistic invariance conditions.

Role:
**LOCAL METRIC GEOMETRY.**

### Blackwell comparison

Across experiments with the same state/decision substrate, it supplies a garbling / decision-dominance order.

Role:
**LOCAL EXPERIMENT PARTIAL ORDER.**

A pair of experiments can be Blackwell-incomparable while each possesses rich Fisher geometry internally.

Therefore:

~~~text
METRIC GEOMETRY
and
EXPERIMENT ORDER
ARE ORTHOGONAL STRUCTURES.
~~~

This is further evidence against searching for one master progress coordinate.

## 21. Constitutive progress theories become rival chart semantics

The philosophy-of-progress literature should not be collapsed into a list of opinions.

It defines rival semantics for what the local order is supposed to preserve.

### Epistemic chart
Bird-style increase in scientific knowledge.

### Noetic chart
Dellsén-style increase in publicly available resources for scientific understanding.

### Semantic/verisimilitude chart
Niiniluoto/Kuipers-style relation to truthlikeness or empirical progress.

### Functional/problem-solving chart
Laudan/Shan-style improvement in defining and solving scientific problems.

### Practice/measurement chart
Chang/Steinle/Beauchemin–Staley-style improvement in measurement, exploration, operational coherence or usefulness.

MQR-4.53 does not choose one by fiat.

It asks a higher-order question:

> under what transformations would each alleged progress relation remain meaningful, and which cross-chart maps preserve enough structure to justify comparison?

That converts the philosophical dispute from “pick the right definition” into a testable **transport and invariance programme**.

## 23. Correction-sensitive progress requires defeat receipts

OPERA-style correction reveals a deeper problem for monotone state orders.

Suppose state A grants a claim high authority.

Later world contact defeats the claim and state B withdraws it.

If progress were simply componentwise authority growth, B would look worse.

The repair is not to invent a hidden truth-distance.

Instead distinguish:

~~~text
AUTHORITY LEVEL
from
DEFEAT-RESPONSIVE WARRANTEDNESS.
~~~

A corrective transition is provisionally progressive when:
- the predecessor authority is defeated by an admissible world-contact receipt;
- the successor state withdraws or localizes the defeated authority;
- the successor preserves the defeat receipt and relevant ancestry;
- no post-hoc scope manipulation hides the loss.

Thus **corrigibility can constitute local progress without a truth oracle**.

This is a central bridge from MQR's defeat/reopening lineage to the progress atlas.

## 24. “Progress enough to stop” likely has no standalone measure

The title asks whether inquiry has a representation-invariant notion of “progress enough to stop.”

The emerging answer is split.

### Progress

Local representation-invariant progress structures can exist.

### Enough

“Enough” is not naturally a level on those structures.

It is indexed by:
- current claim/use contract;
- material live obligation debt;
- available next inquiry actions;
- expected marginal value of those actions;
- opportunity costs;
- reopening reserve.

Hence:

~~~text
PROGRESS CAN BE CONSTITUTIVE.

ENOUGH IS RELATIONAL.

STOPPING IS PROSPECTIVE.
~~~

A Fisher distance travelled, number of problems solved, amount of understanding gained or number of obligations discharged can all be large while a decisive cheap probe remains available.

Conversely a modestly progressed inquiry can rationally stop under a narrow use contract when no currently registered continuation has sufficient marginal value.

Therefore MQR-4.53's strongest possible answer may be:

~~~text
REPRESENTATION-INVARIANT
LOCAL PROGRESS: YES, SOMETIMES.

REPRESENTATION-INVARIANT
GLOBAL “PROGRESS ENOUGH” THRESHOLD:
NO.

CONTRACT-RELATIVE
STOP ADMISSIBILITY REGION:
POSSIBLY YES.
~~~
