## Governing question

MQR-4.53 tests whether there exists any scientifically useful progress object P satisfying all of:

1. **nontriviality** — distinguishes materially different inquiry trajectories;
2. **representation invariance** — ignores admissible redescription and inert serialization changes;
3. **world sensitivity** — changes under materially new world contact;
4. **mode awareness** — does not identify claim closure with probe, use, archive or reopening closure;
5. **path sensitivity where scientifically required** — preserves order effects when interventions/measurements do not commute;
6. **quotientability where scientifically warranted** — collapses path differences that are merely representational;
7. **cross-domain transportability** — admits a principled morphism across domains without pretending that seconds, papers, bits, dollars, samples or obligations are universally commensurable;
8. **stop relevance** — can support a notion of “progress enough to stop” without becoming a final truth oracle.

No scalar is privileged in advance.

## Candidate progress families under equal attack

### P0 — Event Count

~~~text
P_event = number of recorded inquiry transitions
~~~

Known inherited weakness:
checkpoint split/merge can change count without new world contact.

### P1 — Elapsed Physical Time

~~~text
P_time = t - t0
~~~

Potential virtue:
representation-independent under report batching.

Potential weakness:
calendar duration is not scientific contact; waiting and active interrogation can have equal time.

### P2 — Resource / Cost Accumulation

~~~text
P_cost = spent resources
~~~

Potential virtue:
decision-theoretic relevance.

Potential weakness:
unit/currency/infrastructure dependence; expensive failure need not be epistemic progress.

### P3 — Shannon / KL / Mutual-Information Gain

~~~text
P_info = reduction in uncertainty or divergence between prior/posterior states
~~~

Potential virtue:
invariant under many probabilistically equivalent recodings.

Potential weakness:
depends on state-space/prior/model constitution; high information can concern the wrong distinction; identical information gain can differ in intervention/action authority.

### P4 — Expected Information Gain / Value of Information

~~~text
P_eig / P_voi
~~~

Potential virtue:
connects inquiry to future choice.

Potential weakness:
utility/action-model dependence; decision contract can change without new descriptive information.

### P5 — Precision / Fisher-Information / Identifiability Gain

~~~text
P_precision
~~~

Potential virtue:
measurement geometry.

Potential weakness:
precision can increase while bias, construct error, wrong model family or action irrelevance remain.

### P6 — Rival-Separation / Identified-Set Contraction

~~~text
P_sep = contraction of currently live rival set / separation burden
~~~

Potential virtue:
directly tied to discriminability.

Potential weakness:
frontier-relative; hidden rivals and representation changes can expand the set; cardinality/volume depends on parameterization.

### P7 — Obligation Discharge

~~~text
P_obl = reduction of live, inherited, material inquiry obligations
~~~

Potential virtue:
connects directly to MQR's governance lineage.

Potential weakness:
raw obligation count is already known to be partition-sensitive. A geometry must quotient split/merge while preserving burden ancestry and materiality.

### P8 — Intervention / Control Reach

~~~text
P_int = increase in reliable manipulability, controllability, or interventionally distinguishable structure
~~~

Potential virtue:
captures world-coupled action that pure information may miss.

Potential weakness:
greater control over one regime may reduce descriptive generality; control and information need not order each other.

### P9 — Authority-Mode Path Length

~~~text
P_mode = path through
(claim, probe, use, obligation, criterion, reopening)
authority space
~~~

Potential virtue:
faithful to REALTRACE 0.19.

Potential weakness:
coordinate scaling and metric choice may be arbitrary; loops/reversals can have long path length without net progress.

### P10 — World-Contact Vector / Partial Order

~~~text
P_vec = (
  discrimination gain,
  obligation discharge,
  intervention reach,
  action authority,
  calibration/measurement competence,
  reopening competence,
  cost
)
~~~

Potential virtue:
avoids forced commensurability.

Potential weakness:
may fail to produce a total stopping threshold; coordinate constitution itself is contestable.

### P11 — Quotient-State Reachability / Admissible Region

~~~text
PROGRESS is not distance.
PROGRESS = movement into a set of authority states
that dominates or discharges the current claim/use obligations
under the declared world-contact quotient.
~~~

Potential virtue:
invariance can be structural rather than metric.

Potential weakness:
may only yield a preorder or reachability relation, not a measure.

## Presealed transformation / invariance group

A candidate measure is attacked by the following transformations before any literature-derived rescue is allowed.

### T1 — Inert checkpoint refinement

Insert a duplicate checkpoint carrying no new world contact, obligation discharge, decision-contract change, or authority transition.

Required behavior:
progress must not increase merely because the history was serialized more finely.

### T2 — Checkpoint coarsening / batching

Merge adjacent observationally equivalent reports into one packet.

Required behavior:
scientific progress must not depend on whether equivalent contact was logged separately or batched.

### T3 — Monotone time reparameterization

Replace calendar coordinate t with any strictly monotone f(t) while preserving event/world-contact order.

A universal progress *ordering* should survive arbitrary change of time units and nonlinear administrative timestamp scales unless physical duration itself is causally load-bearing.

### T4 — Cost-unit rescaling

Change currency, compute pricing, lab-accounting convention or normalized resource unit by positive affine/ratio-preserving transform.

A cost-aware policy may change under real opportunity-cost change, but not under mere unit recoding.

### T5 — Obligation split

Replace one obligation O carrying burden set B by successors O1...Ok whose declared burden union and ancestry equal B.

Required behavior:
raw progress cannot increase merely because debt was relabeled into more cells.

### T6 — Obligation merge

Merge several carrier obligations while retaining their distinct inherited burden ancestry.

Required behavior:
progress cannot jump because administrative labels were compressed.

### T7 — Evidence batching / sufficient-statistic recoding

Replace raw evidence packets by an information-preserving sufficient representation for the declared target.

Required behavior:
a target-relative progress object should be invariant if all load-bearing distinctions are preserved.

### T8 — Bijective parameter / coordinate recoding

Reparameterize a model or measurement surface by a bijection preserving empirical content.

Required behavior:
progress cannot depend on arbitrary coordinate labels or parameter units.

### T9 — Authority-mode factorization

Represent one binary STOP state as distinct CLAIM_FREEZE / PROBE_ACTIVE / USE_AUTHORIZED / ARCHIVE / REOPEN coordinates.

Required behavior:
a valid progress object must not silently identify materially distinct modes.

### T10 — Path-order permutation

Swap two noncommuting inquiry operations, e.g. intervention before calibration versus calibration before intervention.

Required behavior:
invariance is **not** required when the world-facing consequences differ.
A measure that quotients all order is invalid.

### T11 — Inert path homotopy

Insert/remove a loop of steps that leaves world-contact state, live obligations, decision contract, ancestry and authority mode unchanged.

Required behavior:
net progress should remain unchanged even if raw path length increases.

### T12 — Domain-preserving morphism

Transport a claim/use obligation structure across a domain mapping that preserves:
- load-bearing distinctions;
- ancestry;
- intervention semantics;
- decision/use contract roles;
- reopening triggers.

Required behavior:
only structural progress claims justified by the preserved subspace may transport.

## Representation-invariance versus world-sensitivity firewall

MQR-4.53 distinguishes:

~~~text
INERT REDESCRIPTION
from
MATERIAL WORLD-STATE CHANGE
~~~

A candidate fails if:
- it changes under T1/T2/T4/T5/T6/T7/T8/T11 when the declared world-contact state is preserved;
- or it remains invariant under T10 when operation order materially changes the attainable authority state.

Thus “invariance” is typed, not absolute.

## Information–intervention non-equivalence preseal

Before literature contact, MQR-4.53 freezes the following countermodel target.

Construct two inquiry transitions with equal descriptive information gain about a declared hypothesis family but different intervention consequences:

~~~text
ΔI(A) = ΔI(B)

but

REACH_int(A) != REACH_int(B)
or
USE_AUTH(A) != USE_AUTH(B)
~~~

And conversely construct transitions with similar intervention success but different residual rival/representation uncertainty.

If such countermodels exist, no universal scalar based only on information gain or only on control reach can constitute scientific progress.

## Obligation-discharge geometry

Raw obligation count is forbidden.

Let each live obligation carry:
- burden ancestry;
- materiality class;
- claim/use dependency;
- independent world-contact routes;
- discharge condition;
- reopening condition.

Candidate geometry:

~~~text
O_t = finite burden-ancestry hypergraph
D_t = discharged subgraph
L_t = live material subgraph
~~~

Progress may be represented by a preorder induced by ancestry-preserving discharge:

~~~text
state b >=_O state a
iff
every material burden discharged at a remains discharged at b
and b discharges at least all obligations discharged at a
unless a qualifying reopening event has occurred.
~~~

This is only a candidate. Literature cannot retroactively make it presealed truth.

## Authority-mode geometry

REALTRACE 0.19 state:

~~~text
A_t = (
  claim,
  probe,
  use,
  live obligations,
  criterion,
  reopening
)
~~~

MQR-4.53 tests three non-equivalent notions:

1. **net state order** — whether final authority state dominates initial state under a declared contract;
2. **path length** — cumulative change through authority space;
3. **world-contact reachability** — whether an admissible sequence of probes/interventions can reach a target authority region.

Preseal prediction:
path length alone will fail because loops can be long but epistemically null.

## Stop semantics under attack

MQR-4.53 rejects the assumption that stopping must be triggered by:

~~~text
P >= theta
~~~

Alternatives under equal consideration:

### Scalar threshold
`P >= theta`

### Vector admissible region
`P_vec in A_stop`

### Obligation closure
all currently admitted material obligations for the declared claim/use are discharged.

### Reachability fixed point
additional admissible world-contact actions do not change the current claim/use authority region within the registered envelope.

### Pareto frontier
no admissible continuation improves one load-bearing authority coordinate without worsening another beyond the decision contract.

### Decision-relative VOI stop
expected action value of further inquiry falls below declared opportunity cost.

No one is privileged before the court.

## Promotion criteria

A candidate progress object may be promoted only if:

C1. survives all transformations declared inert for its scope;

C2. reacts to at least one material world-contact change that an inert transform cannot mimic;

C3. does not collapse information and intervention when a frozen countermodel separates them;

C4. survives obligation split/merge without count laundering;

C5. preserves noncommuting path-order effects when intervention order matters;

C6. supports at least one nonvacuous stop/admissibility statement;

C7. transports across at least two heterogeneous domains only through an explicit structure-preserving morphism;

C8. does not imply final truth, world completeness or a universal ontology;

C9. is not rescued by post-literature redefinition after a failure unless the repair is versioned as a successor representation and the original failure remains recorded.

## Strong failure conditions

MQR-4.53 must be willing to close with **NO REPRESENTATION-INVARIANT PROGRESS MEASURE** if:

1. every nontrivial scalar fails some admissible inert transformation;
2. only a vector/preorder survives but cannot support a unique stop threshold;
3. cross-domain mappings preserve only partial order, not magnitude;
4. information, intervention, obligation discharge and action authority form genuinely incomparable axes;
5. metric choice on authority-mode space remains arbitrary;
6. stop competence can only be stated as membership in a contract-relative admissible region.

That outcome is scientifically acceptable.
