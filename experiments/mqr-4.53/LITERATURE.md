## D. Literature-level novelty kills

MQR-4.53 cannot claim novelty for:
- defining scientific progress via knowledge, understanding, truthlikeness or problem solving;
- distinguishing progress from expected promotion of progress;
- information-theoretic experiment design;
- decision-theoretic value of information;
- model-discrimination design;
- partial ordering of experiments by informativeness;
- statistical geometry invariant under sufficient statistics;
- causal active learning/intervention design;
- measurement meaningfulness via permissible transformations;
- localism/pluralism in realism;
- structural transport conditions across domains.

## E. Surviving candidate research contribution

After the current novelty kill, the candidate MQR excess is not a new scalar measure of progress.

It is the synthesis:

~~~text
REPRESENTATION-INVARIANCE CONSTITUTION
+ WORLD-CONTACT QUOTIENT
+ LOCAL PROGRESS GEOMETRIES / PREORDERS
+ CORRECTION-SENSITIVE AUTHORITY CHANGE
+ INFORMATION–INTERVENTION NON-EQUIVALENCE

WORLD-CONTACT PROGRESS ATLAS
~~~

The hypothesis is that science may admit **globally governed local progress structures** without a global progress ruler.
