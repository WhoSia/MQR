## 1. Previously Harvested material recovered after the first 4.53 radar

### MQR-0.7-B — Representation, Invariance, Equivalence & Gauge

Already established:
- meaningful quantitative representation is tied to invariance under permissible transformations;
- mathematical equivalence alone does not establish sameness of physical content;
- empirical coupling and interpretation remain load-bearing.

4.53 import:
the progress court is partly a **meaningfulness court**. A progress statement whose truth/ranking changes under scientifically inert permissible transformations cannot carry authority at that scale.

### MQR-3.100 — Unknown-Facing Measurement Portfolios / Blackwell

Already established:
- Blackwell-style dominance is question-independent only relative to a common state space;
- deterministic dominance reduces to quotient refinement by post-processing;
- Blackwell order is generally partial;
- incomparability can motivate portfolios rather than scalar score invention;
- mutual information is not a universal replacement for Blackwell dominance.

4.53 import:
Blackwell order is a pre-existing MQR local-progress chart, not a new 4.53 invention.

### MQR-3.101 — Cross-Ontology Experiment Comparison

Already established:
- experiments over different state spaces are not type-correctly Blackwell-comparable without a bridge;
- a common refinement/lifting can fail or install a hidden master ontology;
- transport authority depends on explicit bridge semantics.

4.53 import:
cross-domain progress magnitude cannot be presumed merely because local charts exist.

### RITHM M05.19 — Incomparable Witness Routes / Blackwell / Cost

Already established:
- witness routes can be incomparable under informativeness and cost;
- experiment cost is a separate policy coordinate rather than an epistemic scalar.

4.53 import:
cost belongs naturally in continuation policy/frontier selection but is not automatically constitutive progress.

### RITHM M04.32 — Target-Sufficient Quotient

Already established:
- coarsest exact quotients are target-relative;
- a representation sufficient for one target need not be sufficient for another.

4.53 import:
the world-contact quotient must state its target/use surface; "representation-invariant" never means target-free.

### EvoNOMOS DIP-9 — Intervention–Observation Entanglement

Already established:
- an intervention changes both the system and what can subsequently be observed;
- information gain and intervention value can have different signs/roles;
- architecture changes can alter regime and identifiability.

4.53 import:
information and intervention progress cannot be silently collapsed.

### Literature Fragment Harvest XLIII — Experiment Selection as Scientific Authority

Reusable fragments:
- search-space authorship is upstream of active-learning value;
- expected information gain is model-family dependent;
- optimization value, epistemic value, transport value and safety value are distinct;
- more experiments does not imply more information.

4.53 import:
a progress measure cannot inherit authority from an acquisition function whose target/search space is itself contestable.

### Literature Fragment Harvest XVI — Partial Identification / Decision-Relative Information Value

Reusable fragments:
- set identification can rationally yield dominance, regret bounds or unresolved action rather than a point winner;
- information value depends on which ambiguity face is decision-binding.

4.53 import:
set-valued/partial-order progress is not a failure to measure; forced scalarization can destroy the actual decision structure.

## 2. Newly verified external anchors

### Blackwell (1951, 1953)

- *Comparison of Experiments* (1951), DOI `10.1525/9780520411586-009`.
- *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments* (1953), DOI `10.1214/aoms/1177729032`.

Verified role:
experiment informativeness admits an order characterized through decision performance / garbling rather than requiring one scalar information number.

### Torgersen (1991), Comparison of Statistical Experiments

Book DOI: `10.1017/CBO9780511666353`.

Verified role:
comparison is generally partial; deficiency quantifies approximate loss relative to a shared parameter set / decision structure.

4.53 pressure:
even when a numerical deficiency exists, it remains typed to the experiment-comparison constitution.

### Lindley (1956)

*On a Measure of the Information Provided by an Experiment*.
DOI `10.1214/aoms/1177728069`.

Verified role:
expected information is prior-relative and was proposed for experiment design aimed at learning rather than direct decision.

### Bernardo (1979)

*Expected Information as Expected Utility*.
DOI `10.1214/aos/1176344689`.

Verified role:
expected Shannon-information design is representable as a special expected-utility problem under constraints.

4.53 pressure:
information design is not independent of all normative/decision structure.

### Bird (2007)

*What Is Scientific Progress?*
DOI `10.1111/j.1468-0068.2007.00638.x`.

Verified role:
knowledge accumulation is a canonical constitutive rival.

### Dellsén (2016, 2021, 2025)

- 2016 *Scientific Progress: Knowledge versus Understanding*, DOI `10.1016/j.shpsa.2016.01.003`.
- 2021 *Understanding Scientific Progress: The Noetic Account*, DOI `10.1007/s11229-021-03289-z`.
- 2025 *Promoting Scientific Progress*, DOI `10.1007/s11098-025-02335-7`.
- 2025 *Scientific Progress: Normative, but Aimless*, DOI `10.1007/s44204-025-00264-0`.

Verified roles:
- understanding can diverge from knowledge accumulation;
- progress constitution and progress promotion are distinct;
- normativity about progress need not presuppose a single overarching aim of science.

4.53 consequence:
a universal ruler cannot be justified merely by invoking one global aim of science.

### Rowbottom (2023)

*Scientific Progress*, Cambridge Elements in Philosophy of Science.
DOI `10.1017/9781108625753`.

Verified role:
meta-normative pressure on where progress standards come from.

4.53 consequence:
the source of standards is itself a constitutional question.

### Niiniluoto (2014)

*Scientific Progress as Increasing Verisimilitude*.
DOI `10.1016/j.shpsa.2014.02.002`.

Verified role:
truthlikeness remains a serious constitutive rival and distinguishes real progress from evidence-relative estimated progress.

4.53 consequence:
MQR may not identify its operational atlas with truth distance.

### Ay, Jost, Lê & Schwachhöfer

*Information Geometry and Sufficient Statistics*.
DOI `10.1007/s00440-014-0574-8`.

Verified role:
Fisher metric and Amari–Chentsov tensor have characterization results tied to invariance under sufficient statistics / statistical transformations.

4.53 consequence:
this kills any blanket anti-geometry thesis and supplies a positive local-invariance control.

### Hauser & Bühlmann (2014)

*Two Optimal Strategies for Active Learning of Causal Models from Interventional Data*.
DOI `10.1016/j.ijar.2013.11.007`.

Verified role:
interventional design improves causal identifiability in a target-dependent way; intervention value depends on intervention targets and learning objective.

### Rauh et al. (2017)

*Coarse-graining and the Blackwell order*.
arXiv:1701.07602.

Verified role:
Blackwell order and mutual-information-based capability orders are non-equivalent.

4.53 consequence:
"more bits" is weaker than decision dominance.

### Luce (2022) / measurement meaningfulness lineage

*Dimensionally Invariant Numerical Laws Correspond to Meaningful Qualitative Relations*.

Verified role:
formal measurement theory connects meaningful numerical claims to invariance under admissible representation transformations.

4.53 consequence:
progress-number meaningfulness must be typed to permissible transformations.
