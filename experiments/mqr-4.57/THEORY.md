# MQR-4.57 — Strategic Discovery-Ecology Theory

Status: **POST-REVEAL / PRE-CLOSURE / QUALIFYING-SAME-HEAD-4_OF_4-PASS / WCSER-UNDER-PROMOTION**

## 1. The problem after WCEPR

MQR-4.56 showed that a search programme can prefer some challenge directions without invoking a universal scientific utility function.

But that result still treated the value-relevant inputs as if they were available for audit independently of the strategic ecology that produced them.

MQR-4.57 attacks that remaining idealization.

~~~text
GOOD LOCAL EXPANSION POLICY
!=
ROBUST DISCOVERY ECOLOGY
~~~

when agents can adapt to:
- funding rules;
- publication rules;
- ranking criteria;
- target definitions;
- proxy definitions;
- cost-reporting incentives;
- receipt-admission criteria.

## 2. Strategic endogeneity is upstream of valuation

Suppose WCEPR receives:

~~~text
C = candidate challenges
V = typed value profiles
O = opportunity costs
T = target/use contract
Π_V = value provenance
~~~

MQR-4.56 asks whether a local comparison over those objects is licensed.

MQR-4.57 asks a different question:

> Why did these objects appear in this form?

If the answer depends on rewards, reputation, funding, selection rules or coalition behavior, then the search surface is endogenous to the institution.

Thus:

~~~text
VALUATION ENDOGENEITY
IS NOT THE SAME AS
SUPPLY ENDOGENEITY.
~~~

4.56 handles the first.
4.57 handles the second.

## 3. The discovery ecology

The live object is:

~~~text
Ξ = (A, Θ, M, Ω, Σ, U, Φ, X, R)
~~~

This is not a sociological total theory.

It is a minimal audit structure.

### A — agents and roles
Who can:
- propose;
- withhold;
- report;
- rank;
- fund;
- publish;
- define targets;
- define proxies;
- ratify revisions?

### Θ — private information
What relevant facts may be privately known?
- cost;
- competence;
- feasibility;
- expected payoff;
- target dependence;
- strategic intent.

### M — mechanism
What institutional rule maps reports and actions into:
- attention;
- money;
- publication;
- prestige;
- authority?

### Ω — inherited WCEPR
What local challenge-value constitution is being used?

### Σ — strategic action surface
What can agents manipulate?
- challenge supply;
- timing;
- bundling;
- splitting;
- cost reports;
- target framing;
- proxy selection;
- identity;
- coalition behavior.

### U — incentives
What does each agent gain or lose?

### Φ — performative feedback
How does the mechanism alter the future population, data, projects, methods and targets?

### X — exterior/mechanism-counterfactual challenge
What can expose mechanism-induced deformation?

### R — reopening
How does evidence of strategic adaptation reopen the constitution?

## 4. Observed supply is not the frontier

The strongest conceptual shift is:

~~~text
OBSERVED CHALLENGE SET
IS AN OUTCOME
OF A MECHANISM.
~~~

This defeats a hidden assumption that survived even open-frontier work.

A challenge can be absent because:
- no one conceived it;
- it is technically impossible;
- it is too expensive;
- the institution under-rewards it;
- it threatens an incumbent;
- the target is framed to exclude it;
- the reporting mechanism hides it.

These explanations are scientifically different.

Therefore absence requires provenance.

## 5. Mechanisms can be scientific interventions

MQR usually treats world-contact through measurement, intervention, transport and counterfactual replay.

MQR-4.57 adds:

~~~text
INSTITUTIONAL RULE CHANGE
CAN BE AN INTERVENTION
ON THE DISCOVERY PROCESS.
~~~

If the same research population under a changed funding/publication/attention rule supplies a different challenge portfolio, the difference is evidence about institutional endogeneity.

This is not merely policy analysis.

It can reveal which scientific distinctions the current institution suppresses or amplifies.

## 6. Performativity generalizes beyond prediction

Performative prediction supplies a precise template:

~~~text
MODEL
-> DECISION
-> DISTRIBUTION SHIFT
-> NEW MODEL INPUT
~~~

MQR-4.57 generalizes the shape, not the theorem:

~~~text
SCIENTIFIC MECHANISM
-> AGENT ADAPTATION
-> SEARCH-ECOLOGY SHIFT
-> NEW EVIDENCE ABOUT MECHANISM
~~~

The central warning is the same:

The evaluator may change the object it evaluates.

## 7. Equilibrium is only a behavioral property

A strategic ecology may converge.

That can mean:
- agents found stable best responses;
- institutions stabilized;
- reports became predictable;
- policy stopped moving.

None of these implies:

~~~text
THE RIGHT QUESTIONS
ARE BEING ASKED.
~~~

Hence the 4.57 ceiling:

~~~text
EQUILIBRIUM
!=
EPISTEMIC ADEQUACY.
~~~

A bad-science equilibrium can be behaviorally stable and scientifically poor.

## 8. Mechanism optimality is local

MQR-4.57 preserves mechanism design.

If:
- utilities are declared;
- information structure is declared;
- costs are verifiable;
- domain and horizon are fixed;

then one mechanism may be locally preferable.

But:

~~~text
LOCALLY INCENTIVE-COMPATIBLE
-/-> UNIVERSAL SCIENCE INSTITUTION.
~~~

Different domains may face:
- different private information;
- different cost structures;
- different replication needs;
- different exploration/exploitation tradeoffs;
- different ethical constraints;
- different target structures.

Mechanism plurality is therefore expected.

## 9. Cost becomes a strategic measurement problem

MQR-4.56 needed opportunity cost.

MQR-4.57 shows why cost cannot simply be read from a form.

~~~text
REPORTED COST
!=
REALIZED COST
!=
SOCIAL COST.
~~~

Scientific cost therefore needs:
- declared units;
- realized-cost follow-up;
- attribution of induced administrative/competitive effort;
- reopening when reports are systematically biased.

This turns cost from a policy parameter into a defeasible measurement object.

## 10. Target revision needs constitutional timing

Target drift can be legitimate.

Scientific goals genuinely change.

The danger is temporal:

~~~text
BAD:
OUTCOME REVEAL
-> TARGET REWRITE
-> FAILURE DISAPPEARS

GOOD:
OUTCOME REVEAL
-> TARGET-CHANGE PROPOSAL
-> INDEPENDENT RATIFICATION
-> NEW RECEIPT
~~~

MQR-4.57 therefore adds timing/provenance to target revision.

## 11. Strategic ancestry

MQR already uses ancestry for:
- evidence;
- challenge generation;
- valuation.

4.57 extends this to incentives.

Two agents are not strategically independent merely because they have:
- different names;
- different institutions;
- different models;
- different challenge packets.

If their payoffs are coupled by the same:
- funding stream;
- publication metric;
- owner;
- coalition;
- prestige system;

they can remain common-mode.

Thus the relevant quotient is:

~~~text
NOMINAL AGENT
/
MATERIAL INCENTIVE ANCESTRY.
~~~

## 12. Transparency creates adaptation

Open constitutions are valuable because they permit audit.

But transparent rules are learnable.

Therefore the programme rejects two naive responses.

### Naive response A
Hide the rules so agents cannot game them.

This undermines auditability.

### Naive response B
Publish fixed rules forever.

This permits adaptive gaming.

The surviving principle is:

~~~text
TRANSPARENT
+
REOPENABLE.
~~~

A constitution may be public while retaining explicit triggers for revision when adaptation changes its meaning.

## 13. Exterior randomness is a reserve, not a religion

Randomized funding/search has several possible epistemic functions:
- weaken strategic prediction;
- expose hidden challenges;
- sample neglected regions;
- reduce contest dissipation.

MQR-4.57 does not promote randomness universally.

It treats a randomized/exterior route as one possible **reserve** against incumbent-mechanism closure.

Thus:

~~~text
RANDOMIZATION
CAN BE A CHALLENGE ROUTE

WITHOUT
BEING A UNIVERSAL
SCIENTIFIC ALLOCATION RULE.
~~~

- reported-vs-realized cost audit;

A passing WCSER says:

~~~text
THIS LOCAL EXPANSION POLICY
HAS SURVIVED
THE DECLARED STRATEGIC ECOLOGY
AND ITS COUNTERFACTUAL TESTS.
~~~

It does not say:

~~~text
THIS INSTITUTION
IS OPTIMAL FOR SCIENCE.
~~~

## 16. Successor-facing remainder

MQR-4.57 still assumes that a strategic ecology can be represented at a useful finite grain.

A deeper successor can attack:
- endogenous agent formation;
- coalition birth/death;
- mechanism co-evolution;
- endogenous utility change;
- strategic ontology creation;
- institutional self-modification.

The surviving debt is therefore:

~~~text
ROBUSTNESS TO
STRATEGIC RESPONSE
!=
ROBUSTNESS TO
ENDOGENOUS ACTOR /
MECHANISM CREATION.
~~~

That issue does not reopen MQR-4.57.
