# MQR-4.2 Masked Claim Packets — Frozen

## Rules
- Evaluators receive only one arm instruction plus this packet file.
- Packet IDs contain no domain/failure hints.
- Do not infer the benchmark's expected answer.
- Preserve each packet's local terminology.
- No new evidence may be requested unless the arm's rules explicitly justify it.

## K17
A published study reports an adjusted association between exposure X and outcome Y using a single-wave observational dataset. Exposure and outcome were measured at the same assessment. The study reports covariate adjustment but no randomization, natural experiment, instrumental variable, longitudinal temporal ordering, or other independent causal-identification strategy. Proposed manuscript sentence: "Exposure X increases outcome Y."

Scope: sampled population and measurement regime described in the study.
Known limitations: residual confounding and reverse causality remain plausible.

## M42
A therapy received conditional regulatory authorization based on an intermediate/surrogate response measure judged sufficient for accelerated action. Continued authorization was explicitly contingent on later evidence verifying clinical benefit. Proposed communication sentence immediately after initial authorization: "The therapy has been shown to improve clinical outcomes."

Successor event: the required confirmatory study later fails to verify the predicted clinical benefit and changes the benefit-risk assessment.

## Q08
A computational model has been compared with referent evidence and judged adequate for a specified intended use over a declared validation domain D. A later team proposes using the model in a materially different operating regime outside D without new referent evidence. Proposed statement: "The validated model is accurate in the new regime."

Known qualification: no direct validation evidence exists in the new regime.

## R31
A measurement result is metrologically traceable through a documented calibration chain. The intended decision has a tolerance/decision boundary. The stated measurement uncertainty is large enough that fitness for the intended decision has not yet been demonstrated. Proposed statement: "Because the result is traceable, it is fit for the intended decision."

## T55
A well-being questionnaire has strong internal consistency, expected factor structure, and correlations with related questionnaires. The intended interpretation is that the score measures a broad normative construct of well-being. The validation dossier contains little explicit argument connecting the questionnaire content to the normative theory of the construct. Proposed statement: "The questionnaire validly measures well-being."

## V26
A prediction model shows imperfect probability calibration in a region that is outside the predeclared clinical action threshold range. Within the action-relevant range, calibration and net-benefit analysis remain acceptable for the specified use. Proposed statement: "The model remains useful for this scoped decision."

Known qualification: global probability calibration is imperfect.
