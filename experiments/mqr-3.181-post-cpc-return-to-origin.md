After MQR-3.180, CPC/CRF distinctness is closed for now.

The question is:

> After quotienting MQR by severe testing, robustness/independence, provenance, assurance/defeater management, reproducibility, context-of-use credibility, reliabilist defeat, perspectival realism, epistemic iteration, and structural realism, does any irreducible **realist content** remain?

No new primitive may be introduced to save MQR.

## 2. Coordinate-by-coordinate reduction

### 2.1 World-contact
World-contact is not MQR-specific.
Empirical contact, prediction, intervention, validation, severe testing, model applicability, and evidential inference are already central across mature frameworks.

**REDUCTION:** world-contact → empirical testing / applicability / evidential support.

### 2.2 Defeat capacity
The requirement that evidence must have been capable of exposing error is strongly reduced by Mayo-style severity.
The requirement that justification can be defeated by available or environmental counterevidence is also native to defeater-sensitive reliabilism and contemporary epistemology.

**REDUCTION:** live defeat → severity + defeater-sensitive justification.

### 2.3 Noncommonness
Independence, robustness, triangulation, common-cause dependence, and correlated evidence already carry the core content.

**REDUCTION:** noncommonness → evidence-independence / robustness / dependence structure.

### 2.4 Replay / reconstruction capacity
Scientific-workflow reproducibility, provenance, preservation, executable research objects, and environment capture already represent whether a challenge can be rerun or reconstructed.

**REDUCTION:** replay/reconstruction → reproducibility/provenance infrastructure.

### 2.5 Context-sensitive authority
ASME V&V 40-family frameworks explicitly make credibility relative to question of interest, model role, other evidence, decision consequence, and context of use.

**REDUCTION:** scoped authority → context-of-use credibility.

### 2.6 Historical path
MQR itself already adopted:
history matters only insofar as it changes present burden or present defeat capacity.

Therefore history is not an independent primitive after quotienting.

[
H_t 	o (D_t,R_t,N_t,	ext{current burden})
]

If history leaves no present difference, it has no independent authority effect.

**REDUCTION:** historical path → present reachable state.

## 3. Strongest remaining philosophical candidate
After these reductions, the strongest possible MQR-specific claim is:

> Realist authority is a temporally indexed epistemic state that is not determined by proposition content or currently displayed evidence alone, but by the current structure of error-detection, defeat, and reliable access to the world.

Call this the **Temporal Defeat-State Thesis (TDST)** only as a diagnostic label, not as a new primitive.

Formally:

[
(Content,Evidence)_A=(Content,Evidence)_B
]

does not by itself imply

[
A_t(A)=A_t(B)
]

if the two cases differ in relevant live defeat/reliability conditions.

## 4. Fatal attack on TDST
TDST is not cleanly irreducible.

### 4.1 Reliabilism / tracking
Reliabilist epistemology already denies that epistemic status supervenes only on internally displayed evidence.
External reliability and defeater structure can alter justification while phenomenology/evidence presentation remains fixed.

Roush's *Tracking Truth* explicitly connects truth-tracking accounts of knowledge/evidence to scientific realism.

### 4.2 Defeater theory
Contemporary epistemology explicitly permits a belief formed by a reliable process to lose justification when relevant defeating information or defeating environmental conditions arise.

Thus “same proposition + same apparent evidence, different epistemic status because defeat conditions differ” is not proprietary to MQR.

### 4.3 Epistemic iteration
Chang's epistemic iteration already treats scientific justification as historically revisable, self-correcting, and non-foundational rather than frozen at a single evidential moment.

### 4.4 Perspectival realism
Massimi already combines realism with historically and epistemically situated scientific perspectives and treats reliability as emerging through plural, intersecting practices.

### 4.5 Structural realism
Worrall already constrains realist commitment in response to theory change by relocating commitment to what survives epistemic transitions.

**TDST VERDICT: STRONGLY REDUCED / NO CLEAN IRREDUCIBLE REALIST NOVELTY ESTABLISHED.**

## 6. Minimal constitutional compression
MQR can now be compressed to four clauses.

### MQR-C1 — Contact
No realist authority without scoped world-contact relevant to the claim.

### MQR-C2 — Defeat
World-contact earns authority only relative to materially capable routes by which the load-bearing claim could have failed.

### MQR-C3 — State
Authority is indexed to the present reachable epistemic state, including current dependence, reconstruction, and defeat conditions; historical ancestry matters only through present state or explicit unresolved burden.

### MQR-C4 — Firewall
Methodological success, executability, provenance, robustness, or institutional acceptance do not individually entail realist promotion. Promotion is licensed only by the composed state, and HOLD is a valid terminal verdict.

These clauses summarize MQR without claiming each clause is individually novel.

## 9. Consequence for Generation IV
Generation IV is still not authorized.

A future promotion would require at least one of:

1. **Composition non-reducibility:** prove that no ordinary federation of mature frameworks can reproduce an MQR authority verdict without importing an MQR-specific cross-framework state or rule.
2. **Prospective differential:** preseal a case where mature frameworks individually and jointly license the same verdict but MQR prospectively predicts a different world-contact outcome or authority transition.
3. **Formal minimality:** show the four-clause constitution is minimal under deletion, with each clause producing an externally checkable failure when removed.
4. **Historical/external discrimination:** identify a real scientific episode where the MQR constitution explains a correction/promotion/refusal pattern that the federation systematically misclassifies.

Until one is obtained:
**NO MQR-4.0.**
