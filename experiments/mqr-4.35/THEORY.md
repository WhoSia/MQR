# MQR-4.35 — Probe-Relative Sufficiency Theory

## 1. Why the 4.34 authority compiler is not the target

If `A = f(MU)` by definition, asking whether identical MU gives identical A merely checks the compiler. MQR-4.35 therefore defines an independent empirical target `E_P`, where `P` is a frozen family of direct probes and `E_P(w)` records how far bridge-mediated predictions survive direct execution.

The court is about whether MU preserves all information relevant to this target.

## 2. Factorization criterion for support reach

Let:

- `W` be the admitted world set;
- `q_R : W -> R` be a receipt representation;
- `E_P : W -> E` be empirical support reach under frozen probe algebra P.

Call `q_R` **P-sufficient for support reach on W** iff there exists a map `g : R -> E` such that

`E_P = g o q_R`.

For a finite deterministic court this is equivalent to the fiber condition

`q_R(x) = q_R(y) => E_P(x) = E_P(y)`.

Equivalently, the partition induced by `q_R` must refine the partition induced by `E_P`.

A same-receipt opposite-reach pair is therefore a direct factorization counterexample.

This is an MQR-specific finite deterministic analogue of a sufficiency criterion; it is not claimed to be the Fisher-Neyman theorem, which concerns statistical families and conditional distributions/likelihood factorization.

## 3. Result of the forcing court

The prospective forcing court was frozen to require collision counts

`NU0,NU1,NU2,NU3 = 3,2,1,0`.

Run `36140733936` reproduced exactly that vector.

Therefore:

- `NU0 = MU` is not P-sufficient even on the forcing court;
- adding map algebra alone is insufficient;
- adding intervention alignment as well is still insufficient;
- `NU3 = MU + map algebra + intervention alignment + sensitivity scope` is the coarsest **predeclared** candidate that separates the forcing corpus.

The universal sufficiency claim for MU is defeated. NU3 is not thereby universally sufficient.

## 4. What the collision means

MQR-4.34 was correct that support authority is relational rather than Boolean. MQR-4.35 shows that **relation type alone is still too coarse**.

Two bridge-direct systems can share the same declared domain relation, semantic-map class, component relation, quotient relation, state relation and endpoint contract while differing in whether composition actually preserves the target probe family.

Thus the object carrying support cannot be only a taxonomic relation label. It must also be sensitive to the mechanism by which maps compose, how interventions align across levels/domains, and where the support boundary lies.

## 5. Why this is not radical skepticism

The result does not say that every finite representation is epistemically useless or that no support relation can ever be learned.

It says:

1. sufficiency is always relative to an explicit target and probe/decision family;
2. a representation earns authority by surviving attempts to find collisions in its fibers;
3. new probes can refine the empirical partition and defeat a previously adequate quotient;
4. absence of a collision in a finite corpus licenses only observed separation, not proof that no future collision exists.

This is an open-world fallibilist claim, not a global empirical-equivalence thesis.

## 6. Minimality is court-relative

`NU3` is minimal only in the ordered candidate ladder frozen before the forcing reveal. MQR-4.35 does not claim that NU3 is mathematically minimal among every conceivable representation.

The strongest legitimate statement is:

`NU3 = COARSEST_PREDECLARED_FORCING_SEPARATOR`.

To earn a stronger measurement status, NU3 must survive independently admitted naturalistic worlds and new probe families without post-reveal coordinate invention.

## 7. Composition is now an empirical object

The central shift is:

`bridge support + bridge support -> direct support`

is not treated as a logical transitivity rule.

Instead MQR measures whether a declared composition diagram commutes on a specified probe family, and if not, localizes the failure to a predeclared mechanism coordinate.

This turns composition support from an assumed algebraic convenience into a defeasible empirical claim.

## 8. Claim ceiling

Earned on the forcing court:

- same-MU opposite-reach collisions exist;
- MU universal sufficiency is false;
- the predeclared NU3 ladder separates the forcing court;
- empirical support reach can be formalized independently of the authority compiler.

Not earned:

- NU3 universal sufficiency;
- a unique minimal sufficient morphism;
- naturalistic prevalence of MU collisions;
- universal composition law;
- universal causal abstraction theorem;
- truth distance or scalar realist strength.
