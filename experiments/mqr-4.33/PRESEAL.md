B -> C PASS
sealed mediated A -> C prediction
held-out direct A -> C PASS
path congruent at the declared quotient

This did not establish general transitivity.

MQR-4.33 therefore begins from the adverse question:

**What is the smallest prospective world in which adjacent transport survives but composition fails, diverges, or requires a split?**

The purpose is not to collect more green triangles.
The purpose is to map the boundary on which a green triangle is licensed.

## Composition failure types

4.33 distinguishes four phenomena.

### C0 — scoped composition pass

Adjacent edges survive, the mediated target is well typed, direct A -> C survives, and the mediated/direct paths agree at the declared quotient.

Verdict:
COMPOSITION_PASS.

### C1 — generalization nontransitivity witness

Adjacent edges survive on their frozen bridge surfaces, but the independently frozen direct A -> C surface fails.

Verdict:
NONTRANSITIVITY_WITNESS.

This is not a proof that equality itself is nontransitive.
It is a witness that a **transport predicate quantified over a support domain** failed to generalize transitively.

### C2 — path-divergent pass

Direct A -> C survives at the declared endpoint claim, but the mediated A -> B -> C path produces a materially different endpoint representation, refinement, uncertainty state, or downstream consequence.

Verdict:
PATH_DIVERGENT_PASS.

### C3 — split/loss obstruction

The mediated path deletes or conflates a distinction required at C, or target identity cannot be preserved without splitting the claim.

Verdict:
COMPOSITION_SPLIT_REQUIRED or NO_CANDIDATE.

## Frozen structural support classifier

The classifier sees only pre-direct structural metadata.

### S0 — STRICT_SUPPORT

Predict COMPOSITION_PASS only if all hold:

1. component identity is SAME_COMPONENT or an explicitly declared SAME_QUOTIENT;
2. the adjacent bridge surfaces cover the direct target domain rather than merely sampling disjoint/nonnested subdomains;
3. no load-bearing information required at C is lost on either edge;
4. hidden state is absent or explicitly reset before every execution;
5. adapter composition is commutative by construction at the declared claim scope;
6. direct observable and mediated observable use the same declared endpoint contract;
7. the direct surface is independently admissible under the 4.32 independence rule.

Any unknown on items 1–6 prevents STRICT_SUPPORT.

### S1 — GENERALIZATION_RISK

Predict NONTRANSITIVITY_WITNESS pressure when:

- adjacent PASS is established on bridge surfaces that do not cover the frozen direct surface;
- or the direct surface contains a predeclared boundary family absent from both adjacent bridge surfaces;
- while component identity is otherwise meaningful enough to test.

This prediction concerns support-domain failure, not logical nontransitivity of equality.

### S2 — PATH_RISK

Predict PATH_DIVERGENT_PASS or COMPOSITION_SPLIT_REQUIRED pressure when:

- the A -> B -> C adapter chain is order-sensitive;
- or an intermediate normalization/rounding/refinement is lossy;
- or C introduces a distinction not representable in B;
- or state carried through B can change C while direct A -> C is reset.

### S3 — UNRESOLVED

No positive support claim is allowed.

Verdict prediction:
HOLD.

## Known-outcome forcing worlds

Before any fresh external outcome is inspected, 4.33 will materialize diagnostic forcing worlds.

These are not external confirmation.

F0 — exact/stateless/nested positive control:
must yield COMPOSITION_PASS.

F1 — coverage mismatch:
adjacent bridges pass on frozen subdomains; one held-out boundary case fails direct.
must yield NONTRANSITIVITY_WITNESS.

F2 — double-rounding/order world:
direct A -> C endpoint passes at coarse scope, while A -> B -> C differs because B rounds before C.
must yield PATH_DIVERGENT_PASS.

F3 — lossy refinement world:
B deletes a distinction required by C.
must yield COMPOSITION_SPLIT_REQUIRED or NO_CANDIDATE.

If the Rust algebra cannot classify these four worlds correctly, external 4.33 promotion is forbidden.

## Deterministic minimal-witness algorithm

The minimizer is frozen before external outcomes.

### Nontransitivity witness minimization

Given a frozen direct fixture list in canonical lexical order:

1. hold A/B/C regimes and adjacent receipts fixed;
2. attempt deletion of each direct fixture in lexical order;
3. retain a deletion iff the same NONTRANSITIVITY_WITNESS verdict survives;
4. iterate until no deletion is retained.

The result is a 1-minimal direct witness under this deletion order.

No new fixture may be added after reveal.

### Path-divergence minimization

1. minimize direct/mediated input cases by the same lexical deletion rule;
2. then remove optional adapter operations in declared order while preserving PATH_DIVERGENT_PASS;
3. mandatory regime transitions A -> B -> C may not be removed.

This is diagnostic compression, not a new discovery test.

## Strong falsifiers

The support-domain hypothesis is defeated or must contract if any occurs:

- an S0 STRICT_SUPPORT triangle becomes NONTRANSITIVITY_WITNESS;
- an S0 STRICT_SUPPORT triangle becomes PATH_DIVERGENT_PASS for an undeclared reason;
- an S1 GENERALIZATION_RISK triangle repeatedly composes across independent held-out boundary surfaces;
- an S2 PATH_RISK triangle repeatedly commutes under conditions claimed to force order/loss sensitivity;
- research and engineering lineages require incompatible support predicates that cannot be expressed by the frozen structural variables.

## Claim ceiling

4.33 can at most earn a discoverable **support domain for the typed composition predicate**.

It cannot establish:
- universal transport transitivity;
- universal category/functor laws;
- ontological identity of regimes;
- truth by software compatibility;
- scientific authority from repository engineering quality.
