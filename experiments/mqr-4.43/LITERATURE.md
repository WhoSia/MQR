## Search / allocation theory

### Weitzman 1979

Martin L. Weitzman, “Optimal Search for the Best Alternative,” *Econometrica* 47(3), 1979, 641–654. DOI: `10.2307/1910412`.

Machinery donor and boundary witness: optimal search becomes determinate after probability distributions, payoff/reward structure, and search costs are specified. It does not authorize an optimal policy over unconceived rivals when those inputs have not been constituted.

### Bayesian experimental design

Kathryn Chaloner and Isabella Verdinelli, “Bayesian Experimental Design: A Review,” *Statistical Science* 10(3), 1995. DOI: `10.1214/ss/1177009939`.

Strong donor for the explicit-decision-contract branch. Prior and utility structure make expected-utility design meaningful; this supports separating `DECLARED_MODEL_ONLY` optimization from open-world scientific optimality.

### Multi-armed bandits

Peter Auer, Nicolò Cesa-Bianchi and Paul Fischer, “Finite-time Analysis of the Multiarmed Bandit Problem,” *Machine Learning* 47, 2002. DOI: `10.1023/A:1013689704352`.

Donor for explicit exploration/exploitation policies and finite-time guarantees. Regret/reward comparison is relative to a constituted arm/reward problem and does not certify completeness of the arm/generator ontology.

### No-Free-Lunch pressure

David H. Wolpert and William G. Macready, “No Free Lunch Theorems for Optimization,” *IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation* 1(1), 1997. DOI: `10.1109/4235.585893`.

General prior art against an unrestricted universally privileged optimizer. MQR-4.43's twin-world result is intentionally narrower and executable at the RSAR surface; it is not presented as a new NFL theorem.

## Scientific pursuit and search diversity

### Epistemic landscapes

Michael Weisberg and Ryan Muldoon, “Epistemic Landscapes and the Division of Cognitive Labor,” *Philosophy of Science* 76(2), 2009. DOI: `10.1086/644786`.

Donor for path dependence, division of cognitive labor, and the difference between local exploitation and community-level exploration.

### Novelty search

Joel Lehman and Kenneth O. Stanley, “Abandoning Objectives: Evolution Through the Search for Novelty Alone,” *Evolutionary Computation* 19(2), 2011. DOI: `10.1162/EVCO_a_00025`.

Donor for objective-escape search machinery. Novelty itself still depends on a representation/metric and does not supply a utility-free world oracle.
