## Explanandum lock

Can a composed transfer path ever substitute for fresh direct endpoint adjudication?

The court must distinguish:

```text
DIRECT ENDPOINT WORLD CONTACT
from
WORLD CONTACT ALREADY PRESENT IN A SOURCE BASIS
from
INTERNAL COHERENCE OF TRANSFER PATHS
```

No internal proof, compiler, naturality diagram, path agreement, or implementation diversity may create a world-contact root.

But MQR must also avoid the opposite error: requiring redundant fresh measurement when the endpoint claim contains no new empirical degree of freedom beyond an already world-anchored basis.

## Frozen attacks

### 1. Direct-Receipt Fetishism Attack

Construct a transport-only endpoint:

```text
world measurement x
-> exact/definitional re-expression f(x)
-> endpoint claim only about f(x)
```

If the endpoint introduces:
- no new measurement predicate;
- no new intervention class;
- no new empirical assumption;
- and no new world-facing degree of freedom,

then mandatory fresh endpoint measurement may be epistemically redundant.

Target question:

```text
Can fresh direct adjudication be replaced for TRANSPORT-ONLY claims?
```

### 2. Closed Coherence Loop Attack

Construct a perfectly commuting internal diagram with no external world-contact root.

```text
A -> B -> C
^         |
|---------|
```

All internal witnesses may agree.

Target failure:

**Coherence-as-World-Contact Laundering**

A WCSC must fail if its authority ancestry terminates only in transfer receipts.

### 3. Common-Mode Path-Independence Attack

Two or more paths may agree because they share:
- the same defective measurement root;
- the same interpretation map;
- the same hidden assumption;
- or the same compiled source.

Path agreement therefore counts as coherence evidence only.

```text
PATH INDEPENDENCE != INDEPENDENT WORLD CONTACT
```

### 4. Query-Basis Leakage Attack

Naturality may be tested on a finite basis while the endpoint claim uses a query outside the basis generated by the source world-contact family.

Construct functions that agree on the tested generator but disagree on an untested query.

Target failure:

**Basis-Closure Laundering**

Substitution requires that every endpoint-relevant query/intervention be generated from anchored source queries under declared transformations.

### 5. Non-Congruent Path-Equivalence Attack

Two paths may agree at an endpoint yet their agreement fail after a further admissible pre- or post-composition.

A usable path-equivalence witness must be a congruence under extension:

```text
p ~ q
=> r o p ~ r o q
=> p o s ~ q o s
```

within the declared query/intervention class.

Pairwise endpoint agreement alone is insufficient.

### 6. Higher-Coherence Attack

Triangle/diamond agreement may hold for every currently checked face while a larger diagram contains an untested coherence obstruction.

The court must not infer universal higher coherence from finitely many commuting faces.

A finite coherence certificate must expose:
- its diagram class;
- generators;
- checked faces;
- and closure theorem, if any.

Otherwise higher coherence remains HOLD outside the declared class.

### 7. Stale-Anchor / Freshness Reset Attack

Composition must not make old world contact appear new.

```text
root_freshness(composite)
<=
oldest/load-bearing root validity actually required
```

A derived receipt may inherit a validity window but may not reset it.

If bridge/world drift is possible outside the certified window, fresh world contact re-enters.

### 8. Recursive Substitution Inflation Attack

A substituted endpoint may later become the source for another substitution.

The chain may not:
- forget the original world root;
- reset freshness;
- gain independence credit;
- or increase authority simply because more transfer steps exist.

Target failure:

**Receipt-Renewal by Derivation**

### 9. Unanchored Strongly-Connected-Component Attack

Authority justification may contain cycles.

A cycle is not automatically invalid, but every load-bearing justification SCC must have a path to an external world-contact anchor.

An SCC with no external anchor cannot earn substitution PASS.

### 10. Direct-vs-Composed Conflict Attack

If fresh direct evidence later disagrees with a WCSC, the direct route must be able to defeat/reopen the composed authority.

But:

```text
DIRECT != INFALLIBLE
```

Conflict triggers re-adjudication/HOLD rather than a universal rule that the direct route always wins.

## Minimal world-contact burden hypothesis

MQR-4.40 will test the following stronger principle:

```text
Fresh direct endpoint contact is not required per transformation.
World contact is required per independent load-bearing empirical degree of freedom.
```

Candidate object:

**World-Contact Basis (WCB)**

A WCB is the smallest declared set of world-facing measurement/intervention receipts from which the endpoint-relevant claim family is generated under certified transformations.

Candidate anti-closure invariant:

**External Root Cut (ERC)**

Every load-bearing authority path must cross at least one external world-contact root whose authority is not itself justified solely by the transfer graph being evaluated.
