## Explanandum lock

Given a finite, explicit, auditable rival-search allocation contract, when may its mandatory exploration obligations themselves earn provisional legitimacy if:

- the obligation ontology is fallible;
- the agenda setter may be theory-laden or institutionally interested;
- one underlying burden can be split or merged into different obligation partitions;
- current search outcomes may feed back into later mandate formation;
- obligation debt can be hidden by relabeling, merging, retirement or successor claims;
- no finite court may infer that all scientifically relevant search burdens have been represented?

The court must separate:

```text
EXPLICIT != JUSTIFIED
AUDITABLE != ADEQUATE
MANDATORY != EPISTEMICALLY WARRANTED
FAIR ALLOCATION OVER O != ADEQUATE CONSTITUTION OF O
```

## Candidate canonical objects

### 1. Exploration-Obligation Constitution Receipt (EOCR)

Records:

- claim scope;
- obligation IDs and mandatory/optional status;
- burden atoms/classes each obligation covers;
- agenda-source identity and ancestry;
- source kind;
- constitution rule;
- predecessor/successor relations;
- explicit withdrawals;
- challenge/reopening routes;
- unresolved omitted-burden witness status;
- forbidden inferences.

EOCR is a constitutional provenance receipt, not an adequacy oracle.

### 2. Search-Burden Atom (SBA)

A burden atom is the smallest declared unit used by this court for coverage comparison.

Examples may include a query challenge, representation mutation, instrument/root challenge, anomaly class or external-case transfer.

SBA is declaration-relative. MQR-4.44 does not claim that the chosen atoms are world-fundamental.

### 3. Obligation-Burden Map (OBM)

A relation:

```text
obligation -> {declared burden atoms}
```

The union of burdens, not obligation count, is the primary partition-audit surface.

### 4. Agenda Provenance Graph (APG)

Tracks agenda-source identity, ancestry and source kind.

Named-source diversity does not imply agenda-ancestry diversity.

### 5. Successor-Coverage Witness (SCW)

A predecessor obligation may be retired in favor of successors only if the declared burden of the predecessor is covered by the successors, or an explicit withdrawal changes the constitution with provenance.

```text
SUCCESSOR LABEL != SUCCESSOR COVERAGE
```

### 6. Obligation Debt Continuity Ledger (ODCL)

Debt attaches to unresolved burden/provenance obligations, not merely current labels.

Renaming, splitting, merging or deleting an obligation cannot by itself erase unpaid burden.

### 7. Constitution Admissibility Envelope (CAE)

A constitution is provisionally admissible only relative to declared burden atoms and rules when it:

- has complete provenance for mandatory obligations;
- exposes agenda ancestry/common mode;
- preserves declared burden under harmless split/merge refactoring;
- refuses debt erasure by relabeling;
- requires SCW or explicit withdrawal for retirement;
- reopens on admitted omitted-burden/frontier escape;
- carries open-world incompleteness explicitly.

```text
CONSTITUTIONALLY ADMISSIBLE != WORLD-COMPLETE
```

## Core representation attack

Let declared burden atoms be A and B.

Constitution C1:

```text
O1 -> {A,B}
obligation_count = 1
burden_union = {A,B}
```

Constitution C2:

```text
O1 -> {A}
O2 -> {B}
obligation_count = 2
burden_union = {A,B}
```

Therefore:

```text
SAME DECLARED BURDEN
!=
SAME OBLIGATION COUNT
```

Any governance metric that treats obligation count as constitutional substance is vulnerable to search-class gerrymandering.

## Frozen attacks

### Attack 1 — Search-Class Split Gerrymander
Split one burden-equivalent obligation into many labels and show that obligation/debt counts change while burden union is unchanged.

### Attack 2 — Search-Class Merge Gerrymander
Merge heterogeneous burdens into one obligation so a single exercise can appear to discharge multiple scientifically distinct burdens unless burden-level coverage remains explicit.

### Attack 3 — Agenda-Source Inflation
Create multiple named agenda sources with one common ancestry.

Target:
```text
SOURCE COUNT != AGENDA-ANCESTRY DIVERSITY
```

### Attack 4 — Incumbent-Only Agenda
Construct a constitution in which every mandatory obligation is generated by the currently favored representation/theory lineage.

Target:
```text
ENDOGENOUS EXPLICITNESS != EXTERNAL CHALLENGE
```

This does not make endogenous mandates invalid; it requires the dependence to remain visible.

### Attack 5 — Burden Laundering by Rename
Rename an overdue obligation without changing its burden.

Target:
```text
NEW LABEL != DEBT RETIREMENT
```

### Attack 6 — Burden Laundering by Deletion
Remove an obligation from the live set without SCW or explicit withdrawal provenance.

Target:
```text
ABSENT FROM CURRENT LIST != DISCHARGED
```

### Attack 7 — Successor-Coverage Overclaim
Retire O_old covering {A,B} into O_new covering {A} and call it full succession.

Target:
```text
PARTIAL COVERAGE != SUCCESSOR COVERAGE
```

### Attack 8 — Valid Successor Refinement
Split O_old->{A,B} into O_A->{A}, O_B->{B} with full predecessor burden union preserved.

Positive control:
```text
LABEL CHANGE + FULL BURDEN PRESERVATION
CAN SUPPORT SUCCESSION
```

### Attack 9 — Endogenous Mandate Self-Licensing
A search lane generates outcomes that create only future obligations favorable to the same lane/representation.

Require provenance and no inference from self-generation to adequacy.

### Attack 10 — Constitution-by-Recent-Silence
Retire a burden because recent searches found nothing.

Target:
```text
RECENT SILENCE != BURDEN RETIREMENT AUTHORITY
```

### Attack 11 — Same Visible Constitution, Omitted-Burden Twin Worlds
Two worlds share the same EOCR surface; only one contains an omitted load-bearing search burden.

Target:
```text
VISIBLE CONSTITUTION != WORLD-COMPLETE OBLIGATION ONTOLOGY
```

### Attack 12 — Escape-Triggered Constitution Reopening
A frontier escape introduces or reveals burden B_new absent from the current OBM.

Target:
```text
ESCAPE -> OBLIGATION CONSTITUTION REOPENED
```

### Attack 13 — Partition-Sensitive Debt Score
Two equivalent burden unions yield different debt counts under split vs merged obligations.

Target:
```text
OBLIGATION-COUNT DEBT != PARTITION-INVARIANT BURDEN DEBT
```

### Attack 14 — Successor-Coverage by Name Similarity
A successor with similar name but no burden inclusion is rejected.

### Attack 15 — Externality Overclaim
External provenance alone does not establish epistemic adequacy.

Target:
```text
EXTERNAL SOURCE != AUTOMATICALLY CORRECT AGENDA
```

### Attack 16 — Constitution Fatalism
Open-world fallibility does not imply that all obligation constitutions are equally illegitimate.

Target:
```text
FALLIBLE CONSTITUTION != NO GOVERNANCE POSSIBLE
```

## Positive candidate principles

### Principle A — Burden Semantics Dominate Label Count

Split/merge refactoring that preserves declared burden union should not, by itself, alter constitutional adequacy.

### Principle B — Provenance Must Be Preserved, Not Idolized

Agenda provenance and ancestry are mandatory audit coordinates, but independent or external origin is not itself a truth certificate.

### Principle C — Debt Tracks Burden Continuity

Debt cannot disappear solely because labels, classes or current set membership change.

### Principle D — Successor Claims Require Coverage or Explicit Withdrawal

A successor relation is earned by declared burden preservation, not asserted by naming.

### Principle E — Endogenous Mandates Are Allowed but Marked

A mandate may emerge from current research, but its endogenous dependence cannot be laundered into independent confirmation.

### Principle F — Constitution Guidance Is Set-Valued and Open

Multiple burden partitions may be admissible if they preserve the same declared burdens and provenance constraints.

### Principle G — No World-Complete Obligation Ontology

Even a constitution passing every finite audit remains vulnerable to omitted burden classes.
