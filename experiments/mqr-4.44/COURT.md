## Successor coverage and burden laundering

MQR-4.44 separates three objects that a label-based system can collapse:

```text
SUCCESSOR NAME
SUCCESSOR BURDEN COVERAGE
PREDECESSOR DEBT CONTINUITY
```

### Partial successor

If:

```text
OLD -> {A,B}
NEW -> {A}
```

then successor coverage fails.

### Valid refinement

If:

```text
OLD -> {A,B}
NEW_A -> {A}
NEW_B -> {B}
```

then the declared predecessor burden can be preserved by successor union.

### Debt continuity

Even full successor burden coverage does not automatically discharge an unresolved predecessor debt.

For debt-bearing predecessors, MQR-4.44 requires either:

- explicit withdrawal with provenance; or
- a declared debt transfer whose successor burden union covers the predecessor burden.

Thus:

```text
RENAME != DEBT RETIREMENT
DELETE != DEBT RETIREMENT
SUCCESSOR COVERAGE != DEBT DISCHARGE
```

Executable fixtures distinguish:
- rename + full burden coverage + no debt transfer -> laundering detected;
- deletion of debt-bearing predecessor -> laundering detected;
- full successor coverage + explicit debt transfer -> continuity preserved.
