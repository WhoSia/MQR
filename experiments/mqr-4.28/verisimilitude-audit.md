# MQR-4.28 — Verisimilitude & Composite-Indicator Validity Audit

## Question
Can Real-Language's W/N/I/T/D profile or a scalar projection be interpreted as distance to truth without an independent truth oracle?

## Verisimilitude literature

### Popper
Popper's early formal programme compared theories through true and false consequences.

### Tichy–Miller failure
Tichy (1974) and Miller (1974) showed that Popper's original content-based definition cannot provide the required ordering among false theories.
This is a direct warning against assuming that an intuitively plausible scalar "closeness to truth" automatically has the intended formal behavior.

### Post-Popper approaches
Later approaches include:
- possible-world / similarity measures associated with Tichy, Oddie, and Niiniluoto;
- relevant-consequence/content-element approaches associated with Schurz and Weingartner;
- truth-approximation and structure-likeness programmes associated with Kuipers and related work.

These programmes differ in what counts as distance, relevant content, model structure, or similarity.
Their existence supports the legitimacy of truthlikeness as a research problem, not a unique ready-made metric for MQR.

Niiniluoto explicitly distinguishes verisimilitude from corroboration/confirmation and argues that verisimilitude theory does not by itself solve theoretical preference without an independent solution to induction.

## Laudan pressure
The canonical Drive copy of Laudan (1981), *A Confutation of Convergent Realism*, attacks simple moves from scientific success to approximate truth/convergence.
MQR therefore cannot validate TPX merely because historically successful theories tend to score well.

## Composite-indicator methodology
OECD/JRC guidance on composite indicators treats:
- variable selection,
- normalization,
- weighting,
- aggregation,
- uncertainty,
- sensitivity analysis

as load-bearing methodological choices.

The same guidance warns that ranking/messages can change under methodological choices and recommends explicit sensitivity/robustness analysis.

Therefore a geometric mean over W/N/I/T/D is not self-justifying.

## MQR consequence
Without an independently warranted metric connecting W/N/I/T/D to a truth-state space:

- W/N/I/T/D may be interpreted as an epistemic/world-constraint profile;
- Pareto dominance may support a partial ordering when one profile weakly dominates another in all coordinates;
- crossing profiles do not receive a unique scientific order from an arbitrary aggregation rule;
- a scalar may be displayed only as an uncalibrated projection;
- FINAL_TRUTH_DISTANCE remains UNIDENTIFIED.
