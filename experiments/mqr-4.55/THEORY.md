# MQR-4.55 — Consequence-Family Constitution Theory

## 1. Upstream of invariance

MQR-4.54 made the constitution of equivalence explicit.

MQR-4.55 moves upstream again.

A constitution can survive every admitted consequence and still be fragile if the admitted family was generated by one narrow ecology.

Therefore:

~~~text
PASSING F
DOES NOT BY ITSELF
AUTHORIZE F.
~~~

The new scientific object is not merely a test set.

It is a **consequence ecology**:

~~~text
Ψ = (T, F, Γ_F, Π, E, H, R)
~~~

with contents, generation grammar, ancestry, exterior expansion, heuristic proposal routes and reopening.

## 2. Generation–adjudication asymmetry

The strongest positive synthesis after the first Court reveal is:

~~~text
GENERATION SHOULD BE
PLURAL, PERMISSIVE,
AND CAPABLE OF
PRE-FORMAL FOREIGNNESS

WHILE

AUTHORITY SHOULD BE
SCOPED, PROSPECTIVE,
DEFEAT-RESPONSIVE,
AND WORLD-CONTACTED.
~~~

This is not a compromise between rigor and intuition.

The two occupy different jobs.

### Generation side
Candidate consequences may originate in:
- formal derivation;
- anomaly;
- intervention design;
- diagrammatic manipulation;
- model simulation;
- analogy;
- expert recognition;
- tacit experimental skill;
- aesthetic or fluency signals;
- an outsider representation;
- random or deliberately foreign search.

### Adjudication side
No origin route may mark its own product scientifically authoritative merely because it generated it.

The candidate must enter a separate consequence/authority court.

Thus:

~~~text
DISCOVERY PEDIGREE
!=
EVIDENTIAL AUTHORITY.
~~~

## 3. The rigor trap has a precise failure mode

A methodology can demand explicit formalizability **before admitting a candidate consequence into search**.

That policy looks rigorous.

But if the missing scientific distinction cannot yet be expressed in the current grammar, the policy deletes the very route by which the grammar could be repaired.

This yields:

~~~text
EX-ANTE FORMALIZABILITY
AS AN ADMISSION REQUIREMENT
CAN CREATE
ENDOGENOUS BLINDNESS.
~~~

The corrective is not lower standards of proof.

It is to place the strictest proof/authority burden **after candidate generation but before claim promotion**.

## 4. Selective rigorization

The calculus analogy suggests a useful model when stripped of triumphalist mythology.

Rigorization can relocate the boundary between:
- what may remain compressed/tacit;
- what must be expanded;
- what counts as an admissible inference;
- which failure modes deserve explicit treatment.

MQR-4.55 therefore rejects both:

~~~text
MATURE SCIENCE = INTUITION ELIMINATED
~~~

and:

~~~text
INTUITION IS IRREDUCIBLE
SO FORMAL AUDIT IS MISGUIDED.
~~~

The stronger candidate is **selective rigorization**:

~~~text
WHEN A TACIT / HIGH-LEVEL
MOVE BECOMES LOAD-BEARING
OR DEFEAT-SENSITIVE,

MOVE ENOUGH OF IT
INTO EXPLICIT OBLIGATIONS
TO MAKE ITS FAILURE
AUDITABLE.
~~~

Not every upstream generative act must be formalized in advance.

Every downstream authority claim must expose the load-bearing parts relevant to its scope.

## 5. Formal completeness can be scientifically incomplete

Suppose a language L is complete for its declared syntax and semantics.

A later experiment reveals that the material scientific distinction requires a primitive not expressible in L.

There is no contradiction.

The formal completeness claim was language-relative.

The scientific consequence claim was world-facing.

Therefore:

~~~text
FORMAL COMPLETENESS(L)
DOES NOT IMPLY
WORLD CONSEQUENCE COMPLETENESS.
~~~

This is the 4.55 analogue of MQR's recurring rule:

~~~text
FORMAL CUSTODY
!=
WORLD AUTHORITY.
~~~

## 6. Common-mode capture

A family can be large and still narrow.

Ten probes may inherit:
- one representation;
- one instrument calibration;
- one theory-dependent preprocessor;
- one training tradition;
- one benchmark ontology.

The load-bearing object is therefore not family cardinality but **escape topology**.

A consequence route has special value when it can reach a failure surface unavailable to the incumbent ancestry.

This motivates a structural distinction:

~~~text
NOMINAL DIVERSITY
!=
GENERATOR DIVERSITY
!=
ESCAPE CAPACITY.
~~~

MQR-4.55 deliberately does not scalarize these.

## 7. Why intuition belongs in H but not in authority

“Intuition” is too heterogeneous to be a primitive epistemic faculty.

It can name:
- compressed expert pattern recognition;
- unarticulated model simulation;
- learned perceptual salience;
- analogy;
- fluency;
- aesthetic preference;
- bodily warning;
- mere familiarity;
- cultural training;
- bias.

Therefore MQR places heuristic/intuitive channels in `H`, the **proposal ecology**.

A productive intuition becomes scientifically interesting when it generates a separator that later survives prospective world contact.

A seductive intuition becomes scientifically informative when it fails and teaches us about the generator.

Both cases support:

~~~text
INTUITION CAN HAVE
SEARCH VALUE

WITHOUT HAVING
SELF-CERTIFYING
TRUTH VALUE.
~~~

## 8. Tacit competence is a capability state

MQR-2.65/2.66 already ruled out the false choice between:
- “everything important is in the written protocol”; and
- “tacit knowledge is ineffable insider magic.”

MQR-4.55 imports that result into consequence-family adequacy.

If a consequence test requires competence C, then losing C can remove an actual discrimination route even if the protocol file survives.

But C can be:
- elicited;
- trained;
- transferred;
- substituted;
- reconstructed;
- held-out tested.

Thus tacit dependence is an empirical capability obligation.

## 9. Consequence-family adequacy is future-facing

Two programmes may contain the same current family F.

Programme A has:
- exterior generator routes;
- heterogeneous ancestry;
- holdout expansion;
- reopening.

Programme B does not.

They have the same current test contents and different scientific vulnerability.

Therefore:

~~~text
CURRENT F
DOES NOT DETERMINE
FUTURE CORRIGIBILITY.
~~~

This makes consequence-family constitution partly a property of **reachable challenge futures**, not only of present inventory.
