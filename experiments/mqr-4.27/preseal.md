# MQR-4.27 — Historical Authority without Finality, Newton–Mercury / Antarctic-Ozone / H. pylori Cross-Epoch Replay, Unconceived-Successor Shock, Mystery-Preserving Realism & Whether Science Can Know Reality before It Knows Its Future Rivals

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEAL

## Objective
Test whether MQR can represent historically warranted scientific authority before later successor theories, methods, or rival vocabularies exist.

Three historical lineages:
1. Newtonian celestial mechanics -> Mercury perihelion residue -> successor gravity.
2. Antarctic ozone monitoring -> ozone-hole recognition -> regional/chemical discrimination.
3. Gastric disease orthodoxy -> H. pylori observation/culture -> causal/interventional consolidation.

## Hindsight firewall
Every epoch packet is compiled only from evidence, methods, and rivals available at that historical stage.

Later information may appear only in:
SUCCESSOR_SHOCK
and may:
- contract prior scope;
- reinterpret residuals;
- reveal missed rival space;
- preserve or retire specific constraint claims.

Later success may NOT retroactively inflate an earlier truth-proximity score.

## Core hypothesis
Science can possess genuine realist authority before future rivals are known iff:
H1. current authority is attached to constraint-level invariants rather than final theory identity;
H2. adverse residue is retained rather than normalized away;
H3. scope is explicit;
H4. open-world rival reserve remains active;
H5. successor shock updates the packet without rewriting the prior historical receipt as fraudulent.

## R-language requirement
MQR-4.27 must create an executable active language packet for incoming scientific events.

The language must represent:
- claim and claim type;
- scope;
- C / E / P / R_open;
- world-resistance profile;
- noncommon contact;
- rival-generator provenance;
- surviving rival / identified-set state;
- adverse residue;
- transport;
- successor vulnerability;
- mystery/ontic reserve;
- current realist authority state.

It may compute a bounded truth-proximity proxy, but:
- it is NOT a probability that the claim is true;
- it is NOT metaphysical distance to Truth;
- it may not compensate for a failed C/E/P/R gate;
- every scalar must be reconstructible from explicit profile coordinates;
- residue and open-world status must remain visible beside the scalar.

## Truth-proximity semantics
Define a Truth-Proximity Profile TPP, not a primitive truth meter.

Required continuous axes, each in [0,1]:
W = world resistance / intervention-exclusion strength.
N = noncommon evidence-route strength.
I = current rival-invariance / identification strength.
T = transport across declared scope/regimes.
D = live defeat exposure / corrigibility.

Optional scalar:
TPX = geometric mean(W,N,I,T,D), only when C=E=P=R_open=PASS.

TPX is interpreted as:
CURRENT WORLD-CONSTRAINED PROXIMITY PROXY.

TPX is not:
P(claim is true),
final truth distance,
or exhaustive-rival confidence.

If any authority gate fails:
TPX = NA.

## Mystery preservation
Every packet must include RESIDUE and ONTIC_RESERVE.

A packet may therefore say:
SCOPED_REALIST_AUTHORITY = HIGH
while:
FINAL_ONTOLOGY = HOLD.

This is a feature, not inconsistency.

## Cross-epoch replay success
PASS if:
1. predecessor science retains some justified constraint authority where historically warranted;
2. successor shock can contract or redirect ontology without erasing all predecessor authority;
3. at least one case shows inquiry architecture suppressing/limiting recognition;
4. at least one case shows method change creating a previously weak/unavailable rival;
5. R-language packet correctly separates current authority from later truth hindsight;
6. historical cases can be serialized and mechanically evaluated by one common language;
7. no new metaphysical primitive is required.
