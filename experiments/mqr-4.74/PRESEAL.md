## Typed perturbation candidate

For claim phi at scope S and live obligation family Omega, a perturbation candidate pi carries:

- `target` — what part of the claim/system is perturbed;
- `generator` — THEORY / RIVAL / ANOMALY / INSTRUMENT / DOMAIN_GRAMMAR / EXTERNAL;
- `coherence` — whether pi describes a physically, mathematically or semantically coherent alternative;
- `claim_relevance` — whether failure under pi can change authority for phi at S;
- `independent_motivation` — whether pi is motivated independently of a desired verdict;
- `realizability` — AVAILABLE / CURRENTLY_UNREALIZABLE / IMPOSSIBLE_IN_PRINCIPLE / NOT_APPLICABLE;
- `execution_permission` — PERMITTED / BLOCKED / NOT_APPLICABLE;
- `ancestry` — source/provenance of the candidate;
- `entry_epoch` — whether admitted before or after the certificate under challenge;
- `exclusion_receipt` — typed reason when excluded.

No scalar perturbation severity is primitive.

## Candidate admissibility rule

A perturbation may enter the robustness envelope when it is:

1. coherent for the declared semantics/domain;
2. claim-relevant to at least one live authority-changing obligation;
3. independently motivated or generated by a pre-authorized challenge route;
4. provenance-addressable;
5. not excluded merely because it is currently inconvenient, costly or technically unrealizable.

Execution permission is not required for scientific relevance.
