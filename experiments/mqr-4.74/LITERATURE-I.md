## 1. Robust optimization / DRO absorbs envelope optimization

Kuhn, Shafiee & Wiesemann (2025), *Distributionally Robust Optimization*, treats the ambiguity set as a central mathematical object: a family of probability distributions compatible with structural or statistical information, over which worst-case performance is optimized.

MQR-4.74 therefore claims no invention of:
- uncertainty sets;
- ambiguity sets;
- worst-case optimization;
- distributional robustness;
- ambiguity-set calibration;
- adversarial robustness.

Roos & den Hertog (2020), *Reducing Conservatism in Robust Optimization*, explicitly treats excessive conservatism as a failure mode of standard robust formulations.

Collision:

    BIGGER / HARDER UNCERTAINTY SET
    !=
    AUTOMATICALLY BETTER DECISION MODEL.

Thus the MQR distinction

    MORE STRESS TESTS != STRONGER SCIENTIFIC ROBUSTNESS TEST

is not mathematical novelty.

Primary external baselines:
- Kuhn, Shafiee & Wiesemann (2025), Acta Numerica, DOI 10.1017/S0962492924000084.
- Roos & den Hertog (2020), INFORMS Journal on Computing, DOI 10.1287/ijoc.2019.0913.

## 2. Misspecification breaks naive informativeness authority

Callahan & Catanach (2026), *On the Misinformation in a Statistical Experiment*, is held in the Research OS Literature Commons.

Their central pressure is stronger than generic robustness rhetoric:
under model or inferential misspecification, experiments classically ranked as more informative can amplify bias and produce confident but incorrect inference. They explicitly diagnose failures of Blackwell monotonicity under misspecification and introduce robust experimental-design criteria.

MQR consequence:

    FIXED-MODEL INFORMATION ORDER
    !=
    ROBUST SCIENTIFIC AUTHORITY ORDER.

This kills any 4.74 attempt to use one nominal information ordering as the final perturbation-envelope authority.

## 3. Acquisition under misspecification is already occupied

Tang, Sloman & Kaski (2026), *Representative, Informative, and De-Amplifying*, is also held in the Commons.

They show that targeting informativeness alone under misspecification can interact badly with covariate shift/error amplification, motivating representativeness and de-amplification terms.

Novelty kill:

MQR claims no invention of robust acquisition, representative sampling, de-amplification, or misspecification-aware experimental design.

Residual use:

The admissibility of a perturbation cannot be reduced to nominal informativeness alone.

## 4. Measurement / metrology baseline

JCGM VIM3 is held in the Commons.

Measurement authority is typed by measurand, measurement model, calibration, traceability, uncertainty and related concepts.

MQR consequence:

A cross-instrument perturbation that changes the measurand or measurement semantics cannot be treated as a same-scope defeat merely because the test label looks similar.

This is a transport/authority boundary, not a new theory of measurement.

## 5. Mathematical interpretation/equivalence pressure

Hudetz (2019), *Definable Categorical Equivalence*, is held in the Commons.

The paper argues that purely formal equivalence criteria are not enough for full theoretical equivalence; preservation conditions concerning interpretation matter. Both internal model structure and morphism choices can matter to interpretation.

MQR consequence:

A mathematical framework perturbation may preserve a formal label or some derivations while failing the semantic/theorem-family transport required for an inherited authority claim.

Novelty kill:

MQR claims no invention of definitional, Morita, categorical, definable-categorical or interpretive equivalence criteria.

## 7. Surviving candidate

The residue is constitutional/representational:

> A robustness certificate must identify the perturbation-envelope version and authority conditions under which it was earned. Later admissible expansion may reopen successor authority without making the earlier correctly scoped certificate retroactively false. Conversely, adding incoherent, irrelevant or scope-changing perturbations cannot manufacture defeat of the original claim without an explicit transport receipt.

This is not:
- an uncertainty-set optimizer;
- a universal robustness metric;
- a perturbation severity order;
- an open-world completeness theorem.
