## Explanandum lock

Let:
- O be a declared defeat-content ontology;
- D(O) be its named content set;
- M(d) be declared mechanism lineage for content d;
- V(d) be manifestation/observable signature lineage;
- C(d) be the counterfactual defeat profile of d over a frozen challenge family;
- A(d) be ancestry/dependence;
- T(O,O') be typed content transport across representations/versions;
- H be newly discovered defeat content or distinction.

The court asks when defeat-content identity can legitimately support route identity and coverage authority when:
- one mechanism yields multiple manifestations;
- one manifestation admits multiple mechanisms;
- two content labels have identical current counterfactual defeat profiles;
- one content is split into several names without new counterfactual distinction;
- several contents are merged despite separable counterfactual consequences;
- shared common cause makes nominal content plurality nonindependent;
- a new representation reveals previously hidden distinction;
- a new intervention separates contents previously treated as equivalent;
- different representations preserve the same defeat role while changing labels/granularity;
- counterfactual equivalence is itself family-relative and open to expansion.

- causal/failure mechanism;
- observable manifestation;
- defeat role under the claim/challenge scope.

This blocks both:
- mechanism identity laundering through same manifestation;
- manifestation proliferation from being mistaken for independent mechanisms.

This is family-relative operational equivalence only.

## Core countermodel 1 — manifestation multiplication

One mechanism m produces five observable manifestations:

~~~text
m -> v1,v2,v3,v4,v5
~~~

If each manifestation is counted as an independent defeat atom:

~~~text
1 mechanism -> 5 "independent" contents
~~~

without new counterfactual failure dimensions.

Therefore:

~~~text
MANIFESTATION COUNT != DEFEAT-DIMENSION COUNT
~~~

## Core countermodel 2 — mechanism aliasing behind one manifestation

Two distinct mechanisms m1 and m2 yield the same current observable manifestation v.

Under current passive observation they alias.

A new intervention separates them.

Therefore:

~~~text
SAME MANIFESTATION
!=
SAME DEFEAT CONTENT
~~~

unless the claim scope genuinely quotients away that mechanism distinction.

## Core countermodel 3 — split gerrymander

O1:


~~~text

d2 -> {q1,q2}
~~~

Therefore content count rises while defeat geometry does not.

## Core countermodel 5 — merge laundering

Positive control against CONTENT-MERGE-FATAL.
