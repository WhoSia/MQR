## Strongest pressure against the preseal

The most dangerous reduction is:

~~~text
DEFEAT CONTENT
=
CAUSAL VARIABLE / MECHANISM / ABSTRACTION CELL
~~~

MQR-4.49 rejects that identification.

A defeat content is claim- and challenge-relative:

~~~text
DEFEAT CONTENT
=
A DECLARED WAY IN WHICH
A SPECIFIC AUTHORITY-BEARING CLAIM
CAN LOSE WARRANT
UNDER A DECLARED CHALLENGE FAMILY
~~~

The same causal mechanism may instantiate multiple defeat contents at different claim scopes.
Different causal mechanisms may be equivalent for a current defeat role.
A noncausal calibration/access/translation failure may be a live defeat content without being a causal mechanism of the target phenomenon.

Therefore the proposed CDP/CDEE layer is a governance quotient over **authority-changing consequences**, not a replacement ontology for causation.
