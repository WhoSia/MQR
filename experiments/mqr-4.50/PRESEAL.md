## Explanandum lock

Let:

- S be a declared claim scope;
- Q be the frozen challenge family currently admitted to the court;
- W be the frozen world-contact family relevant to S and Q;
- D be the declared decision/use contract, if action is at issue;
- R be the registered refinement family currently admitted;
- K be the registered family of admissible materiality/meta-criteria;
- C be the current constitutional state, including defeat-role quotients and unresolved residue;
- A(C;S,Q,W,D,K) be the current scientific-authority state;
- rho in R be a candidate constitutional refinement;
- kappa in K be a criterion for whether a change is authority-relevant at the declared scope.

The court asks when recursive primitive critique may stop *operationally* even though deeper ontology remains open.

Candidate operational sufficiency condition:

~~~text
OQS(C | S,Q,W,D,R,K)
iff
1. S,Q,W,D,R,K are frozen with provenance before adjudication;
2. S is non-vacuous for the promoted claim/use;
3. every admitted rho in R has been replayed or explicitly debt-carried;
4. for every replayed rho and every live kappa in K,
   the authority-relevant projection is invariant;
5. no registered counterexample changes defeat, discrimination,
   transport, or decision-relevant status;
6. unresolved material refinement debt is empty;
7. future refinement-space completeness is NOT inferred;
8. an activation-capable reopening rule is preserved.
~~~

This is a constitution of current sufficiency, not a theorem that reality has no finer structure.

## Candidate canonical objects

### 1. Constitutional Regress Boundary Receipt (CRBR)

Records:
- claim scope S and provenance;
- challenge family Q and provenance;
- world-contact family W;
- decision/use contract D;
- refinement registry R;
- meta-criterion family K;
- current authority state;
- replay results;
- unresolved debt;
- stop verdict;
- reopening triggers;
- anti-finality guards.

### 2. Operational Quotient Sufficiency Certificate (OQSC)

A certificate that all currently admitted refinements are authority-inert at the declared surface.

~~~text
OQSC PASS
!=
FINAL ONTOLOGY
!=
TRUTH ORACLE
!=
FUTURE CLOSURE
~~~

### 3. Claim-Scope Provenance Receipt (CSPR)

Tracks whether S was:
- frozen prospectively;
- inherited from an external scientific/use commitment;
- widened or narrowed;
- changed after outcome exposure;
- reconstructed after a challenge.

A scope change is not forbidden, but it must trigger replay.

### 4. Scope-Endogeneity / Capture Sentinel (SECS)

Detects:

~~~text
KNOWN DISTINCTION
+ POST-HOC SCOPE SHRINK
+ DISTINCTION DISAPPEARS
->
HOLD_SCOPE_CAPTURE
~~~

A claim cannot earn sufficiency merely by redefining itself until the live counterexample becomes irrelevant.

### 5. Meta-Criterion Recursion Receipt (MCRR)

Tracks the criteria K used to classify a refinement as material or inert.

It distinguishes:
- criterion declaration;
- criterion ancestry;
- outcome timing;
- cross-criterion agreement;
- criterion-sensitive stop verdicts;
- unresolved meta-criterion debt.

### 6. Criterion-Sensitivity Envelope (CSE)

If all currently admissible criteria agree on the same authority state, the stop verdict may be criterion-robust on the registered envelope.

If admissible criteria disagree:

~~~text
HOLD_META_CRITERION_NONINVARIANCE
~~~

The response is not to invent an infinite hierarchy of meta-meta-criteria.

### 7. World-Contact Termination Receipt (WCTR)

Records whether further currently registered constitutional refinement changes any world-facing discrimination or authority consequence under W.

~~~text
STRUCTURAL REFINEMENT
+ NO WORLD-CONTACT DIFFERENCE
+ NO DECISION-RELEVANT DIFFERENCE
->
CANDIDATE OPERATIONAL STOP
~~~

### 8. Reopening Fixed-Point State (RFPS)

A fixed-point-like state relative to the currently admitted update operator F:

~~~text
F_{S,Q,W,D,R,K}(C) = C
~~~

means only that replaying the registered admissible refinements does not change the current authority state.

It does not imply:

~~~text
for every future F*,
F*(C)=C
~~~

### 9. Refinement Debt Ledger (RDL)

Carries:
- unexecuted registered refinements;
- unresolved scope challenges;
- criterion conflicts;
- missing world-contact replay;
- decision-contract ambiguity;
- path-dependent revision conflict;
- deferred but activation-capable reopening obligations.

Operational sufficiency requires no *material live debt* to be silently dropped.

### 10. Reopening Activation Receipt (RAR)

Triggers reopening on:
- new admitted challenge;
- new world-contact route;
- new refinement with authority-changing witness;
- scope change;
- decision-contract change;
- criterion-envelope expansion;
- direct/composed revision conflict;
- previously deferred material debt becoming executable.
