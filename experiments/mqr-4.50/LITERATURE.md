## Internal novelty kill

MQR doctrine already contains a Meta-Regress Boundary principle: finite unresolved meta-debt may remain as an explicit HOLD rather than being converted into either an infinite completed regress or a final meta-standard.

Therefore MQR-4.50 cannot claim novelty for:
- recognizing a regress of criteria;
- refusing a final unquestionable criterion;
- permitting inquiry to stop administratively at an explicit unresolved boundary.

The live 4.50 question is narrower: when may a regress boundary earn **positive scoped authorization** rather than merely an explicit HOLD?

## External novelty kill

### Epistemic regress

Classical and contemporary epistemology already supplies foundationalist, coherentist and infinitist responses to epistemic regress. MQR-4.50 is not a new solution to the regress problem in general.

### Experimenters' regress

Godin & Gingras (2002), engaging Collins, make explicit the circular pressure between recognizing facts and recognizing good instruments and argue that formal outcome-independent criteria do not simply close the controversy.

Novelty killed:
- scientific practice can contain criterion/fact circularity;
- regress is not resolved merely by demanding one more formal criterion.

DOI: 10.1016/S0039-3681(01)00032-2.

### Reflective equilibrium

Rechnitzer (2026) distinguishes epistemology, methodology and method in reflective-equilibrium debates and treats RE as especially plausible as methodology for guiding inquiry.

Novelty killed:
- iterative revision toward a revisable equilibrium;
- the generic thought that inquiry can be methodologically guided without an unrevisable foundation.

DOI: 10.1007/s11229-026-05612-y.

### Robustness

Stegenga & Menon show that evidential diversity/ontic independence does not automatically supply the probabilistic independence required for a warranted robustness argument. McLoone, Orzack & Sober (2025) likewise challenge automatic epistemic elevation from derivational robustness.

Novelty killed:
- stability across multiple derivations/criteria is not by itself confirmation;
- agreement is not automatically an authority oracle.

DOIs:
- 10.1086/692141
- 10.1007/s13194-025-00673-1

### Decision under deep uncertainty

Robust Decision Making explicitly seeks useful decisions without requiring a uniquely correct prediction of the future.

Novelty killed:
- action can be rationally organized despite unresolved model/world uncertainty;
- action authority and descriptive resolution need not have identical stopping conditions.

### Inductive risk

Douglas argues that consequences of error can matter inside scientific reasoning. This blocks any attempt to make MQR's action threshold independent of the declared stakes/use contract.

Novelty killed:
- practical consequences can alter evidential/acceptance thresholds;
- decision/use contracts are not an MQR invention.
