~~~text

~~~

means:

1. the submitted surface is constitutionally admissible for the current claim/use;
2. claim scope is nonvacuous and appropriately frozen/prospectively revised;

5. all registered admissible criteria agree on inertness of those refinements;

10. reopening remains activation-capable.

The certificate is a current sufficiency certificate, not an ontology certificate.

## Claim-scope endogeneity

A post-outcome scope shrink can hide an already observed distinction without erasing the
original witness.

That yields:

~~~text
HOLD_SCOPE_CAPTURE
~~~

A genuine prospective scope revision is permitted, but the old certificate does not simply
transfer. The revised claim/use receives a new CSPR and replay at the new scope.

Thus scope plasticity is allowed while scope laundering is not.
