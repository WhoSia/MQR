## 1. Bayesian experimental design

Bayesian experimental design is the clean positive control for local optimization.

Once a model, prior, design space and utility are constituted, experiment selection can be optimized relative to that decision problem.

Canonical Drive donor:
- Chaloner & Verdinelli (1995), _Bayesian Experimental Design: A Review_ — `16qQr0YjZw8A06r6lucP5ZIknHYchHfBg`.

MQR pressure:

## 2. Metareasoning / value of computation

Russell & Wefald (1991) derive value of computation from its expected effect on external decisions under resource bounds.

Canonical Drive donor:
- Russell & Wefald (1991), _Principles of Metareasoning_ — `1SAvlhQSvPEXTV4QxmsnrCZtzWk-x9ZWT`.

MQR pressure:
- computation/information does not have context-free intrinsic value;
- value depends on downstream actions and the declared decision problem;
- resource bounds are load-bearing.

## 4. Novelty search

Lehman & Stanley (2011) provide strong prior art for the claim that optimizing an explicit objective can be deceptive and that novelty-driven search can escape deceptive gradients.

Canonical Drive donor:
- Lehman & Stanley (2011), _Abandoning Objectives: Evolution Through the Search for Novelty Alone_ — `1SXALLyuLBMBMop9zb7qU_U5Zoit_K33k`.

MQR-4.56 uses this only as pressure against objective fetishism.

It does not infer:

~~~text
NOVELTY = SCIENTIFIC UTILITY.
~~~

The Court contains an explicit novelty-drift countermodel.
