## Symmetric errors

### False independence

Multiple generators appear diverse but share the same authority-relevant assumption ancestry.

### False dependence

Generators share benign infrastructure or data but expose failures through genuinely distinct authority-changing routes.

Therefore:

`SHARED COMPONENT != AUTOMATIC COMMON-MODE DEFEAT`.

Common-mode must be authority-relevant, not merely implementation-common.
