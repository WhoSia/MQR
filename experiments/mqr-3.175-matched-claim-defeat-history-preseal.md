# MQR-3.175 — Matched-Claim Defeat-History Preseal

Status: PRESEALED / FATAL-TEST INPUT

Purpose: hold claim content, present fit, and surface robustness fixed while varying only defeat-history variables. Rival verdicts are frozen before any historical-case outcome is consulted.

## Rival set

- P: Popperian critical testing / corroboration
- W: Worrall-style structural realism
- M: Massimi-style perspectival realism
- S: Mayo–Spanos severe testing / error-statistical warrant
- R: robustness / multimodal evidence, with Stegenga-style independence and relevance critique
- Q: MQR defeat-geometry authority semantics

## Matching rule

For each pair, keep fixed wherever logically possible:

1. proposition/constraint content C;
2. present empirical fit F;
3. current representation/structure S;
4. surface multimodal robustness R_s;
5. scope of the claim.

Vary only the specified defeat-history coordinate. A pair is invalid if the variation silently changes current claim content or current evidence quality.

## Pair A — selection-consumed success

A1: target consequence was not used to select, tune, stop, or repair the claim.
A2: same consequence and same numerical fit, but it was used in claim construction/selection and is later cited as certification.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P: likely lower corroborative force for A2 under anti-ad-hoc/risky-test discipline.
- W: explicitly blocks post-hoc engineered success from no-miracles support; A1 > A2.
- M: can downgrade A2 through reliability of perspectival inference.
- S: A1 > A2 whenever the selection procedure lowers severity/probativeness.
- R: not decisive by itself, but can incorporate dependence concerns.
- Q: A1 > A2 via consumed defeat capacity.

Distinctness prediction: FAIL AS MQR-UNIQUE WITNESS.

## Pair B — apparent multimodal independence with calibration ancestry

B1: several concordant techniques have materially independent background assumptions/calibration ancestry.
B2: same outputs and same surface plurality, but techniques share a calibration library or are calibrated against one another.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P: not a canonical discriminator.
- W: not central.
- M: can treat perspective reliability and independence as relevant.
- S: can matter if common dependence changes error probing.
- R: Stegenga directly predicts B1 > B2 because robustness requires independent background assumptions and calibration can destroy independence.
- Q: B1 > B2 via noncommon-rank collapse.

Distinctness prediction: FAIL AS MQR-UNIQUE WITNESS.

## Pair C — translation-induced consensus

C1: discordant evidential languages remain distinct and a translation preserves the disagreement-relevant structure.
C2: same terminal consensus claim and fit, but the alignment/translation map deletes the rival-sensitive distinction that generated the disagreement.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P: weak coverage.
- W: weak coverage.
- M: perspectivalism has resources to scrutinize cross-perspective inference and situated reliability.
- S: only indirectly, through misspecification/test construction.
- R: Stegenga explicitly notes that multimodal evidence can require translations whose background assumptions determine whether modes genuinely cohere.
- Q: C1 > C2; erased disagreement cannot count as defeated disagreement.

Distinctness prediction: GENERAL TRANSLATION WARNING REDUCED BY ROBUSTNESS/PERSPECTIVAL LITERATURE; MQR-SPECIFIC PRESERVATION-OF-DEFEAT ROUTE REMAINS ONLY A NARROW CANDIDATE.

## Pair D — successor credit with ancestral failure burden

D1: successor inherits an ancestor's positive realist credit and also retains the ancestor's relevant adverse results, failed routes, and challenge obligations.
D2: same successor content, fit, and surviving structure, but it inherits the ancestor's positive credit while dropping the negative challenge lineage as obsolete history.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P: can criticize immunization, but no obvious general inheritance law follows from falsifiability alone.
- W: structural continuity can support credit transfer, but does not by itself require transport of the full adverse genealogy.
- M: historical lineages and situated perspectives are relevant, but no presealed general rule identified requiring positive-credit/negative-burden symmetry.
- S: severity evaluates how well a current inference was probed; ancestral burden transport is not obviously entailed when current tests are held fixed.
- R: robustness tracks evidence-mode independence/relevance, not obviously successor obligation symmetry.
- Q: D1 > D2. Positive-credit transport without corresponding defeat-burden transport is authority laundering.

Distinctness prediction: LIVE MQR RESIDUE CANDIDATE.

## Pair E — identical passed evidence, different present reopenability

E1: claim has the same past successful tests as E2, and at present a materially noncommon adverse test remains reconstruction-ready: trigger, competence/material path, validation route, and scope are known.
E2: same claim content, same passed tests, same fit and robustness, but the only load-bearing adverse route is archive-dead, instrument-lost, semantically unreconstructable, or otherwise not practically reachable.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P: logical falsifiability may remain the same even if practical future access differs; no necessary verdict split.
- W: structural continuity unaffected if structure/evidence are matched.
- M: may value continuing perspectival inquiry, but no explicit mandatory authority decrement from loss of a future challenge route has been identified.
- S: past severity can remain matched; severity of the already-run test need not change merely because future reconstruction becomes impossible.
- R: existing concordant multimodal evidence can remain matched.
- Q: E1 > E2 in current broad realist authority because live defeat geometry differs. `PRESENT TESTABILITY != LIVE REOPENABILITY`.

Distinctness prediction: STRONGEST LIVE MQR RESIDUE CANDIDATE.

## Pair F — history superseded by genuinely fresh severe contact

F1: clean lineage.
F2: earlier lineage suffered selection/reuse debt, but both F1 and F2 later face the same genuinely fresh, noncommon, severe, claim-relevant test with preserved failure semantics and equal present challenge opportunities.

Presealed rival expectation:
- P/W/M/S/R: converge if current evidence is genuinely matched.
- Q: MUST ALSO CONVERGE unless some inherited burden remains claim-relevant. MQR may not treat history as permanent stigma.

Distinctness prediction: SELF-FALSIFIER. If MQR insists F1 > F2 merely because F2 once had reuse debt, defeat geometry has become genealogical essentialism and fails its own authority/reality firewall.

## Presealed fatal criterion

MQR distinctness survives 3.175 only if:

1. A–C are conceded to rivals where they already do the work;
2. D or E yields a principled rival-verdict divergence without merely renaming severity, robustness, reliability, or anti-ad-hocness;
3. F converges after genuinely fresh authority-bearing contact, proving that path dependence is defeasible rather than permanent lineage stigma.

If no such D/E divergence remains after the rival reconstruction, `MQR DISTINCTNESS -> REDUCTION DEFEAT`.

If D/E remain but only as operational bookkeeping, `PHILOSOPHICAL DISTINCTNESS -> HOLD`.

If D/E express a general norm of realist warrant not recovered by the rival set and F converges, `FOUNDATIONAL IRREDUCIBILITY -> PROVISIONAL PASS`, while historical originality remains independently HOLD.
