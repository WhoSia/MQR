## Governing question

The stage does not ask:

> which progress measure is invariant under a declared equivalence relation?

It asks:

> by what world-contacted conditions may a scientific programme earn authority to treat two states, histories, charts or representations as equivalent for the purpose at hand?

The authority problem is epistemic, not sociological.

## Core constitutional object

A local invariance constitution is:

~~~text
C = (T, X, Γ, P, A, M, D, S)
~~~

where:

- `T` — target / chart semantics: what scientific content the chart is intended to preserve;
- `X` — state/process domain on which sameness is being asserted;
- `Γ` — candidate transformation grammar;
- `P` — observational probe/context family;
- `A` — intervention/action family;
- `M` — admissible morphisms / cross-chart transport grammar;
- `D` — defeat-relevant distinctions that must remain recoverable;
- `S` — scope, boundary conditions and embedding regime.

### Γ is typed, not presumed globally group-valued

`Γ` must declare one of:

~~~text
GROUP
GROUPOID
PSEUDOGROUP
MONOID
PARTIAL_FAMILY
~~~

plus its domain, composition law where defined, identity/inverse obligations where claimed, and closure status.

MQR-4.54 forbids calling a set of individually tested transformations a **symmetry group** merely because each generator passed isolated probes.

## Actual symmetry vs certification

The court distinguishes:

~~~text
ACTUAL_CONSEQUENCE_PRESERVING_TRANSFORMATION
!=
TRANSFORMATION_CERTIFIED_INERT
UNDER CURRENT P/A/M/D/S
~~~

A certification may be defeated by:
- composition;
- embedding;
- a richer probe family;
- a fresh intervention;
- an admissible morphism previously omitted;
- a reopened defeat distinction.

This distinction repairs an ambiguity in the predecessor pressure note: if two maps are already genuine symmetries of the same fully specified consequence structure and that structure is compositionally closed, their composition does not suddenly cease to preserve it. The live failure mode is instead **generator-wise or context-limited certification without closure/embedding coverage**.

## Constitution-induced equivalence

Write:

~~~text
x ~_C y
~~~

only when the declared constitution licenses treating the difference between `x` and `y` as inert for `T` within `S`.

The court does not assume that one maximally coarse or maximally fine equivalence is always preferable.

A coarser quotient can erase material distinctions.
A finer quotient can count pure representational redundancy as scientific change.

## Frozen admissibility obligations

A constitution may earn only local authority and only if the following are satisfied at the declared scope.

### A1 — Probe preservation
For every certified probe/context in `P`, quotient-related states preserve the target-relevant observational consequence.

### A2 — Intervention preservation
For every certified action/intervention in `A`, quotient-related states do not split on target-relevant reachable consequences unless the split is explicitly outside `T`.

### A3 — Composition closure
Claims about a compositional transformation grammar must survive the grammar's declared composition law. Generator-wise success alone is insufficient.

### A4 — Defeat preservation
Quotienting must not erase a distinction required by a live defeat route in `D`.

### A5 — Embedding robustness
A transformation certified inert in a subsystem may not be exported to a larger relational embedding without a new receipt.

### A6 — Morphism admission integrity
Atlas coherence may not be manufactured by excluding a morphism merely because it carries a dissenting consequence.

### A7 — Anti-capture / preseal provenance
Target, scope, quotient and morphism choices may not be selected after reveal merely to make a progress or authority trajectory appear monotone.

### A8 — Reopening reserve
Sufficient provenance/raw structure must remain available to restore a distinction that later becomes material.

## Frozen countermodel bank

### CM1 — Subsystem inert / relationally active
Two states agree under all internal probes. After composition with an external reference, a relational probe distinguishes them.

Pressure:
`INERT@SUBSYSTEM` does not automatically transport to `INERT@COMPOSITE`.

### CM2 — Generator-wise certification / closure failure
Candidate generators `g1` and `g2` each pass the frozen isolated certification contexts. The declared composition `g2 ∘ g1` reaches a context not covered by those certifications and exposes a material consequence.

Pressure:
isolated generator certification does not establish grammar-level inertness.

This is **not** a claim that genuine symmetries of a closed consequence structure fail under composition.

### CM3 — Over-quotient intervention split
A coarse quotient identifies `x ~ y`, but a frozen intervention has different reachable consequences from `x` and `y`.

Pressure:
the quotient is scientifically too coarse for the declared target/action contract.

### CM4 — Under-quotient representational duplication
Two states differ only by a frozen pure renaming/reparameterization and have identical certified consequences, but a finer constitution counts the transition as progress-bearing change.

Pressure:
the quotient is scientifically too fine for the declared target.

### CM5 — Target-capture laundering
After observing a regress/authority-loss path, a broader quotient is selected that identifies the pre-loss and post-loss states and makes the path appear monotone.

Pressure:
post-reveal chart selection cannot manufacture invariance authority.

### CM6 — Morphism-exclusion coherence
Two charts disagree under a candidate transport. Deleting that arrow yields a coherent atlas.

Pressure:
coherence obtained by pruning dissenting arrows is not evidence unless arrow exclusion has independent warrant.

### CM7 — Rival admissible constitutions
`C1` and `C2` agree on all currently frozen probes but make different predictions for a fresh admissible intervention.

Pressure:
current observational equivalence does not imply unique constitution.

### CM8 — Meta-invariance regress
A meta-rule selects `Γ` because it is invariant under `H`; a rival asks why `H` is privileged.

Pressure:
higher-order symmetry does not terminate warrant by itself.

## Frozen primary hypotheses

### H1 — No self-authorizing invariance grammar
Neither group structure, formal elegance, local invariance nor meta-invariance alone licenses scientific inertness.

### H2 — Local invariance constitutions can earn authority
A constitution can earn scoped, reopenable authority when target-relevant consequences survive prospective probe/intervention/composition/defeat/embedding attacks.

### H3 — Over- and under-quotienting are symmetric constitutional risks
Too much identification and too little identification can both corrupt progress geometry.

### H4 — Morphism choice is load-bearing
Changing admissible arrows can change atlas coherence and transport claims; arrow classes therefore require receipts.

### H5 — Constitution plurality can remain rational
Two surviving constitutions may be incomparable when no current material consequence separates them.

### H6 — No infinite meta-constitution regress is required for local use
The court targets a reopenable operational fixed point, not a final metaphysical foundation.

## Constitution relations

MQR-4.54 freezes three distinct relations and forbids collapsing them into one scalar.

### Refinement
`C2` is finer than `C1` when its equivalence kernel identifies weakly fewer state/process pairs on the same declared domain.

### Compression
`C2` is coarser than `C1` when it identifies weakly more pairs.

### Authority transport
Refinement/compression alone does not imply stronger/weaker scientific authority.

Authority transport requires a separate consequence-preservation receipt over the declared `P/A/M/D/S`.
