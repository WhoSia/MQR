## 1. Measurement theory: permissible transformations are not arbitrary decorations

Elina Vessonen's 2021 discussion of representational measurement theory distinguishes representation theorems from uniqueness theorems.

On that framework:
- a representation theorem links an empirical relational structure to a numerical structure;
- a uniqueness theorem characterizes transformations that preserve the representation and thereby determines the scale type.

MQR consequence:

~~~text
PERMISSIBLE TRANSFORMATION
IS DOWNSTREAM OF
A DECLARED EMPIRICAL / REPRESENTATIONAL STRUCTURE
~~~

not a free choice made after a numerical result is observed.

Source:
- Vessonen (2021), “Representation in measurement”, *European Journal for Philosophy of Science*.
  DOI: `10.1007/s13194-021-00365-6`.

## 2. Symmetry: subsystem inertness can become relationally significant

Brading & Brown (2004) distinguish forms of empirical significance for symmetries and analyse gauge/general-covariance cases rather than treating “symmetry” as automatically synonymous with “unobservable”.

Gomes (2021) develops the subsystem/relational issue sharply: a transformation can be undetectable from the intrinsic perspective of a subsystem while the composite system registers a relational difference under appropriate conditions.

MQR consequence:

~~~text
INTERNAL PROBE INERTNESS
DOES NOT BY ITSELF LICENSE
COMPOSITE-SYSTEM INERTNESS.
~~~

This is prior art pressure behind CM1 and the embedding obligation.

Sources:
- Brading & Brown (2004), “Are Gauge Symmetry Transformations Observable?”, *British Journal for the Philosophy of Science* 55(4), 645–665. DOI: `10.1093/bjps/55.4.645`.
- Gomes (2021), “Holism as the empirical significance of symmetries”, *European Journal for Philosophy of Science* 11, 87. DOI: `10.1007/s13194-021-00397-y`.
- Greaves & Wallace (2014), “Empirical Consequences of Symmetries”, *British Journal for the Philosophy of Science*. DOI: `10.1093/bjps/axt005`.

## 3. Representational schemes: reduction can be plural

Gomes (2025) explicitly develops representational schemes that provide reduced, symmetry-invariant descriptions of isomorphism classes and emphasizes that multiple equally valid reduced formalisms can be available.

The paper therefore blocks a cheap inference from:

~~~text
A REDUCED / QUOTIENTED
REPRESENTATION EXISTS
~~~

to:

~~~text
THIS PARTICULAR REDUCTION
IS UNIQUELY PRIVILEGED.
~~~

MQR consequence:
representational reduction alone does not settle chart-target selection or unique-constitution authority.

Source:
- Gomes (2025), “Representational schemes for theories with symmetry”, *Synthese* 206, 22. DOI: `10.1007/s11229-025-05045-z`.

## 6. Countermodel interpretation ceiling
