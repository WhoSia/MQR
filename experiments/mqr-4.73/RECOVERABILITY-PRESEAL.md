# MQR-4.73 — Recoverability Preseal

Status: PRESEALED / RECOVERABILITY-ORACLE-UNDER-ATTACK / LOSSLESS-vs-SUFFICIENT-vs-POSTHOC-SEPARATED / BLACKWELL-BASELINE-LOCKED / CONSERVATIVITY-BASELINE-LOCKED

## Problem

The first 14-case Court treated predecessor-distinction recoverability as a declared Boolean.

That is too strong. MQR-4.73 must now ask what earns RECOVERABLE.

## Frozen distinctions

RAW-DATA INVERTIBILITY != CLAIM-SCOPE RECOVERABILITY
POST-HOC PREDICTABILITY != RECOVERABILITY
APPROXIMATE RECONSTRUCTION != EXACT OLD-DISTINCTION ACCESS
LOWER UNCERTAINTY != RECOVERABILITY
SUFFICIENT STATISTIC FOR ONE CLAIM != UNIVERSAL INFORMATION PRESERVATION
BLACKWELL DOMINANCE ON FIXED MODEL != OPEN-WORLD SCIENTIFIC DOMINANCE
CONSERVATIVE EXTENSION ON OLD LANGUAGE != GLOBAL FRAMEWORK IDENTITY
CHECKABLE CERTIFICATE != SEMANTIC CONSERVATIVITY

## Candidate recoverability witnesses

R1 LOSSLESS:
An explicit inverse or left-inverse reconstructs the predecessor distinction.

R2 CLAIM-SUFFICIENT:
Raw predecessor data are not reconstructible, but every predecessor authority-changing decision/distinction required by the declared claim scope is preserved.

R3 BLACKWELL-LOCAL:
On a fixed statistical state/action model, the predecessor experiment can be generated from the successor by an admissible garbling kernel. This is prior art and only a local positive control.

R4 CALIBRATION-BRIDGED:
A changed measurement model preserves the predecessor measurand distinction and relevant influence-quantity sensitivity through an explicit calibration/uncertainty bridge.

R5 CONSERVATIVE-MATH:
A framework extension preserves all predecessor-language consequences relevant to the inherited theorem family.

R6 CERTIFICATE-RECONSTRUCTED:
A new proof-search medium emits a certificate checked by a trusted kernel under unchanged declared semantics.

## Negative witnesses

N1 POSTHOC_ONLY:
A learned predictor can guess old classes after irreversible merging, but individual old distinctions are not recoverable.

N2 LOSSY_QUOTIENT:
Two predecessor defeat distinctions map to one successor state and no claim-sufficient decoder exists.

N3 MODEL-DEPENDENT_SIMULATION:
Recovery works only by assuming the very successor model whose authority is at issue.

N4 NONCONSERVATIVE_EXTENSION:
The stronger framework changes predecessor-language consequences or semantics.

N5 NEW-CAPABILITY-COMPENSATION:
A novel distinction is exposed while an old live distinction remains lost.

## Court rule candidate

For declared claim scope S and live predecessor obligation family Omega:

RECOVERABLE_S(old -> new)

may be certified by exact inversion OR by a weaker claim-sufficient witness that preserves every authority-changing predecessor distinction required by S.

Post-hoc correlation or model-dependent reconstruction does not suffice by itself.

## Open-world firewall

Any local recoverability certificate remains relative to:
- declared claim scope;
- declared predecessor obligations;
- declared semantic/measurand bridge;
- current challenge family.

It does not prove future defeat-space closure.

## Main falsifier

If CLAIM-SUFFICIENT recoverability cannot be distinguished from MQR-4.49 local defeat-role equivalence, then this entire layer is absorbed and 4.73 must retreat to a narrower dominance receipt only.
