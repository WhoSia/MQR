## Intake sweep donors

### Callahan & Catanach 2026 — On the Misinformation in a Statistical Experiment
Under model and inference misspecification, an experiment ranked as more informative under the working model can amplify bias and produce confidently wrong inference. Classical Blackwell monotonicity is therefore not available as an open-world dominance oracle.

MQR consequence:
BLACKWELL DOMINANCE UNDER A FIXED WORKING MODEL
!=
ROBUST DEFEAT-CAPABILITY DOMINANCE.

### Tang, Sloman & Kaski 2026 — Robust Bayesian Active Learning under Model Misspecification
Acquisition aimed only at informativeness can create harmful train/test mismatch and error amplification under model misspecification. Their representativeness and de-amplification terms are prior-art donors for robustness pressure.

MQR consequence:
INFORMATIVENESS
!=
ROBUST CONTACT ADEQUACY.

### Kalman 1963 — Mathematical Description of Linear Dynamical Systems
Input/output behavior determines only the observable and controllable part of a linear realization. Internal distinctions outside that surface are not automatically recoverable.

MQR consequence:
RECOVERABILITY MUST BE TIED TO AN EXPLICIT SEPARATOR/OBSERVABILITY SURFACE,
NOT TO A CLAIM THAT THE SUCCESSOR CONTAINS "MORE STATE".

### Hyttinen, Eberhardt & Hoyer 2013 / Hauser & Bühlmann 2014
Interventional causal discovery already formalizes experiment families whose purpose is to separate otherwise indistinguishable causal structures. MQR claims no novelty for separating systems, identifiability, or optimal intervention selection.

Residual use:
a predecessor defeat distinction counts as preserved only if some admissible successor challenge still separates the alternatives when that distinction remains authority-relevant.

### Vessonen 2021 / JCGM VIM
Measurement representation, measurand, calibration, traceability, uncertainty and compatibility are typed. A numerical improvement or traceability chain does not by itself establish preserved sensitivity to every old systematic failure.

### Norton 2003
Inductive warrant is domain-local rather than supplied by a universal inductive schema. This supports keeping the final MQR dominance relation scope- and background-relative.

## Surviving constitutional residue

The remaining candidate is narrower:

A successor may inherit a claim of stronger defeat capability only when each still-live predecessor authority-changing distinction remains recoverable through an explicit separator or transport witness across a declared admissible perturbation/misspecification envelope.

Nominal decision equivalence under one working model is insufficient when the witness disappears under an admissible perturbation.

This is not a universal information order, truth metric, or final failure ontology.
