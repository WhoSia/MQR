## Court P1 — Axiom-Laundering Paradox

### Argument

A proof assistant can kernel-check a theorem derived from an empirical premise encoded as an axiom.

### Attack

Let empirical premise E remain HOLD.
Encode E as a proposition available to a checked proof of Q.

Kernel checking establishes:

```text
E |- Q
```

not:

```text
World |= E
```

Therefore Q cannot inherit empirical PASS merely from CHECKED formal validity.

### Surviving principle

```text
FORMAL_VALIDITY != EMPIRICAL_ENTITLEMENT
FORMAL_CERTAINTY_CANNOT_LAUNDER_EMPIRICAL_UNCERTAINTY
```

Real-Language v0.4 implements this as an authority ceiling.

## Court P2 — Probe-Closure Paradox

### Argument

Suppose a finite probe class P is fully enumerated and every D-relevant probe is discharged.

A proof assistant may certify:

```text
R_int(P,D)=empty
```

### Attack

Construct a strict extension P+ with a new well-formed probe not represented in P.

The old certificate remains valid about P while saying nothing about whether the new probe is world-relevant.

### Surviving principle

```text
ZERO_INTERNAL_RESIDUE(P,D)
DOES_NOT_IMPLY
ZERO_OPEN_WORLD_RESIDUE
```

The formal extension theorem shows the logical gap. It does not assert that nature realizes every syntactic extension.

## Court P3 — Kernel–World Cut Problem

Every proof-carrying scientific system must contain a boundary at which a world-facing event becomes a formal proposition.

Move the boundary downstream:
- more of the physical/measurement chain remains unverified by the prover.

Move it upstream:
- increasingly strong modeling assumptions are required to encode physical coupling.

There is no free placement that turns the kernel into a physical oracle.

### Surviving principle

Every proof-bearing claim must expose:
- the kernel-checked region;
- the empirical/model premise frontier;
- the provenance path crossing that frontier.

## Court P4 — Receipt Reification Problem

### Temptation

A cryptographically stable, typed and formally verified measurement receipt looks increasingly object-like.

### Attack

Receipt identity certifies a preserved representation of an event.
It does not imply that the represented measurement model exhausts the event's possible physical interpretations.

### Surviving principle

```text
IMMUTABLE_RECEIPT != IMMUTABLE_ONTOLOGY
```

Receipt custody strengthens provenance and reproducibility, not final metaphysical authority.

## Court P5 — Counterexample-Generator Exteriority Problem

A formal system may contain an extremely powerful automated adversary.

But if every generated counterexample must already be expressible in the current ontology, type universe or probe grammar, the adversary can be exhaustive internally while blind externally.

This mirrors MQR's earlier monitor/exteriority problem.

### Surviving principle

```text
INTERNAL_SEARCH_COMPLETENESS != REPRESENTATIONAL_EXTERIORITY
```

A proof assistant can exhaust a formal search space and still fail to invent the primitive needed to describe a real anomaly.

## Court P6 — Model-Soundness / Kernel-Soundness Split

A kernel may correctly certify:

```text
M and A1 and ... and An |- Q
```

while M is a poor model of the target world.

Thus kernel soundness does not dominate model adequacy.

### Surviving principle

Scientific proof-bearing authority is conjunctive:

```text
FORMAL_CERTIFICATE
AND
EMPIRICAL_RECEIPT
AND
MODEL/BRIDGE ENTITLEMENT
```

No component may replace the others.

## Court P7 — Decision-Relative Closure Problem

A finite probe algebra may be complete for one action family D even while failing to identify full empirical reach.

Example:

```text
E0_FULL and E1_QUOTIENT_ONLY
```

can legitimately collapse to the same action when D asks only whether core reuse is permitted.

### Attack

Change the decision to one sensitive to refinement-only behavior.
The old quotient may cease to be sufficient without any factual change in the world.

### Surviving principle

```text
SUFFICIENCY IS RELATIVE TO A DECLARED DECISION LOSS / ACTION FAMILY
```

Decision-relative sufficiency is not truth-relative sufficiency.

## Court P8 — Proof-Carrying Measurement Regress

If every empirical premise requires a proof of calibration, and that calibration proof itself relies on empirical premises, demanding theorem-level discharge of every dependency creates a regress.

### Attack

Do not solve the regress by declaring foundational empirical axioms true.

Instead terminate formal proof at typed world-contact receipts whose authority is independently governed by measurement/provenance/corrigibility rules.

### Surviving architecture

```text
WORLD CONTACT
 -> TYPED EMPIRICAL RECEIPT
 -> FORMALIZED PREMISE WITH AUTHORITY STATE
 -> KERNEL-CHECKED CONSEQUENCE
 -> AUTHORITY = meet(formal validity, empirical entitlement)
```

This is provisionally named:

**Proof-Carrying Realist Authority (PCRA)**.

PCRA is a design hypothesis, not a new metaphysical primitive.

## Court P9 — Theorem-Prover Realism Trap

A tempting slogan is:

> If the entire scientific pipeline were encoded in Lean, scientific truth would become theoremhood.

The UV–Vis origin defeats this.

No amount of verified post-processing can recover a distinction never coupled into the sensor signal.

The theorem prover may establish the correctness of:

```text
ADC bits -> baseline correction -> absorbance -> constrained inference
```

relative to its premises.

It does not replace:

```text
sample -> photons -> detector
```

or prove that the measurement model is exhaustive.

### Surviving principle

```text
PROOF ASSISTANT = LOGICAL PROCESSOR AUDITOR
NOT
PHYSICAL ORACLE
```

## Court P10 — Open-World Residue Bounding Question

Question:

> Can residue be finitely bounded for an explicitly declared intervention/probe class without closing the world?

Answer form allowed in MQR-4.36:

YES, internally, if all five obligations are earned:
1. finite/provably normal-form closure;
2. complete enumerator;
3. explicit decision relevance predicate;
4. exhaustive execution/discharge of all relevant members;
5. typed missingness/failure semantics.

Then:

```text
INTERNAL_RESIDUE(P,D)=ZERO
```

is a real finite theorem.

But:

```text
OUTSIDE(P)
```

remains constitutively open unless a stronger world-adequacy result is separately earned.

Thus finite closure and open-world realism are compatible when their quantifiers are not silently exchanged.

## Court P11 — Decision-Compression Sanctuary

### Temptation

If a representation gives the same action under the current decision rule, call it sufficient.

### Attack

F-R1 contains:

```text
JSCHEMA-001 -> E1_QUOTIENT_ONLY
OASV-001    -> E0_FULL
```

so exact empirical reach differs.

Yet the frozen coarse decision maps both to:

```text
D36_CORE -> ALLOW_CORE_REUSE
```

Therefore action agreement can arise because the decision ignores a real distinction.

### Surviving principle

```text
DECISION_SUFFICIENCY
!=
REPRESENTATIONAL_SUFFICIENCY
```

Decision-relative sufficiency is legitimate only when its action/loss family is explicit and frozen.

It cannot be promoted into a claim that the representation captures all scientifically relevant distinctions.

## Court P15 — Boundary-Migration Problem

### Temptation

Move more of the scientific pipeline into Lean until the uncertain region becomes negligible.

### Attack

Formalizing detector arithmetic, calibration transforms, normalization and inference moves the kernel/world frontier.

It does not eliminate the frontier.

The remaining empirical/model premises become fewer but more load-bearing.

### Surviving principle

```text
FORMALIZATION CAN MOVE EPISTEMIC DEBT
BUT CANNOT ERASE WORLD-CONTACT DEBT
```

The correct design goal is not maximal theoremization.

It is maximal auditability of where formal certainty ends and empirical entitlement begins.
