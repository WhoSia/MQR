## Primary object: bridge–direct domain morphism

Let:

- B = the declared support family on which adjacent edges are tested;
- D = the independently declared direct A→C support family;
- q_B = the bridge claim quotient;
- q_D = the direct claim quotient.

The MQR-4.34 support object is:

```text
MU(B -> D) = {
  DOMAIN_RELATION,
  SEMANTIC_MAP,
  COMPONENT_RELATION,
  QUOTIENT_RELATION,
  STATE_RELATION,
  ENDPOINT_CONTRACT
}
```

The object is relational.

It is not a score.

## Frozen domain-relation types

### R0 — SAME_GENERATOR_HOLDOUT

B and D are disjoint observed fixture sets but are sampled/generated under the same declared support-generating constitution G.

Required:
- same semantic grammar/specification scope;
- same component contract;
- D does not introduce a new admissible input family or new semantic distinction;
- fixture identity may differ.

This is not equality of observed sets.

It is common support ancestry.

### R1 — STRICT_DOMAIN_EXTENSION

The direct family D contains a predeclared admissible family not contained in the bridge support constitution.

Formally:

```text
G_B proper-subset G_D
```

at the declared semantic scope.

D therefore asks the adjacent evidence to cross a support boundary.

### R2 — SUCCESSOR_REFINEMENT

D preserves the bridge-observable quotient but introduces distinctions that B cannot express.

There must exist a declared forgetting/coarsening map:

```text
pi : q_D -> q_B
```

such that old distinctions are recoverable at q_B while D may carry additional successor-only structure.

Support may therefore be quotient-indexed.

### R3 — CONSTITUTIVE_COMPONENT_SHIFT

The bridge and direct surfaces do not instantiate the same component or an explicitly translated quotient.

No support-preserving morphism is admitted by default.

An explicit translator may reopen the question only in a successor stage or a separately presealed subcase.

### R4 — OVERLAP_NONNESTED

B and D overlap materially but neither support constitution contains the other and no single declared generator relation justifies SAME_GENERATOR_HOLDOUT.

Composition support is unresolved without a more specific bridge.

### R5 — UNRESOLVED

Available metadata is insufficient to type the bridge–direct relation.

No positive support authority may be inferred.

## Prospective support-authority map

MQR-4.34 does not use relation type to predict raw PASS/FAIL deterministically.

It uses relation type to determine what adjacent evidence is entitled to support before direct reveal.

### A0 — HOLDOUT_SUPPORT_ADMISSIBLE

Required:
- R0 SAME_GENERATOR_HOLDOUT;
- SAME_COMPONENT or SAME_QUOTIENT;
- SAME_QUOTIENT;
- reset/state equivalence adequate to the claim;
- same endpoint contract.

Meaning:
adjacent evidence may license a sealed mediated A→C prediction at the existing claim scope.

The direct test may still falsify that prediction.

A direct failure does not retroactively turn R0 into R1.

### A1 — EXTENSION_REQUIRES_FRESH_CONTACT

Required:
R1 STRICT_DOMAIN_EXTENSION.

Meaning:
adjacent PASS over B does not license the direct D claim.

The direct extension is a new world-contact burden.

Even if the direct result later passes, adjacent evidence alone did not already support it.

### A2 — REFINEMENT_SUPPORT_IS_QUOTIENT_INDEXED

Required:
R2 SUCCESSOR_REFINEMENT.

Meaning:
adjacent evidence may support transport only at the preserved coarse quotient q_B.

Successor-only distinctions in q_D require fresh direct contact.

A direct PASS at q_B and divergence at q_D is not contradiction.

### A3 — CONSTITUTIVE_BREAK_NO_CANDIDATE

Required:
R3 CONSTITUTIVE_COMPONENT_SHIFT.

Meaning:
no composition candidate exists without an explicit translator.

### A4 — OVERLAP_HOLD

Required:
R4 OVERLAP_NONNESTED.

Meaning:
HOLD.

### A5 — RELATION_UNRESOLVED_HOLD

Required:
R5 UNRESOLVED.

Meaning:
HOLD.

## What “identifiable” means in MQR-4.34

MQR-4.34 does NOT define identifiability as perfect prediction of direct PASS/FAIL.

It defines prospective support identifiability as all of:

1. the relation object MU is computable from pre-direct evidence;
2. independent implementations/adjudicators agree on MU;
3. irrelevant metadata mutations do not change MU;
4. known relation-changing interventions do change MU in the frozen direction;
5. the authority state A0..A5 is uniquely determined by MU;
6. the authority state is frozen before direct reveal;
7. direct outcome does not cause retrospective relation retyping;
8. fresh external cases across research and engineering survive this procedure without unresolved profile collision at the authority-state level.

This is identifiability of evidential reach, not truth prediction.

## Strong falsifiers

The relational support representation is defeated or must contract if any occurs:

- two fresh cases have identical full MU receipts but incompatible authority states;
- a fresh R0 case is discovered to contain an undeclared semantic/domain extension;
- an R1 case can only be recognized using the direct outcome;
- an R2 case lacks a valid forgetting map;
- relation type changes under irrelevant repository metadata mutation;
- relation type fails to change after a known support-domain intervention;
- research and engineering cases require mutually incompatible semantics for the same relation label;
- inherited TKN/TOML can be separated only by adding a rule after their mapping.
