# MQR-4.82 — PRESEAL

Status: **PRESEALED / OUTCOME-BLIND / COHERENCE-TERMINATION-AUTHORITY-UNDER-ATTACK**

## Explanandum

Suppose MQR-4.81 requires higher-order coherence data before pairwise coverage warrants descend globally.

When may inquiry stop asking for certificates of those certificates?

The Court does not ask whether infinite regress exists in epistemology. It asks what specific conditions authorize a **finite coherence truncation** for a declared scientific claim scope.

## Frozen candidate objects

- claim scope `S`;
- declared coherence levels `L_0 ... L_n`;
- checked obstruction family `O_{≤n}`;
- unchecked higher levels `O_{>n}`;
- finite coherence basis `B_n`;
- basis-authority source `W(B_n)`;
- claim-relevance rule for higher levels;
- explicit residual higher-order debt;
- reopening trigger;
- scope/version receipt.

## Frozen nonidentities

```text
FINITE_COHERENCE_PASS != OPEN_WORLD_COHERENCE_COMPLETENESS
NO_OBSTRUCTION_BELOW_N != NO_OBSTRUCTION_ABOVE_N
TRUNCATION_LEVEL != NATURAL_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY
MINIMAL_BASIS != GLOBAL_COMPLETENESS
HIGHER_ORDER_CERTIFICATE != SELF_WARRANTING_CERTIFICATE
CERTIFICATE_OF_CERTIFICATE != AUTOMATIC_NEW_REQUIREMENT
FINITE_TERMINATION != FINALITY
REOPENABILITY != FAILURE_OF_PRIOR_SCOPED_AUTHORITY
```

## Candidate positive rule

A finite coherence basis may terminate certificate recursion only when:
1. the claim scope is explicit;
2. the relevance rule for higher-order distinctions is independently warranted;
3. the checked basis is sufficient relative to that rule;
4. residual unchecked levels are represented as bounded debt rather than silently denied;
5. new claim-relevant higher-order obstruction reopens the certificate;
6. closure is versioned and scope-relative.

## Forbidden moves

- choose `n` merely because computation stopped;
- treat no obstruction through `n` as proof of no obstruction above `n`;
- require another certificate solely because the current certificate exists;
- infer open-world coherence from finite truncation;
- deny all finite authority because open-world completeness is unavailable;
- make the termination rule self-certifying.
