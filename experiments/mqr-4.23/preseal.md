# MQR-4.23 — Persistent-Authorship Necessity Attack, Keyless Principal Equivalence, Attribution–Epistemic Authority Separation, Missing-Key Counterfactual & Whether Author Continuity Adds Any Scientific Authority

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEAL

## Objective
Test whether persistent authorship continuity contributes scientific/epistemic authority, or only attribution/accountability continuity.

## Frozen distinction
AUTHORITY classes:
A. ATTRIBUTION AUTHORITY
   - who/what signing principal authorized or authored a receipt;
   - continuity, accountability, provenance linkage.

B. EPISTEMIC AUTHORITY
   - whether a scientific claim may be promoted under the MQR evidence constitution.

The two classes may correlate operationally but are not assumed identical.

## Missing-key counterfactual
Hold fixed:
- constitutional authority = PASS;
- execution receipt = PASS;
- provenance/time authority = PASS;
- public auditability = PASS.

Compare:
WORLD K+: persistent-author-continuity attestation present.
WORLD K-: persistent-author-continuity attestation absent.

Frozen promotion law:
PROMOTE := C AND E AND P.

Author-key term is deliberately excluded unless 4.23 produces independent evidence that it is epistemically necessary.

## Necessity criteria
Persistent authorship adds SCIENTIFIC authority only if at least one of the following is established prospectively:
N1. Removing author continuity makes an otherwise identical scientific claim non-identifiable or non-auditable.
N2. Removing author continuity invalidates a constitutional/execution/provenance condition rather than merely attribution.
N3. An independent adjudicator identifies a non-attribution scientific failure caused specifically by missing persistent authorship.

If K+ and K- produce the same admissible scientific promotion state while differing only in attribution continuity:
SCIENTIFIC-AUTHORITY CONTRIBUTION = ZERO under this contract.

## Anti-laundering rule
Keyless OIDC principal continuity may serve as an attribution comparator.
It may not be promoted to persistent-key equivalence unless the same scientific consequence is demonstrated.

## Scope
This stage asks necessity, not convenience.
Persistent keys may still improve:
- authorship continuity;
- accountability;
- archival attribution;
- compromise recovery.
Those benefits do not automatically constitute scientific authority.
