# MQR-4.26 — Origin Return: Science, Mystery, History and Authority

## Why this stage returns to the beginning
MQR began from an instrumentation problem but was never merely an instrumentation theory.

Generation I asked:
when measurement/intervention regimes induce local distinctions, which of those distinctions deserve world-side authority rather than being artifacts of the regime?

MQR-1.10 then stepped backward ontically:
- can A and B both be real without multiplying reality whenever descriptions multiply?
- is there a non-vacuous higher law implementing observed and unobserved cases?
- why is a lookup-table "law" not enough?
- can simulation relocate the implementation question without answering it?

MQR-3.30 returned to the original uncertainty:
when a restriction survives inquiry, what licenses attributing it to the world rather than to observation, representation, model class, protocol, search policy, or theory lineage?

MQR-3.76 then reached the historical/Pessimistic-Induction pressure:
current victory over represented rivals does not imply exhaustion of unconceived successor alternatives.

MQR-4.26 treats that not as a side issue but as a founding constraint.

## Origin Compass

### O1 — World resistance before theory identity
Scientific realist authority belongs first to non-vacuous constraints that survive admissible defeat, not to the proper name of a theory.

### O2 — Reality may be nonexclusive
Two levels or representations may both carry real constraint structure.
But redescription alone does not multiply ontology.

### O3 — Global fit is not global law
A law earns stronger authority only by modal excess:
it must exclude alternatives, survive new domains, and remain exposed to defeat.
A total lookup table is not enough.

### O4 — Current rival victory is not exhaustive alternative elimination
Unconceived alternatives are not present live rivals.
Their possibility therefore cannot be mechanically "defeated".
Instead it blocks claims of exhaustive closure.

### O5 — Authority can be strong without being final
Scientific authority is temporally indexed, scope-bound, and successor-vulnerable.
A later theory may contract or reinterpret an earlier authority receipt without proving that all earlier success was illusory.

### O6 — Mystery is residue, not embarrassment
Where science cannot distinguish governing law from Humean system, simulator from empirically equivalent nonsimulator, or one unseen rival from another, MQR must preserve that remainder explicitly.
Unresolved ontology is not equivalent to no knowledge.

## Historical mirrors

### H1 — Mercury: authority with residue
Newtonian celestial mechanics had extraordinary successful reach.
Mercury's residual perihelion discrepancy nevertheless persisted after known planetary perturbations were accounted for.
Le Verrier proposed an intra-Mercurial planet, Vulcan.
General relativity later accounted for the anomalous precession through a different gravitational framework.

MQR lesson:
a powerful theory may possess real scoped authority while an adverse residue remains live.
The later successor does not imply that all Newtonian constraint authority was unreal.
Nor did nineteenth-century success exhaust future gravitational rivals.

Classification:
SUCCESS_WITH_RESIDUE / SUCCESSOR_VULNERABILITY / NO_EXHAUSTIVE_RIVAL_CLOSURE.

### H2 — Antarctic ozone: architecture can hide surprise
British Antarctic Survey ground measurements produced the 1985 ozone-hole report.
NASA satellite records also contained extreme Antarctic ozone behavior, but did not produce the first published discovery.
Historical accounts disagree on the exact contribution of automated flagging/fill-value processing, which itself is a useful warning against turning a simplified retrospective story into a new myth.

MQR lesson:
world-contact may exist while processing, quality-control expectations, and attention architecture delay recognition.
The epistemic problem is not simply whether data bytes existed, but whether the inquiry architecture allowed an anomalous pattern to become an admissible scientific object.

Classification:
WORLD-CONTACT-PRESENT / ARCHITECTURE-MEDIATED-RECOGNITION / HISTORIOGRAPHIC-RESIDUE-RETAINED.

### H3 — H. pylori: method changes rival availability
Gastric spiral bacteria had been reported long before Warren and Marshall.
The stomach was widely treated as inhospitable to persistent bacterial colonization.
Early culture attempts failed.
A longer incubation over the Easter interval yielded colonies after about five days rather than the shorter Campylobacter-style incubation routine.
Subsequent intervention and replication transformed the causal status of H. pylori in gastritis and ulcer disease.

MQR lesson:
a rival may be neither logically impossible nor "defeated"; it may be practically unavailable under the active method grammar.
Changing the measurement/culture regime can change which hypotheses are even empirically expressible.

Classification:
METHOD-GRAMMAR-EXPANSION / RIVAL-AVAILABILITY-MUTATION / WORLD-CONTACT-RECONSTITUTION.

It answers with:
EARNED PRESENT AUTHORITY + OPEN SUCCESSOR RESERVE.

## Formal bridge to MQR-4.26
Let Gamma_t be the rival-generating resources historically available at time t.

Let:
S_t = {h generated/admitted by Gamma_t that survive current E_t under A_t}.

For claim phi:
R_t(phi)=PASS iff every h in S_t entails phi.

But:
R_t(phi) never entails S_t = ALL POSSIBLE RIVALS.

Therefore every realist promotion is typed:

REALIST_AUTHORITY(phi, t, Gamma_t, A_t, Scope_t)
+
OPEN_WORLD_RESIDUE.

The historical meaning is precise:
Newton can be authoritative without already containing Einstein.
A culture protocol can be rational without proving that slow-growing rivals do not exist.
A satellite pipeline can be sophisticated without guaranteeing that unprecedented patterns will be epistemically foregrounded.
A present theory can genuinely constrain the world without possessing its own future vocabulary.

## Open-world identified-set closure
MQR therefore rejects two symmetric failures.

### Failure 1 — Finalist realism
"We have defeated every serious current rival, therefore the promoted theory is approximately the final ontology."

REJECT.

### Failure 2 — Unconceived-alternative paralysis
"Because some future rival might exist, no current realist authority is ever possible."

REJECT.

### MQR replacement
Promote only the strongest claim invariant across the current adversarially generated surviving set;
make the rival-generation provenance explicit;
retain open-world residue;
and precommit to contraction when a new surviving rival breaks the claim.

This is not weak realism.
It is realism whose authority is earned by what the world has actually forced, rather than borrowed from imagined completeness.

## Relation to the old global-law question
The finite-evidence escape construction also returns directly to MQR-1.10.

A candidate global law that merely fits all observed points can always be rivaled by another function that agrees on those points and diverges elsewhere unless additional structure is independently warranted.

Therefore:
FINITE FIT != GLOBAL LAW.

The non-triviality burden for a deeper law is exactly what MQR-1.10 anticipated:
- independent specification;
- compression;
- generative excess;
- novel exclusion;
- cross-domain transport;
- defeat exposure.

MQR-4.26 gives that old intuition a constructive open-world proof pattern.

## Root-return verdict
MQR has not become a governance/security project whose subject happens to be science.

The cryptographic/governance sequence of 4.9–4.24 answered a narrower question:
what makes authority receipts independent, auditable and non-self-certifying?

MQR-4.25–4.26 return those receipts to the original target:
what, if anything, has the world actually forced us to keep believing when representation, method, history and future rival vocabularies can change?

The project remains about the authority of reality over inquiry, not the authority of inquiry over itself.
