# MQR-4.26 — Rival-Generator Incompleteness Attack, Open-World Hypothesis Escape, Claim-Invariance under Expanding Rival Classes, Identified-Set Closure & Whether R Can Be Operational without Exhaustive Hypothesis Enumeration

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEAL

## Origin return
MQR-4.26 is not a rival-taxonomy expansion exercise.

It returns to the founding uncertainty:
When a restriction survives inquiry, what licenses attributing it to the world rather than to the architecture of observation, representation, model class, search policy, or theory lineage?

Three origin questions are explicitly active:
O1. Can multiple non-identical structures be real without ontology inflation?
O2. Can a global law earn authority by modal/exclusion excess rather than total historical fit?
O3. Can scientific authority be strong and realist while future unconceived rivals remain possible?

MQR-4.26 is primarily O3, constrained by O1/O2.

## Attack target
MQR-4.25 added:
R = claim-level rival separation / inferential identifiability.

The attack:
Any operational rival generator is finite, grammar-bounded, or otherwise historically situated.
A hidden rival outside the current generator may survive all current C/E/P/R checks.

Question:
Can R remain operational without pretending that every possible hypothesis has been enumerated?

## Absolute-closure prohibition
The following inference is forbidden:

NO CURRENT SURVIVING RIVAL
therefore
NO POSSIBLE RIVAL EXISTS.

No finite rival search may certify exhaustive hypothesis closure.

## Finite-evidence escape construction
For any finite observed set O and baseline function f0 over a domain containing some unobserved x*:

h_c(x) = f0(x) + c * product_{o in O}(x-o)

For c != 0:
- h_c(o)=f0(o) for every observed o in O;
- for generic x* outside O, h_c(x*) != f0(x*).

Therefore finite observations alone do not identify unrestricted extrapolative lawhood.

## Operational R_open
R is mutated, not replaced.

At time t:
- E_t = admitted world-contact record;
- A_t = constitutionally licensed structural assumptions;
- Gamma_t = current rival-generator portfolio;
- S_t = surviving rivals generated under A_t and consistent with E_t;
- phi = claim under evaluation.

Define:
R_t(phi)=PASS iff every h in S_t entails phi.

But every PASS carries:
OPEN_WORLD_RESIDUE = ACTIVE.

This means:
- R_t is a current claim-invariance certificate;
- it is not a proof that S_t exhausts all possible rivals;
- future generator expansion may contract or revoke phi.

## Expanding-rival update
For Gamma_t subset Gamma_{t+1}:

S_{t+1} may include newly generated survivors.

If all new survivors still entail phi:
authority for phi survives.

If some new survivor violates phi:
- revoke unique-claim authority;
- contract to the invariant/identified core shared by survivors;
- preserve the prior receipt as historically valid relative to Gamma_t;
- do not rewrite the earlier state as fraudulent merely because a successor rival was unconceived.

## Identified-set closure
Closure means:
closed under the CURRENT admitted rival-generating process.

It does NOT mean:
metaphysically exhaustive closure over all conceivable theories.

Authorized outputs:
- UNIQUE_WITHIN_CURRENT_OPEN_WORLD_TEST
- IDENTIFIED_SET_CURRENT
- SCOPE_CONTRACTED
- SUCCESSOR_VULNERABLE
- GLOBAL_EXHAUSTIVENESS_HOLD

## Generator-diversity requirement
A single rival generator cannot certify R_open.

At least these distinct pressures must be representable:
G1. parameter/basis mutation;
G2. structural/grammar mutation;
G3. assumption relaxation;
G4. adversarial diagonal escape targeted at unobserved claim consequences;
G5. historically imported successor pattern when available.

Passing several generators increases current authority but never converts finite search into exhaustive closure.

## Success criterion
R can be operational without exhaustive enumeration iff:
1. an over-strong claim is broken by a later generator expansion;
2. a weaker invariant core survives that expansion;
3. the system automatically contracts rather than retroactively laundering the old claim;
4. a generic diagonal escape shows absolute finite rival closure is impossible for unrestricted extrapolation;
5. R_open remains executable as a claim-relative, time-indexed, successor-vulnerable certificate;
6. no new authority dimension is required beyond C/E/P/R.

## Historical-root requirement
This stage must connect the formal result to at least three historical patterns:
- authority with unresolved anomaly / successor theory;
- inquiry architecture initially suppressing or discounting surprising contact;
- method/rival availability changing what the scientific community can even formulate or recover.

History is used as adversarial pressure on the constitution, not as decoration.

## Metaphysical ceiling
MQR may warrant:
world-supported constraint authority.

MQR may not infer merely from R_open:
- final ontology;
- unique ultimate law;
- exhaustive rival elimination;
- metaphysical governing necessity.

Mystery/residue is retained as an explicit scientific state, not treated as failure.
