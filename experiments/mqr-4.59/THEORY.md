# MQR-4.59 — Promotion Without a Final Meta-Objective

Status: **POST-REVEAL / PRE-CLOSURE / FULL-STRESS-QUALIFYING-PASS**

## 1. Promotion is a governance act

A scientific programme can distinguish:

- whether inquiry has progressed;
- whether a candidate claim is warranted;
- whether a successor constitution should replace or modify the live one.

MQR-4.59 concerns the third.

Therefore:

~~~text
PROMOTION
IS A GOVERNANCE DECISION

NOT

A DEFINITION
OF SCIENTIFIC PROGRESS.
~~~

## 2. Why additive novelty fails

The inherited RCSR rule rewarded:
- a new executable distinction;
- a new world-contact route;
- a real operational repair.

These are often valuable.

They are not a complete basis.

A successor can improve the programme by subtraction:
- remove a false primitive;
- merge redundant receipts;
- narrow an authority claim;
- eliminate a live rival;
- block an invalid cast.

Conversely, a successor can add:
- new code;
- new terminology;
- new status labels;
- new tests;

while changing no material scientific distinction.

Hence:

~~~text
ADD
!=
ADVANCE

SUBTRACT
!=
RETREAT.
~~~

## 3. Promotion is typed

The candidate contribution profile is not one number.

It separates:
- material executable separation;
- independent world contact;
- scientific repair;
- simplification;
- claim narrowing;
- failure localization;
- scope correction;
- option value;
- archive value;
- duplication;
- criterion provenance.

This gives a partial decision relation:

~~~text
PROMOTE
COMPRESS
ARCHIVE
REJECT
HOLD.
~~~

## 4. Compression is not merely storage economy

Compression can be epistemic when it shows that two constitutional distinctions make no difference across the live consequence surface.

That can:
- reduce false ontology;
- lower audit debt;
- prevent double counting;
- simplify future failure localization.

But compression is not automatically good.

If a removed distinction later matters under new consequences, reopening is mandatory.

## 5. Negative knowledge is structurally asymmetric

A strong prospective result may remove a live possibility without supplying a replacement.

The live space becomes smaller.

That can be an epistemic gain because future claims and experiments are constrained differently.

MQR-4.59 therefore refuses:

~~~text
POSITIVE REPLACEMENT
AS A NECESSARY CONDITION
FOR PROMOTION.
~~~

## 6. Failure localization can matter before prediction changes

A successor can preserve all current predictions yet alter what a future failure would mean.

This changes:
- attribution;
- reopening;
- experimental priority;
- authority routing.

Diagnostic structure therefore has possible promotion authority.

## 7. Option value is not expected-progress scalarization

Some contributions matter because they open a later testable route.

This does not license:
- one expected scientific value number;
- one universal horizon;
- one optimal portfolio.

Option value is typed and declared.

It remains defeasible when the future route fails to materialize.

## 8. Archive is not reject

A historical or conceptual contribution may remain valuable without changing the live constitution.

MQR-4.59 therefore separates:

~~~text
ARCHIVE
!=
REJECT.
~~~

This prevents the live formal surface from becoming a museum while preserving intellectual lineage.

## 9. Engineering repair is not theory promotion

Execution matters.

A broken compiler, parser or CI gate can block real work.

Repairing it deserves engineering authority.

But:

~~~text
RESTORED EXECUTION
!=
NEW SCIENTIFIC DISTINCTION.
~~~

This keeps release/version governance distinct from theoretical-stage promotion.

## 10. Meta-criterion lineage

Any promotion rule changes what research artifacts are rewarded.

Therefore the criterion itself needs provenance.

MCL records:
- which criterion was active;
- when it was frozen;
- what candidate was still hidden;
- why it changed;
- what defect forced reopening.

This blocks post-result criterion substitution.

## 11. Reflexive Goodhart pressure

Once researchers know that promotion rewards:
- more executable distinctions;
- more countermodels;
- more CI;
- more receipts;

they can produce exactly those artifacts.

Therefore a recursive research system needs a distinction between:

~~~text
VISIBLE AUDIT OUTPUT
AND
MATERIAL SCIENTIFIC LEVERAGE.
~~~

The audit system itself is a proxy surface.

## 12. No cheap localism

Rejecting a universal promotion scalar does not imply arbitrary case-by-case judgment.

A local decision still requires:
- explicit reasons;
- declared scope;
- frozen criterion version;
- comparison to predecessor;
- material consequence;
- reopening.

Thus MQR-4.59 occupies a middle position:

~~~text
NO UNIVERSAL META-OBJECTIVE

BUT

NO UNTRACKED
CASE-BY-CASE DISCRETION.
~~~

## 13. Relation to scientific progress literature

The stage is compatible with multiple accounts of progress.

Truthlikeness, knowledge, understanding, problem-solving and other progress theories can disagree about what ultimately constitutes progress.

RPAR does not resolve that dispute.

Instead it governs whether a successor has earned a place in the current operational constitution.

## 14. Relation to research assessment

Research evaluation criteria are performative.

They can shape:
- what researchers produce;
- which outputs become legible;
- what gets optimized.

Therefore promotion criteria must be:
- transparent;
- typed;
- contextual;
- revisable;
- provenance-bearing.

But transparency does not make the criterion final.

## 15. Strong synthesis

~~~text
A RECURSIVE SCIENCE
NEEDS RULES
FOR WHAT BECOMES LIVE.

BUT

THE RULE FOR
WHAT BECOMES LIVE

MUST NOT BECOME
THE FINAL THEORY
OF WHAT SCIENCE
IS FOR.
~~~

## 16. Successor-facing remainder

MQR-4.59 still uses reasons such as:
- simplification;
- localization;
- scope correction;
- option value.

A deeper successor can ask:

> When these reasons conflict, what licenses their relative force without rebuilding a scalar utility or silently reverting to unconstrained judgment?

Thus the surviving debt is:

~~~text
TYPED PROMOTION REASONS
!=
A COMPLETE THEORY
OF REASON CONFLICT.
~~~

That issue does not reopen MQR-4.59.
