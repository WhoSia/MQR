# MQR-4.59 — Literature Pressure and Novelty Kill

Status: **PRE-REVEAL / PRIOR-ART FROZEN**

## Boundary

MQR-4.59 does not define scientific progress.

It governs whether a proposed successor should alter the live MQR constitution.

## Progress versus promotion

Dellsén (2025), _Promoting scientific progress_, distinguishes what constitutes scientific progress from what promotes it. The account is compatible with multiple constitutive theories of progress.

Pressure:

`PROMOTION GOVERNANCE != DEFINITION OF PROGRESS`

## No single scientific aim

Dellsén (2025), _Scientific progress: normative, but aimless_, argues that science need not possess a single aim for objective/normative progress discourse to remain possible.

Pressure:

A promotion constitution must not smuggle in one final scientific meta-objective.

## Research assessment

The Leiden Manifesto and DORA emphasize transparent, contextual and plural assessment and warn against allowing narrow quantitative indicators to stand in for the quality of research.

Pressure:

A promotion criterion is itself an institutional intervention surface.

## Novelty

Empirical work on peer review does not support a simple claim that scientific institutions uniformly suppress novelty. Teplitskiy et al. (2022), across 49 journals, report a positive association between their novelty measure and acceptance.

Pressure:

MQR-4.59 is not anti-novelty.

It rejects only:

`NOVELTY = UNIVERSAL PROMOTION VALUE`

## Research programmes

Lakatos supplies prior art against judging scientific development by isolated local events alone. Programme-level trajectories, problem shifts and subsequent development matter.

Pressure:

Promotion decisions require declared scope and horizon.

## Compression

Model-selection and information-theoretic traditions provide broad prior art for preferring simpler representations under appropriate adequacy constraints.

MQR does not identify compression with scientific progress.

It uses the weaker donor:

`MORE CONSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE != MORE AUTHORITY`

## Eliminative results

The scientific literature on publication bias and null-result visibility supplies ample prior art that non-positive findings can be systematically undervalued.

Pressure:

`NO POSITIVE REPLACEMENT != NO MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE CHANGE`

## Internal prior art

RAVEL already freezes criteria, tracks provenance and conditions later waves on evidence that they could alter conclusions.

MQR-4.58 already rejects:

`MORE SELF-CRITIQUE = MORE SCIENTIFIC VALUE`

MQR-4.59 therefore treats RAVEL's promotion rule itself as the new explanandum.

## Novelty kill

MQR-4.59 claims no invention of:
- progress pluralism;
- research assessment reform;
- novelty-bias analysis;
- complexity penalties;
- negative-result value;
- conceptual change;
- reflexive evaluation.

The candidate MQR excess is narrower:

a versioned, presealed, non-scalar promotion-admissibility receipt that can recognize addition, simplification, claim narrowing, localization and scope correction without treating any one of them as a universal meta-objective.
