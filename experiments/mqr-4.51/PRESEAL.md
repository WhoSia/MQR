## Unit of analysis: inquiry trace

For each world w and declared decision/use contract D, expose a finite inquiry trace:

~~~text
T_w = (x_0, x_1, ..., x_T)
~~~

where x_t is the information legitimately available to the stopping policy at time t.

The scorer additionally receives a sequestered suffix / adjudication record unavailable to the policy during stopping.

Each trace contains:
- current authority state;
- upstream HOLD/REOPEN state;
- live refinement debt;
- criterion state;
- world-contact events;
- decision-contract state;
- inquiry cost per step;
- materiality of newly exposed distinctions;
- action consequence under the declared D.

The stopping policy emits only:

~~~text
CONTINUE
STOP
REOPEN
~~~

plus a reason code. It may not inspect hidden future events, gold stop indices, generator seed classes, post-reveal labels, or scorer-only loss fields.

## Gold evaluation boundary

Define the **minimal suffix-stable stop index** tau*(w,D) only for scoring.

tau* is the earliest index such that:
1. the current action/authority verdict is the same as the full-trace verdict under D;
2. every later world event material to the declared claim/use is either already represented by an active reopening obligation or does not change the final action/authority projection;
3. no live upstream obligation existing at tau* has been silently dropped;
4. the stop remains retrospectively safe under the fully revealed finite trace.

tau* is not a metaphysical stopping point. It is a finite known-outcome benchmark label.

The policy never receives tau*.

## Primary calibration outcomes

For a policy stop time tau:

### Premature-Stop Error (PSE)

~~~text
PSE = 1[tau < tau* AND hidden suffix changes the declared authority/action projection]
~~~

Also record premature depth:

~~~text
PD = max(0, tau* - tau)
~~~

and material severity:
- AUTHORITY_ONLY;
- ACTION_CHANGE;
- SAFETY/LOSS_ORDER_CHANGE.

### Over-Inquiry Waste (OIW)

~~~text
OIW = max(0, tau - tau*)
~~~

measured in additional inquiry steps and accumulated declared inquiry cost.

### Action Error (AE)

Whether stopping at tau yields a different action under D than the full-trace adjudication.

### Reopening Latency (RL)

When a post-stop admissible event breaks the operational fixed point:

~~~text
RL = first_reopen_step - first_break_step
~~~

Failure to reopen by trace end is right-censored and counted separately as MISSED_REOPEN.

### Budget Exhaustion Rate (BER)

Fraction of traces where inquiry consumes the full budget without a licensed stop.

No single scalar score is primary.

## Anti-scalarization rule

A policy is not declared superior by an arbitrary weighted sum.

Primary comparison uses the vector:

~~~text
(PSE, PD, AE, OIW, RL, MISSED_REOPEN, BER)
~~~

A scalar loss may appear only as a prospectively frozen sensitivity analysis over multiple declared cost contracts. It cannot override a primary-vector failure.

## Policies under court

### P-OQSC

MQR-4.50 operational stop rule, with:
- upstream-admissibility firewall;
- live-debt requirement;
- criterion-invariance requirement;
- active reopening reserve;
- decision-contract indexing.

No fitted parameter.

### P-STOP-NOW

Stop at first legally parseable opportunity.

Purpose: aggressive premature-stop fatal control.

### P-CONTINUE-ALL

Never voluntarily stop before budget exhaustion.

Purpose: over-inquiry fatal control / premature-stop lower bound.

### P-FIXED-k

Stop after k inquiry steps unless a mandatory reopen/HOLD is active.

k is selected on a calibration split only and frozen before holdout scoring.

### P-LAST-CHANGE-h

Stop after h consecutive authority-inert steps.

h is selected on the calibration split only.

This baseline directly attacks the possibility that OQSC merely repackages a simple patience heuristic.

## Cross-domain benchmark constitution

At least three independently coded domain adapters must compile into one neutral inquiry-trace interface.

### Domain A — Engineering Fault Localization

World contact is a sequence of diagnostic probes.
Late separators and common-mode aliases can make early stop unsafe.

### Domain B — Measurement / Model Discrimination

Inquiry adds calibration or discrimination evidence.
Long inert plateaus may be followed by a representation- or regime-sensitive separator.

### Domain C — Search / Scientific Hypothesis Allocation

Inquiry allocates a finite search budget across rival-generating routes.
Open obligations, newly activated rivals and diminishing-return tails create both premature-stop and over-inquiry risks.

The same stopping-policy kernel must run unchanged across domains.

Domain-specific generators may differ. The scorer is shared.

## Required world families

Each domain must contain prospectively generated cases from at least these families:

1. INERT_TAIL — no future material distinction; continuing wastes budget.
2. LATE_SEPARATOR — long inert plateau followed by material world contact.
3. EARLY_SEPARATOR — stopping early is clearly unsafe.
4. REOPEN_AFTER_STOP — local stop is initially safe but later admissible evidence forces reopening.
5. DECISION_CONTRACT_SHIFT — descriptive state stable, action relevance changes.
6. CRITERION_DISAGREEMENT — OQSC must HOLD rather than stop.
7. LIVE_DEBT — registered material work remains unexecuted.
8. UPSTREAM_HOLD — 4.51 may not launder an upstream HOLD into calibrated stopping.
9. NOISY_IRRELEVANT_EVENTS — activity without authority-changing information.
10. MULTIPLE_REOPENINGS — repeated local stopping and reopening.
11. BUDGET_PRESSURE — no policy can fully resolve before finite budget.
12. PATH_CONFLICT — direct/composed constitutional revisions disagree.

## Balanced forcing design

The benchmark must not allow a policy to win by always stopping or always continuing.

Each domain's hidden holdout contains both:
- worlds where tau* is early;
- worlds where tau* is late;
- worlds where no voluntary stop is licensed before budget exhaustion;
- worlds requiring at least one reopen after an initially legitimate stop.

No family label is exposed to the policy.

## Promotion criteria

### C1 — Premature-stop competence

On untouched holdout, P-OQSC must have lower PSE than P-STOP-NOW and no higher AE than the best non-oracular baseline within each domain.

### C2 — Over-inquiry competence

P-OQSC must reduce OIW relative to P-CONTINUE-ALL in every domain.

### C3 — Patience-heuristic defeat

P-OQSC must not be Pareto-dominated by tuned P-LAST-CHANGE-h on the primary vector.

### C4 — Fixed-budget defeat

P-OQSC must not be Pareto-dominated by tuned P-FIXED-k.

### C5 — Reopening competence

Every admitted post-stop fixed-point break must either reopen immediately or within the prospectively declared latency bound.

Candidate bound:

~~~text

~~~

because the v0.17 receipt treats an observed qualifying break as an activation event, not as optional future evidence.

### C6 — Cross-domain transport

The same P-OQSC kernel and reason-code semantics must satisfy C1–C5 in all three domain adapters without domain-specific thresholds.

### C7 — Anti-self-scoring

No policy field may encode tau*, hidden family, final verdict, future separator time or scorer-only loss.

### C8 — Holdout integrity

No holdout-derived modification to policy logic is permitted after first holdout reveal. Any such modification invalidates the confirmatory result and reclassifies the run as development.

## Counterfactual continue/stop replay

For every holdout trace, replay both interventions:

~~~text
do(STOP at tau)
do(CONTINUE beyond tau)
~~~

The scorer records whether later inquiry:
- changes authority;
- changes action under D;
- only adds inert detail;
- triggers reopening;
- consumes cost without changing the relevant projection.

This is an evaluation intervention on a frozen trace, not a causal claim about human scientists.
