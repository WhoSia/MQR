# MQR-4.61 — Frozen Stress Manifest

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE EXECUTABLE REVEAL**

## Frozen countermodels

1. **Resolve-then-reopen** — initially incomparable reasons are resolved by fresh evidence, then a later scope-expanding result reintroduces a live conflict.
2. **Evidence-order noncommutativity** — event A first destroys the provenance needed to interpret B independently; B first preserves A as an independent test.
3. **Intervention contamination** — performing a destructive probe first makes a later baseline comparison impossible.
4. **Lost-option asymmetry** — promotion now closes a future intervention family; HOLD preserves it. Returning to the same label later does not restore the option.
5. **Same-relation/different-debt** — two paths end in INCOMPARABLE, but only one carries irreversible replication debt.
6. **Same-decision/different-authority** — both paths end in PROMOTE, but one retains independent world contact and one reaches promotion only after authority correction.
7. **Veto activation** — new fatal-scope evidence activates a previously absent veto.
8. **Veto retirement** — mechanism repair and independent replication remove the factual basis of an earlier local veto.
9. **Veto pseudo-monotonicity** — a naive constitution treats any historical veto as permanent despite repair.
10. **Reason mutation** — OPTION_VALUE becomes INDEPENDENT_WORLD_CONTACT after the option is actually exercised and independently measured.
11. **Reason duplication by mutation** — mutated reason is naively counted alongside its ancestor as a second independent warrant.
12. **Scope reversal** — DOMINATES under LOCAL becomes INCOMPARABLE under CROSS_DOMAIN without either receipt being false.
13. **Horizon reversal** — short-horizon VETOES becomes medium-horizon INCOMPARABLE when the relevant harm is repaired but new maintenance debt appears.
14. **Cycle persistence** — A dominates B under one scope, B dominates C under another, C defeats A under a third, and new evidence leaves the cycle intact.
15. **Cycle exit** — one independent evidence event breaks a previously live cycle without creating a total order over all remaining candidates.
16. **False hysteresis from sunk cost** — identical current typed state is treated differently solely because one path consumed more effort.
17. **Material hysteresis** — identical visible relation labels conceal different lost-option/provenance states, making path-sensitive treatment warranted.
18. **History-score Goodharting** — maximizing number of "successful revisions" encourages gratuitous relation churn.
19. **State-erasure aliasing** — two terminal summaries look identical only because lost-option and ancestry fields were erased.
20. **Reopen-loop** — repeated resolve↔reopen events remain scientifically legitimate and do not converge within the observed finite history.
21. **Evidence accumulation reversal** — adding evidence weakens rather than strengthens promotion authority because it defeats independence or expands the relevant scope.
22. **Restoration illusion** — VETO_RETIRED followed by the old relation label is mistaken for exact restoration despite irreversible debt.
23. **Chronology-only path preference** — two materially identical enriched states are ranked differently merely because one was reached earlier.
24. **Universal transition priority failure** — fixed rule "world contact always outranks option preservation" fails when the contact event itself irreversibly destroys the only discriminating future probe.

## Positive witnesses

1. Fresh independent evidence resolves a frozen INCOMPARABLE relation to DOMINATES with provenance preserved.
2. Later counterevidence legitimately reopens that resolution.
3. Two revision orders yield different authority because the first event changes the evidential status of the second.
4. Veto activates only under explicit scope and evidence.
5. Veto retires when its factual basis is repaired and independently checked.
6. Reason mutation preserves ancestry and does not double-count authority.
7. Lost-option debt remains active after superficial return to a prior relation label.
8. Two materially identical enriched states erase chronology-only differences.
9. Conflict cycle persists as a legitimate unresolved structure.
10. Conflict cycle can exit locally without producing a universal total order.
11. Horizon/scope reversal is represented as indexed transition, not contradiction.
12. Material hysteresis is admitted while sunk-cost hysteresis is rejected.
