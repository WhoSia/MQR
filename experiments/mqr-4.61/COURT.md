## Constitutional result

A reflexive scientific constitution may retain history only when history leaves a typed material consequence for current authority, option availability, provenance, reversibility or debt.

Therefore:

~~~text
SAME TERMINAL RELATION
!=
SAME CONSTITUTIONAL STATE
~~~

when material history differs.

But:

~~~text
DIFFERENT CHRONOLOGY
+ SAME ENRICHED MATERIAL STATE
!=
DIFFERENT AUTHORITY
~~~

Chronology, sunk effort, prestige and revision count are not authority sources.

Revision noncommutativity is admissible when one event changes the evidential status, availability or interpretation of a later event. Resolution may later reopen; a local veto may retire; a reason may mutate type while preserving ancestry; a conflict cycle may persist without licensing scalar tie-breaking.
