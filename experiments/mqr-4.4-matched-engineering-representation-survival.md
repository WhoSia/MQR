# MQR-4.4 — Matched Engineering Representation Survival, Analytical-vs-Graphical / Empirical-vs-Theoretical Pair Reconstruction, Transport–Counterfactual–Institutional Decomposition & Whether World-Contact Predicts Which Representations Persist

## 0. Question
MQR-4.3 proposed engineering as a harsh representation-selection environment.
MQR-4.4 tests whether repeated world-contact predicts which representation persists when competing representations target the same engineering problem.

The unit of analysis is not which representation is true, but which representation or representation stack remains operationally authoritative across changing tasks.

## 1. Frozen comparison dimensions
Each matched pair is evaluated on:
- W: world-contact robustness;
- T: transport across scale/regime/device;
- K: counterfactual reach;
- C: composition/control;
- I: institutional embedding/standardization;
- H: dependence on hidden empirical/tacit scaffolding.

These dimensions are analytic diagnostics only, not new MQR primitives.

## 2. Pair A — graphical statics vs analytical/numerical structural analysis
Graphic statics developed through Stevin, Varignon, Rankine, Maxwell, Culmann and Cremona. Reciprocal form/force diagrams encoded equilibrium geometrically.
Historically it became a leading truss-analysis method in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

Algebraic and later matrix/numerical structural analysis progressively moved equilibrium, compatibility and stiffness calculations into symbolic/numeric form. Digital computation greatly expanded solvable system size and complexity.

Modern literature notes that graphic statics was largely abandoned in the second half of the twentieth century partly because of computers and numerical tools, while recent digital/interactive work has revived it for early-stage structural design.

Therefore the history is not graphical false -> analytical true.
It is environment-relative selection:
- graphical representation had high visual/intervention transparency and low hand-computation cost;
- analytical/numerical representation gained overwhelming scalability and composability under digital computation;
- graphical representation retained/recovered advantages for form-finding and interactive design.

Result: SURVIVAL DIFFERENCE IS POORLY EXPLAINED BY WORLD FIT ALONE.
Both can represent the same equilibrium structure; computational and institutional environments strongly changed which representation dominated.

## 3. Pair B — empirical airfoil catalogs vs aerodynamic theory
Early NACA airfoil design remained heavily empirical: intuition, systematic wind-tunnel programs and published catalogs guided designers.
NACA standardized heterogeneous laboratory results into common coefficients, charts and tables for engineering use.

Potential-flow and Kutta–Joukowski style theory supplied useful regularities but initially predicted only restricted characteristics and failed on important viscous and leading-edge phenomena.

By 1930 NACA prediction explicitly combined aerodynamic theory with empirical formulas derived from variable-density wind-tunnel data.
By the 1940s airfoil design combined theoretical pressure-distribution methods, boundary-layer calculation, empirical transition criteria, low-turbulence tunnel validation and standardized data collections.

NACA's own historical material records cases where inadequate theory forced empirical modification, while later theory and tests jointly produced improved families.

Matched verdict:
empirical catalog + theoretical model + wind-tunnel correction/validation -> persistent hybrid design stack.

Result: THEORY-OVER-EMPIRICISM REPLACEMENT MODEL FAILS.
The survivor is a stack whose layers compensate for one another's deficits.

## 4. Pair C — empirical cable practice vs mathematical transmission theory
Early underground and submarine telegraph cables exhibited signal retardation and blurring. The Atlantic cable made this a severe performance and commercial problem.

Practical cable engineering involved empirical behavior, instruments, conductor/material choices and competing intuitions about voltage, conductor size and retardation.
William Thomson developed a mathematical long-cable model using a diffusion/heat-conduction analogy.
Later Heaviside generalized transmission-line treatment by incorporating inductive/leakage effects and a wave/transmission framework suitable for faster signaling regimes.

The cable problem was a strong discriminator because wrong assumptions degraded communication and proposed remedies generated new observable outcomes.

Survival pattern:
cable failure -> measurement/problem localization -> mathematical model -> instrument/cable redesign -> new transmission evidence -> model revision/generalization.

Thomson's diffusion-style model remained appropriate for low-rate cable conditions; Heaviside's later treatment extended the representation where inductive/wave effects mattered.

Result: world-contact discriminates, but only relative to regime.
MODEL SURVIVAL = SCOPE-SENSITIVE TRANSPORT.

## 5. Pair D — empirical wing-shape selection vs theory-driven shape construction
Empirical direction: choose candidate shapes, test them, retain better-performing sections.
Theory-driven direction: specify desired aerodynamic behavior/pressure distribution and construct a geometry predicted to realize it.

Historical NACA material shows neither approach was independently sufficient.
At times theory was too weak near leading edges or viscous regimes, forcing empirical modification.
At other times theory enabled designers to move beyond catalog lookup and intentionally generate families with target characteristics.

Counterfactual reach mattered:
theory gained authority where it could support designing a not-yet-tested shape to achieve property P, rather than only choosing among already tested shapes.
But that authority remained conditional on wind-tunnel and flight world-contact.

This is a clear historical example of constructive counterfactual exposure.

## 6. Does world-contact predict survival?
Naive hypothesis: more world-contact robustness directly implies historical survival.
The matched cases reject this as sufficient.

Graphical and analytical statics can both survive equilibrium world-contact, yet computational/institutional change can reverse dominance.
Empirical airfoil catalogs had excellent world-contact but limited transport.
Early aerodynamic theory had wider conceptual reach but insufficient local adequacy.
Telegraph theory survived by changing form as the engineering regime changed.

Better result:
representation persistence requires at least:
1. Contact adequacy — it must not fail too badly when coupled to material systems.
2. Mobility — it must remain useful as tasks, scales, instruments, institutions and computational resources change.

Conceptually:
SURVIVAL ≈ CONTACT ADEQUACY × MOBILITY THROUGH CHANGING ENGINEERING ENVIRONMENTS.
The multiplication sign is conceptual, not a fitted quantitative law.

## 7. Representation-stack result
The strongest result is that engineering often selects stacks, not individual representations.

Examples:
- airfoil theory + tunnel data + empirical transition criteria + correction procedures;
- transmission-line equations + measured line parameters + instruments + operational constraints;
- numerical structural models + graphical/form intuition + material/safety conventions.

Therefore the historically surviving object is frequently a representation ecology rather than one formalism.

## 8. Why more mathematical wins fails
Across the matched cases:
- graphic statics is mathematical but lost institutional dominance to numerical methods;
- empirical airfoil data remained indispensable even as theory improved;
- telegraph mathematics changed when the physical regime demanded it;
- theory-driven aerodynamic design required empirical correction.

Therefore DEGREE OF MATHEMATIZATION DOES NOT PREDICT HISTORICAL SURVIVAL.

The stronger predictor is whether a representation can be embedded in a stack that:
- survives world-contact;
- travels;
- produces useful counterfactuals;
- interfaces with available instruments/computation;
- fits institutional coordination.

## 9. World-contact's actual role
World-contact is better interpreted as a constraint/filter than a unique selector.
It can eliminate materially failing representations, reveal scope boundaries, expose omitted mechanisms, force parameter/model revision and discriminate rival intervention predictions.

It usually cannot by itself choose among representations that make equivalent local predictions but differ in computational convenience, visualization, institutional compatibility or task-equivalent preserved structures.

CENTRAL RESULT:
WORLD-CONTACT CONSTRAINS THE SURVIVOR SET; ENGINEERING ECOLOGY OFTEN SELECTS WITHIN IT.

## 10. Realist consequence
If a representation survives only because it is easy to teach, cheap to compute, standardized or locked in, persistence adds little realist warrant.

If it repeatedly survives new regimes, unforeseen interventions, novel artifact design, independent measurements and rival-form comparison, persistence has stronger world-side evidential content.

Even then, warranted commitment attaches only to the load-bearing invariant structure actually responsible for success, not automatically to the full ontology or every mathematical object.

## 11. Pair summary
Graphical vs analytical/numerical statics:
- world-contact discrimination LOW;
- environment-selection effect HIGH.

Empirical airfoil catalogs vs theory:
- world-contact discrimination HIGH against inadequate pure theory;
- hybridization effect VERY HIGH.

Empirical cable practice vs transmission theory:
- world-contact discrimination HIGH;
- scope-sensitive theoretical succession HIGH.

Empirical wing selection vs theory-driven design:
- constructive counterfactual advantage HIGH;
- pure-theory autonomy FAIL.

## 12. Revised representation-selection claim
Engineering is a representation-ecology selection environment in which world-contact filters materially inadequate candidates, while computational, task, institutional and measurement environments select and recombine survivors into operational stacks.

This is stronger historically and weaker metaphysically than the 4.3 formulation.

## 13. Anti-novelty audit
The result is compatible with mature history/philosophy of engineering: theory/practice coevolution, engineering knowledge pluralism, model pluralism and infrastructural standardization.

No standalone philosophical novelty is claimed.
MQR contribution remains limited to its authority rule:
successful representation does not entail unrestricted realist commitment.
The authority ceiling follows the specific invariant(s) that survived discriminating world-contact.

## 14. Next discriminator
A stronger successor should isolate load-bearing invariant transport rather than historical persistence alone.

For a matched engineering success, ask:
1. Which part of representation R was actually necessary for successful intervention?
2. Could rival R' have generated the same artifact success?
3. Which distinctions differ between R and R'?
4. Can a new designed intervention make those differences materially consequential?
5. Does success transport when institutional/computational convenience is held fixed?

This moves from representation survival to representation-specific intervention dependence.
