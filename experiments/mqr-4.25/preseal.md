# MQR-4.25 — Minimal-Basis Adversarial Escape Search, Novel Failure-Mode Generation, Out-of-Basis Counterexample Hunt, Successor-Dimension Admission Rule & Whether C–E–P Is Stable under Deliberately Unmodeled Threats

Status: ACTIVE / PRESEAL

## Objective
Attack the MQR-4.24 minimal basis {C,E,P} by searching for worlds in which:
C = PASS
E = PASS
P = PASS
yet scientific promotion should still be rejected for a reason not reducible to any of C, E, or P.

## Frozen anti-proliferation rule
A newly observed failure does NOT automatically justify a new authority dimension.

For every candidate failure F:
1. REDUCIBILITY TEST
   Can F be represented as a failure/refinement of C, E, or P without changing their role semantics?
2. INDEPENDENCE TEST
   Does F vary independently while C=E=P remain genuinely satisfied under their frozen meanings?
3. COUNTERFACTUAL TEST
   Holding C/E/P fixed PASS, does toggling F change the warranted promotion state?
4. OPERATIONALIZATION TEST
   Can F be prospectively measured or governed before outcome-dependent repair?
5. NON-DUPLICATION TEST
   Is F distinct from claim scope, world-contact admissibility, source integrity already required by E, or provenance/time auditability already required by P?

## Successor-dimension admission rule
Admit successor dimension D only if ALL hold:
D1. A concrete C=E=P=PASS counterexample exists.
D2. Promotion should be HOLD in that counterexample.
D3. The defect cannot be represented as mis-specified C, invalid E, or failed P without semantic distortion.
D4. D is prospectively operationalizable.
D5. Adding D blocks the counterexample while preserving previously valid cases.
D6. D is not merely another credential/provider/notary/identity lane.

If D1-D6 do not all hold:
DO NOT EXPAND THE BASIS.

## Deliberately unmodeled threat families to search
- source-origin corruption despite intact provenance;
- calibration/measurement validity drift;
- construct mismatch / semantic aliasing;
- adversarial environment manipulation between preseal and observation;
- collusion or common-mode dependence among nominally independent authorities;
- cryptographic trust-root compromise;
- claim-scope laundering after valid execution;
- causal overreach from non-causal world-contact;
- hidden selection/censoring that survives visible provenance;
- evaluator/adjudicator incentives that preserve formal independence but distort decisions.

## Classification outputs
Each candidate must be assigned one:
- ABSORBED_BY_C
- ABSORBED_BY_E
- ABSORBED_BY_P
- AUXILIARY_NONAUTHORITY
- GENUINE_OUT_OF_BASIS_CANDIDATE

## Promotion ceiling
MQR-4.24 minimality remains in force unless a GENUINE_OUT_OF_BASIS_CANDIDATE satisfies D1-D6.
