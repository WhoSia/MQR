## Dependency-cut representation

For claim `phi` at scope `S`, challenge authority is evaluated against an explicit authority-relevant dependency set:

`D = {model, measurement, data, representation, semantics, search_grammar, proof_encoding, calibration, selection_rule, adjudication}`.

A transport route `tau` may:
- BREAK an edge;
- PRESERVE an edge;
- REPLACE an edge with a new dependency;
- leave an edge UNRESOLVED;
- introduce a NEW dependency not present in the source route.

No raw count of broken edges is an authority score.

Only claim-relevant dependency cuts matter.

## Candidate upgrade relation

A historically internal challenge may earn `AUTHORITY_UPGRADE` only when all of the following hold at the declared scope:

1. **Origin preservation** — source origin remains explicitly INTERNAL/MIXED rather than being rewritten.
2. **Challenge correspondence** — the transported witness targets the same claim-relevant failure content, or a declared refinement/merge relation exists.
3. **Relevant common-mode cut** — at least one authority-changing dependency inherited from the target is actually broken.
4. **Cut provenance** — the replacement witness route has independently inspectable provenance for the edge claimed to be broken.
5. **No outcome-conditioned rescue** — the authority-changing route was not tuned after seeing the target outcome unless the result is explicitly diagnostic-only.
6. **No authority amplification by restatement** — semantic reconstruction, reimplementation or rehosting alone cannot upgrade authority.
7. **Residual dependence visibility** — preserved or newly introduced dependencies remain explicit.
8. **Defeasibility** — later evidence that restores common-mode ancestry or defeats correspondence reopens the certificate.

## Split / merge rule

One internal challenge may split into multiple external witnesses.

Those witnesses count separately only to the extent that their authority-changing routes do not collapse to the same relevant ancestry.

Conversely, several apparently independent external witnesses may merge into one common-mode class if later ancestry reconstruction identifies a shared authority-changing dependency.

Therefore witness cardinality is not conserved authority.
