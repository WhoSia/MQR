# MQR-4.76 — Cross-Domain Stress I

Status: **POST-PRESEAL / POST-LITERATURE-COLLISION / NATURAL-SCIENCE-AND-MATHEMATICS-ROLE-STRESS / CROSS-DOMAIN-EVIDENCE-TYPE-IDENTITY-REJECTED**

## Purpose

MQR-4.76 proposed a cross-domain witness role:

`BREAK_RELEVANT_DEPENDENCY + PRESERVE_CHALLENGE_CORRESPONDENCE`.

This stress does not ask whether natural-science measurement and mathematical proof are the same evidence type. They are not.

It asks whether the same **authority-transport grammar** survives two materially different historical cases.

## Stress A — SNO solar-neutrino neutral-current measurement

### Historical fact pattern

The Sudbury Neutrino Observatory reported charged-current (CC), elastic-scattering (ES), and neutral-current (NC) measurements using heavy water.

In the 2002 SNO result:
- the electron-neutrino component of the solar (^{8}B) flux was measured separately;
- a non-electron active-neutrino component was inferred at **5.3 standard deviations above zero**;
- the total active-neutrino flux measured through the neutral-current channel was consistent with solar-model predictions.

Reference:

SNO Collaboration (2002), *Direct Evidence for Neutrino Flavor Transformation from Neutral-Current Interactions in the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory*, arXiv:nucl-ex/0204008.

### MQR reconstruction

The historically live solar-neutrino deficit admitted several broad dependency routes:
- source-model dependence: the Sun may emit fewer neutrinos than expected;
- detector/reaction-channel dependence: a detector may be selectively sensitive to electron neutrinos;
- flavor-content dependence: neutrinos may arrive in active non-electron flavors.

SNO's NC channel did not become authoritative merely because it was a different detector or a new experiment.

Its importance for MQR-4.76 is structural:

1. the **challenge correspondence** was preserved: the same solar-neutrino flux discrepancy was under adjudication;
2. the measurement architecture introduced a route sensitive to the **total active flux**, not only the electron-neutrino component;
3. this broke an authority-relevant ambiguity that could persist under an electron-flavor-selective channel;
4. the resulting total active flux being consistent with solar-model predictions constrained the solar-source-deficit explanation while the non-electron component directly supported flavor transformation.

Candidate MQR receipt:

```text
SOURCE_ORIGIN = HISTORICAL_ANOMALY / MIXED
CONTACT_KIND = MEASUREMENT
CORRESPONDENCE = EXACT-AT-PHENOMENON / REFINED-AT-MECHANISM
BREAK = FLAVOR-SELECTIVE-DETECTION DEPENDENCY
PRESERVE = DETECTOR-CALIBRATION / NUCLEAR-REACTION / SOLAR-MODEL RESIDUALS
AUTHORITY_CHANGE = LOCAL-TO-CROSS-ROUTE UPGRADE
ORIGINAL_ORIGIN_REWRITTEN = NO
```

### Stress verdict

**PASS as a natural-science dependence-cut analogue.**

The case supports:

`NEW_MEASUREMENT_ROUTE CAN UPGRADE AUTHORITY WHEN IT CHANGES WHICH LIVE EXPLANATIONS CAN SURVIVE`.

It does not support:

`NEW_EXPERIMENT => INDEPENDENT TRUTH AUTHORITY`.

Residual calibration, reaction-model and analysis dependencies remain visible.

## Stress B — Flyspeck formal proof of the Kepler conjecture

### Historical fact pattern

Hales et al. (2017) published the completed Flyspeck project as a formal proof of the Kepler conjecture using a combination of **HOL Light and Isabelle**.

The project formalized substantial mathematical and numerical parts of the original proof architecture. In the nonlinear-inequality component, an informal search procedure could find subdivisions and auxiliary information, while the final formal verification produced HOL Light theorems; arithmetic used by the formal verification was carried out inside HOL Light.

Reference:

Hales et al. (2017), *A Formal Proof of the Kepler Conjecture*, Forum of Mathematics, Pi 5:e2. DOI: 10.1017/fmp.2017.1.

### MQR reconstruction

Formal reconstruction can break several dependencies inherited by an informal/computer-assisted proof route:
- trust in unchecked numerical computation;
- trust in manual transcription of local deductions;
- trust in an opaque search result as though search success itself were proof.

But formalization does not automatically break:
- statement translation;
- formal encoding;
- chosen axiom/kernel assumptions;
- semantic correspondence between the informal theorem and formal target.

Therefore:

```text
INDEPENDENT_CHECKING != INDEPENDENT_FORMALIZATION
FORMAL_RECONSTRUCTION != SEMANTIC-INDEPENDENCE
SECOND_PROOF_ASSISTANT != AUTOMATIC_SECOND-MEANING ROUTE
```

Candidate MQR receipt:

```text
SOURCE_ORIGIN = PRIOR COMPUTER-ASSISTED / INFORMAL PROOF LINEAGE
CONTACT_KIND = PROOF
CORRESPONDENCE = FORMAL-REFINEMENT
BREAK = UNCHECKED-COMPUTATION / MANUAL-VERIFICATION DEPENDENCIES
PRESERVE = FORMAL-STATEMENT / ENCODING / KERNEL-TRUST DEPENDENCIES
AUTHORITY_CHANGE = LOCAL FORMAL-VERIFICATION UPGRADE
ORIGINAL_ORIGIN_REWRITTEN = NO
```

### Stress verdict

**PASS only at role level.**

Flyspeck supports the idea that a challenge/proof lineage can gain stronger authority through reconstruction that cuts specific verification dependencies.

It does **not** justify identifying formal proof evidence with physical replication evidence.

## Cross-domain adjudication

The strong equivalence fails:

`NATURAL-SCIENCE-WITNESS == MATHEMATICAL-WITNESS` — **REJECTED**.

The weaker structural equivalence survives:

```text
CROSS_DOMAIN_WITNESS_EQUIVALENCE =
  SAME AUTHORITY-TRANSPORT ROLE
  WHEN:
    (1) CLAIM-RELEVANT CORRESPONDENCE IS PRESERVED
    (2) AN AUTHORITY-RELEVANT DEPENDENCY IS BROKEN
    (3) THE CUT HAS INSPECTABLE PROVENANCE
    (4) RESIDUAL DEPENDENCIES REMAIN EXPLICIT
    (5) THE CERTIFICATE REMAINS DEFEASIBLE
```

Therefore:

`CROSS_DOMAIN_ROLE_EQUIVALENCE != EVIDENCE_TYPE_IDENTITY`.

## RAVEL consequence

The stress **shrinks** one possible overclaim.

MQR-4.76 should not claim a universal evidence ontology spanning experiment and proof.

It may claim a common **typed authority-transport grammar** over heterogeneous evidence regimes.

That grammar remains compatible with the founding MQR question because the authority upgrade must still cash out in a changed discrimination structure:
- in natural science, which world-model / measurement alternatives remain observationally live;
- in mathematics, which proof/encoding/checking failure routes remain capable of invalidating the claim.
