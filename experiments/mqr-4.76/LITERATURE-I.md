# MQR-4.76 — Literature Collision I

Status: **POST-PRESEAL / GENERIC-REPLICATION-AND-TRIANGULATION-NOVELTY-ABSORBED / METROLOGICAL-ANCESTRY-PRESSURE-ACTIVE / FORMAL-CHECKING-PRIOR-ART-ABSORBED / ORIGIN-NONLAUNDERING-AND-DEPENDENCY-CUT-RESIDUE-LIVE**

## 1. Triangulation absorbs the generic claim that diverse independent evidence can strengthen support

Kuorikoski & Marchionni (2016), *Evidential Diversity and the Triangulation of Phenomena*, Philosophy of Science 83(2):227–247, DOI 10.1086/684960, explicitly defend triangulation as the use of multiple and independent evidence sources and explain its value through control of likely errors and biases in data-generating procedures.

Stegenga & Menon (2017), *Robustness and Independent Evidence*, Philosophy of Science 84(3):414–435, DOI 10.1086/692141, further separate kinds of independence used by robustness arguments, including ontic independence and probabilistic independence.

Therefore MQR-4.76 claims no novelty for:

- the proposition that multiple independent routes can strengthen evidence;
- the idea that diversity matters because different procedures have different error profiles;
- the idea that not all evidential diversity is epistemically equivalent;
- generic robustness or triangulation as a route to stronger support.

Residual question:

**How can a challenge whose historical origin is explicitly dependent acquire a stronger *current* authority state without laundering its origin, and how should the relevant independence change be represented when routes split, merge, or later reveal shared ancestry?**

## 2. Replication theory absorbs simple external-lab and method-distance upgrade claims

Vachon et al. (2021; accepted 2020), *A concept analysis and meta-narrative review established a comprehensive theoretical definition of replication research to improve its use*, Journal of Clinical Epidemiology, surveys 153 articles and distinguishes repetition, extension and theory road-testing. It also treats investigator distance and methodological difference as attributes of replication and notes that different methods can test whether original procedures drove a result.

Munafò & Davey Smith (2018), *Robust research needs many lines of evidence*, Nature 553:399–401, DOI 10.1038/d41586-018-01023-3, explicitly argue that replication alone is insufficient and motivate triangulation across methods with different assumptions.

Therefore:

`OTHER_LAB != AUTOMATIC_AUTHORITY_UPGRADE`

and:

`REPLICATION_COUNT != INDEPENDENCE`

are not novel as broad intuitions.

MQR residue is narrower: the external route must identify which authority-relevant dependency was actually cut, while preserving the challenge correspondence and historical source origin.

## 3. Metrology makes shared calibration / traceability ancestry a first-class obstacle

JCGM 200:2012 (VIM3) defines metrological traceability through a documented calibration hierarchy. Current BIPM/JCGM materials retain the central point that traceability requires an established calibration chain and that traceability alone does **not** guarantee adequate uncertainty or absence of mistakes.

NIST's metrological traceability policy likewise states that traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose.

This defeats any MQR rule of the form:

`DIFFERENT_INSTRUMENT_OR_LAB => INDEPENDENT_MEASUREMENT_AUTHORITY`.

Two nominally distinct instruments or laboratories may remain connected through the same standards, calibration models, reference materials, uncertainty assumptions, or preprocessing chain.

MQR-4.76 therefore treats calibration/traceability as **ancestry information**, not an independence oracle.

## 4. Tal's model-based calibration analysis blocks naive measurement externality

Tal (2017), *Calibration: Modelling the Measurement Process*, Studies in History and Philosophy of Science, DOI 10.1016/j.shpsa.2017.09.001, argues that calibration is itself model-based. Measurement outcomes depend not only on indications but on theoretical/statistical assumptions, and systematic errors may remain stable under repetition.

This is directly hostile to a naive rule that fresh measurement or a distinct instrument is automatically an external authority root.

An external measurement can break one common mode while preserving:
- the same calibration model;
- the same standard hierarchy;
- the same theoretical correction;
- the same semantic measurand constitution.

Therefore:

`WORLD_CONTACT != ALL_COMMON_MODES_BROKEN`.

The Court must preserve residual dependence after upgrade.

## 5. Robustness literature absorbs simple "independent techniques secure the result" claims

Staley (2004), *Robust Evidence and Secure Evidence Claims*, Philosophy of Science 71(4):467–488, DOI 10.1086/423748, distinguishes the epistemic role of robust results from assumptions about testing reliability.

Staley (2020), *Securing the Empirical Value of Measurement Results*, British Journal for the Philosophy of Science 71(1):87–113, DOI 10.1093/bjps/axx036, analyzes systematic uncertainty in measurement as a minimal form of robustness analysis.

Stegenga (2009), *Robustness, Discordance, and Relevance*, Philosophy of Science 76(5):650–661, DOI 10.1086/605819, explicitly warns that robustness arguments can mislead.

MQR-4.76 therefore gets no novelty from a generic "agreement across techniques increases confidence" thesis.

The surviving target must remain **typed authority transport under known historical dependence**, including downgrade/reopening when apparent independence later collapses.

## 6. Mathematical proof checking absorbs generic independent-verifier authority

Barendregt & Wiedijk (2005), *The Challenge of Computer Mathematics*, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A 363:2351–2375, DOI 10.1098/rsta.2005.1650, describe the de Bruijn criterion: formal proofs should permit independent checking by a small trusted proof-checking program.

Modern proof assistants embody the generator/checker separation. This absorbs novelty for:
- independent proof checking;
- a small trusted kernel;
- separating proof search from final proof verification;
- using a second checker to raise confidence in a proof object.

But the mathematical analogue of MQR-4.76 survives at a different boundary:

`INDEPENDENT_CHECKER != INDEPENDENT_FORMALIZATION`.

Two checkers can share:
- the same formal statement;
- the same axiom set;
- the same encoding;
- the same mistranslation from the informal theorem.

Thus a second prover/checker may cut implementation/search dependence while leaving semantic/formalization dependence intact.

## 7. Strong literature-induced repair

The preseal language "external replication, semantic reconstruction or world contact" is too coarse if read as an ordering of evidence types.

Literature pressure repairs the primitive to:

`AUTHORITY_UPGRADE = CLAIM-RELATIVE DEPENDENCY-CUT + CHALLENGE-CORRESPONDENCE + CUT-PROVENANCE + RESIDUAL-DEPENDENCE DISCLOSURE`.

This is not a scalar equation. It is a typed admissibility relation.

Consequences:

1. **Externality is evidentially inert by itself.**
2. **Replication is not a monotone authority counter.**
3. **Traceability records ancestry but does not certify epistemic independence.**
4. **Triangulation supplies the broad epistemic rationale but does not solve historical-origin bookkeeping or witness split/merge.**
5. **Independent proof checking cuts some software/search risks but does not automatically cut statement/encoding common modes.**

## 8. Strongest surviving candidate

After prior-art absorption, the strongest MQR-specific candidate is:

> A challenge may acquire stronger scoped authority after its dependent origin only when a later witness route breaks a declared authority-relevant dependency while preserving claim-relevant challenge correspondence; the original dependence remains immutable provenance, surviving common modes remain explicit, witness multiplicity is quotientable by ancestry, and the upgrade certificate is defeasible under later ancestry or correspondence revision.

This yields four nontrivial bookkeeping constraints not supplied merely by generic triangulation:

- **origin nonlaundering** — current upgrade does not rewrite source history;
- **edge-specific upgrade** — authority changes are indexed to the dependency actually cut;
- **ancestry-aware split/merge** — witness counts can refine or collapse without additive authority;
- **certificate defeasibility** — later common-mode discovery can revoke an apparent independence upgrade.

## 9. Novelty ceiling

MQR-4.76 claims no invention of:
- replication;
- independent replication;
- triangulation;
- robustness;
- metrological traceability;
- calibration modeling;
- independent proof checking;
- proof-assistant trusted kernels;
- multi-method confirmation.

The live claim is a **typed authority/provenance transport discipline** joining these known practices without identifying their evidence types.

## 10. Court consequence

The frozen 17-case Court remains admissible after literature review, but interpretation is tightened:

- `LOCAL_AUTHORITY_UPGRADE` means at least one relevant dependency is cut, with residual common modes still visible;
- `CROSS_ROUTE_INDEPENDENCE_UPGRADE` means the frozen route breaks the relevant source common mode more deeply, not that timeless/global independence has been achieved;
- `DIAGNOSTIC_REPLICATION_ONLY` preserves evidential usefulness without independence promotion;
- `SPLIT_REQUIRED` requires failure-content refinement before authority can transport;
- `MERGE_COLLAPSE` revokes multiplicity when supposedly distinct witnesses collapse to shared authority ancestry;
- `NO_UPGRADE` is compatible with a useful diagnostic result.

## 11. Real-Language implication

`REALUPGRADE 0.33-CANDIDATE` remains justified as a development-only receipt layer if it encodes:
- immutable origin;
- correspondence;
- typed dependency cuts;
- residual/new dependencies;
- split/merge lineage;
- versioned, defeasible certificate state.

It must not encode:
- scalar independence;
- replication-count authority;
- "external" as an automatic promotion bit;
- evidence-type equivalence across natural science and mathematics;
- permanent independence.
