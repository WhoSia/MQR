## Explanandum lock

MQR-4.62 asks what a reflexive scientific constitution may legitimately use when choosing **which evidence-generating action to perform next** when the action itself can alter:
- later identifiability;
- option availability;
- relation-transition topology;
- independence/provenance;
- reopening capacity;
- debt;
- cost;
- exposure to defeat.

The stage does **not** assume:
- one universal epistemic utility;
- one globally calibrated value-of-information scalar;
- myopic optimality;
- adaptive submodularity;
- separable action values;
- order-independent probe value;
- monotone information gain;
- known prior/model family;
- complete hypothesis space;
- perfect intervention semantics;
- recoverability of destroyed options;
- a unique optimal experiment.

## Prospective action object

For each candidate action `a`, the constitution may track a typed profile:

~~~text
A(a) = (
  target claim / relation,
  probe or intervention type,
  admissible outcome partition,
  identifiability contribution,
  expected relation-transition possibilities,
  option creation/loss,
  provenance impact,
  contamination/destructiveness,
  reversibility,
  reopening contribution,
  current debt addressed,
  new debt induced,
  horizon,
  scope,
  cost/resource contract,
  model/prior dependence,
  counterfactual alternative set
)
~~~

This is **not** a scalar by default.

## Candidate live objects

### EASR — Evidence-Acquisition Selection Receipt
Records the candidate set, local admissibility, selected action, rejected alternatives, explicit non-selection reasons, and scope/horizon contract.

### RAER — Relation-Anticipation Envelope Receipt
Records which promotion-relation transitions an action could discriminate or trigger under declared outcome partitions without claiming world-completeness.

### OPR — Option-Preservation Receipt
Records option creation, option destruction, reversibility, contamination and whether a sequence preserves future discriminating capacity.

### IDR — Identifiability-Delta Receipt
Records the declared local change in identifiability/observability/separation under an action; does not equate identifiability gain with universal scientific value.

### ARR — Adaptive Reopening Reserve
Records protected future action capacity maintained specifically to reopen relations or challenge current local optima.

### NEDL — Nonmyopic Exploration-Debt Ledger
Records local debt created when short-horizon optimization starves probes needed for future discrimination, model challenge or reopening.

## Primary hypotheses

1. More expected information need not imply more scientific authority.
2. A myopically highest-yield action can be globally worse under option loss or identifiability destruction.
3. A locally inferior immediate probe can be admissible when it preserves a unique future discriminating route.
4. Query value can be representation/model/prior dependent.
5. Identifiability gain is a local scientific good but not a universal utility.
6. Probe order may matter when early action changes later observability, independence or availability.
7. A policy can create its own blind spots by repeatedly sampling the region its own model scores as valuable.
8. Reopening reserve can be constitutionally required without defining a universal exploration bonus.
9. A fixed exploration fraction can Goodhart and need not be universally optimal.
10. Evidence acquisition can legitimately target future relation transitions rather than immediate claim confirmation.
11. Adaptive choice can create common-mode evidence even while nominal sample count and information gain rise.
12. Destructive interventions may require explicit lost-option debt.
13. Nonmyopic exploration debt can accumulate under repeated locally rational choices.
14. Two actions can remain genuinely incomparable under distinct scientific obligations.
15. Pareto or vector candidate sets do not select one experiment by themselves.
16. A universal expected-epistemic-utility optimizer is not earned.
