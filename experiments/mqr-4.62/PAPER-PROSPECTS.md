# MQR — Paper Prospect Ledger after 4.61 / during 4.62

Status: **EXPLORATORY / NON-BINDING / NOT A PROMOTION VERDICT**

This ledger asks which repeated MQR results have enough coherence to become an academic paper rather than another internal stage.

## Prospect A — Local Scientific Progress without a Global Progress Scalar

Lineage:
- 4.49 defeat-content instability
- 4.53 World-Contact Progress Atlas
- 4.54 invariance constitution
- 4.59 promotion != progress
- 4.60 partial conflict relations
- 4.61 non-scalar historical dynamics

Core thesis:
Scientific progress can support locally earned metrics/orders/preorders and representation-sensitive transport rules without requiring one global scalar, while promotion governance remains a distinct partial relation.

Potential paper shape:
- philosophy of science / formal epistemology;
- theorem/countermodel core;
- 2–3 worked scientific/engineering cases;
- executable appendix from Real-Language / Lean.

Novelty risk: **medium**.
Pressure: pluralist accounts of progress, measurement theory, Blackwell/Le Cam, information geometry, Pareto/MCDA.
MQR excess must come from the integrated executable constitution, not the slogan "progress is plural".

Tractability: **high**.
Already-closed material is unusually coherent.

## Prospect B — Open Rival Frontiers and Reopenable Scientific Search

Lineage:
- 4.41 Frontier-Relative Contact Rank
- 4.42 rival-frontier adequacy / generator dependence
- 4.43 allocation
- 4.44 obligation constitution
- 4.55 open consequence families
- 4.56 challenge-value constitution
- 4.62 prospective evidence acquisition

Core thesis:
Scientific search should not certify frontier completeness; instead it can use frontier-relative local discrimination plus explicit generator provenance, exterior-search capacity and reopening reserves.

Potential paper shape:

- formal open-frontier model;
- generator-portfolio counterexamples;
- simulation comparing closed-generator vs reopenable policies.

Novelty risk: **medium-low to medium** if tied tightly to unconceived alternatives and executable search governance.
Tractability: **high**, especially if 4.62 supplies the prospective policy layer.

## Prospect C — Scientific Experiment Selection as a Performative Evidence Ecology

Lineage:
- 4.42 open frontier
- 4.56 challenge-value Goodharting
- 4.57 strategic discovery ecology
- 4.61 material path dependence
- 4.62 endogenous evidence acquisition

Core thesis:
Experiment-selection policies do not merely sample evidence; they can alter which evidence becomes available, independent, interpretable and likely to be generated, producing self-confirming or self-blinding evidence ecologies.

Closest pressure:
- Dubova–Moskvichev–Zollman (2026)
- active-learning sampling bias
- performative prediction
- dual control / active sensing
- Bayesian experimental design

Possible MQR excess:
connect policy-induced sampling bias to **rival-frontier escape, evidence ancestry/common mode, option loss and reopening capacity** rather than prediction error alone.

Potential paper shape:
- computational philosophy of science / AI & science;
- simulation-heavy;
- compare EIG, disagreement, random, novelty, reopening-preserving and ancestry-aware policies;
- quantify perceived fit vs exterior-rival recovery / option preservation.

Novelty risk: **medium-high**, because 2026 work is very close.
Tractability: **very high** for a concrete empirical/computational paper.
This could become the most experimentally legible MQR paper if the extra mechanism is real.

## Prospect D — Option-Preserving Experimental Design under Open-World Model Uncertainty

Lineage:
- 4.42 open rival frontier
- 4.53 information != intervention != action value
- 4.56 option value / challenge value
- 4.61 lost-option debt / noncommutative revision
- 4.62 option-preserving sequencing

Core thesis:
Under open model uncertainty, experiment choice should sometimes preserve future discriminatory interventions even when a destructive experiment has larger local expected information or identifiability gain.

Potential formal target:
construct conditions/counterexamples where:
- local EIG maximization,
- identifiability maximization,
- or cost-normalized design
destroys a unique future separator,
while a typed option-preserving policy retains it.

Potential paper shape:
- formal ML / causal discovery / sequential design;
- theorem + synthetic benchmark;
- later real scientific case if one can be reconstructed honestly.

Novelty risk: **high but interesting**.
Real-options, robust experimental design, Bayes-adaptive planning and dual control are dangerous prior-art neighbors.
MQR needs a genuinely distinct open-rival/reopening theorem, not merely a new name for nonmyopic planning.

Tractability: **medium**.

## Prospect E — Executable Scientific Constitutions

Lineage:
- 4.36 onward preseal + adversarial courts
- cumulative Real-Language
- independent Rust/Prolog implementations
- Lean boundaries
- exact-head closure / reopening doctrine
- 4.59–4.62 reflexive governance

Core thesis:
Methodological claims about scientific authority can be represented as executable, versioned, adversarially testable constitutions with explicit claim ceilings and reopening conditions.

Potential paper shape:

- architecture paper plus case-study lineage.

Novelty risk: **high**.
Executable epistemology, provenance systems, formal scientific workflows and argumentation tools create a broad prior-art surface.
Tractability: **high as a system paper**, lower as a pure philosophy paper unless the formal semantics are sharpened.

## Current synthesis

Do not choose yet.

The two most internally mature clusters are:
1. **Local progress without a global scalar** — already theoretically coherent and mostly closed.
2. **Open rival frontiers → performative experiment selection** — more ambitious, more current, and potentially much more empirical after 4.62.

A plausible long-form architecture is not necessarily one giant MQR paper. It may be better to separate:
- a formal philosophy paper on local progress / partial authority;
- a computational paper on open-frontier evidence acquisition;
- later, an executable-constitution methods/system paper.

This ledger is exploratory only. No publication claim is promoted by its existence.

## 2026 pressure update

The paper prospects changed after reading current 2026 work.

### Prospect C pressure increased

The computational paper on **performative evidence ecologies** remains attractive, but the claim cannot be merely:
- theory-guided experiment selection can bias the evidence ecology;
- informative acquisition can fail under misspecification;
- representativeness matters.

Those points now have very direct 2026 neighbors.

The stronger MQR-specific version would need to test whether policies differ on:
- exterior-rival recovery;
- evidence-ancestry independence;
- retained future separator count;
- branchwise option destruction;
- reopening latency/capacity;
- nonmyopic exploration debt;

while conventional metrics such as current predictive fit or expected information look favorable.

This raises novelty risk to **high**, while keeping empirical tractability **very high**.

### Prospect D pressure increased but became sharper

Robust Bayesian experimental design already attacks misspecification directly. Therefore an option-preserving MQR paper needs a theorem or benchmark in which **no robust local informativeness correction alone captures destruction of a future scientific discrimination route**.

A promising formal target is:
> construct paired acquisition histories with equal/current robust acquisition score but different future rival-separation reachability because one action irreversibly removes a unique separator.

If that can be proved under an explicit open-rival/reopening model, Prospect D remains bold rather than merely terminological.

### Prospect A remains the most mature

The local-progress / non-global-scalar paper is less exposed to the fast-moving autonomous-science literature and already has a long, internally closed theorem/countermodel chain.

### Working publication architecture

Current non-binding architecture:
1. **Paper I — Local Progress Geometry and Partial Scientific Authority**: theory-first.
2. **Paper II — Reopenable Evidence Acquisition in Open Rival Frontiers**: computational/formal, dependent on 4.62+.
3. **Paper III — Executable Scientific Constitutions**: methods/system paper only after the semantics and external cases are stable.

Do not merge these merely for grandeur. The strongest MQR publication may be a small family of papers whose claims can each survive a much narrower novelty court.

## 4.63 calibration update — broad Paper II thesis not supported

MQR-4.63 materially changes the publication landscape.

Five frozen calibration worlds and 3,392 paired runs were completed under strong matched baselines. The first-reveal classification was:

~~~text

ARR       COLLAPSES_TO_BASELINE
EAI       COLLAPSES_TO_BASELINE
EXTERIOR  COLLAPSES_TO_BASELINE
NEDL      SURVIVES_LOCAL (SCOUT-PARK only)
~~~

No typed acquisition object reached the frozen `SURVIVES_CROSS_DOMAIN` gate.

### Prospect B — open rival frontiers

**Status after 4.63: THEORY PROSPECT REMAINS; POLICY CLAIM NARROWED.**

The 4.42 thesis about open frontiers, generator-relative saturation and reopening competence is not defeated by 4.63. What is defeated is the easy move from those diagnostics to a generally independent acquisition controller.

A publishable version should stay closer to:
- frontier construction;
- generator dependence;
- reopening as an authority condition;
- diagnostic separation;

and avoid claiming that an MQR-specific acquisition policy is broadly superior.

### Prospect C — performative evidence ecology

**Status after 4.63: DIAGNOSTIC VERSION PLAUSIBLE / CONTROL-LAYER VERSION NOT SUPPORTED.**

THEORY-ECOLOGY produced a strong descriptive separation:
- confirmation and falsification had the same conventional terminal success in the frozen world;
- yet ERR, RCR, NED and BSI differed sharply.

So the typed coordinates can reveal acquisition differences hidden by one terminal-performance metric.

However, disagreement, representative, random and robust-multifactor baselines already avoided the blind spot. MQR's separate ARR/EAI/NEDL/exterior controls added no independent own-ablation value there.

Therefore a paper about **diagnostic geometry of evidence ecologies** remains plausible; a paper claiming a new generally superior MQR controller does not.

### Prospect D — option-preserving experimental design

**Status after 4.63: HOLD / BINDING-CONDITION CHARACTERIZATION REQUIRED.**

OPR collapsed in both SCOUT-PARK and SAMPLE-RESERVE under the frozen own-ablation test.

This does not show that option preservation is meaningless. It shows that:
- ordinary competent policies can already preserve the relevant option in some regimes;
- merely naming a future separator does not make an MQR-specific rule independently action-guiding.

A stronger future theorem would need to characterize the exact **binding conditions** under which:
1. a future separator is unique;
2. it is genuinely destroyable;
3. current strong baselines do not already preserve it;
4. preserving it changes later scientific reachability;
5. the condition is not merely standard real-option / constrained planning machinery in new vocabulary.

Until then, Prospect D is not ready.

### Prospect A — local progress geometry

**Relative status after 4.63: strengthened.**

4.63 did not directly validate Prospect A, but it removed the strongest reason to rush into a broad acquisition-policy paper.

The mature 4.49–4.61 chain remains:
- theoretically coherent;
- less dependent on fast-moving autonomous-science baselines;
- already supported by formal/countermodel machinery;
- naturally compatible with a narrower claim ceiling.

Prospect A is now the clearest candidate for the first serious MQR manuscript.

### Prospect E — executable scientific constitutions

**Relative status: strengthened as a methodology story, but not yet a standalone novelty claim.**

4.63 supplies an important anti-inflation example:
the executable constitution did not automatically promote its own descendants. Four proposed control coordinates collapsed under strong-baseline calibration, and no `REALACQUIRE 0.30` was created.

That behavior is evidence that the Research OS / executable-constitution machinery can produce substantive negative results rather than only accumulate abstractions.

This is methodologically valuable, but still needs external case depth and prior-art comparison before becoming a standalone systems paper.
