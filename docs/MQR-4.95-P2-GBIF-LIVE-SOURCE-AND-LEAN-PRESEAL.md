# MQR-4.95 — P2: Original GBIF Parent-Root Audit, Verified Swedish Detection/Non-Detection, and Scope-Nontransport Court

**Date:** 2026-10-09. **Status:** 4.95 OPEN; P2 audit after public-API discovery. User explicitly requested continued research with **Rust and Lean**. This entry is **post-discovery, pre-executable-verification**, not independent prospective/blinded discovery.

**Question:** Is independently collected **Anemone nemorosa L.** binary observed presence/absence available, and is it legitimately a calibration witness for Serov's specific 64 Finnish target sites? Separate **same species**, **independent data-generating field collection**, **correctly defined observed non-detection**, **matching target locations and time/effort**, and **design-sampling authority**.

## Direct original GBIF parent (not just a guess)
GBIF original cited download `0031144-240626123714530`, DOI `10.15468/dl.7jsjca`. Actual public API endpoints:
- `https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/download/0031144-240626123714530` reports `totalRecords=29543`, `numberDatasets=27`, country `FI`, taxonKey `3033263`, year 2000–2024; `SUCCEEDED` on 2024-07-20.
- `https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/download/0031144-240626123714530/datasets?limit=100` actually lists 27 contributing publisher datasets whose `numberRecords` sum to precisely 29543, equal to original pinned Serov `anemone.csv` raw row count. This establishes shared original GBIF download **lineage**; it does *not*, without original gbifID-level row matching, establish that every transformed CSV row is bytewise identical.
- Top contributors: Finnish Nature League Spring monitoring 14651, Kastikka Floristic Archives 9686, Hatikka.fi 2308, iNaturalist 1560, FinBIF Notebook 585; smaller included LajiGIS 301/135 and others. **FinBIF Notebook dataset UUID `df12ca07-f133-4550-ab3b-fde13f0e76ba` is ALREADY in original**; new FinBIF portal request is not independent by default. Source datasets may overlap in contributors and this endpoint is one **download**, not 27 mutually independent studies.

## A genuinely external field-data-generating survey: Swedish NFI

The **Swedish National Forest Inventory** (SLU), not a Finnish citizen-observation publisher, is the original **field survey organization** of these independent ecological presence/absence measurements (GBIF acts as a discovery portal). Swedish datasets are not among those 27 FI-selected original parent data sets, and underlying observation territory is Swedish forest plots; this is strong evidence of **distinct source study/observation frame**, not a cryptographic guarantee against every possible cross-portal record reuse.

Two actual GBIF datasets:
- `d59ccafb-251e-46cf-9319-37b58d88e7c5`, DOI `10.15468/b9c3d7`, Swedish NFI 1995–current harmonized **reduced** presence/absence site sample.
- `e1a32bea-73df-4b9b-af2e-6e148ab2641b`, Swedish NFI 1995–2017 **discontinued sample** (supplement, not necessarily independent field staff/protocol or publication root; classify same Swedish NFI field-source family).

GBIF occurrence API filtered on `taxonKey=3033263` (Anemone nemorosa L.) and source dataset UUID finds **actual records** with `occurrenceStatus=PRESENT` and **actual observed non-detection** `ABSENT`, including occurrenceID, sampling eventID and original field sampling protocol. At live-query time current/harmonized `PRESENT=3491`, `ABSENT=32944` total **36435** sampled events. Discontinued supplement `PRESENT=2434`, `ABSENT=23668` total **26102**. These are *GBIF indexed occurrence counts* for that taxon, not 62,537 independent ecosystems or 62,537 additional Serov Finnish target labels; same 5-year revisited plots, strata clusters and geography may repeat. The Swedish dataset metadata describes expert plot field survey, and points are intentionally obfuscated **200–1000 m**. Site and period labels include specific Swedish event IDs; no Finnish Serov site ID match.

**Scope decision fixed before code:**
`DISTINCT_FIELD_STUDY_BINARY_CONTACT_PASS` for the existence and sampled actual records of a same-taxon, separately originated Swedish survey with detection and non-detection.
`SEROV_FINLAND_64_TARGET_CALIBRATION_HOLD` for **the existing finite target** because it is Finnish and its site identities/times/effort and observation protocols are not aligned, and Swedish original coordinates are intentionally obfuscated. Do not transfer labels by nearest location, call 200–1000m noisy Swedish point matching true point, or substitute newly created Swedish risk for the fixed Finland risk without a separately authorized new study.
`DESIGN_CI_HOLD` for actual inclusion probabilities and spatial-cluster joint inclusion law; a field survey being probabilistic is not sufficient to determine design variance from the GBIF endpoint.
