## Why 4.97 is not 4.96 repeated

4.96 CLOSED bounded: 2024 original Finnish `Anemone nemorosa` recorded `ABSENT` is concentrated in one collector (11,165/11,166), and all Spring rows report coordinate uncertainty ≥10km; source-specific recorded label does not identify latent true ecological occupancy or fine-resolution true sampling positions. The Swedish NFI has genuinely distinct field-collected binary detection data, but its permanent vegetation observations occur at about 10-year recurrence and true coordinates are masked 200–1000m. Treating repeated decades as same-season closure is unwarranted. Thus Swedish NFI is **provenance/longitudinal protocol context**, NOT admitted as repeated within-closure secondary visits for 4.97.
