# MQR-4.120 P1 — Dual-Ledger Contestability Contract

2026-10-17 · ACTIVE research, P1 CONSTRUCTED / CONTRACT ONLY.

## Research question
An inference may satisfy a fixed scientific rubric while that rubric's admissibility, authority or revision mechanism remains unjustified. Conversely a disputed court does not automatically falsify the underlying measurement. Keep the two warrants typed, inspectable and independently defeasible.

J1(e,c|r) evaluates claim c given evidence e and frozen rubric r. J2(r,a|s) evaluates a court's authorization and contestability for scope s. A judgement pair is not a global scalar and is not an automatic logical implication in either direction.

## Required typed record
- Identity: case_id, claim_id, claim_scope, scenario_origin (CONSTRUCTED or OBSERVED), source_refs.
- J1: evidence_refs, source_ancestry, measurement_or_inference_model, frozen_rubric, assumptions, judgement (PASS/FAIL/HOLD/OUT_OF_SCOPE), reasons, missing_discriminator.
- J2: authority_source, its ancestry, selection_exclusions, challenge_channel, review_or_revision_authority, judgement (PASS/FAIL/HOLD/OUT_OF_SCOPE), reasons, possible_remedy.
- Joint: material_dissent, alternative_question, next_test, reopening_trigger, residual_debt, authority_ceiling. Agreement count may be recorded but never silently sets J2.
