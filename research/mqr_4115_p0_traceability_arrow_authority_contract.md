## Two loops, explicitly separated
**EPISTEMIC_PRODUCTION_LOOP:** world-facing physical measurement / original historical document / logically legitimate argument -> claim-specific evidence -> institutional adoption -> independently new observation or reasoned correction -> revised claim warrant.

**AUTHORITY_REPRODUCTION_LOOP:** success -> reputation and institutional authority -> repeated citation or standardized instruction -> selective evidence gathering and apparent consensus -> further authority. Authority's social effectiveness can become an empirical fact without independently warranting each adopted claim.

Authority can change later real-world experiments, textbook source selection, technological standards, metrics, purchasing and behavior: **WORLD_FEEDBACK**. Later world observations must be indexed to time and intervention, so we do not mistake authority-induced changes in the world for proof that the original proposition was independently true.

These processes have extensive precursors: Merton (1968) cumulative advantage; Fricker (1998) rational authority and social power; Greenberg (2009) citation distortions; Arthur (1989) path dependence; Espeland and Sauder (2007) reactive rankings. Claiming novelty for authority feedback as such is prohibited. Original contribution remains to be demonstrated by operationalized, source-independent competing mechanism falsification.

**Evidence gate:** No `AUTHORITY_TRANSFER -> EVIDENTIAL_SUPPORT` automatic cast. No nominal shared SRM 917c catalogue identifier -> physically shared bottle / calibrated error correlation. No multiple cites of one ancestral source -> independent observations. Document identity, edition collation, textual fidelity and claim-level warrant are different witness types. A physical calibration certificate for the chemical and a certified measurand on the original serum are different object types.

## Testable divergences and rival models
- Rival A: Logical-operation concentration is a genuine stable scientific bottleneck. Rival B: It is driven by coding granularity and corpus method selection. Challenge with preregistered operation taxonomy, granularity transforms, and out-of-domain methods.
- Rival C: High citation convergence reflects independent replication. Rival D: It is authority-driven reuse of the same underlying source. Challenge with independent source-ancestry clustering, chronological adoption patterns, correction propagation, and document-level falsifiers. Citation-network shape alone does not identify causal authority flow.
- Rival E: Shared reference label means common calibration error. Rival F: Shared label coexists with independent stock preparations or heterogeneous biases. Demand actual physical provenance, time-matched certificates and independently witnessed same-material observations.
- The inverse arrow `claimed evidential support <- authority prestige` can be a social causal mechanism, but remains distinct from legitimate epistemic support. Both can coexist; no black-and-white norm imposed by graph degree.
