## Explicit continuous family
Set mass=1, common physical position x, normalized energy zero at the minimum. Define f_lambda(y)=y+lambda*y^2, V_lambda(f_lambda(y))=y^2/2 on a common bounded branch |y|<=a_max with 2|lambda|a_max<1. Then f is strictly increasing and V has a smooth single minimum at x=0. For E=A^2/2, 0<A<a_max, turning points x_±=±A+lambda*A^2, width=2A, midpoint=lambda*A^2. Both potentials have the usual constant-mass Hamiltonian p^2/2+V_lambda(x). These are not a mere coordinate transformation of the same constant-mass Hamiltonian: pulling the original kinetic energy back to x would change its coefficient; holding kinetic term and x-position calibration fixed defines distinct force laws -dV/dx=-y/(1+2lambda*y). Do not conflate this with a canonical change of variables or a proof of metaphysical inequivalence.
The period is 2 integral_{-A}^A (1+2lambda*y)/sqrt(A^2-y^2) dy = 2pi, by parity. The time average of position is the ratio of integrals of (y+lambda*y^2)(1+2lambda*y) and (1+2lambda*y) against dy/sqrt(A^2-y^2). Odd terms integrate to zero, mean y^2=A^2/2, so <x>=3lambda*A^2/2=3lambda E. One known positive E and independently calibrated signed mean position identify lambda in this family. The width and period alone cannot identify it at any allowed energy. Unknown position zero, energy calibration, orientation, mass, other functional forms or dissipation are separate conditions. Thus a law may be exact across an entire continuum of experiments and remain incomplete with respect to a different physically accessible question.

## General single-well reconstruction from two observable functions
Let V be a regular single-well potential with two monotone inverse branches x_-(u)<x_+(u) for 0<u<E_max, joining at the minimum at u=0. Assume common fixed mass=1, calibrated x and energy, conservative motion, local absolute continuity and sufficient integrability for the following Abel transforms and Fubini (including signed first-moment integrand); e.g. smooth nondegenerate minimum and regular branches. Define w(u)=x_+(u)-x_-(u), z(u)=(x_+(u)^2-x_-(u)^2)/2. Both vanish at zero even if the minimum is not at zero in x. Let T(E) be the full period, M(E)=T(E)<x>_E its time-integrated first moment. Change variables on the outgoing and incoming portions:
T(E)=sqrt(2) integral_0^E w'(u)/sqrt(E-u) du.
M(E)=sqrt(2) integral_0^E z'(u)/sqrt(E-u) du.
Apply the integrated Abel inverse: integral_0^U [integral_0^E h(u)/sqrt(E-u) du]/sqrt(U-E) dE = pi integral_0^U h(u)du, since the inner integral over E from u to U is pi (beta(1/2,1/2)). Therefore
w(U)=1/(pi*sqrt(2)) integral_0^U T(E)/sqrt(U-E) dE,
z(U)=1/(pi*sqrt(2)) integral_0^U M(E)/sqrt(U-E) dE.
For U>0 the midpoint c=z/w; x_±=c±w/2. Thus the pair of complete functions determines both branches on the covered energy interval, subject to the stated model class and calibrations. This is a direct standard Abel-transform argument, not a claim of priority. Period alone recovers width, and symmetry about a known center is an alternative sufficient prior. For the polynomial shear above w=2sqrt(2U), z=4sqrt(2)*lambda*U^(3/2), reproducing the turning points. This is a positive result about which added question closes an inverse problem, not a guarantee against unconsidered model classes or a claim about finite/noisy observations. Observing a finite number of energies is not equivalent to having these continuous functions; estimation/inversion conditioning not established.

## Return to the wider program
A mathematical regularity can be shared exactly across physically different descriptions or candidate systems. Its value is real under the assumed model, but its information content depends on which quantity is observed and on the class of admissible dynamics. Here a new question (time-averaged signed position) reveals the missing shear without altering the dynamics. This connects Wigner's mathematical efficacy and nonuniqueness, original UV-Vis observational quotient, alien-science partial bridges, structural explanation, and the genesis of a new useful question. It is not an alien-language experiment: position/time/energy and the candidate model class were supplied by us. An agent with only period readouts may not know or access the missing spatial observable. Discoverability, ability to establish common coordinates, and physical access remain open. The next distinct work should address which observations/operations can be jointly recognized across differently constructed scientific languages, not extend this oscillator grid indefinitely.
