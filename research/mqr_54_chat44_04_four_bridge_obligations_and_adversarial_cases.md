# MQR 5.4 · MQR 44 · Record 04 — Four distinct failure modes of cross-science connection

Date: 2026-11-21. MQR 5.4 OPEN. This is a proposed cross-domain synthesis and falsification agenda, not a new universal theorem.

## Thesis worth testing
A pair of scientific practices can be connected without a universal language, but **the evidential authority of a partial connection is typed by what it preserves**. Four non-equivalent obligations:
1. Observable comparability: shared outcome statistics and calibration lineage at a specified operational scope.
2. Intervention comparability: causal response under a physically implementable and jointly interpretable intervention; MQR44 record02 gives an observational-equivalence counterexample.
3. Semantic preservation: preservation of declared inferential roles, reference and taxonomic exclusions; Kuhnian taxonomic non-translation (SEP Incommensurability) is not just a low empirical agreement score.
4. Global coherence: existence of a joint representation for all declared local probability interfaces; MQR44 record03 gives a finite parity obstruction.

Do not infer (4) from pairwise checks (1), nor infer global semantic translation from (4); either implication has additional unstated premises. Similarly, a failed single global model does not exclude a larger representation using contextual variables or changed ontology.

## Sharp conceptual risks
- *Projection risk*: historical scientific concepts do not arrive with canonical finite probability spaces. Identifying context overlaps and translating observables are theoretical acts requiring dated justification.
- *Ontology inflation*: an experimental mismatch may be due to uncontrolled calibration or different estimands, rather than deep incommensurability.
- *Historical compression*: Lakatos's 1976 book contains a deliberately rationally reconstructed dialogue and a warning from editors about Cauchy/Fourier historical gaps. Do not treat it as a verbatim scientific discovery transcript.
- *Mistaken uniqueness*: even a successful joint distribution does not identify a uniquely true ontology or global semantics.
- *Prior-art*: Kuhn/Feyerabend, Masterman, Friedman, Galison, Nersessian, Pearl, Abramsky–Brandenburger already address crucial portions of this territory. Their results should be learned from, not relabeled as MQR discoveries.

## Prospective demands
A. Pre-register each historical candidate's original vocabulary, date-specific available instruments, exemplars, and original publication (not late translation substituted as autograph).
B. Write a **bridge contract** listing variables/propositions, overlap map, permissible operations, independent evidence, precision, failure modes, and ontology withheld.
C. Attempt constructive global comparison only when data support mathematical assumptions. If no canonical finite outcome space exists, say NO APPLICABLE GLUING TEST, not FAIL.
D. Hold out at least one rival reconstruction where the proposed conceptual innovation is merely a relabeling, and a case where new experimentally informative questions arise without a change in primitive syntax.
E. Evaluate whether this four-type scheme predicts any documented cross-science success/failure better than Kuhn + causal inference + sheaf compatibility taken separately. If it does not, the meaningful output is an explanatory synthesis and taxonomy, not a novel unified theory.

## Three concrete adversarial prompts for subsequent session
1. Find a genuine historical transition where locally agreed observational outputs plus available intervention contracts still lead to an explicitly documented semantic/taxonomic disagreement. Which primary dated records settle each claim?
2. Find a counterexample where two practices share semantic and experimental interface maps but their preferred metaphysical interpretations remain distinct (Lorentz–Einstein is a candidate with live literature dispute).
3. Build a prospective comparison of bridge-coverage before and after a conceptual invention, with no ability to post-hoc expand the pre period's accessible query set based on knowledge of the later outcome.

## Assessment
The new valuable move is not the parity theorem, but a systematically separated **research program** for cross-science comparability: observation, intervention, semantics, and global coherence must be adjudicated independently. Philosophical synthesis is legitimate under the 2026-10-18 doctrine override even if not unprecedented; physical or universal claims require more evidence. No 5.5 promotion.
