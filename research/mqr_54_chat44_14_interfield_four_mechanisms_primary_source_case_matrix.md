## 1. Source and witness levels (do not flatten into a generic case-study claim)

A. **Mitchell (2020), 'Through the Fractured Looking Glass'**, direct read from [already-held original author journal preprint](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LnEhdfgvcOQtIwGmL1arFveInkE2AZXm/view), DOI 10.1086/710787. Describes cases where X-ray crystallographic protein structure inference and NMR structure inference can diverge due to different physical preparation and instrument-specific bias. **Joint refinement** is an attempted mutual correction: disagreement must be investigated, not blindly averaged. A useful epistemic result is identifying a bias/condition under which two structures are compatible, or preserving a genuine target difference when they are not. **No paired raw X-ray/NMR instrument files have been verified in this MQR session.** The original text is a philosopher's case reconstruction and no specific retrospective experimental validation receipt is being claimed.

B. **Weiskopf (2025), 'Trading Evidence: The Role of Models in Interfield Unification'**, [publisher original freely accessible HTML](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy-of-science/article/trading-evidence-the-role-of-models-in-interfield-unification/B62FEBF229A1498F4FD729AF101672E4), DOI 10.1017/psa.2025.14. Author's explicit case concerns **model-based cognitive neuroscience** integrating behavioral decision-task data and neuroimaging/EEG by **embedding models and full pipelines**, often a drift-diffusion model (DDM) inside a richer model. An embedded cognitive model alone is **not enough**: data preprocessing, model embedding, linking relations, hyperparameters, and field-specific measurement models establish evidence relations. These are not primarily new physical measurement capabilities. The author already recognized that data reuse is evidence-relative; MQR's 'data-only new questions' is therefore NOT an independent novelty claim. No original participant-level EEG/fMRI/behavior data or standalone model fit was reproduced here.

C. **Deulofeu & Suárez (2023)**, [Research OS full PDF verified HELD](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bgd9vw84e3tZ80MEGBPmonjg0xUk-Kb_/view), *Theoria* 38(3):299–317, DOI 10.1387/theoria.23871. The authors use two cases whose argumentative scope is different:
  - following **Fagan (2016)**, some mechanistic stem-cell experimentalists prioritize molecular components and actionable manipulations, while some systems-style mathematical researchers seek general dynamical patterns. The failure mechanism described is **epistemic goal and explanatory norm mismatch**, not necessarily literal inability to read each other's measurements or absence of a shared world. Fagan's [2016 published study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-015-0776-3) describes a failed interfield collaboration yet also proposes a reconciliation route. Fagan full source not verified held; only its publisher/abstract and Deulofeu's body were used here.
  - drawing on **Batterman & Green (2021)**, some bone-fracture questions are answered at a mesoscopic scale capturing cortical/trabecular structure. Adding nano-level detail may be unhelpful or misleading **for that particular fracture target** if the chosen micro-model omits functional mesoscopic geometry. [Author-hosted original](https://sites.pitt.edu/~rbatterm/bones-steel-synth.pdf) located and [university catalog](https://researchprofiles.ku.dk/en/publications/steel-and-bone-mesoscale-modeling-and-middle-out-strategies-in-ph/) confirms 2021 bibliography. These are the authors' philosophical/modeling claims; there was no fresh bone-FEM benchmark.
  - Crucial critical fairness: Deulofeu & Suárez concede that Mitchell could reply that integration is pragmatic, target-dependent and that joint refinement may repair differences. Their criticism applies to overly strong necessity claims, not a proof that Mitchell's actual pluralism is false.

D. **Galison (1999) and Star & Griesemer (1989)** [original documents already held and checked in R08](mqr_54_chat44_08_joint_action_genesis_galison_star_congruence.md). Instrument co-design and stabilized boundary objects are distinct mechanisms. Neither must produce an exact field-level dictionary or identify a single final ontology.

## 3. Philosophical discriminators for future independent empirical work

Before calling a new multidisciplinary method a scientific success, assess **separately**:
- **FEASIBLE**: a new question / operator / evidence link could be physically executed or statistically evaluated in a specified target regime.
- **PRESERVES**: existing native scientific predictions and critical procedures survived at their original scope; if not, identify the lost target.
- **CONTACT**: independent evidence exists for same specimen/event, valid preprocessing, sensor calibration, and transfer.
- **TARGET**: the new integrated question, acceptable error and research relevance are defined without presupposing a universal common score.
- **NOVELTY**: what is not already explained by Galison, Star, Nersessian, Mitchell, Weiskopf, Fagan, Batterman/Green, standard sufficient statistics and automated experiment design?

These are **questions for a prospective gate**, not measured metrics or a newly validated normative measurement scale. DO NOT collapse them into a total scalar progress index. A 'scientists disagree' interview snippet is not evidence of a specific instrument's failure, and a strong analytic toy is not evidence of historical adoption.
