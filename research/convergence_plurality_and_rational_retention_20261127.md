# MQR 5.4 — Rational change without presumed global convergence
2026-11-27 KST | MQR 46 | revisable macro research, not version promotion.

## Source-grounded competition
Read selected sections from held Friedman (2001), Dynamics of Reason, Drive 1JAnxiRCtjaDEeTI_ZG1iLaCyLqKVmsNt, lecture III pp.53–66. He distinguishes predictive/instrumental success from communicative rationality; pp.64–66 propose convergence of constitutive principles as a regulative ideal, explicitly acknowledging that future principles are unknown. His three conditions include approximate limiting recovery, natural conceptual transformation and a productive philosophical meta-framework. Do not attribute demonstrated historical convergence or simple monism to him.

Read held Kitcher (1993), The Advancement of Science, Drive 1-dqXnIObLoPTVXFyeRAKCG0Astd9oqM-, pp.74–81, 95–98 and relevant life-cycle passages. Individual practice already contains language, significant questions, accepted statements, explanatory patterns, credibility, instruments/experiments and methodology. Personal and impersonal question priorities are partial orders. The MQR five-component snapshot is therefore a simplifying projection with major prior-art collision, not a new ontology. Kitcher also connects conceptual/explanatory progress and question significance with ordering nature.

Read held Kuhn (1983), Commensurability, Comparability, Communicability, Drive 1Ep81ueXQGpa5lqeDZfKLj9na0jSYRHxc, pp.670–674, 683. Local incommensurability does not entail incomparability; identifying a term's referent in modern language differs from translating the native term. Interpretation and language acquisition can permit communication. Held Kuhn (1974), Second Thoughts on Paradigms, Drive 1-jK0eaCXH_RvXRvFgrUCXMO_Yh8GLyiy, web-print pages 3–4, distinguishes symbolic generalizations, models and exemplars. Existing MQR-4.118 historical and Kuhn audits already record this; avoid duplicate novelty.

Read held Chang (2012), Is Water H2O?, Drive 1ZZPVnMOCco8J2ORdtJxs5fC27kQ9sE15, chapter 1 opening and book preface. Chang's historical interpretation challenges treating the phlogistonist practice as straightforwardly inferior and proposes that retaining alternative practices could have been beneficial. This is an argued historical counterfactual, not a measured outcome. Kusch (2015), Scientific Pluralism and the Chemical Revolution, DOI 10.1016/j.shpsa.2014.10.001, author penultimate draft https://philarchive.org/archive/KUSSPA, introduction, §5.4 and conclusion read in web text; screenshot failed, image parity not certified. Kusch disputes unity of the supposed phlogistonist system, historical evidence and the inference from underdetermination to prescriptive pluralism. Even granting narrow empirical underdetermination, Kusch argues that indirect reasons (trust, track record, institutional conditions and research vision) can support rational allegiance. He does not reject pluralism universally. Historical claims have not been independently adjudicated here.

## Reconstruction: separate three decisions
For practices A,B, distinguish (i) comparative warrant of a scoped claim, (ii) allocation of effort to a practice or component, (iii) commitment to eventual global unification. Warrant for a proposition is not identical to value of maintaining a method, or to a convergence claim. These relations are logical distinctions, not empirical proof of a historical alternative. A rational preference for one framework need not entail eliminating every capability of another, and an unresolved empirical comparison need not entail funding both equally. A local bridge sufficient for one shared question need not provide semantic equivalence or transitive/global translation.

Research hypothesis: explain cross-practice rational change by specifying what problem-solving capacities become accessible or lost, what independent world-facing consequences are available, and why retention, replacement, or recombination is justified under actual resource constraints. Do not smuggle in a final universal grammar or assume perpetual pluralism. Must confront Friedman, Kitcher, Kuhn, Chang and Kusch rather than rename them. The macro question is whether a revisable network of partial bridges can sustain rationality without a regulative global limit; the present analysis neither disproves Friedman's normative thesis nor establishes sufficiency. For historical study, distinguish a contemporaneous scientist's reasons, later historical reconstruction, and our counterfactual evaluation.

## Concrete next discriminators
Chemical controversy: compare which experimental consequences, referents, conceptual categories, and justified credibility judgments are preserved when theory changes. Require dated first-person primary sources and a chronology of what was available at the time, not only later commentators. Reopen MQR 4.118 rather than make a new disconnected case. Compare successful convergence (local limiting recovery) with genuinely non-reducible productive coexistence (X-ray/NMR and Grosholz as prior leads); avoid treating differences of mathematical representation as physical ontological plurality. A useful negative result would show that a supposed novel bridge was available before the alleged conceptual change or that preserving a method adds no feasible discriminating capacity.
