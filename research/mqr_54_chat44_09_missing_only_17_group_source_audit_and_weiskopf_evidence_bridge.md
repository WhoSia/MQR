## 2. Direct new prior-art reading (publisher original accessible, not a 'PDF owned' claim)

**Daniel A. Weiskopf (2025)**, *Trading Evidence: The Role of Models in Interfield Unification*, DOI 10.1017/psa.2025.14, publisher full-text HTML read in substantive sections 1,2,3,5,6. Open Access CC BY 4.0 on publisher page. Author distinguishes **theoretical/explanatory**, **methodological** and **data/evidential** integration; model-based cognitive neuroscience offers examples in which a model turns existing data created in one specialty into legitimate evidence for questions of another. Such *evidence-making* can be an autonomous practice rather than a special case of explanatory unification. Crucially, Weiskopf distinguishes locally constructed transitory embedding models from persistent expertise/know-how to reconnect data.

His strong thesis creates a near-direct collision with any claim that a *new material interaction* is required for expansion of scientific questions. It isn't necessarily: new standards, representational links, feasible data-pairing, and models can make *existing* material records epistemically useful for new questions. No paper-level assertion that every cross-field integration preserves physical action or that models automatically create ontological truth. This existing work builds upon Leonelli and Galison; MQR cannot describe it as a new insight.

New scientific/philosophical question arising: when does new contact **create a physical affordance** versus merely **extend the use of previously produced evidence**? We should not evaluate both by one operational partition count. Their warrants and failures are different:
- evidence integration risks target mismatch, undocumented preprocessing/paired event false positives, nontransportable target models;
- physical coupling risks nonrealizable operations, calibration failure, unstable interventions, misleading stage-specific outputs;
- either may open new *questions*, and neither alone grants realist identity.

## 3. Executed finite counterexample to 'joint physical coupling is necessary'

See [source](../experiments/mqr-5.4/mqr44_interfield_reuse_versus_joint_action.py). A has x sensor and bit flip, B has y sensor and bit flip. Native A distinctions = 2 even under arbitrarily many separate local flips. Earlier record08 gave a physical controlled port c(x,y)=(x XOR y,y) that extends A-readable distinctions to 4.

**New negative test:** suppose A and B can securely, nondestructively exchange **paired records for the same state**, including A's x and B's y. Then A+B's joint record distinguishes all 4 states *without any new physically coupled transformation*. This is an epistemic affordance produced by valid record exchange; it is NOT material generation of a new action.

A further counterargument: with reliable *real-time* B measurement and message to A, A can conditionally execute its existing flip only when y=1. This feedforward composition **physically realizes c** even though no standalone controlled coupling device is fabricated. Conversely no finite **fixed, unconditioned** word of fa/fb equals c. Thus scope must distinguish:
- unconditioned local action monoid;
- conditional actions with communication and feedback;
- physical new component/instrument;
- new joint observational report;
- justified cross-field inferential target.

The exact four-state experiment was executed locally with assertions, and [source committed](../experiments/mqr-5.4/mqr44_interfield_reuse_versus_joint_action.py). Output: A-only 2; A+B reliable paired record 4; A+new controlled port 4. The matching of events, nondisturbance, channel reliability, time alignment and possibility of genuine real-time feedback are **provided assumptions**. Without them, independent uniform x and y marginals do not establish a joint record or referential bridge.

**Verdict:** joint cross-conditional physical operator is *sufficient* for A to access another state coordinate under model conditions, but *not necessary* to jointly answer some new questions; even physically implementing a controlled operator may require only messaging plus conditional existing operations. This limits the scope of R08 and prevents causal overclaim. The new distinction is already anticipated by data integration, classical distributed algorithms and active systems identification: new philosophical novelty HOLD.

## 4. Strong prospective research design, not another four-state treadmill

Create a historical/engineering comparative matrix for:
1. **data-only coordination** (shared standardized data / boundary object; no new material intervention);
2. **software/procedural coupling** (new model, inference or conditional feedback on existing hardware);
3. **material coupling** (physically new interaction made possible);
4. **normative reconstitution** (community judges a different problem significant or a different evidence standard acceptable).

Compare two documented historical cases from Galison (1999/2010) and Star & Griesemer (1989) with Weiskopf's 2025 cognitive-neuroscience cases, separating what exactly became executable or inferentially valid, by which actor and at what date. The eventual *ambitious* target is an explanatory account of how a self-maintaining hybrid scientific practice creates **new families of answerable questions**, not a magic universal definition or a newly invented XOR gate.

Do not automatically turn shared data into causal evidence or material consistency into cross-language equivalence. A proposed public, reusable criticism procedure for **who decides when a data type is evidentially valid in another science** is a genuinely important open normative question, but Lari/Kitcher/Leonelli/Weiskopf are strong rivals.
