## 2. Three distinct bridges and two nonimplications

A **material bridge** establishes what shared specimen/preparation or physically transmitted object an experiment acts on. A **measurement bridge** establishes reference, units, timing, calibration and novel intervention predictions. A **conceptual bridge** establishes how incompatible native ontologies or deductions translate, if at all. Even a successful material+measurement bridge **does not uniquely determine a theory**: different ontological accounts may agree on every admitted intervention. Conversely, mathematically isomorphic theories do not guarantee material preparation alignment. Treat these as *separately testable claims*.

For candidate correspondence `B` between native preparations `p` and native actions `a`, test on a held-out physically independent intervention:
`d( Y_A(p,a), Y_B(B_P(p), B_A(a)) ) ≤ δ`
where **`d` and `δ` must themselves be operationally grounded**, not chosen after seeing both outputs. If neither side shares an observable outcome scale or a physical reference operation, this comparison isn't even well-defined: the *bootstrap* is the research question, not something we have solved.

## 3. Decisive experiments, milestones and stopping rules

**Q branch (first: source output semantics).** Independently interpret `Uref`, `M/S`, calibration `DIGITIZER` and QWTB `TWM-QPSW/TWM-PSFE`; explain the 1.13137 V vs nominal 0.8 V RMS naming without manipulating the archive. If an authoritative method paper/code doesn't support mapping, keep `SEMANTIC_UNRESOLVED`. Do not claim the four run differences prove ground-loop mitigation. The archived result file is publisher computation, **not** an independent replay.

**O branch (second: synchronization sensitivity).** Build an *independent* dark/bright/timing/probe protocol; challenge the graph alignment with a physically registered sample/clock test. In the short term PTB graph-index shifts are sensitivity analyses, not reruns with new hardware. A new Malus AB/BA optical order intervention remains prospective; app and LEGO model allow *planning* but do not supply AB/BA outcomes.

**C branch (third: primitive science bootstrap).** Construct two agents whose preparation labels and outcome categories are independently permuted; no shared anchors or semantics. Give them a feasible world-manipulation/transport channel under explicit resource bounds. Ask which experimental interactions collapse their possible preparation/action mappings. Include a negative witness where no interaction can break symmetry, and a positive witness where the first independent cross-lab material token is available. The prior `n!` finite enumeration presumes shared output semantics when a channel is admitted; a stronger result must model acquiring that semantic bridge, not add more permutations.

**H branch (fourth: historical/practical comparison).** With [Hadot 1995/2002](mqr_54_chat42_03_hadot_discourse_practice_and_world_bridges.md) and [Rorty 1984](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jFjhCBBvTNVt1EoDXW8WzNmJLQCvXyWT9/view), reconstruct specific historical practices without projecting present mathematical categories backwards. Miranda Vilchis (2022) and Sharpe (2016) constrain an easy but false "practice eliminates theory" reading. The goal isn't to declare modern physics a lifestyle; it's to study why training, laboratory operation and theoretical articulation become jointly meaningful.

**Stop/return rule:** If two successive iterations add only identical-looking counters, source titles, or new code wrappers without a new independently testable distinction, **return to the root and change the experiment**, not increase the commit-count target. Keep small verified commits, but a dozen commits is evidence of versioned work, not of philosophical novelty. A successful research unit yields at least one: new falsifier, new empirical observation, stronger limitation theorem, or independently checked prior-art distinction.
