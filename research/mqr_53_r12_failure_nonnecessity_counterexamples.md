# MQR-5.3 R12 — Failure Not Necessary or Sufficient for Scientific Problem Generation

Date: 2026-10-31. Canonical Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/40aef561cf928165977af8e05ffc1b4f (R11 omission backfilled and R12 appended successfully).

## Three source-bounded challenges

1. **Recognized failure without immediate continuation**. Rheinberger (1997), *Toward a History of Epistemic Things*, chapter 6, pp.84–86, Google Drive 1DDrn0i1jOZ79tx8VxftIgQmdTrNH4tr8: Zamecnik, Stephenson and Novelli's around-1951 bacterial cell-free amino-acid incorporation attempt could not rule out residual intact bacteria; historical account says effort was shelved. Failure localization did not suffice for prompt continuation. Do not treat an abandoned local pathway as permanently sterile.
2. **Constructive conceptual innovation without preceding empirical refutation as a logical prerequisite**. Poincare (1902), *Science and Hypothesis*, translator's English copy Google Drive 1C9vLadRkpou7xm1m18Y533wzyFM1vaf0, preface and non-Euclidean geometry chapters. Choice of geometrical concepts and conventions is intellectually constructive yet constrained by experience, fertility, convenience, not sheer arbitrariness. Philosophical counterexample, NOT independently verified full discovery genealogy of non-Euclidean geometry.
3. **Representation as problem generator**. Grosholz (2007), *Representation and Productive Ambiguity in Mathematics and the Sciences*, Google Drive 1rO8HNGJGkn89Mq3KNJuy71uA6liRXIco, chapter 3 concluding text, printed p.90: symbolic/iconic and multiple descriptive scales permit fruitful bridge-building between molecular and macroscopic/biological descriptions despite irreducible gaps. Does not imply every ambiguity is productive.

## Revision to R11
The linear story failure -> new question -> novel concept -> progress is neither a general necessity nor sufficiency thesis. Better distinguish a failure-triggered pathway, a constructive/formal exploration pathway, and representational transposition. Source evidence warrants specific historical observations only; analytic pathway classification is our proposal. Independently track dead ends, false leads and post-hoc success selection; distinguish question generation from its rational certification. No empirical experiment, original theorem or universal historical law claimed.
