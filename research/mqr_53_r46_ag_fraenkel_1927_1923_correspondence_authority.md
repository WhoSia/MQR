## The letter has a historic shelfmark

April 18, 1923 Fraenkel-to-Brouwer letter: Kish Bar-On (2022), Synthese 200:521, footnote 9 transmits Hesseling (2003), Gnomes in the Fog, printed p.150. Hesseling p.150 fn235 explicitly gives 'Letter from Fraenkel to Brouwer, 18/4/1923, [MI Brouwer, CB.AFR 3]'. German extract on p.151 and translator note 236. Thus previous 'no archival identifier' claim is falsified. Old shelfmark does not prove current extant autograph or current digitized location. Dirk van Dalen (2000), Brouwer and Fraenkel on Intuitionism, Bulletin of Symbolic Logic 6(3):284–310, DOI 10.2307/421057, says Fraenkel letters had not been preserved; the scope of this assertion must be reconciled with Hesseling's locator. Do not invent why they differ.

## Interpretation and comparison

Public 1923 and 1927 texts register Fraenkel's consciously publishable stance on constructivity, mathematical existence and excluded middle, but a private letter may register a different provisional attitude. A German passage transmitted Hesseling -> Kish Bar-On is ONE source chain, not two independent attestations. Strong prior art already addresses this case: Hesseling, van Dalen, Kish Bar-On. The user asks for new conceptual history of paper authority, but it requires a new discriminating historical observation, not another taxonomy.
