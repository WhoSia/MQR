## Important asymmetric findings

**0.18:** The Court explicitly rejects both neglect romanticism ('marginalized' means true) and power reductionism (institutional provenance means false). An altruistic funder can *improve* evidence. Thus the correct thesis is about materially possible criticisms, not blanket institution-skepticism.

**0.29:** A self-defeating risk model may become apparently inaccurate precisely because deploying it successfully prevents predicted harms. Observational outcomes after action differ from the pre-intervention target; not every looping class becomes unreal.

**1.19:** A reconfigured neural population can maintain a task but lack evidence that it is the same numerically identical mechanism. Conversely, graph non-isomorphism need not imply functional replacement if transition/intervention bridges are warranted.

**1.67:** Chain successor failure does not imply *automatic* reversion to the earliest actor; task-specific duties and presently available lawful repair capacities determine candidate obligations. The Courts do not override actual positive law.

**1.69:** Doubling nominal audit frequency without capacity/trace retention can worsen effective resolution. Faster or more continuous observation is not automatically superior; primary empirical causal identification remains absent.
