## Model and counterexamples
Three shared abstract state labels 0,1,2; two interventions 0,1; each trial starts at 0; deterministic time-invariant transitions; common observation function coarse=(0,0,1) or fine=(0,1,2). The latter assumes meaningful cross-world correspondence of internal states; it is not evidence that a physically available sensor exists. Transition table (F00,F01,F10,F11,F20,F21). A=(0,1,0,2,0,0), B=(0,1,0,2,0,1). For 1111, A states 1,2,0,1, B states 1,2,1,2. Coarse traces 0,1,0,0 vs 0,1,0,1. All words through depth 2 yield equal internal endpoints; all words through depth 3 yield equal coarse endpoints. First fine distinction at depth 3, coarse at 4. No common deterministic instantaneous state sensor can distinguish equal states. A second pair: constant-0 versus constant-1 transition systems, common reset 0, same coarse observation on every finite word; fine separates at one step. Longer experiments cannot compensate for this particular observation loss. Not all loss is repaired by refinement: unreachable transitions remain unidentified even with identity sensor.

## Analytic upper bound
For two such Moore machines form their disjoint union of six states. At depth 0 partition states by current label; at depth k+1 refine by (current label, depth-k successor class for each action). By induction two states share a class iff all words through depth k yield equal endpoint labels. Every strict refinement increases class count; a non-strict refinement is a fixed point of this recurrence. Starting with two label classes leaves at most four strict refinements; starting with three leaves at most three. Therefore if distinguishable, reset states separate by length <=4 coarse or <=3 fine. These bounds are attained in this family. This is a standard finite partition-refinement argument, not a novelty claim or a Lean-checked proof of the general bound. Endpoint equivalence for all words implies trace equivalence via prefixes.

## MQR interpretation
Different inquiry moves change different components: longer word extends depth, refined observation changes initial partition, extra feasible intervention changes successor signature. They can complement one another; no universal ranking without feasibility, noise and resource cost. Observational equivalence is relative to reset, actions and sensor; it neither establishes same ontology nor rules out new world contact.
