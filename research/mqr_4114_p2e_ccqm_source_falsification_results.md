## R0 conditional strict model test, locked before data rows

Source reported original 9 participant institute combined results (Table 5, glucose mass fraction **mg/g**) with source standard uncertainties `u_i`. The *test* is `y_i = θ + B_common + epsilon_i` with mutually independent mean-zero Gaussian epsilon and the reported u_i as true standard deviations; `B_common` shifts location indistinguishably from θ and is fit away. Define `w_i=1/u_i²`, `μ=sum(w_i*y_i)/sum(w_i)`, `Q=sum(w_i*(y_i−μ)²)`. The **chi-square df=8** 99% threshold is **20.09023502966**, frozen `alpha=.01`.

**Observed from original Table 5:** n=9, μ=**1.1570068137044 mg/g**, Q=**22.12069570267**, exact conditional df=8 survival probability **0.00469711766**. Therefore **R0 strict reported-uncertainty/independence/zero-residual-bias bundle REJECT_CONDITIONAL** at its declared 1% gate. Rejection does **not** determine source identity, direction, source, magnitude, or existence of a true common calibration drift. The result is much narrower than "institutes not independent"; there was no external physical truth and no known covariance matrix. The report's own visually approximately Gaussian cross-institute histogram tests a **different** property and is not logically contradicted by a weighted uncertainty inconsistency.

**Falsification brittleness (clearly diagnostic/post-hoc):** increasing every quoted standard uncertainty by just **0.0005 mg/g** as an illustrative uncertainty-resolution probe (NOT a claim that every true uncertainty was systematically understated) changes Q to **19.1403529684**, BELOW the 1% cutoff. Removing any one of the CENAM, INMETRO or NMIJ institute summaries makes the 1% test no longer reject at df=7; remaining six single deletions still nominally reject. Thus even the conditional rejection is not robust to the printed standard uncertainty's precision or several source-case deletions. Do not select exclusions to claim a desired result.

**Source-method secondary diagnostic from original Appendix A, not new prereg primary gate:** 4 institutes exclusive GC-MS, 4 exclusive LC-MS/MS, HSA used both and published one **combined** result. Weighted GC-minus-LC mean difference **−0.0051388512 mg/g**, indicator `z=-1.189828`, ABS below predeclared diagnostic 3.0 rule. Apparatus family alone does not explain the pattern. No causal claim from instrument type labels or unverified calibration/error covariance.

**Reference diagnostic, not held-out prediction:** NIST (1.188±0.008 standard), KRISS (1.186±0.006), PTB (1.141±0.007) mg/g; Q=**29.05607996**, df=2, descriptive p≈**4.904×10⁻⁷** if uncertainties were truly independent and calibrated; however KRISS/PTB were selected after seeing discrepancy. KRISS's reported measurement of a **different** in-house control CRM agreed with its separate certified control value, but a control material is not automatically the exact test serum's independent truth. Across all twelve selected source rows, descriptive Q=**62.9971724**, not the primary prereg statistic; interpreting it as an exogenous truth-test would be illegitimate.

Appendix A3, original PDF p. 33 (and A2 pp. 31–32), names **NIST SRM 917c** among the reported calibrant sources for seven coded K11.2 institute method *records*: HSA, INMETRO, LNE, NMIJ, NIST, KRISS and PTB. Those include both GC and LC platforms and the solicited reference labs. VNIIM has an unresolved original **Appendix A2 (SRM 917a) versus A3 (SRM 917c) reported source-label discrepancy**, explicitly typed `CONFLICT_A2_917a_A3_917c` and **NOT** silently added to the firm '917c' census. The labels indicate shared *catalogue-level* referential ancestry but not demonstrated identical physical lot, identical purity uncertainty, batch handling or numerical covariance. Other K11.2 institute methods cite their own standard materials or older serum reference matrices. Also, HSA's two reported analytical routes produce a **single published lab result**, and must not be multiplied into independent physical experiments merely because its method ontology has two labels.

**Strongest mechanistic negative result:** a uniform additive common bias `b` applied to every reported institute shifts `μ` but leaves `Q` exactly unchanged: `Q(y_i+b)=Q(y_i)`. [Code test](../experiments/mqr-4.114/p2e_ccqm_locked_rival_falsification.rs) checks on these actual published summaries. Thus **a *single identical additive* common calibration shift alone cannot reconcile this reported between-lab scatter** under R0's other assumptions; nor can Q establish that any common shift exists at all, because `θ` and `b` are unseparable. More structured lab/family/lot-specific biases, uncertainty miscalibration, sample instability or correlated errors remain candidates requiring external evidence. This is conventional location invariance, not a novel MQR mathematics theorem.

The actual original report describes the original participants/reference discordance, the attempt to investigate contributors, the final **Track C** classification, and a **consensus KCRV 1.164 mg/g** from all 9 participants + 3 reference labs. **The KCRV is not an extra 13th independently measured channel**, and the same participants used to construct it cannot use it as an entirely independent external validation oracle. Our precision-weighted participant μ=1.1570 mg/g is not the source's unweighted consensus KCRV estimand: both are labelled properly and not mixed.

## Ontology and negative legacy of original MQR

The original UV–Vis logic-reduction question is now anchored in actual metrology: **same materially grounded chemical measurand -> separately documented instrument-method routes -> sometimes common calibration-source node -> source-published institute results -> data-derived consensus reference**. Counting each derived/GC/LC/result/KCRV node as a separate "physical truth source" manufactures evidence. A genuinely defensible graph requires edge types `SAME_MATERIAL_CLASS`, `PHYSICAL_METHOD_READOUT`, `SHARED_CALIBRANT_CATALOG`, `ACTUAL_CERTIFICATE_LOT_VERIFIED`, `RESULT_COMBINED_FROM_VIALS`, `REFERENCE_SOLICITED_POST_DISCORDANCE`, `CONSENSUS_DERIVED_FROM_PARTICIPANTS`, and explicit `INDEPENDENCE_UNVERIFIED`. EXPO/OBI already provide general ontologies; the MQR distinguishing test is claim-relative independent world-contact and measurable rival defeat, not new names.
