This record adds a **different uncertainty variable**: why the claimed physical event-matching receipts themselves require independent reliability and lineage checks.

## 1. A conditional impossibility fact: internal transcripts cannot decide a symmetric reference hypothesis

Let `H0` and `H1` be two fully specified external alignment hypotheses, and let `T` denote the joint internal transcript generated by any adaptive protocol whose choices depend only on previously seen internal messages and laboratory output. If

`P(T | H0, π) = P(T | H1, π)`

for every protocol `π` in the *admissible* family, then for any decision rule `d(T)` and prior 1/2–1/2, the maximal probability of correctly distinguishing the true alignment is **1/2**. Proof: `P(d(T)=H0 | H0)=p` and `P(d(T)=H1 | H1)=1-p` when both hypotheses give the same law; average `(p+1-p)/2=1/2`. Randomization does not help when conditioned-on seed transcript laws also coincide.

**Critical assumptions:** (i) the two hypotheses have identical distributions on all actually permitted internal/adaptive data, not just passive single-measurement marginals; (ii) the identity of a common material event is not already an observed bit of `T`; (iii) the prior is symmetric for the 1/2 figure; (iv) the inference is about the **external alignment** not the observable equivalence class. If an intervention changes the distribution differently under the two hypotheses, the premise fails and active discrimination may become possible. This is standard identifiability/data-processing reasoning, not a novel theorem.

This result is weaker than the metaphysical assertion that there are *no* shared physical events and stronger than mere numerical matching. It explicitly exposes where the needed evidence is being assumed.

## 2. Three different meanings of 'anchor'

A. **Externally granted anchor:** an outside verifier asserts that A-preparation `s` corresponds to B-preparation `t`. The old MQR41 result goes from two candidate maps to one, but does NOT explain how the agents learned the match.

B. **Physically engineered prospective anchor:** two labs construct a reproducible joint stimulus, then verify preparation identity, time alignment, channel polarity, environmental stability and noncommon error through separate receipts. If independently established, this can produce a likelihood ratio; the evidential result is *bounded by the tested interface*.

C. **Counterfeit/shared-error anchor:** the event is incorrectly paired, the timing protocol has systematic offsets, all witnesses inherit a common upstream calibration, or multiple displays are copies of one sample. More nominal 'replications' can create *artificial* certainty. A text label 'independent' does not measure independence.

The original MQR question is centered on **B**. The present code only models inference **conditional on B's reliability**; it does not itself engineer an authentic new physical anchor.

## 3. An exact rational likelihood witness (executed locally)

Two exhaustive residual maps of the earlier four-state model are now labelled `H0` (matching native readout polarity) and `H1` (inverted polarity), purely to keep arithmetic legible. A **matched** externally verified anchor record yields the binary result `z=1` for agreement. Condition on:
- fair prior `P(H0)=P(H1)=1/2`;
- conditional error rate `ε=1/5` that is known and **the same for both directions**;
- distinct evidence records are **conditionally independent given the true mapping**;
- `P(z=1|H0)=4/5`, `P(z=1|H1)=1/5`.

For n anchor records containing k matches,

`P(H0 | k,n) = (4/5)^k(1/5)^(n-k) / [(4/5)^k(1/5)^(n-k) + (1/5)^k(4/5)^(n-k)]`

`= 4^(2k-n)/(1+4^(2k-n))`, interpreted using ordinary nonzero odds for negative exponents.

| Input | P(H0 | data) | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| no physical anchor | 1/2 | internally undecidable |
| one credible matching anchor | 4/5 | a probabilistic preference, not certainty |
| three independent matching anchors | 64/65 | high posterior **conditional** on independent calibration |
| one matching anchor copied into five displayed records, but falsely treated as five independent anchors | reported 1024/1025 vs correct one-event 4/5 | pseudoreplication, same evidence reused |
| five consistently inverted observations from systematic measurement reversal | model would prefer H1 with 1024/1025, even if H0 is physically true | likelihood misspecification and biased bridge |

Executable Python `Fraction` code [mqr44_noisy_anchor_identifiability.py](../experiments/mqr-5.4/mqr44_noisy_anchor_identifiability.py) was executed in the local container in this research turn: seven exact assertions passed; results printed for zero, one, three and five records. **Zero actual physical anchoring events executed**, no CI of the whole repo claimed. Source [commit 5f8fec7](https://github.com/WhoSia/MQR/commit/5f8fec718f6c58b54db3bf0804e69c4de7748dd2).

The likelihood function's observable `z` already presupposes that someone has established **which events are jointly matched**. Without the relevant external physical procedure and audits, it is not a native observation accessible in the original unsolved translation setting. Consequently this Bayesian demonstration is an **epistemic ceiling check on a proposed bridge**, not a bootstrapping solution. A different prior, asymmetric errors, hidden correlation or unmodelled alternatives can change every numerical posterior.

## 4. What is and is not genuinely shared between possible sciences

- **Native operational regularity**: may be established independently without shared linguistic referents; structural isomorphism alone yields only *candidate* translation maps.
- **Shared material perturbation**: can supply new constraints, but its sharedness must be *learned and verified*, not assumed by typing the same action label in both models.
- **Evidence receipt**: calibration, clocks, independent witness channels, provenance and negative controls delimit authority. Same readout sequence is insufficient if all channels reuse one error lineage.
- **Hybrid interface**: a third experimentally produced practice may gradually define joint terms without any complete bijective translation of the native logics or theoretical ontologies; such a partial bridge can be useful and falsifiable.

The last is a **research hypothesis rooted in Kuhn's lexicon learning, Galison's trading zones, Harnad's grounding, active automata learning and sensor-network alignment**. A useful strong external analogy is sensor registration with gauge freedom (e.g. [Princeton seminar 2010](https://www.math.princeton.edu/events/sensor-registration-and-synchronisation-networks-2010-05-03t203005)); active automata-learning lower bounds / apartness and query models are existing algorithms ([Kruger et al. 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v217/kruger23a.html), [Vaandrager et al., L#](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.05419)). They don't establish that human scientists share native ontology. They ARE substantial novelty threats against naive identifiability claims.

## 5. One warranted answer and the hardest next problem

**Warranted conditional answer:** with a fixed menu of symmetric experiments and no genuinely cross-indexed evidence, exact alignment cannot be recovered. Credible joint interventions or measurements can favor one map if they distinguish rival predictions under established preparation, channel and provenance rules. Claims remain local to that interface and may need to reopen under a different experimental grammar.

**Still unresolved:** how can two communities *invent* the first verifiable shared intervention when they cannot already exchange operation names, common event identity, reset states, or clock units? A model that simply adds an alignment oracle has silently assumed a solution; a model that declares all understanding impossible has overreached. Possible next realistic research targets are material demonstrations of cross-coupled signals and historical trading-zone formation with time-stamped artifact construction.

**Stop/backtrack rule:** the toy already demonstrates the twofold ambiguity, noisy anchor limits and false replication. Do not endlessly vary `ε` or state count. Next unit must be either a materially sourced real-world co-construction, or a stronger mathematically specified **anchor-creation** interface that does not hide the target pairing in its inputs. No new global Harvest fragments, no MQR 5.5 promotion, no authority transferred from old donors.
