## 1. Exact source partition (and limits)

**Harvest source**: [Literature Harvest — Historical Borderlands of Science — Source Fragments (working)](https://app.notion.com/p/3fcef561cf92813491fde1b62254ab32), read directly in Notion. It explicitly distinguishes four *analyst axes*: E empirical standing, R responsiveness to research criticism, I institutional recognition, M explanatory/measurement coherence. These are not scientifically established universal coordinates, nor a monotone science/pseudoscience ladder. Examples include astrology's gradual institutional retreat (Aroney 2023; Vermij 2014), quasicrystal diffraction (Shechtman et al. 1984), historical chymistry (Newman and Principe 1998), plate tectonics (Vine and Matthews 1963), phrenology's failed cranial personality measure versus separate localization questions, and debated scientific standing of string theory. The Harvest source marks many historical claims as abstract-only or incomplete originals; retain those C1/C2/C3 limits rather than treating cases as independently re-adjudicated facts.

## 2. Five discriminations obscured by single-science-status stories

1. Empirical observation change ≠ the community recognizing it.
2. Local instrument correction ≠ change in chemical ontology.
3. A valid legacy question may survive a failed *specific measuring bridge*, without vindicating its previous answers (e.g. localization question vs cranial personality maps).
4. A long accepted explanatory programme can lose empirical standing without thereby retroactively becoming fraudulent or pseudo-scientific.
5. Robust normal-science instrumentation can reveal anomalies against a local model. An anomaly need not trigger a Kuhnian global crisis. Distinguish routine nuisance repair, extension of local effective model, reclassification of target kind, and broad disciplinary-matrix revision.

## 3. Optical experiment as an intervention-conditioned model comparison

Let y denote RAW detector intensities; Q be dark correction, calibration, reference division, spectral integration and logarithm map; h be sample history. A restricted chemical hypothesis H_C specifies c_j(pH,T,h) and species eps_j(lambda); finite bandpass H_B is convolution in **intensity/transmittance before log**, whereas spectral stray light H_S couples neighboring wavelength/intensity channels before log. In general
`-log10(Integral(w*T)) != Integral(w*(-log10 T))` and `Q_i Q_j != Q_j Q_i`.
Thus changed digital spectral pattern cannot be identified with changed chemical equilibria absent the measurement-operator genealogy.

A proposed *intervention-repair ladder*, **not** a universal demarcation law:
- D1 nuisance/control reconciliation: same chemical state supported, discrepancy removed by independently verified optical response changes.
- D2 local model extension: persistent physically controlled residual, orthogonal measurement favors extra species or additional interaction; chemical **scope** revised.
- D3 conceptual measurement redesign: old categories cannot define a stable operational target across declared feasible interventions, suggesting new observable, calibration target, or mode of individuation.
- D4 community-level disciplinary revision: requires separate historical evidence about exemplars, training, vocabulary and standards; **cannot be declared from one spectral anomaly**.

Each upward step has a defeater and does not imply progress in the E/I/R/M four axes simultaneously.

## 4. A candidate 'boundary of normal science' as a *question-relative* notion

For an established research practice N at time t, claim c, admissible intervention family U, and measurement model class K, define a tentative boundary region B where at least one of:
(A) no existing model in K, with pre-authorized nuisance repair constraints, fits independent evidence under U;
(B) otherwise-fitting models entail incompatible *future interventional* responses, but U lacks a separator;
(C) a valid separator exists but is institutionally or technologically infeasible;
(D) criterion for which discrepancy counts as evidence is contested among practitioners.

These are **distinct boundary types**, not a single frontier. B is a classificatory framework offered for criticism; neither necessary nor sufficient for a Kuhnian crisis. Institutional I must be studied separately. Criticism: selecting K and U endogenously permits self-sealing; explicit historical source, rival generation and independent negative controls are required. An unmeasurable 'B' should not be promoted to a real entity.

## 5. Strong counterargument to our own MQR

A practice's success in responding to anomalies can be *in part* enabled by idealizations, conventions and scientific exemplars, not solely by continuous fresh independent calibration. Requiring exhaustive original provenance and independently re-running every inferential step may erase the possibility of cumulative normal science, just as unconditional trust in established instruments can hide common-mode failure.

MQR should seek **proportionate, target-conditional warrant**: show which measurement/interpretation link is currently under challenge, which legacy warrants can still be relied on, what *feasible* experiment would change the contested claim, and what remains genuinely underdetermined. Do not confuse 'lack of retained raw files' with 'no scientific warrant at all'.
