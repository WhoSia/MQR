# MQR-4.119 P5 — Two-Level Judgment Audit and Continuous Handoff to 4.120

Date: 2026-10-17. This is a bounded conceptual construction, not empirical adjudication.

## Inherited question
P4 showed how an agreement about rubric compliance can erase a challenge to admissibility. P5 separates (J1) correctness conditional on a declared assessment regime from (J2) grounds for authorizing, disputing and revising that regime. The two are neither interchangeable nor jointly reducible to a vote.

## Typed audit contract
For each challenge preserve: case_id; claim; empirical_or_formal_witness; assumption_scope; J1_verdict; J1_reasons; court_id; admissibility_basis; authority_provenance; J2_objection; response_or_revision_path; dissent_record; unresolved_debt; reopening_trigger; authority_ceiling.
No J1 PASS may silently fill J2. J2 HOLD need not falsify observations in J1. No numerical aggregation into an omnibus legitimacy score.

## Countermodels
C1 STANDARD-CUSTODY: panel agrees an absorbance-derived concentration conforms to a calibration formula but reference custody is unverified. Computational J1 is conditional; traceability claim stays HOLD; a dissenter identifying missing custody is not overruled by panel majority.
C2 PEAK-DIAGNOSIS: dissent between interference and baseline drift can be resolved by a discriminating measurement. Record evidence-needed rather than automatically framing the dispute as institutional illegitimacy.
C3 EXCLUDED-QUESTION: court excludes a question about excluded standards under its own rules. That fact is relevant to J2; the objection must be heard under an independently contestable escalation path, not awarded truth by virtue of dissent.
C4 SELF-AUTHORIZATION: an appeals panel certifies its own mandate by its internal majority. This is circular absent external delegated authorization or independent review; endless appellate recursion is also not presumed required.
