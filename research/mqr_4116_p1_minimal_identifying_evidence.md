## Finite model

Exhaustively examining subsets of these three features yields the unique inclusion-minimal identifying set {cert, defeated}. Importantly this is minimal **relative to the four worlds, three fixed fields, and specified verdict policy**. It is not a universal minimal experiment design, nor evidence that a real independent certificate can be acquired or is sufficient in metrological/social contexts.

## Research implication
A new citation, duplicate source description or nominal independent paper count is not necessarily a separating observation. A credible source-root witness without a defeater check may be insufficient. Information value must be measured relative to the *remaining competing evidence worlds and the claim being adjudicated*, not as file-count growth.
