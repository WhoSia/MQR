# MQR 42 · 기록 02 — Research director's next-stage gates

**MQR 5.4 central question:** How can agents whose primitive preparations, operators, and inferential languages do not match discover an actionable shared experiment while remaining answerable to one world? This is stronger than aligning publication data fields or calibrating one already-shared instrument.

## Gate Q — repeatable correction of actual digitizer signals
[Šíra 2023 exact raw archive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/178ypBIq_G460hGi7oHoweTBfNfb5qmK_/view), 4×3.42 million samples, 60kSa/s, 24-bit PXI-5922. Reconstruct `TWM-QPSW` segmentation from `qwtb.info` arrays `M`, `S`, `Uref`, identify ground switching times and digitizer correction file. Compare raw vs corrected voltage only when matching source and stable reference are confirmed. Identify whether independent differential PJVS vs QuantumPower measurements are truly paired or simply sequential. Preserve raw reference, corrected waveform and uncertainty separately. **Fail-closed** if any mapping stage is missing; do not reverse-engineer a 'quantum accuracy' number from raw 0.813V alone.

## Gate O — physically validated reference in optical preparation
[PTB 2025 graph data](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iE96PNpjXKVqROx1GXprQ7N7D42KOabF/view) gives a hypothesis of near-linear superposition with a constant offset but no independent source for that offset. Record physical blocked readings for *each channel*, positive/negative/full timing and comparison setting. Pre-register an offset correction from independent blocked trials, not the same signal closure being tested. Probe whether error follows channel, source, or software-processing chain under permutation and replication. The actual Malus/LEGO [App source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dmiuP_h92Qv0PMvoZKLLrkJjk-rmhkgh/view) and [CAD model](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Bz6_KSRf5nzB4P34lmn134uIRoamOa2l/view) support low-risk pilot planning; but AB/BA order reversal must be actually implemented, not inferred from an angular sweep.

## Gate C — new concepts before source measurements
Clement (1988) held original supports a test that model-construction/revision may precede novel empirical acquisition. Construct a pre-data theory family with multiple observationally equivalent explanatory models, classify which *prospective* interventions each invents, score against physical-world outcomes when samples exist. Without available data, keep hypothesis status provisional. The MQR goal is generating a test menu that distinguishes different preparation mappings, not just counting partitions of a pre-given state space.

## Gate H — historical plurality without anachronism
New [Rorty 1984](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jFjhCBBvTNVt1EoDXW8zNmJLQCvXyWT9/view) distinguishes historical from rational reconstruction. Use this to prevent representing retrospective analytic variables as period actors' own primitive notions; new [Timmenga 2024 image scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hrd4TzGJmkYQlW8WQANAobSTB6JniAB8/view) remains full-argument reading HOLD. Lower-priority exact first editions are only sought when a concrete thesis turns on original wording or archival form.

**Budget allocation:** 50% Q+O raw-data/references, 30% cross-science concept formation and identification, 15% historiography/institutional incentives, 5% precise archival-edition acquisition. This is a research-director scheduling recommendation, not a quantified retrospective allocation. Three stopping rules: do not claim calibrated accuracy from uncorrected samples, do not claim physical co-preparation from matching time-grid exports, do not claim an unseen document's contents.
