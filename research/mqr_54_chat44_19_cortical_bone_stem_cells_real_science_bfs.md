# MQR 5.4 — MQR44 R19: move beyond proteins — measured cortical bone fracture and cross-field goal mismatch

Date 2026-11-21 KST. This is a **BFS return to engineering and the theory of scientific explanation**, not an attempt to force different evidence types into a single progress score.

## Primary biomechanical engineering comparator

Anna Gustafsson, Mathias Wallin and Hanna Isaksson (2020), 'The influence of microstructure on crack propagation in cortical bone at the mesoscale', *Journal of Biomechanics* **112:110020**, DOI [10.1016/j.jbiomech.2020.110020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2020.110020), original [PubMed source and abstract PMID 32980752](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32980752/), published online Sept 1 2020.

The actual study targets **crack propagation paths relative to osteon orientation** in cortical bone, rather than generic 'more fine-grained is always more true.' It constructs high-resolution micro-CT tissue orientation maps, introduces weak interface distribution/orientation in image-based meso models, and combines extended finite element modelling with interface damage. The authors report that their computed crack paths reproduce broad experimental trends—cracks perpendicular to osteon orientation more irregular than parallel—and that efficient image-based meso representation better captures the experimentally relevant structural heterogeneity than previous small idealized osteon domains.

**Limits:** We read the authors' full PubMed abstract and bibliographic original, NOT their full figures, numerical mesh, original micro-CT volumes, raw crack-path coordinates or a recreation of the solver. The article demonstrates a domain-specific modelling/experiment comparison as **reported by the experimenters**, not independently benchmarked superiority of mesoscale models over every molecular or continuum alternative. It illustrates a **task-target selection and computational resource problem**, not a proof that 'microphysics fails' or that complex tissue violates fundamental physics.

This case is distinct from the philosophical interpretation in [Batterman & Green 2021, 'Steel and bone'](https://researchprofiles.ku.dk/en/publications/steel-and-bone-mesoscale-modeling-and-middle-out-strategies-in-ph/) (published 2021). The latter gives a broader argument for middle-out representation; Gustafsson et al. offers a named engineering study with experimental trend comparison. Whether Batterman/Green specifically analyze *this exact paper* has not been established.

## Stem-cell case: conflict of goals is not contradiction of measurements

Melinda Bonnie Fagan (2016), 'Stem Cells and Systems Models: Clashing Views of Explanation', *Synthese* **193(3):873–907**, DOI [10.1007/s11229-015-0776-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-015-0776-3), source [PhilPapers abstract and bibliography](https://philpapers.org/rec/FAGSCA). The author's abstract explicitly reports a failed interaction between systems-model-oriented theoretical biologists and mechanism-oriented experimental stem-cell practitioners. It also states a proposal to **reconcile** the two explanatory approaches, so do not quote it as evidence that their integration is intrinsically impossible. We did not read the complete 2016 article or contemporaneous lab emails; their raw accounts remain outside the present empirical receipt.

The difference versus Rinaldelli (2014) is a genuine **type distinction**:
- Protein X-ray/NMR: both methods may aim to describe the same atomic protein model but observe *different physical regimes*, leading to a potential **empirical restriction** on a single model.
- Bone meso modelling: researchers specify a *macroscopic crack-path prediction target* for which physical meso features are relevant; choice of scale and numerical feasibility are key, and a real experimental trend comparison exists in the published report.
- Stem-cell model collaboration: failure may arise in what counts as a satisfactory **explanation** (lawlike/general dynamics versus manipulable mechanisms) even when no side has demonstrated the other's laboratory reading false.

## What might constitute the eventual ambitious falsifying comparison

Require typed questions and explicit observations:
A. **Shared physical target and incompatible data**: identical declared protein, distinct solid/solution context; independent NMR/X-ray restraints make an unexpected disagreement informative.
B. **Different mathematical scale and physical target score**: crack path, osteon orientation and model-resolution/cost; measure prediction and execution resources rather than abstract 'microscopic truth.'
C. **Different epistemic goal**: mechanistic intervention versus dynamical law; require documented community practices, not fabricated success metrics.

An MQR 'paradigm of paradigms' is not a universal hierarchy of integration. Its meaningful contribution could be a comparative protocol identifying **why and when a community makes formerly peripheral scientific questions central**, without privileging one extant research tradition's criteria by fiat. That is a serious historical/philosophical synthesis rather than a new mathematical theorem.
