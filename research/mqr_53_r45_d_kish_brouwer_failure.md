# R45-D — Kish Bar-On: non-adoption is not the same as non-contribution

**Source:** [Kish Bar-On (2022), *From Philosophical Traditions to Scientific Developments: Reconsidering the Response to Brouwer's Intuitionism*, Synthese 200:521](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Qp1YwuFje-6Ei4eNVWxNEdECqZSdyF10/view), publication PDF; narrative, comparison to Friedman and Fraenkel/Weyl sections read.

Brouwer's intuitionism did not replace classical mathematics. Kish Bar-On contrasts Friedman's reconstruction of Einstein's transition (rich preexisting philosophical continuity) with Brouwer's foundational project, which did not enjoy a comparable accepted tradition in the community. Weyl and Fraenkel's reception illustrates differences among investigating, adopting, disseminating and ultimately declining a foundational alternative.
