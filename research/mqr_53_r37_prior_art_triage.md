# MQR-5.3 R37 — Prior-art triage (2026-11-04)

Closer reading of Steinle (1997), Rheinberger (1997), and Nersessian (2008) shows that exploratory experimentation, changing experimental objects, and modeling-based conceptual innovation are already substantive philosophical research programs.

Further competitors: Feest and Steinle (2012), Schickore (2016), Haueis (2023), and Feest (2025). The generic novelty proposals are withdrawn. The remaining research question about how prior observations retain or lose authority across conceptual revision remains unproven as an original contribution.

This is a critical literature review, not a new theory. Detailed historical source comparisons and prior-art references are recorded in the canonical MQR-5.3 Notion research page. No original scientific experiment was conducted.
