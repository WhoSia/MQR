## B. Important conflict that should NOT be patched away

The contact-isomorphism model assumes identical graph-theoretic operational access. Yet the *same physical states* can be acted on under C4 relations in one lab and under path P4 relations in the other. An isomorphism-finder would report zero. It is a **counterexample to an overstrong modeling assumption**, not evidence against one world.

This conflict is a productive reason to **stop treating graph alignment as the ultimate research goal**. The next model must separately represent native action *grammars*, feasible instrument capabilities, and the capacity to create/combine operations. Relations between sciences are often partial, many-to-one and context-dependent; bijections are a special case. If 5.4 continues to produce only different matching scores under a fixed graph, the research has drifted again.

## D. Directional choice

**Primary DFS next:** abandon the assumption that cross-lab structural invariance is freely available. Construct a pair of agents with the *same underlying four physical states* and different feasible action grammars; permit a minimal authentic physical transport event. Ask how the exchange leads one agent to **invent an action that changes its discrimination partition or operation composition**. Compare null variants with no transfer, transfer without stability, and matching table-labels only. This shifts the target from “which mapping?” to “what new question can now be asked?”.

**Secondary:** independently replay QWTB on one source run only if the exact original algorithm and calibration input are in hand; no invented uncertainty interval. In the meantime the data may serve as a cautionary example of numeric-vs-operational meaning, not a flagship “AI discovered quantum discrepancy” story.

## E. Honest novelty filter

Mathematical graph automorphism, query partition entropy, the idea that experiments and concepts co-evolve, and the separation of prediction from explanatory identity have substantial prior art. The credible research contribution at present is an **integrated, source-auditable research architecture and negative/positive test harness** for cross-science contact. General originality must be evaluated against established philosophy of science, model-based science, category/measurement equivalence, causal identifiability, active learning, and scientific concept formation **after** a substantive new action-generation result exists.

**Operational acceptance:** every next cycle needs at least one actual change in admissible experiment, a new falsifying counterexample, a proof under clearly narrower assumptions, or an external source-check that resolves a material disagreement. Do not confuse 15/15 tests or a large commit count with scientific discovery. Keep [17 precise source groups](MQR_LIVE_MISSING_SOURCES_REGISTRY.md) live but deprioritize 13 archival editions, and do not pretend A3/A4 are extant downloadable archives.
