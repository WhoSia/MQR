## 1. Direct sources and the non-novelty discovery

**Margaret Masterman (1970), *The Nature of a Paradigm***, directly read from [the held PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dqLn5hrvxS29QTZC_2qzrYEfE8SpohBf/view), especially printed pp.63–66 (the file's source text has occasional scan/OCR errors). She famously discusses **21 senses** of Kuhn's "paradigm" grouped into **metaphysical/metaparadigms**, **sociological paradigms**, and **artefact/construct paradigms**. An exemplar can organize the practices of a scientific community even **before** fully explicit theory. Thus the word *metaparadigms* and the distinction between abstract ontology, communal habits, and concrete puzzle devices were already in 1970. The MQR 5.4 "paradigm of paradigms" is an authentic founder research problem but not a claim to have coined this term or the threefold distinction.

**Michael Friedman (2001), *Dynamics of Reason***, directly read from [the held complete book PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JAnxiRCtjaDEeTI_ZG1iLaCyLqKVmsNt/view), with relevant original text in *The Idea of a Scientific Philosophy* around printed pp.22–24 and *Historical Perspectives on the Stratification of Knowledge* around pp.30–32. The publisher [CSLI bibliography](https://web.stanford.edu/group/cslipublications/cslipublications/site/1575862921.shtml) confirms the source. Friedman uses **meta-frameworks/meta-paradigms** to describe philosophical resources that make revolutionary transitions communicatively rational across changes in a scientific framework, against both a fixed Kantian a priori and undifferentiated empiricist/Quinean holism. In his reconstruction of Reichenbach (1920), "necessary for constituting scientific objecthood within a given theory" need not mean "unchangeable across all future theories." A constitutive/relativized a priori may be historically replaced. Friedman explicitly cites the availability of Lorentz-FitzGerald aether theory as a conceptually distinct but *in an important sense* empirically/mathematically equivalent rival of Einstein's 1905 theory: **his own original argument already reaches the physical case of record21.** It would be inaccurate for MQR to call such a comparison a new philosophical theorem.

**Peter Galison (1999), "Trading Zone: Coordinating Action and Belief"**, directly read from [the held anthology chapter](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-/view), whose original chapter text begins around printed p.137 and develops the **trading zone / locality of exchange** around pp.148 ff. Galison explicitly rejects an empirical-observation protocol language shared across all sciences and **also rejects total fragmentation**: different finite traditions of theory, experiments, instrumentation and engineering may retain different dynamics and meanings while still coordinating particular exchanges. The primary text uses the analogy of local contact languages/pidgins/creoles. An author's [Harvard publication listing](https://galison.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/trading-zone-coordinating-action-and-belief-1998-abridgment) also exists but labels its edition **1998 abridgment**: it must not be silently substituted for the held 1999 anthology version.

## 2. Three distinct explanatory targets, not one magic "meta" concept

| Historian/philosopher | Target of explanation | What it permits | What it does not entail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masterman | **How a puzzle-solving practice is sustained** in a community and by concrete exemplars | Non-propositional skills/artifacts shape what counts as a scientific question; no exhaustive explicit formal theory needed | Any paradigm is interchangeable with any other, or that the 21 senses are all distinct scientific kinds |
| Friedman | **How radical successive frameworks remain rationally discussable** | Constitutive principles and philosophical meta-discourse can themselves change while mediating transitions | A universally fixed superior meta-framework or a proven necessary role of one historical philosopher in every scientific revolution |
| Galison | **How partially different contemporaneous traditions coordinate without globally homogeneous meanings** | Local technical transactions may succeed without complete ontology translation | Complete cross-tradition semantic equivalence, or that every local contact is empirically reliable |

The difference between **successive theory revolutions** (Friedman), **how ongoing puzzle practices operate** (Masterman), and **coexisting differently specialized practices** (Galison) matters. It would be false to claim Galison and Friedman are strictly contradictory accounts of the same unit of analysis. A common MQR model must show exactly which phenomena each explains, rather than dissolve them into another indiscriminate "Court" score.

## 3. Three-way prior-art challenge to MQR's recent tendency

**Against the "new sensor bits" threshold:** Masterman's construct exemplars and the [Lakatos mathematical counterexample from record20](mqr_54_chat43_20_masterman_poincare_lakatos_question_genesis.md) show that making a question newly salient to a community may not introduce previously inexpressible formal symbols or a newly measured observable. Mere computable derivation is not sufficient to dismiss conceptual invention.

**Against pure epistemic relativism:** Friedman argues why changes of constitutive scientific principles need not destroy possibilities for rational mediation. Galison likewise explains practical coordination, even when global semantic unification fails. This does not alone certify the physical truth of the resulting picture. A world-resistance constraint has work to do, but should not swallow the philosophy of invention.

**Against "we invented meta-paradigms":** both Masterman (1970) and Friedman (2001) use this actual vocabulary, and the latter already highlights Lorentz–Einstein equivalence and the conceptual change in simultaneity. Any thesis that simply restates "new paradigms open new questions" has formidable prior art. We should not proliferate acronyms to hide it.

## 4. The sharper physically grounded question

**C1 (algebraically proved in record21):** Galilean boosts preserve absolute simultaneity, whereas Lorentz boosts preserve the Minkowski interval. This shows *different invariant structures*, not that the physicist using them has an irreducibly new mathematical language.

**C2 (historically nuanced):** comparing Newtonian/Galilean physics with special relativity is **not identical** to comparing historically Lorentzian aether theory with Einstein's interpretation; Lorentz employed Lorentz transformations. Robust historical research must distinguish (i) different physical predictions, (ii) the same predictions with alternative ontology/explanation, and (iii) differences only in coordinate conventions. See [SEP "Space and Time: Inertial Frames"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/spacetime-iframes/) and the opposed journal arguments [empirical equivalence (2014)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1355219814000124) versus [non-equivalence (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1093/BJPS/AXZ014). We have inspected their publisher summaries; we have **not** line-by-line audited both complete papers.

**C3 (MQR candidate):** A theory may transform the physically privileged **invariants, admissible questions, and puzzle techniques** without thereby becoming empirically distinguishable from every rival conception of the same data. The evidence for **real new question-generating practice** is the specific emergence of consequential experiments, calculations, or explanations—not a count of renamed predicates.

## 5. Next DFS and boundary

To eliminate residual dependence on convention-sensitive distant simultaneity, the next physical subproblem should compare the **same two reunion events along different timelike worldlines**. Special relativity predicts different ideal-clock proper times along different paths, whereas Newtonian ideal absolute time is path-independent. A simple rational exact calculation can be reproduced without any imagined clock measurement, while actual testing would require controlled clocks and trajectory details. This is stronger conceptual/evidential differentiation than treating every re-synchronization convention as a new physics.

**Research question generated:** Which historically successful practice made "compare physically recorded times on different paths linking the same events" a discriminating scientific question, and how can *locally shared experimental outcomes* support rational disagreement over the deeper ontology of space/time? This connects Masterman (exemplars), Friedman (changing constitutive commitments), Galison (partial local coordination) and the world as a defeater, without asserting a universal novelty theorem.
