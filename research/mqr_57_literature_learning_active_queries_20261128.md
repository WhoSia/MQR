## Consequence for causal abstraction
For earlier MQR weak macro model, macro A contains a,b with next B probabilities 1/4 and 3/4. Given independently randomized micro lift weight q on a, macro A reset leads to probability 3/4−q/2. The previous observational uniform mixture q=1/2 yields 1/2 exactly. Using absolute Bernoulli probability difference (equal to TV), worst-case error of a single macro prediction 1/2 across q∈[0,1] is 1/4, minimax by midpoint. If q only lies in [l,h], attainable range [3/4−h/2,3/4−l/2], minimax radius (h−l)/4. This is a simple application of the literature's approximation and projection perspective to our old constructed example, not a verification of the full projected-abstraction theorem or a determination of real-world q. Rather than discard the macro variable, specify the lift policy or retain partial identification of its effect. Counterfactual joint queries require additional joint-policy/context information. Next reproduce actual author methods on their data and assess causal feature learning, stochastic interventions, and transport rather than indefinitely enlarging synthetic tests.
