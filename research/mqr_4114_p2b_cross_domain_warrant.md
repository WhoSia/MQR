## Exact structural contrast: classic, not novel mathematics

Constructed physical rivals W1=(true quantity 1, shared bias 0) and W2=(true quantity 0, shared bias 1); two same-ancestry readouts both equal 1 in both W1 and W2. A 'third instrument' whose output is a function of these shared readouts does not split the observational equivalence. A genuinely externally witnessed reference reading *true quantity alone* distinguishes W1 and W2, **only if its independence and uncertainty are real and separately verified**.

Constructed SST rivals have baseline observations with Boolean perceived horizon H equal to Boolean age A. Model 1 sets goal G=H; model 2 sets G=A. At baseline both fit every observed pair. Under a hypothetical valid perturbation changing H without changing A, the rivals predict different G. This is a textbook interventional identifiability distinction, **not** proof that a survey or hypothetical horizon prompt actually performed do(H) or identified mediation. The published SST literature offers external motivation, while the code remains a toy.

Formal general result (pre-existing mathematical logic): for any observation f:W->O and deterministic derived channel q:O->R, if f(w1)=f(w2) then (f(w1),q(f(w1)))=(f(w2),q(f(w2))); duplicating or postprocessing observations cannot resolve a pre-existing observational equivalence cell. A new external reference r:W->R can separate a pair only if r(w1) != r(w2). In a stochastic setting, Blackwell/Le Cam comparison still cannot by itself prove independent physical ancestry.
