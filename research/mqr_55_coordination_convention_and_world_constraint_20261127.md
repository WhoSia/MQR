## Narrow discriminator
Two agents have private binary action labels; the analyst encodes them a,b in {0,1}. Unknown orientation m in {0,1}. Each agent can recognize own success, with feedback r=1 iff a xor b=m. No translation table shared. Stipulate A holds its action and B tries its other option if first feedback fails. All eight combinations m,a,b_initial reach r=1 in at most two trials. This uses an externally specified role, reliable common-interest feedback, stable local alternatives and trial boundaries; not discovery of the roles or communication physics. It is trivial coordination search, not a new learning algorithm.
Now define two candidate worlds h=0,1 and environment context c=0,1. Target success y=1 iff a xor b=m xor (h AND c). Coordination feedback r is unchanged across contexts and independent of h. At c=0, y=r in both worlds. For ANY adaptive history based exclusively on r, the observations are identical across h, since transition/feedback laws are identical; randomization with identical seed does not help. Freeze the coordinated action pair, vary c to 1 and independently measure y: h=0 yields y=1; h=1 yields y=0 despite r=1. The discriminating test requires actual physical access to c and an independently warranted target y; the model stipulates both, so it does not solve their genesis. A task requiring only r has no loss; the distinction matters only if y is a relevant external target. Same coordination success can coexist with different world-facing success.

## Macro return
Distinguish (a) emergence of a usable contact convention, (b) establishment of stable material co-reference and target-sensitive evidence, (c) new joint ability to ask/test questions. (a) can enable (b)/(c) but does not entail either. Even independently observed target y could be a shared proxy; independent physical grounding remains an additional task. This refines the prior contact problem rather than replacing it with a universal communication game. Conversely agreement can be scientifically useful without shared ontology; do not over-demand full translation. Relevant historical investigation: reconstruct which shared artifacts, independently checked outcomes, and new tasks appeared in actual cooperation, including failures and power asymmetry. Galison and Star already explain local coordination, Young convention dynamics. No originality for that general distinction. The 5.5 comparative research question is whether source-dated changes in shared empirical capabilities can discriminate coordination-only accounts from world-facing concept generation.
