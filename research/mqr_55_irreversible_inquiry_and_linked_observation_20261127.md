## Partial linkage theorem and finite verification
For a closed finite cohort of N units, exact binary marginal positive counts A,B and correctly matched subset 2x2 counts q00,q01,q10,q11, define m=N-sum(q), a=A-q10-q11, b=B-q01-q11. Require 0<=a,b<=m. Let C be joint-positive total. Sharp integer bounds: q11+max(0,a+b-m)<=C<=q11+min(a,b). Necessity and sufficiency: unlinked table entries u11=c, u10=a-c, u01=b-c, u00=m-a-b+c are nonnegative integers precisely within interval. Covariance sign is sign(N*C-A*B). Actual linkage values, not number of matches alone, can determine sign. For N=8,A=B=4, five linked cells with q=(2,0,0,3) yield C in [3,4], positive covariance; q=(0,2,3,0) yields [0,1], negative. Four linked cells may leave zero feasible. This is finite residual Frechet bounds, no novelty claim; not the Live-seq data. Same-cohort exact marginals, stable target and linkage are indispensable; no MAR assumption needed for this deterministic partial-ID result, but selection prevents naive generalization of observed pairs. Code paired_observation_bounds.py independent exhaustive enumeration N=1..7, 5147 distinct feasible marginal/paired tables; all sharp bounds matched and 2990 strictly signed cases (no marginal-only strict sign). Count over table configurations, not scientific probabilities. Local replay blob matches aeac7d5a825201ed881a7b41b877f7c1688e9795.
