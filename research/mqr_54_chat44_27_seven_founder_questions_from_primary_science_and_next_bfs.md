# MQR 5.4 · MQR 44 · R27 — Return to the seven founding questions from real experimental science

**2026-11-21 KST.** Director's research synthesis; neither a new truth metric nor MQR 5.5 proposal. This is an intentionally NON-Real-Lang-centered return from R23–R26. We discovered and checked existing physical biochemistry and NMR/X-ray research and a source-specific replication route. Philosophical novelty is **HOLD** because much of the substantive mechanism was already formulated by working scientists and prior philosophy.

## 1. Measurement and the real world's constraints

The 2014 [Rinaldelli original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4306559/) and 2019 [Carlon original](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10858-018-0212-3) demonstrate why two differently prepared instruments measuring apparently 'one structure' do not automatically have identical estimands. Crystal packing and solution dynamics can be genuine environmental differences; differences in RDC alignment media and model geometry can make *apparent* discrepancies. The new question must be *which one*, not 'how to force a match'. Original physical data would test model alternatives; scientific realism cannot be inferred from cross-model agreement by itself.

## 2. Possible alien/differently organized sciences

X-ray crystallography and solution NMR are **not alien scientific civilizations with no shared physics**; they already share chemistry, atomic structure, mathematics, published protein sequence and instrumentation infrastructures. They are a deliberately **bounded analog** for a harder problem, illustrating how locally distinct data/operations generate an imperfect bridge that can be criticized. This cannot prove global translatability for arbitrary possible sciences. Strongest unresolved question: how to produce the first physically defensible *matched event/sample* when neither practice already accepts the other's reference system (R07 no-go still stands).

## 3. Paradigm genesis and changing inquiry norms

Scientific progress may reframe the target of an experiment from 'fit two datasets with one structure' to 'explain the residual and characterize conformational conditions', but in the studied case the idea predates the 2014 paper (GB3 and CaM primary work in 2003/2009). This is **an example of ongoing technique development**, not evidence of a historically *first* paradigm revolution. Establishing a genuine history of genesis needs earlier documents, failed trial changes, who initiated new measurements and independent contemporary witnesses; the fact of later publication alone is insufficient.

## 4. Where new meaningful questions come from

The 2019 paper directly compares (i) a new tensor restriction representing physically shared orientation but differing sample alignment magnitude and (ii) a less constrained empirical scale correction. These make a **specific** new empirical question actionable: does the residual signal reflect concentration/preparation or actual interdomain freedom? A stronger discovery claim would require an independent perturbation of alignment-medium concentration with measured tensor magnitudes and protected common-orientation controls, or independent spectroscopy of domain motion. The fact that a question is mathematically expressible does not mean researchers can materially measure it.

## 5. Why mathematics works in engineering and medicine

Mathematical parameter constraints may be effective when they encode **tested physical invariances**, e.g. a shared orientation in a rigid subunit complex with potentially different alignment magnitudes, and are confronted with orthogonal measurement evidence. But a good optimized score cannot certify a correct ontological interpretation; a different tensor constraint may trade false fit for bias. 2019 Table 1 gives (R_free .2362 to .2360, RDC Q .131 to .121) when a new constraint is applied, **not** a law that all physical mathematical constraints improve accuracy. Model use is purpose-, scale-, and preparation-dependent.

## 6. Same external world, nonidentical theories

A shared material protein can generate compatible observations for compact protein systems, incompatible observations for structurally flexible targets and illusory disagreement from sensor/preparation differences. An admissible scientific bridge should preserve the strongest **negative results** as data: do not discard CaM as 'failed', merge all datasets into a scalar or retroactively assume all biological preparations identical. Distinguish condition-specific real asymmetry from instrument artifact and still permit epistemic disagreement.

## 7. Limits and counterexamples

Strong rivals already exist: [Mitchell 2020](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LnEhdfgvcOQtIwGmL1arFveInkE2AZXm/view) argues perspectival integration with X-ray/NMR protein cases; [Deulofeu & Suárez 2023](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bgd9vw84e3tZ80MEGBPmonjg0xUk-Kb_/view) question when integration is desirable; [Galison 1999](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-/view) analyzes local coordination; [Weiskopf 2025](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2025.14) describes evidence-making without a new physical device. MQR cannot claim it first identified these possibilities.

**Research director's hard limit:** do not let the original cross-science ontology ambition become an endless REFMAC data-cleaning project. The empirical sample is a **testbed** for general questions, not the entire theory.
