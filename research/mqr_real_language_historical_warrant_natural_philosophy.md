## 2. Six live axes of MQR natural-philosophical attack

### A. Representation versus measurement

A vector called “observation” may be numerical output from a sophisticated pipeline, not an operation that is empirically grounded as a measure of the target. In Real-Language there are now three separate questions: is a field declared observable, does the mathematical target factor through it in the finite declared histories, and is the field physically justified as measuring that target? The v0.42 compiler checks only the second after the first is declared. **The third is not compiled into PASS.**

**Rival:** van Fraassen can accept empirical adequacy while declining MQR's realist extrapolation. A future real matched operation–quantity comparison must distinguish what, if anything, realism licenses over and above empirical adequacy.

### B. Calibration genealogy versus coincident numerical agreement

Two downstream instruments or models can agree while sharing exactly the same compromised reference. This has been studied extensively in metrology and is NOT a novel counterexample. Real-Language v0.42 shows a finite `common-root` ambiguity; a differently named probe remains structurally conditional until calibration ancestry and uncertainty dependence are evidenced.

**Rival:** Tal does not require a simple strict “independent standard” condition for every form of calibration. MQR must avoid imposing one particular calibration network as a universal philosophical prerequisite.

### C. Selection as an information-destroying map

Write measured candidate data (X), eligibility/measurement history (S), and released record (Y=g_S(X)). Distinct ((X,S)) histories can share identical (Y); the target historical property may not factor through (Y). **Causal identification of selection** requires more than a missing count and publication-age delta. Existing **actual FER data** demonstrate selection-conditioned posterior sensitivity only on the **public** 49+49 selected grain responses; full 2015/2021 author FER1/FER3 original records remain unavailable.

**Rival:** the original 2021 paper's full-age difference could also involve model specification, dose-rate covariance, coding vintage, or historical cohort lineage, and the actual causal contrast is currently unidentified. The joint-ancestry v0.42 fixture deliberately makes a *full-roster-only* explanation fail.

### D. Irreversibility and the physical past

A measurement intervention can alter the property or object being measured. A post-experimental signal cannot by itself reconstruct a unique pre-intervention state if two physically possible prior histories converge. This is classic many-to-one inverse inference logic, not an MQR universal metaphysical impossibility theorem. A preserved external independent pre-state trace might yet resolve it.

**Rival:** a physics model with constraints or ancillary observations could identify pre-state even if a particular baseline record was destroyed. Therefore `DESTROYED` is a **declared available-evidence constraint** in the current finite history court, not absolute omniscient inaccessibility.

### E. Fallible standards and selective corroboration

The same scientific claim may be measured, certified and evaluated using labels from the same imperfect apparatus. Real-Language's earlier v0.41 candidate already prevents self-declared external truth certificates from promoting real world authority. MQR-4.101–4.103 used actual 487-beat QTDB-derived data to distinguish annotator disagreement from genuine truth verification. **New Real-Language 0.42 now separately checks whether different experiment histories yield the same visible assessment**, without assuming complete missing-at-random validation.

**Rival:** fully validated independently sampled targets with known selection mechanism may support particular identification even without a complete object-by-object historical registry.

### F. Finite formal sufficiency versus world-generated rival expansion

Classic finite factorization (Q=f\circ O) is a *conditional* logical statement about a **declared** (H). It is not equivalent to `H` being complete, independent or representative. Multiple possible-world generators can share a hidden background, even if the visible labels differ. This is why Real-Language's default `world_authority=HOLD` applies both when internal factorization FAILs and when it PASSes. Research must seek *counterinstances to the currently accepted projection*, not endless restatement of formally decidable closure.

**Rival:** a sufficiently constrained, justified physical theory could reduce the admissible histories, but those constraints require independent world and theoretical warrant outside the finite parser.
