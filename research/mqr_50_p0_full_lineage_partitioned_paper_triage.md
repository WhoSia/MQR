## Manuscript A draft thesis (working, subject to falsification)

A scientific claim's experimental authority depends on **which relations are preserved** when a result is compressed, simulated by another statistical experiment, re-calibrated, selected into a dataset, or transported across a physical apparatus. Equal reported readouts, equal likelihoods under restricted conditions, and even statistically equivalent channels do not by themselves justify claim identity. A defensible paper must identify (i) the precise equivalence claim; (ii) transformations it permits; (iii) independent evidence for calibration/source/selection/intervention correspondence; and (iv) one possible falsifier or observed failure at each transition. This diagnostic schema must be compared against existing philosophy-of-measurement frameworks rather than marketed as unprecedented.
