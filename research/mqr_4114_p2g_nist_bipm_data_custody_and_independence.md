## 2. NIST SRM 917c: verified chemical-reference documentation, not participant-specific custody

[NIST archived 917c Certificate of Analysis](https://tsapps.nist.gov/srmext/certificates/archives/917c.pdf), 2-page original institutional document directly inspected. Reports certified D-glucose mass fraction **99.7% ±0.3 percentage points expanded, k=2**, current downloadable certificate *revision* **2020-06-22**, expiration **2026-05-01** conditional on intact material and other stated requirements. Certificate revision history: **2009-06-30 original**, **2013-11-21 extended**, **2016-02-10 editorial**, **2020-06-22 validity update**. NIST archive [catalog listing](https://shop.nist.gov/ccrz__CCPage?pageKey=SRMArchive) shows **latest archived issued revision**, not the original 2009 or 2013 certificate text that every study participant actually held. The archive explicitly routes **other certificate requests** to `srmdocuments@nist.gov`. [NIST SRM FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/srm/faqs) clarifies **separate lot number is NOT always assigned**: if CoA has no additional serial/lot, `SRM 917c` and physical label match may be valid material identity. Avoid asking for a non-existent mandatory serial number.

**What could be shared:** an institution citing the same `SRM 917c` **nominal certified purity and source document** may share a metrological *reference-number ancestor*. This claim requires validation against its then-used CoA, material identifier, and uncertainty sensitivities; nothing in the catalog label alone determines participant-to-participant `Cov(e_i,e_j)`, the common component size, the sign of sensitivity, stock-preparation differences, or the *actual* physical shared bottle. `SRM_917c` in Appendix A is `COMMON_CATALOG_LABEL_VERIFIED`, **NOT** `SHARED_ERROR_COVARIANCE_VERIFIED` and **NOT** `DISJOINT_TRACEABILITY_CERTIFIED`. The old discrepancy between `SRM 917a` in VNIIM Appendix A2 and `SRM 917c` in A3 remains explicit `SOURCE_CONFLICT_HOLD`.

Four of nine original glucose participants — **HSA, INMETRO, LNE, NMIJ** — explicitly use 917c in Appendix A3. Additional NIST, KRISS, PTB reference laboratories were recruited/used in original or post-discordance roles and must not be counted as independent original participants. The CCQM distributed SRM 1951b comparison serum material was **certified for cholesterol, NOT given a certified glucose mass fraction** (BIPM report, pp. 7–8); the glucose SRM 917c pure *calibration chemical* is **not** a certified glucose mass fraction for the distributed serum. The common calibrant therefore cannot independently certify that serum's actual glucose quantity.
