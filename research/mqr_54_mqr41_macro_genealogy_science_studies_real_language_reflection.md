## 1. Three intellectual levels, not an undifferentiated 'meta-science'

### A. Historiography of science (과학사학)

The explanatory object is the **historically variable practice of forming scientific things, instruments, disciplines and standards**, not simply first-publication priority. Ask how investigators recognized experimental effects as phenomena and why later terminology retrospectively makes early work appear linear.

**Direct held comparisons**:
- Lorraine Daston & Peter Galison (2007), *Objectivity*, 2021 paperback printing, Drive `1PImEgbQ_XOjKYWWIn_tQr-Ah3qty5vJY` — read contents, preface and 'mechanical objectivity'/trained judgment discussion. Objectivity itself has changing epistemic virtues and image-making practices.
- Hans-Jörg Rheinberger (1997), *Toward a History of Epistemic Things* (previous MQR41 close reading), source `1DDrn0i1jOZ79tx8VxftIgQmdTrNH4tr8` — technical objects and epistemic things change roles; historical research is not a ready-made ontology dictionary.
- Steinle (1997/2016), Ørsted/Faraday/Ampère experiment variation in held originals — experimental concept formation not reducible to testing pre-written hypotheses.

MQR's **legitimate historical contribution** can be careful genealogy and correction of retold evidence, but a *single* disputed thermometric passage cannot establish a universal science-history theory. Historical analyses are plural across instruments, communities, and periods.

### B. Science studies (과학학)

The unit is **distributed coordination of researchers, machinery, resources, techniques, norms, records and institutional credibility**. This goes beyond scientific propositions and provides **positive cases for successful incomplete translations**.

**Direct held comparisons**:
- Peter Galison (1999), 'Trading Zone: Coordinating Action and Belief', Drive `1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-` — introduction and pidgin/creole analogy read. Scientists from distinct cultures may coordinate in local practical interfaces without globally shared theoretical foundations. Not proof that all distinct civilizations can easily establish a bridge.
- Steven Shapin (1994), *A Social History of Truth*, Drive `1EIXL6jv0ThqAhCeY21CrDq4vB-tGyw2f` — contents/introduction sections on trust, gentlemanly credibility, testimony and Boyle. What counts as trustworthy evidence has *social and moral genealogies*. Do not equate 17th-century credibility arrangements with objective scientific truth or with present normative validity.
- Helen Longino (2002), *The Fate of Knowledge*, Drive `14AVrxujqX41GbDmiS4dlA5cKwQVdlFdu` — introduction and critique of rational/social dichotomy read. Critical social interaction is epistemically relevant, not merely an administrative nuisance or external popularity statistic.

This level exposes why a Real Lang boolean authority packet cannot replace actual science studies or simply score 'number of independent labs'. The tester must check material provenance, resource inequality and what shared instrumentation can conceal.

### C. Philosophy of science (과학철학)

The core is **what legitimately constitutes an explanation, reason, evidence, concept, realist commitment, and change of the criteria themselves**. An external historian may describe a shift; philosophy must assess why competing standards are good and how criticism can operate when criteria evolve.

**Direct held comparisons**:
- Michael Friedman (2001), *Dynamics of Reason*, Drive `1JAnxiRCtjaDEeTI_ZG1iLaCyLqKVmsNt` — introduction, relativized/constitutive a priori framework discussion, and rational communication problem. Pre-existing philosophical attempt to connect Carnap's framework plurality and Kuhn's revolutions without collapsing into mere relative truth. Do not claim 'meta-paradigms change' as unprecedented MQR novelty.
- Worrall (1989), Psillos (1999), Massimi (2022), Chang (2012), de Regt (2017), Kuhn (1983), Carnap (1950), Quine (1951): the original MQR disputes on structural continuity, perspectival realism, plurality, understanding and the revisability of logic are extensive.
- Harnad's symbol grounding; Simon's formal discovery; Wigner and Mac Lane's mathematics/engineering: science's question vocabulary is materially and mathematically constructed, yet neither symbol choice nor internal coherence alone generates a worldly referent.

### These disciplines correct one another

History asks: 'How actually did these evidentiary norms emerge?' Science studies: 'Through which technologies, social relations and resources could they function?' Philosophy: 'What made their claims justified rather than merely influential?' There is no inference from **historical adoption ⇒ truth**, from **formal elegance ⇒ physical reference**, or from **successful local collaboration ⇒ complete translation**. Conversely, the social provenance of a method alone does not show its conclusions are false.

## 2. How MQR drifted: a causal-process hypothesis, NOT a proven quantitative attribution

I distinguish four mechanisms inferred from repeated archive passages:

**G1 Local-tractability inversion.** Broad questions generated specific, tractable weaknesses (calibration, bridge, original table). Those weaknesses became the operative unit of progress and recursively spawned new source work. The metric 'next verificational step' displaced the motivating explanandum.

**G2 Antinovelty to antiambition.** Legitimate prior-art scrutiny turned into an implicit policy that if a question resembled Kuhn/Steinle/Quine/Friedman, it should be 'killed' and replaced by a smaller differentiated metric. But the Oct 18 contribution override explicitly recognizes synthesis, careful comparison and replication. Prior art blocks false originality, not the right to study fundamental questions.

**G3 Authority language becoming ontology.** The Real Lang vocabulary of C/E/P/R, partial orders, transport and typed evidence is a useful protective device. If it becomes the only admissible way of expressing scientific questions, it reproduces the very closed-language mistake anticipated in MQR0.11. **Failure receipt coverage and scientific discovery should not be confused**.

**G4 Source-collection feedback.** Discovering a volume or discrepancy creates further meticulous bibliographic checks. This is essential when an historical claim rests upon that source, but when source acquisition itself selects the next global question, MQR 5.4's breadth contract fails. The relative amount of time spent on each subtopic is not statistically estimated from complete chat logs; the narrative is an interpretive diagnosis supported by specific dated user interventions and archived research outputs.

A useful empirical falsification of this diagnosis: examine whether any Nov 1–9 history-audit result actually changes a higher-order verdict about communicability, paradigm creation, or math/engineering, and track that link to a source-backed test. If most did, the 'unreturned DFS' criticism would be weaker than suggested here.

## 4. Three renewed expansive research programs (distinct, mutually criticizable)

**Programme A — One World, Many Sciences.** Explain how differently socialized scientific groups might acquire *partial practical coordination* across radically different concept/measurement languages. Compare Galison's actual trading zones, Kuhn's local untranslatability, Harnad's reference acquisition, and physical apparatus bridges. Prediction: coordinated work can precede complete conceptual harmonization; failure mode: common hardware can encode unnoticed shared biases. Requires historical case with actual inter-group instruments, not another state-name permutation exercise.

**Programme B — Epistemic Virtues and the Making of Paradigms.** How did 'what counts as objective observation' and 'what counts as an intelligible explanation' change, and what makes a change rational? Put Daston/Galison's truth-to-nature / mechanical objectivity / trained judgment, Friedman on constitutive principles, de Regt on intelligibility and Shapin/Longino on testimony and criticism into **historical competition**. Prediction: standards may be multiple, sometimes tensioned; no monotonic scale 'newer is better' implied.

**Programme C — Why Mathematics Survives Engineering.** Pursue Wigner's effective abstraction and Mac Lane's form/function jointly with the material selection of instruments, design constraints, useful approximation, education and institutions. A priori mathematical proof, effective numerical algorithm, physical modelling and engineering reliability are *not interchangeable*. Need at least a concrete failure case as well as a success case; MQR 7 already warned against purely success-selected samples.

Cross-program question (the 2026-02 founding HWPX): **How can a scientific practice create the very representation within which its next questions become formulable, yet still be answerable to an independently resisting world?** This is a philosophical research *problem*, not a theorem now solved by a packet language.

- `language/real/experiments/OPEN-HORIZON-BRIDGE-V54.md`

The audit takes scoped paired outputs and **declared** witnesses for common operation, independent calibration, and separate evidence ancestry. Returns HOLD for no bridge/no calibration/shared ancestry, FAIL_SCOPED_MISMATCH for a tested countercase, or **BOUNDED_OPERATIONAL_MATCH** where all finite declared receipts match. It **never returns world truth, complete theory equivalence, or shared ontology**. One contradiction cannot be washed out by a hundred matches. Duplicate/empty case IDs rejected. Nine local stdlib Python unittests PASS (9/9), using the exact source before connector upload; GitHub user commits must be verified. CI is automatically triggered under `language/real/**`, but successful cloud run is not inferred from a commit.

This is consciously less ambitious than Real Lang v0.5/v0.6: a bounded handoff and research transparency tool, not canonical Rust syntax replacement or a release bump. The witness flags are user-supplied *claims*, not physically audited premises. It does not perform case-specific alignment discovery; the act of creating a joint reference is the larger social/physical problem to study. That limitation is the philosophical point, not a defect hidden in a PASS tag.
