## Recover the research hierarchy

ROOT A 'One world, many primitive concept systems?': Let A/B different native scientific languages (sorts/quantities/inferences/measurement apparatus) that may jointly interact with same unknown physical environment W. What evidence and possible interventions justify calling their accounts of W equivalent, incommensurable, compatible-but-partial or mutually refuting? Account for scientific communication protocols being physically embodied in theories.

ROOT B 'What selects mathematical representations?': If radically different forms can predict equally, why do mathematical structures survive engineering/model deployment? Test structure-fit vs selection of tasks vs institution vs coevolution with unanticipated interventions and cost of transferring methods; do not casually infer universe literally is math.

ROOT C 'Can inquiry invent rather than merely refine concepts?': An existing experimental vocabulary can fail to distinguish outcomes or fail on new intervention; characterizing how novel observable/operation/concept enters and changes previous equivalence classes is the constructive epistemic part. C depends on A/B not just operational error control.

Supporting methods: UV-vis digitization, historical pyrometry, source lineages, calibration, causal abstraction, statistical identifiability, active experiment design, publication authority histories. They are valuable if and only if they alter a decision about A/B/C, reveal counterexample or generate/replace experiment. A beautifully transcribed historical argument without root-level consequence is a historical scholarship deliverable, but not automatically a new MQR research milestone.

## Explicit non-triviality tests

A trivial renaming of units and symbol strings must NOT qualify as 'alien science'. Two theories must differ in ontology, primitives, observation instruments and/or inferential structure; user-specified radical counterfactual tested only if difference matters operationally. Abstract isomorphism between fully specified models is NOT adequate as epistemic bootstrap; must specify accessible and achievable experiments and communications to uncover the mapping. Finite observational agreement is not proof of global sameness; equality of predicted distributions under a finite E gives E-relative equivalence only.

Positive branches: Rival scientific systems may be fully behaviorally equivalent yet semantically/metaphysically distinct (warrant for equivalence of practice, not ontology); they may initially disagree but a newly invented experiment make translation accessible; they may be permanently not mutually interpretable despite coexisting in one physical world; an engineered interface may make only a partial task-restricted translation viable. All possibilities should remain live until experiment.

## Concrete adversarial baseline, no rediscovery claim

Predefine finite observation suite E={e1,...,ek} and sets of implementations A,B. E-relative indistinguishability: a~E b iff their response distributions agree for every e∈E (or differ ≤ε in specified metric). Introduce unknown physical interface map π between parties' actions and readouts. An *operational bridge* is a candidate correspondence φ: experimental tasks/actions and ψ: outputs satisfying P_A(o|e)≈ψ# P_B(o'|φ(e)) on held-out intervention families, with confidence, costs and scope stated. This is ordinary experimental design/model equivalence theory; MQR contribution if any is the NEW question or source-grounded synthesis, not the notation.

Distinguish: (1) bridge EXISTS mathematically, (2) bridge IMPLEMENTABLE with permitted actions/materials, (3) bridge IDENTIFIABLE from permitted observations, (4) bridge DISCOVERABLE by agents with available inquiry budget and own concepts, (5) bridge ROBUST/PORTABLE under novel interventions. Important NOT ALL implication arrows are valid; implementability need not imply discoverability, finite success no realism.

Candidate engineering demonstration: two controllers of a shared black-box system, one reports voltages/currents, other classifies periodic motion as finite symbolic cycles; create matched tasks that agree on passive trajectories but diverge after a load/temperature intervention not representable by one controller's original primitive. Do not claim this toy proves radical incommensurability; use it to show protocol bootstrap and concept expansion have empirically distinguishable stakes. Predefine a baseline (standard automata learning/causal abstraction/active-learning algorithms) and measure how much novel intervention is needed to recover bridge.
