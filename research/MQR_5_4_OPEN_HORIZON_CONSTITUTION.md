## Seven BFS frontier branches
1. **Measurement/computational reduction:** circuits, digitization, transduction, finite observable quotients vs world itself.
2. **Possible sciences:** different logics, primitives, inference, categorizations, and physical technologies confronting one shared world.
3. **Paradigm genesis / paradigm of paradigms:** the emergence and evolution of standards for what counts as question, observation, explanation, and success.
4. **Concept creation / new observables:** how inquiry introduces a concept not representable by its initial vocabulary; changing distinguishability.
5. **Mathematics and engineering:** why certain symbolic forms survive physical prediction/control and whether that is world-fitness, selection bias, institutional uptake, or coevolution.
6. **World-resistance and realism:** what survives cross-architecture tests, and what kind of realist commitment follows, rather than declaring every stable representation real.
7. **Open-ended inquiry:** when progress requires inventing a new problem rather than optimizing within a fixed objective.

## DFS/BFS policy
- `BFS`: generate 2–4 substantive question branches in cycles and compare what each predicts, changes and cannot yet express; do not arbitrarily freeze to one.
- `DFS`: deepen one branch by primary reading, technical analysis, experimental benchmark and critique. After 1–3 meaningful deep steps, explicitly check return to root and whether a real discrimination/conceptual expansion has occurred.
- `BACKTRACK`: when this branch becomes marginally repetitive, carries multiple unresolved source gates, or rivals already explain it, preserve a resumable capsule (question, strongest evidence, limit, next discriminating observation) and switch back to breadth.
- `KEEP`: a historically interesting branch can survive as standalone intellectual scholarship without pretending it is a new MQR master theorem.
- `NO FORCED NOVELTY`: read intensively, learn, follow genuine childhood curiosity and resist narrowing ambitions to ordinary publishable graduate-scope problems. The 2026-10-18 research-contribution doctrine recognizes careful synthesis as real work; genuine novelty is an *outcome*, not a compulsory planning target.

## Evidence gates
- Do not equate a digital circuit realizing output generation with ontology being a computation.
- Do not equate different strings for the same mathematics with radically different primitives.
- Do not assume a world-independent translator is accessible to actual theory-laden agents.
- Do not infer realist identity from matching finitely many observed distributions.
- Record positive possibility (successful bridge, concept invention, paradigm reorganization) as carefully as negative boundaries.
