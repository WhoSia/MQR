# MQR-5.3 R46-W — Target continuity, model-relative quantity individuation and the prior-art kill gate

Date: 2026-11-05. Source of record: canonical Google Drive papers (directly fetched and read in relevant sections) and SciSpace scholarly discovery, with no automated literal full-PDF page-image comparison. The philosophical conclusions below are analyses, not quoted historical authors' verdicts about these exact cross-domain cases.

## 1. Research problem frozen *before* further theory building

Given measurements from protocols O1 and O2, when may one treat them as evidence for the *same target* T, and under what conditions can evidence for T in regime R1 be transported to regime R2? Separate:
- identity of quantity / scientific referent,
- construct validity in each protocol,
- reliability independence of the operations,
- scope/transportability of the resulting evidence,
- the specific question being answered (e.g. generic labeled protein vs newly synthesized virus).
Neither literal outcome equality nor shared verbal label supplies these jointly.

## 2. Three much stronger existing prior-art baselines (all Research OS source texts on Drive)

**Eran Tal (2019)**, 'Individuating quantities', *Philosophical Studies* **176:853–878**, DOI **10.1007/s11098-018-1216-2**, Drive `1dm4Oea-l6BVMEuZGTwe-OeUp05gYTzrA`. Abstract, introduction, criterion R1–R3, Regnault thermometry passages, and conclusion inspected directly. This directly poses practically the same question: do divergent instruments measure a common quantity or different quantities? Claims that no evidence-only, context-free decision rule can disentangle systematic error vs quantity identity; offers **context-relative, coherent model-based individuation**. The metrological three-part 'agreement' criterion is: (R1) instruments adequately modeled as measuring the same specific quantity; (R2) correct systematic error per model; (R3) corrected outcomes converge within model-dependent uncertainty. The author argues the conditions are mutually entangled rather than independently foundational. He explicitly relates this to Regnault and Chang's *Inventing Temperature*. Thus 'same target is a defeasible jointly model-dependent judgment' is already a highly developed pre-existing theory. This is a **strong kill** of general MQR novelty.

**Jaakko Kuorikoski & Caterina Marchionni (2016)**, 'Evidential Diversity and the Triangulation of Phenomena', *Philosophy of Science* **83:227–247**, canonical Drive `1xKFWyQkZVCPN6DYvOTjFsTPCVAtfQQnG`. Abstract, rationale for independent methods, error independence p.232, limitations and conclusion p.245 directly read. Causal-process and *reliability* independence relevant to actual error/bias mechanisms, not the number of nominally different instruments or 1:1 formal independence of all possible errors. Same signal from two methods is epistemically valuable only relative to plausible common error sources; the authors explicitly caution that triangulation does **not** guarantee truth. This already repairs naive 'more operations => proven same object'.

**Samuli Reijula & Sofia Blanco Sequeiros (2024 PREPRINT)**, 'Persistent Evidential Discordance', dated **May 23 2024** and marked forthcoming in *BJPS* in the manuscript (final publication version not independently verified), Drive `1RnvBE9cjjTXJzEoC-RnYMh7KN5bnN6SC`. Abstract, §§3–5, richly annotated data-to-evidence pipelines and conclusion directly read. Classification of discordance: nonsystematic error, noise, and systematic bias; introduces 'enriched lines of evidence' including metadata about preparation, data processing, interpretation and normative role of each step. Which evidence is fit to answer the **same question** depends on these transformation histories. Explicit meta-level warrant analysis already in prior art. Do not label this a newly established MQR concept.

Other robust prior art already held: Garner–Hake–Eriksen (1956), Feest (2005; 2025 Intro), Borsboom et al. (2004), Rheinberger (1997), Engzell (2023), Mathur (2023), Auspurg & Brüderl (2024).

## 3. Actual-source cross-case attempt and limitation

**Stephenson, Thimann & Zamecnik (1956)**, *Archives of Biochemistry and Biophysics* 65(1):194–209, DOI `10.1016/0003-9861(56)90187-4`, canonical Drive `1onfQ0podW-nXkEPlcG7uw_5ux9eQyLqR`:
- Infected intact tobacco leaf disks produce radioactive label recoverable in virus fraction (Table II, TMV **81 cpm/mg**).
- Infected **cell-free** preparation: other fractions label but no labeled TMV product recovered (text pp.207–208).
- A contaminated/heterogeneous 'chloroplast fraction' is an explicitly operational descriptor in original Methods and p.200; RNA provenance uncertain.
- Table IV bacterial viability control reduces one alternative for the observed fraction label but not all compartmental contamination. Table VI light/O2 contrasts do not uniquely identify a complete energy mechanism.
**These are distinct experimental regimes and/or target properties**. The direct 1956 source itself marks the contrast and uncertainty; no new hypothetical MQR source-identity crisis is discovered. Tal's argument about **quantitative measurement identity** is relevant *by analogy* but cannot immediately decide whether a protein product, viral particle or organelle fraction is a singular biological kind: do not force quantitative thermometry into this ontology.

**Breznau et al (2022) with Engzell 2023; Mathur et al 2023; Auspurg–Brüderl 2024**: identical observational dataset and general hypothesis are not sufficient to fix an identical estimand. Engzell identifies populations/operationalizations; Mathur differentiates inferential threshold crossings vs small substantive effect magnitudes; Auspurg–Brüderl emphasizes meta-analytic comparability. This is an **independent sociological analogue** of target misalignment, not proof that explanatory mechanisms are the same as in fractionation. Earlier prior art covers the problem.

## 4. Precommitted judgment gates for one source pair

For source claims A and B:
- G0 TRACE: author/year/version/quote or direct original source + date and textual limitations available? If no, **HOLD**. Avoid misusing 1997 or 2024 conclusions as a 1954 information set.
- G1 SAME QUESTION: agree on sample population, intervention/preparation, specific property, measurement output and time? If no, **NOT_COMPARABLE** as contradictory claims; cross-regime explanation may still be substantive.
- G2 MEASUREMENT MODEL: credible mapping from raw indication to specified target, including allowed systematic errors and uncertainty? If absent, **TARGET_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED**, not automatic FALSE.
- G3 ERROR INDEPENDENCE: credible causal account of independent failure modes for supposedly triangulating operations? If absent, **CONVERGENCE_WEAK**.
- G4 TARGET-SPECIFIC ENDPOINT: does an independent characterization of the precise target (e.g. purified, labeled viral product in a specified fraction) exist? If no, mark **NOT_ESTABLISHED**, not necessarily absent.
- G5 PROSPECTIVE TRANSFER: transport to new preparation requires a validated invariance or controlled bridge; another experimental regime with a common verbal label does not suffice.
These gates are a **comparative audit checklist synthesized from existing sources**, not novel necessary/sufficient conditions or empirical discovered law.

## 5. The hypothesis most clearly killed

**Candidate MQR general novelty**: 'Scientific target identity arises from a revisable, model-dependent assessment of instrumental procedure, measurement agreement, uncertainties, and evidence provenance.' **REJECT AS NOVEL**. Tal 2019 and Reijula/Blanco Sequeiros already explicitly state richer accounts; Kuorikoski/Marchionni already supply an error-independence theory of convergence.

**Residual open target**: Can a researcher predict ex ante when two experimental target claims will split or stay stable under a specific intervention, where Tal's model-coherence, Kuorikoski's error independence and Reijula's enriched-line evidence make *identical* predictions but a truly new proposed principle differs? There is **no such result yet**, and identifying it, rather than renaming their joint account, is necessary before philosophical theory promotion.
