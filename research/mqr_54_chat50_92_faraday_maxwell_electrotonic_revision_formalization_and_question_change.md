# MQR 5.4 R92 — Electro-tonic concept migration: Faraday 1831 → 1832 → 1852 → Maxwell 1855/1856 → 1861

**2026-11-23 KST | OPEN | source-critical comparison, not a new theory.**

## Five distinct transformations, with contemporaneous witnesses

1. **1831-11-29; explicitly coined concept.** [Faraday to Richard Phillips, MS transcribed by Epsilon](https://epsilon.ac.uk/view/faraday/letters/Faraday0522) explains transient induction currents and refers to the “electro-tonic state” in his own correspondence. Faraday is not only following a predetermined vocabulary: he proposes an entity/state to account for a puzzling null interval between momentary current pulses. The same letter pits experimental discovery against the claims of contemporary mathematicians. **This attests his stated view**, not empirical proof that mathematical physics was wrong universally.
2. **1832; specific evidential withdrawal.** [Faraday Second Series, §§231/242](https://library.oarcloud.noaa.gov/docs.lib/htdocs/rescue/Rarebook_treasures/QC503F211839_PDF/QC503F211839v1.pdf) reports that most evidence originally used to require a separate electro-tonic state had been explained by a less hypothetical account of movement and magnetic force lines. He does not establish impossibility of every later use of the concept.
3. **1852; conditional return.** [Faraday *On the Physical Character of the Lines of Magnetic Force*, original *Philosophical Magazine* reproduction §3269](https://vinaire.me/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/1852-Faraday-Physical-Character.pdf) explicitly revisits an electro-tonic state as potentially coincident with the physical existence of lines of magnetic force. **Crucial nuance:** “incline to the opinion,” “whether,” “venture for a time to assume” mark uncertain ontological status, NOT experimental proof of an underlying medium. Specific original §3256 admits experimental data do not warrant an assured physical-vs-action-at-distance conclusion.
4. **1855 read / 1856 published; Maxwell's mathematical bridge.** [*On Faraday's Lines of Force*, original published text as extracted in W. D. Niven's *Scientific Papers* vol. I, pp. 187–189](https://djvu.online/file/djI9c8izZPWoY). Maxwell plainly says the hypothesis was not accepted as physical fact, but might be made mathematically precise. He then builds **electro-tonic functions** representing a vector-like magnitude/direction; elsewhere in the same paper he describes the mathematically defined function as an **artificial notation**, not a physical mechanism. He cites William Thomson's 1847 mechanical representation and 1851 mathematical magnetism, as well as Stokes, as enabling predecessor mathematics: **Faraday→Maxwell was not an exclusive single-author pipeline**.
5. **1861–1862; differential and mechanical analogies.** [Maxwell, *On Physical Lines of Force*, accessible first-person text](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/On_Physical_Lines_of_Force), specifically its introduction and part II, explains a geometrical significance of electro-tonic functions and, under its provisional model, compares electromotive force to a time derivative of electro-tonic quantities. Maxwell explicitly calls parts of the vortex mechanism **provisional and temporary**, warns that a model able to reproduce the known phenomena need not identify the true physical ontology, and contrasts several rival explanatory theories. This is not a license to claim that Maxwell experimentally verified literal molecular vortices.

## What did the *concept* preserve?

| Dimension | Faraday 1831/1832 | Faraday 1852 | Maxwell 1855/1856 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Electro-tonic *state* coined to account for a quiescent but responsive interval | Same term revived to characterize potential physical properties of magnetic lines | “Electro-tonic functions” mathematically model distributed quantities |
| Empirical anchor | On/off or motion-change induces transient currents | Motion through magnetic lines and testable field geometry; skeptical about physical existence inference | Electromagnetic induction and laws of measured electric/magnetic forces |
| Candidate referent | Distinct state of matter under magnetic/electric induction | Possible continuous physical state/field of magnetic lines | Mathematical field-function representation; ontic interpretation deliberately open |
| Question newly made salient | How can no persistent current coexist with ability to generate an induced current when conditions change? | Do the lines themselves have physical existence, static or dynamic? | Which functions/relations represent induction consistently and produce new experimentally testable consequences? |
| Failure / caution | Quiescent ‘state’ does not register directly | Faraday says evidence is not yet conclusive | Algebraic/mechanical success ≠ factual reality of hypothesized medium |

**No conflation:** Modern `A` and `∇×A=B` can illustrate a mathematical descendant only under carefully stated vector-calculus conventions and historical derivation. Maxwell's 1855 original symbols, gauge practices, and physical conception are **not identical** to the later full modern gauge-theoretic electromagnetic potential, and especially not to the present-day Aharonov–Bohm effect. This is an explicit red line.

## Direct counterexample to simplistic MQR narratives

**False:** if an explanatory concept is not accepted as physically real in 1832, it becomes scientifically useless. **False:** when mathematicians formalize an experimentalist's tentative concept, it instantly becomes a proven new material entity. The primary sources show an intermediate path: retaining an unsettled question, reusing a conceptual handle in a new explanatory setting, developing mathematical representations of it, and keeping empirical commitments revisable.

**Established prior art:** Faraday/Maxwell studies, Maxwell's original text, Steinle 1997/2016 and Nappo 2021 already analyze much of this. MQR's legitimate contribution is a cross-case, claim-graded *research apparatus* using evidence ancestry and explicit non-equivalence of theory, question language and physical ontology. No novelty promotion or alleged general law.
