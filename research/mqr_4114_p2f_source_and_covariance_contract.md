## Fixed rival analysis, explicitly NOT empirical covariance estimation

Nine original CCQM K11 participant published combined mean/standard uncertainties `(y_i,u_i)` remain unchanged. The 2018 source Appendix A lists **four original participant institutes** explicitly with NIST `SRM 917c`: HSA, INMETRO, LNE, NMIJ. Include only those four in a hypothetical common-certification component; *exclude* post-discordance NIST/KRISS/PTB references, the ambiguous VNIIM A2/A3 (917a vs 917c), and other materials. **Name-based membership is a documentary pointer, not the validated same physical lot or same calibration-error pathway**.

- R0: `Sigma0 = diag(u_i^2)` as in frozen P2-E. Weighted GLS residual heterogeneity `Q0 = min_theta (y-theta*1)^T Sigma0^-1 (y-theta*1)`, compare same frozen df=8, alpha=.01.
- RH(τ): *counterfactual sensitivity only*: among the four named SRM 917c participants, posit a perfectly shared, positive, additive standard uncertainty component τ (units mg/g) while preserving each published marginal uncertainty `u_i`. Let `s_i` be 1 for those four and 0 otherwise, then `Sigma(τ)=diag(u_i^2 - τ^2 s_i)+τ^2 ss^T`. For `0<=τ<min_{i:s_i=1}u_i` the matrix is positive definite. *No certificate or source report provides such a participant covariance matrix.*
- **Scale definition only (illustrative):** NIST archived 2020 certificate lists expanded purity U=0.3 percentage points of certified purity 99.7%, k=2. Define `tau_ref = weighted_mean_P2E*(0.3/(2*99.7))` mg/g ≈0.00174. This is a **contrived common-uncertainty scale, not an observed calibration error, not an archived 2012-specific purity certification, not a covariance estimate**. The common-purity sensitivity coefficient need not equal 1 for all instruments; real stock preparation, source uncertainties and bottle state vary.
- Evaluate **τ multipliers {0,1,2} times tau_ref**, all preserving positivity of residual diagonal. No fitting τ to observed Q and no changing source participant inclusion. This grid was selected after exploratory analysis and must not be called prospective inference or fitted uncertainty.
- For every τ compute exact GLS fitted mean and Q from the nine published values. Q>χ²(df8,0.99)=20.0902350 is a **hypothetical model consistency diagnostic only**; no extra statistical test of actual sharing, since τ and group membership are unverified. A certificate's mass-purity value cannot be equated to each individual calibration-stock measurement.
- Also test mathematical equality `Q(y+c*1)=Q(y)` with a uniform shift of the entire observed population, and method-dependent deviations. Pure common additive shift **cannot by itself remove inter-lab scatter**, yet same material's true amount plus an additive shared shift remains nonidentifiable.
- **Anti-inflation:** the HSA Appendix B uncertainty percentages are an **institute-local budget**. Calibration standard concentration appears as 28.3% of HSA sample-level variance for each of two vials; its 0.278% relative standard uncertainty is not the institute-to-institute correlation 0.283, nor necessarily a pure 917c certification effect. This particular budget includes bottle/solution preparation terms. HSA one-vial budget is not HSA's published combined nine-institute u. Do not use it to estimate another institute's missing covariance.
