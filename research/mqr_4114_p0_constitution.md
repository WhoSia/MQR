## Two linked P1 investigations, not a forced unification

**P1-A, logic-target edge concentration:** many experimental designs represented as typed *method → operation-role* edges. Count finite, source-bounded arrows and rank operation role recurrence. ## P1 precise claims and failure gates

Let E be a frozen finite set of method descriptions and L a versioned controlled vocabulary of **typed** operations. Let A ⊆ E×L be a binary, explicitly source-coded *uses-operation* edge relation. Then D(l) = sum_{e∈E} 1[(e,l)∈A] and C = sum_l D(l) = |A|. If a method has repeated operations, multiplicity W(l) must be maintained **separately** from D(l). Equality is a mathematical bookkeeping identity, no empirical law.

**Nulls/defeaters:** (1) annotator split/merge of operation vocabulary changes maxima; (2) corpus composition and domain selection change distributions; (3) common ADC/software threshold can create artificial hubs; (4) a hub can be a representation bottleneck but an uninformative physical contact; (5) identical labels across protocols can hide distinct underlying physics; (6) edge is not proof of physical causal dependence; (7) repeated citations are not independent experiments; (8) a scale-free fit on a tiny corpus is inadmissible.

**No silent transmutation:** arithmetic graph PASS does not equal inter-protocol population centrality PASS; high in-degree does not imply ontological fundamentality; source-independent falsification is still absent until physical originals are admitted. Future material is expected to improve this graph under independent coders and held-out protocols, rather than by inventing protocols.
