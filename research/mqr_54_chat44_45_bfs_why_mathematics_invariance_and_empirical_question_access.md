## 1. Wigner is more sophisticated than 'mathematics matches nature'
Wigner explicitly discusses two distinct invariances in Galileo-like physics: repeatability over changes of time/place and **irrelevance of many situational details** (which conditions truly matter). He notes that discovering the narrow experimental conditions within which stable relations hold is part of **experimenters' skill**, not merely imposing universal formulas. Physics subsequently uses powerful mathematical concepts but selects only a tiny portion of all mathematics, often in ways that cannot be justified by conceptual simplicity alone. He also compares different mathematical spaces used in relativity and quantum mechanics, exhibiting that a single representation is not forced across domains.

Consequently the claim 'mathematics is effective because it merely records universal invariance' is **insufficient**: invariance structures may be local, relevant variables experimentally selected, and multiple mathematics fit different targets.

## 2. A 4-world minimalist thought experiment with two layers
The paired evidence experiment R44 uses a four-point world W={00,01,10,11}. A and B are separately measurable. The most elementary expression `A XOR B` is **syntactically definable** once those variables are named, but its expected value is **not identifiable** from two unpaired data sources. A provenance/paired-measurement capability raises the observable function span from 3 to 4, so mathematics becomes operationally *more consequential* despite no change in the formal set of Boolean expressions.

This supports a very precise distinction:
- mathematical expressibility ≠ historical ability to empirically evaluate an expression;
- empirical evaluability ≠ physical causality of the relation;
- causal intervention success ≠ transfer to every future domain or scale.

A formula can be perfectly true within a declared model yet answer no then-accessible empirical question. This does not diminish mathematical truth; it limits the *application interface*.

## 3. How maths earns role without uniqueness
Three defensible candidate mechanisms, all known prior art:
- **Invariance compression:** if transformations of place/time/scale leave relevant relations unchanged, one expression describes many experimental situations; discovering the invariance itself demands tests.
- **Representational economy:** mathematically equivalent coordinate systems or sufficient statistics can express the same observable content economically. The choice of representation may be pragmatic, but choosing a form does not prove it is ontologically primary.
- **Proof-guided experimental question formation:** formal structures make non-obvious contrasts and conservation relationships calculable, suggesting new tests (e.g. R44 parity), yet their execution still needs historical instruments and specimen linkage.

Rivals: anthropic/selection accounts where scientists keep mathematically tractable problem domains and abandon failed models; and Wigner's warning that the scope and accuracy of successful mathematics in physics are genuinely surprising, not wholly 'explained away' by experimental selection.

## 4. Exact negative controls
- A valid mathematical operation like XOR may be unidentifiable from observed archives until pairing exists.
- Two equally good local formal structures need not admit a unique global ordering, as R41 demonstrated with Blackwell-incomparable sensors.
- Excessive abstract mathematical capacity may generate questions that are **neither measurable nor physically well-defined** under existing practices.
- A simple coordinate recoding is not automatically a new empirical science: a reversible relabeling preserving every measurement and intervention is just a change of representation for the declared task.

## 5. Relation to Chang's pluralism
Chang's multiple systems of practice and conservationist pluralism reject discarding earlier successfully usable practices solely on account of a newly dominant representation. An old measurement can remain epistemically useful for a task while newer theories widen available scientific questions; this is compatible with Wigner's local invariance and R41's task-relative Blackwell comparisons. However Chang's stronger philosophical program does **not** prove two contradictory ontologies simultaneously true without qualification; one must specify practical domains, criteria and world contact.

## 6. Research thesis and falsifiability
**Provisional, non-novel thesis:** mathematical structure often increases the scope of *portable relational questions* by compressing empirically discovered invariances, but reliable scientific applicability is a contingent achievement of instruments, measurements, populations and operations. A study that uses mathematical structure to propose a new test must document whether its variables and joint readout can actually be instantiated.

A discriminating empirical programme would compare historically contemporaneous cases where a formal equivalence yielded a genuinely new independently tested prediction versus cases where a rich mathematical model had no feasible experiment. If this says nothing beyond Wigner, model selection and history of science, MQR should record integrative synthesis rather than claim a new theory of mathematics.
