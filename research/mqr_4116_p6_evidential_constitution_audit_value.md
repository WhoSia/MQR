## Mathematical and pragmatic audit choice

In the P5 toy setting q1=q0=1/4, let prevalence p be bounded within [1/4,3/4] without retention restrictions, or [1/3,2/3] if an externally defensible ratio bound G=2 is accepted. Define a **decision-specific** audit value as the reduction of the worst-case width of the identified interval. For these two hypothetical regimes, widths 1/2 and 1/3 imply a width gain 1/6. This is NOT expected utility, Bayesian information gain, or a financial value until a loss function, audit cost, access probabilities and real-world assumptions are introduced. A physical source certificate and an archival sampling frame solve different uncertainties; neither is automatically preferable. Claim-specific acquisition protocol must distinguish audit target, observation offered, adversarial counterworld separated, cost and custody evidence.

## Philosophical turn: constituting what is admissible evidence

Institutions do not merely filter a pre-given archive. By setting protocols, laboratories, journal genres, accredited instruments and citation standards, they can affect which events are made *recordable* and what scientists learn to treat as convincing. This yields two distinct questions: (i) selection bias given an existing world/record pool; (ii) the constitutive effects of an evidentiary practice on which operations, distinctions and challenges are available. Distinguish descriptive authority (what is followed), epistemic warrant (what is justified) and normative authority (who can set rules). The last requires contestability and institutional legitimacy beyond a numeric statistic.

This is a philosophical **research hypothesis and conceptual distinction** with affinities to Kuhn, Feyerabend and Hacking; it is neither attributed as their verbatim view nor empirically demonstrated here. Feyerabend is a prompt against prematurely privileging one methodological language, not a license to discard source authentication, logical validity or counterexamples.
