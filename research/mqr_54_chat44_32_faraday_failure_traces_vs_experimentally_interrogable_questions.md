# MQR 5.4 · MQR44 R32 — Historical source tests, not invented logical states: measured negatives as a route to new questions

**2026-11-21 KST.** Primary-actor diary data obtained [R31](mqr_54_chat44_31_faraday_original_dated_diary_success_negative_controls.md). This unit compares MQR's physical realist and conceptual-generation stakes across **different experiments** with independently stated counterarguments, rather than imposing a universal score.

## A traceable sequence

A bounded factual historical sequence now supported by the 1831 diary text (editorial publication, not autograph):
- **Question before observation:** Faraday writes an explicit intent to investigate producing electricity from magnetism at diary §1, 29 Aug. So exploration did not begin without a question and expectation.
- **First positive / limited scope:** a primary energized ring produces brief secondary indicator response when primary current begins (§3/7), vanishing during maintained primary supply; opposite direction upon break (§7/9). There is no recorded full time-series trace and no numerical uncertainty estimate.
- **Within-device discriminant:** Aug 30 §14 reconfigures *which circuit* is opened and closed; moving B-side detector contact while primary A remains supplied causes no matching needle response. He explicitly infers dependence on battery-side changing conditions.
- **Failed material readout:** Aug 29 §11 tested electrochemical action and found no evidence; Aug 30 §15 detected no spark. Neither establishes that transient electrical action cannot in principle produce sparks or chemistry at another sensitivity.
- **Apparatus variation failure:** Sept 24 §§21–32 present an extended series of no detectable response in several coil geometries, even while original ring/cylinder setups still worked (§32). This undermines automatic generalization and is a serious *historical negative dataset*, though not quantitative instrument logs.
- **A new positive regime:** Sept 24 §33 manipulates permanent-magnet contact with a coil-wound iron cylinder and records momentary secondary output, disappearing if electric indicator communication is disconnected. Interpretation changes from changing electrical currents to changing magnetic contact conditions.

## Negative evidence is task-specific, not a universal defeat

From the historical diary it is defensible to infer a particular **experimental contingency**: positive effects depend upon which physical path and which material arrangement varies. It is NOT defensible to infer a clean logical theorem 'constant current cannot create any magnetic effect', or that Faraday already measured flux change as a general numerical law. In 1831 the magnitude and transient time-profile of needle signals are unquantified; detector sensitivity may explain false negatives. Negative signal under *one configuration* is not proof that all configurations are physically impossible.

The risk of retrospectively re-naming the same question is reduced by a **materially consequential contrast**: under the earlier successful ring, directly disconnecting the *secondary indicating circuit* while leaving the primary connection does not reproduce the same galvanometer behavior; opening the primary does. This is not merely a new interpretation of fixed data but an experiment involving different physical operations and falsifiable detector responses.

The rows are *proposed rival claims*, not asserted historical actors' verbatim theories. Observational sentences come from edited actor-written diary, not MQR laboratory data. The discriminants are thus suitable for criticism without granting the existing historiographical interpretation exclusive authority.

## Transfer to the actual MQR core

The historical actor's scientific question develops **endogenously** as physical acts and failures differentiate experimental dependencies. Neither a new word by itself nor a pre-gifted symbolic XOR port is required. The conjunction of measurement, apparatus, negative observations and interpretive generalization is what makes a question **materially interrogable**, in a concrete finite episode.

However:
- This is also Steinle/Nersessian's established prior art; not a new universal philosophical model.
- Faraday's whole episode includes both hypothesis-driven and exploratory activities, so do not force the false dichotomy 'theory-guided' or 'theory-free'.
- The physically later relation (Maxwell/Faraday induction law) must remain separate from 1831 source claims.
- Historical source evidence is adequate for a *bounded* explanation, not mathematical impossibility of alternative sciences.
