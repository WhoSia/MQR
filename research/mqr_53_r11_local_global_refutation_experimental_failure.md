# MQR-5.3 R11 — Mathematical Counterexamples versus Experimental Failures

Date: 2026-10-31. Philosophical close reading; no empirical or novelty promotion.

## Lakatos source
*Proofs and Refutations*, chapter 1, Google Drive 1qpRtwZLjZBgio5W6ptQorzu1MY7l970G. A local counterexample defeats an auxiliary proof step without necessarily falsifying the conjecture; a global counterexample defeats the conjecture. The hollow nested cubes and crested cube challenge distinct assumptions. In the later cylinder discussion, connectivity of the flattened network appears as a hidden lemma. The dialogue is a philosophical construction, not a literal historical transcript.

## Rheinberger source
*Toward a History of Epistemic Things*, chapter 4 and chapter 6, Google Drive 1DDrn0i1jOZ79tx8VxftIgQmdTrNH4tr8. Weak radiolabel incorporation in early cell-free research was compatible with multiple biochemical processes, including nonspecific binding and contamination. Historical glycine-incorporation attribution was subsequently revised in favor of a serine/phospholipid pathway. Failure of manipulation, interpretation, mechanistic theory, or the research target are analytically distinct.

## Proposed philosophical thesis and defeaters
A failure may become generative if investigators can localize its bearing on a claim, entertain alternative descriptions, and develop new problem-solving moves. Neither necessity nor sufficiency is established. Local versus global mathematical refutation is not identical with experimental instrument versus biological-mechanism failure: experimental evidence is stochastic, conditional on controls, and potentially causally underidentified. Actively search for productive discoveries without localized failure and for well-localized failures with no fruitful successor.
