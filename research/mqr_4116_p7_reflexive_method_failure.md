# MQR-4.116 P7 — Reflexive Failure of the Method

2026-10-17. Active 4.116; this document is a **self-critique**, not another validation of the framework.

The user's challenge is more radical than adding a practice-centered second encoding. MQR itself can turn its preferred objects — typed arrows, proof certificates, quantifiable identification bounds, and even the HOLD ledger — into filters that silence questions which lack ready-made representations. Its rigor may conceal its choice of what deserves rigor.

## Three adversarial tests
1. **Pre-graph exclusion:** A scientist refuses to record tacit judgement, failed informal attempts or institutional coercion because no approved node/edge type exists. The dataset can be perfectly audited yet incapable of expressing the relevant dispute. Question: who had authority to determine the ontology?
2. **Certification capture:** A field's accepted certifier is the same institution whose role is contested. Every accepted proof carries a certificate, yet the validity of the certifier is precisely at issue. A formally valid derivation is not independent scrutiny of its rules of admission.
3. **HOLD as postponement:** Marking a challenge 'insufficient empirical witness' can be appropriate for empirical causation, but inappropriate as a complete response to a conceptual/normative objection about why a research question was excluded. HOLD must be typed by the claim it blocks; it cannot be an all-purpose intellectual veto.

## Disciplined alternative
Do not discard mathematics. Require (a) an **unencoded objection register** recording questions that cannot be expressed without distorting them; (b) adversarial revision of the ontology and admissibility rules; (c) separate empirical, formal, interpretive and normative standards; (d) comparison of what each encoding erases, not only what each accurately measures. A research program also needs permission to change its own question, not merely answer a frozen one.

### Formal limit
If representation e:W -> D maps two worlds to the same description although a philosophically relevant predicate Q differs, there is no q:D -> {0,1} recovering Q. This is the earlier observation-fiber factorization fact repurposed as a *diagnostic* of the representation's blind spots. It does not prove the omitted question is important, or that an alternative ontology is correct: those are independent philosophical disputes.
