## Macro result and limits
Separate (a) physical dependence or realization, (b) mathematical derivation within stipulated assumptions, (c) discovery/computation from the lower-level description, (d) explanatory relevance for a particular question, (e) empirical warrant across representations. Same universality class is restricted equivalence of scaling behavior, not identical materials, complete empirical equivalence or universal ontology. Positive bridge: heterogeneous physical systems, statistical-mechanical models and conformal constraints can share a quantitatively testable structural consequence. Negative: one exponent match cannot identify mechanisms, classes or complete theory; need multiple predictions, alternatives, regime, measurement links and dependence/provenance. No analogy between RG and social selection treated as a mathematical identity. Franklin and Ross already articulate strong versions of these distinctions; do not claim MQR invented explanatory autonomy.

Historical question generation: Wilson's retrospective describes changing the admissible space of effective Hamiltonians, not merely fitting fixed coefficients. The transformation can generate couplings that an initial restricted parametrization omitted. The question shifted from solving in a fixed family to finding a justified approximate truncation in a larger family. This is a concrete case of changing the space of scientific questions, not invention of logically unrepresentable primitives. Bootstrap revival supplies a useful historical example for rational preservation of alternative research capabilities. Need contemporaneous 1970–72 texts and modern papers for causal/historical adjudication.
