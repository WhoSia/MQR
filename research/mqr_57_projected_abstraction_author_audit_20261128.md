## Exact reconstruction and direct code audit
Let R be red, H light, Y red-label. For even digit P(R)=.1 and P(H|R)=.3, P(H|not R)=.7. Conditional-resampling intervention uses P(R|Z even,H)=.03/.66=1/22, hence P(Y=1|Z=0,do_policy(H=1))=.1+.8/22=3/22. Pooled coarse model P(Y|H=1)=.34 (equal parity mix). Missing Z-to-Y dependency after projection produces bias .20363636 for this query.
Generator generate_samples: initially odd_flip=np.bitwise_xor(odd,u_color_misalign), but on rejection retries new_odd_flip=odd[i] | new_u_color_misalign. For even digits the two expressions agree. For odd digits the retry expression always generates red; initial light blue probability .07 survives; thus for odd and light P(R)=.93, P(Y)=.844, rather than intended P(R)=.27/.34, P(Y)=.7352941176, delta .1087058824. For odd/dark code .876 vs intended .86363636. The published Z=0 target is unaffected by this discrepancy. No claim of impact on published training curves or all results.
