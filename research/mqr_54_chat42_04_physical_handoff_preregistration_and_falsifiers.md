# MQR 42 · 기록 04 — Prospective intervention protocol and evidential gate

**Research objective:** distinguish (i) mere relabeling, (ii) physically certified preparation transfer, (iii) measured action semantics, (iv) creation of a novel executable experiment. This is a proposal, **not a record of an experiment already performed**. Avoid expensive/dangerous hardware; an initial classroom-safe manipulation of opaque tokens, paper cards, and randomized order may test the *procedure only*, not precision quantum metrology or independent aliens.

## Gate 0: what are we allowed to assume?

Agents A and B can each name their **own** specimens/actions and log outcomes in uninterpreted native symbols. Each has a recorder for time and local actions. For a physical transfer test, there must be a **continuous tamper-evident identity channel or mutually accessible transfer witness**, plus a way to check preservation of the preparation property. These are NOT derived by the theory; they are the explicit starting primitives of a *relative bootstrap*. Without any jointly observable causal channel, independent A and B data remain permutation-symmetric, and the theory cannot bootstrap an absolute translator from nothing.

## Gate 1: authenticate *material* contact rather than matching labels

- Before a trial, A records a uniquely identifiable physical object and its locally reproducible preparation. Do not assign B's label in advance.
- If transfer occurs, B logs receipt of the token and a local response under B's repertoire. Use a custody ledger with source event, receiver event, and reversible round-trip check; record failed receipts rather than reusing a predicted label.
- Establish which preparation properties may survive passage and independent pre/post controls to test them. A token may survive as an object while its optical/chemical/thermal state changes. Without such controls, assign only *same-token* status, not *same-preparation* status.
- Repeated contacts with the same object should be tested across independent runs and conditions; repeat strings alone do not establish independent stable states.

## Gate 2: test the isomorphism hypothesis, do not decree it

Propose beforehand a specific local operational relation R_A whose meaning ought to survive transfer as R_B (e.g. a classification of which operations are reversibly accessible within a fixed resource budget). This itself is a scientist-created hypothesis. Attempt a falsifier by transferring additional specimens **chosen because rival isomorphisms predict different B relations**, not just reinforcing the first agreement. If no graph map exists, branch to *unequal capabilities* and an action-grammar model rather than declare different universes.

### Pre-registered finite positive/negative control

Use a four-node accessibility cycle in each abstract lab, first valid contact A0→B0, then:
- **negative control:** A2→B2, predicted two surviving maps;
- **discriminating control:** A1→B1, predicted one map.
Alternatively if a transfer is unverified or the preparation drifts, the admissible contact set should **not change**, even if the two labs happened to use matching symbols. To prevent hindsight selection, register the A choice, possible B results and posterior candidate counts before observing receipt.

## Gate 3: separate observational equivalence from *new* action creation

A finite graph alignment is L0 (potentially a structural rename), not a new science. For an L1 claim require a new physical trial that strictly splits an old equivalence class under a known action. For L2 demand a newly executable composition not present in the old lab grammar and independently recorded outcomes. For L3 demand the prior representation cannot even formulate the successful counterfactual, along with a formal expressibility argument and a specific new empirical consequence. L2/L3 are **open** in the current work.

An informative failure of graph preservation is not noise to be hidden: it may indicate unequal apparatus, environmental dependence, invalid transport, or an inadequately formulated primitive. Keep these explanations separate.

## Gate 4: link to held instrument data without fabricated cross-run pairing

**PTB optical JAWS 2025**, [real ZIP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iE96PNpjXKVqROx1GXprQ7N7D42KOabF/view): current positive/negative/full wave graph exports have common time labels, and index shifts change fitted residuals; **not a physically certified same-run contact record**. A proposed follow-up would require independent timing/blocked-reference measurements; the downloaded figure export cannot supply them.

**Šíra 2023 QPSW**, [real raw 7z](https://drive.google.com/file/d/178ypBIq_G460hGi7oHoweTBfNfb5qmK_/view): QWTB processed output exists but source RMS/peak interpretation and exact cross-run pairing are unresolved. An externally calibrated shared reference chain and matched experiment identity would be an *instrumental measurement bridge*; our finite graph has no standing to certify it.

**Feest & Steinle 2012** [held book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WJyJHhYFi3R27QNfNOu5Jr4pmsdq5Ua9/view) warns that theoretical and experimental criteria can co-evolve, so a fixed graph of operations is an epistemically contestable snapshot, not an invariant ontology. **Clement 1988** [held report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X_EeNfK7g5ablkwQweNpyU_Jhy0uIxTk/view) supplies prior art for imaginative candidate model generation, not guaranteed physical realization.

## Acceptance/exit receipt

Result categories must not be collapsed:
- NO CROSS-CHANNEL: no justified empirical physical mapping, mathematical candidate symmetry remains.
- TOKEN CONTACT ONLY: common physical object tracked, preparation stability not certified.
- PREPARATION ANCHORED: verified identity and stability (and possibly bounded environmental transformation).
- OPERATIONAL BRIDGE: independently tested relation preservation and predictive cross-lab intervention outcome.
- NEW ACTION CREATED: new physically executable protocol not in previously enumerated menu; results independently repeated.
- ONTOLOGY/CONCEPT BRIDGE: only after showing old-language limitation, new expressibility and successful novel prediction, with alternative theory objections examined.

For any failure, preserve the negative evidence rather than forcibly reduce the mapping count. This protocol does not state that the latter two high bars have been reached.
