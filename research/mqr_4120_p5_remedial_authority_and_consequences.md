# MQR-4.120 P5 — Remedial Authority, Observed Consequences and the Hearing/Repair Gap

2026-10-17. P5 synthetic protocol research only; no real independent adjudication.

## Continuity
P1 separated J1 claim-relative evidence from J2 evaluative authority. P2 prohibited illicit warrant transfer. P3–P4 tested authority ancestry and missing dependency provenance. P5 separates the *availability of complaint reception* from the *power and actuality of repair*.

## Five distinct questions
(1) May a challenger submit an objection?
(2) Can the appellate reviewer reopen the excluded question?
(3) Does the reviewer have lawful/practical power to demand records or change the rule?
(4) Was a remedy actually executed?
(5) Was any claimed outcome change independently observed?
Each implication is non-automatic. A nominal appeal route does not certify remedy and an issued correction does not show its consequences.

## Synthetic fixture checker
[Eight constructed examples](../experiments/mqr-4.120/p5_remedial_capacity.py) distinguish conditional exercised-and-observed remedy (only under stipulated assumptions), no access, hearing with no reopening, reopening with no remedial power, available-but-unexercised power, recorded remedy with no observed consequence, dissent erased, and absent mandate. The checker tests declared fields; it does not assess document authenticity, external mandates, normative propriety, causal effect, or actual human experiences. The positive example's observed outcome is fictional and **not** an independently verified real effect.

## Methodological caveat and repairs
A boolean can_revise_rule cannot prove lawful authority; evidence provenance and scope certificates must be separately examined in a future court. Source-independent observations of actual consequence require a temporal claim, pre/post records and credible counterfactual or alternative explanations. An appeals process may legitimately correct only a subset of defects; lack of authority outside its mandate should be OUT_OF_SCOPE rather than automatic failure. A real institutional scheme may require multiple specialized bodies; graph disjointness is neither necessary nor sufficient for effective correction.
