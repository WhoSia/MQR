# MQR 5.4 · MQR 44 · Record 08 — Joint-action genesis: a cross-science operation that neither a dictionary nor independent action union supplies

**Date:** 2026-11-21 KST. **Research version:** MQR 5.4 OPEN. **Class:** exact finite mathematical witness + primary philosophical/historical text audit. **Claims NOT earned:** actual tool construction, material coupling, autonomous instrument invention, novel automata theorem, universal epistemic theory.

## 0. Why this actually advances the founding question

In [MQR42 director's four-frontier contract](mqr_54_chat42_04_research_director_four_frontiers.md), the primary missing step was explicit: do not merely find an isomorphism between two *already fixed* action grammars; determine whether interaction yields a new **joint action** with an operational distinction that the original grammar cannot express. [MQR41 compositional horizon](mqr_54_mqr41_compositional_protocol_horizons_and_paradigm_grammar.md) already proved that a *given* swap operator can reveal additional states after composition and that myopic methods can miss two-step experiments. This record tests a stricter *old-alphabet non-definability* condition: a new **cross-conditional** operation from interaction cannot be simulated by words over A's native operations, nor by naïve addition of independently applied A/B flips.

Be cautious: defining a toy joint operator does not explain its invention. The novelty of this record is **a narrower QA target for that future historical/material problem**, not an original mathematical operation.

## 1. Explicit synthetic model: native sciences and an engineered interface

An outside analyst uses underlying world `S = {0,1}²` with coordinates `(x,y)`; neither actual scientist is assumed to know this ontology. Science A's local access:
- native measurement `o_A(x,y)=x`;
- native operation `f_A(x,y)=(1-x,y)`, toggling only the first bit.

A separate science B's access:
- native measurement `o_B(x,y)=y`;
- native operation `f_B(x,y)=(x,1-y)`.

The two scientific practices use distinct sensor and operation interfaces. The hypothetical *newly engineered docking device* creates **controlled joint coupling** `c(x,y)=(x⊕y,y)`. It is a standard XOR/controlled-NOT transformation in the analyst's notation, not a novel physical law. Note carefully:
1. The joint action is **not** part of either local native action set `{f_A}` or `{f_B}`.
2. Sequential native flip actions generated by `{f_A,f_B}` cannot produce `c`, because their effects on x do not depend on y.
3. The model **assumes** a physically reliable port realizing `c`; it does not solve how two unfamiliar civilizations invent compatible connectors or native operation labels.
4. For the A-only observer, the original x-only observational partition has 2 classes: `{(0,0),(0,1)}` and `{(1,0),(1,1)}`. Reading x after c yields `x⊕y`; paired with the original x reading this identifies all four states. A new physical operation has opened an A-accessible distinction that was inaccessible under its old native grammar.
5. B already has its y sensor. If B can transmit y in a **trusted, independently aligned communication channel**, the pair already has both bits; then the example demonstrates *new A-local control*, not necessarily new globally available information. Do not suppress this strongest alternative interpretation.

A falsifying model within this toy is `f_B` added to A's usable operations without cross-conditional coupling: even arbitrarily long sequences of `f_A,f_B` only expose x or 1-x to A. Merely mounting a device with a new name or independently toggling an unseen degree of freedom cannot perform the required observational work.

## 2. Stronger (but standard) mathematical characterization: observational congruence criterion

Let a finite deterministic measurement practice have states S, a readout o, and a set G of old actions. Define `s ~_G t` exactly when `o(w(s))=o(w(t))` for every finite word w over G (including empty). This equivalence is right-invariant for all old operations: if `s~t` then `g(s)~g(t)` for every `g∈G`, because `wg` is an available word.

**Claim, with proof:** For a new deterministic operation c added to G, the old observational equivalence relation remains unchanged for **all future finite words** if and only if `c` preserves `~_G`.

- If c preserves `~_G`, every generator in `G∪{c}` preserves `~_G`; induction over word length shows any new protocol produces the same terminal o for all previously equivalent states. Hence no pair splits.
- If c does **not** preserve it, choose `s~_G t` but `c(s) !~_G c(t)`. By definition, some old word w already distinguishes the post-c states. Therefore the newly executable word 'apply c, then w, then observe' splits s and t. Hence the observational partition strictly refines.

This is an immediate application of automata congruence / Nerode-style behavioral equivalence; **PRIOR ART, not an MQR theorem with claimed priority**. Its research value is the exact falsifiable criterion: does the proposed *material coupling* break the old observational congruence, rather than merely increase the number of labels or devices?

In our example, f_B preserves A's old equivalence while c does not: `(0,0)~(0,1)` under A's old apparatus, but after c their first coordinates are 0 and 1. One newly composed operation suffices.

**Most dangerous undercutting controls not yet executed materially:** miswired docking port; shared upstream calibration; label alignment wrongly assumed; host-sensor spoofing with no physical state change; unstable state preparation; B transmitting y by another reliable channel; intervention cost or irreversible sample disturbance. ## 4. Two primary scholarly documents just re-read: ambitious precedent, not a novelty-free blank slate

**Galison, 'Trading Zone: Coordinating Action and Belief'** (1999 chapter in *The Science Studies Reader*, directly held in Research OS [Drive ID 1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-/view)): directly read early argument for independent theory/experiment/instrument traditions and local coordination without homogenization, plus the wartime MIT Radiation Laboratory discussion. The Rad Lab's laboratory work organization reflected radar's physical architecture: modulator, magnetron, antenna, receiver and display. Galison's concrete historical account supports that engineering, experiment and physics can coordinate productive *instrument work* without needing every participant to adopt one full theoretical language. It does **not** prove that the XOR port or our 2→4 bit classes capture wartime radar history. Galison already made 'local coordination instead of global common framework' a central thesis, so MQR cannot claim it as an invention.

**Star & Griesemer, 'Institutional Ecology, “Translations” and Boundary Objects'** (*Social Studies of Science* 19(3), 1989, pp.387–420; direct Research OS [Drive ID 1bx-2BqywhLTXqhh_wNXkadtyPaMCUKaT](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bx-2BqywhLTXqhh_wNXkadtyPaMCUKaT/view)): directly read the original abstract, description of four boundary-object types (repositories, ideal types, coincident boundaries, standardized forms), and the museum specimen/collector standardization discussion. Cooperation can depend on shared material or record objects that stay robust enough to be recognizable across communities while being locally interpreted and used differently. The museum's standardized specimen and record procedures offer a *different* route to coordination than an engineered physical cross-conditional operator. Standardized record exchange does not necessarily change what a sensor detects and need not be 'new coupling' in the model's sense.

**Galison, 'Trading with the Enemy'** (2010, Research OS [Drive ID 10OCF75Sqg__CgIVPmsQH3x3PYrGIS1dw](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10OCF75Sqg__CgIVPmsQH3x3PYrGIS1dw/view)): direct passages about hybrid scientific contact languages and 1940s radio-engineer/theoretical-physicist exchange; the author explicitly asks about asymmetry of power and contribution to shared technical vocabularies. Important correction: functional hybrid contact languages and local coordination have a detailed existing historiography, including **unequal** actors. A model of two symmetric scientists may miss the institutions and asymmetry that make instrument construction possible or block it. Claims about Schwinger/renormalization's precise causal genealogy remain **Galison's interpretation**, not independently verified by us against Schwinger's original work.

**Cross-reading inference (attribution controlled):** A shared artifact can (i) keep independently meaningful observations comparable (Star & Griesemer-style records), (ii) make new cross-component actions physically possible (Galison-style instruments), or (iii) generate a constrained contact language for the actual work. These may occur jointly, but neither document establishes a universal necessary chronology. The relevance of our model is the checkable difference between **coordinating already possible observations** and **adding a materially new intervention**.

Additional strong prior art: Kuhn (1983) on lexicon learning and limited incommensurability; Nersessian (2008) on model and instrument co-construction; Haueis (2023) on tool-dependent concept formation; existing active-learning alphabet refinement (Henry et al., CONCUR 2025). **Novel global theory NOT EARNED.**

## 5. What MQR should try next, not simply enumerate

The genuine scientific/philosophical advance would be a verifiable **interface-construction history** where two communities with different instruments/aims and no shared abstract vocabulary jointly assemble a reusable operation, retrospectively document its early failed trials, and demonstrate a previously unreachable measurement or prediction *without* inventing the common identification data at analysis time. Historical Galison/Stargate cases supply candidates but not this exact dated step-by-step receipt yet.

A prospective research contract for a real engineered demonstration:
1. record two real native instrument capabilities and their observational equivalence classes independently;
2. freeze predictions for **separate** components and for a proposed physically coupled interface *before* observing cross-port outputs;
3. independently verify sample identity, experimental pairing, reset, calibration and lineage independence;
4. compare actual joint-interface data with independent-port, disconnected cable, placebo and readout-spoof alternatives;
5. ask whether the joint capability remains stable after reuse, not whether the two communities adopted one ontology;
6. document how the parties proposed the interface in their own vocabularies and what new experimental question it made useful.

This would be a **real material/archival program**, not accomplished by the toy. No new 5.5 version or global Harvest fragment authorized.
