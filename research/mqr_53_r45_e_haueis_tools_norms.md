# R45-E — Haueis: operational precision, real-world role and open-endedness

**Source:** [Haueis (2023; accepted manuscript 2022), *Exploratory Concept Formation and Tool Development in Neuroscience*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EqY18HPcrti7CF30nGnc4KS0EV67NbEh/view), DOI 10.1017/psa.2022.79, accepted/preproduction version. Sections 2–4 read, final published wording not independently collated.

Haueis distinguishes concept formation (C1 meaning through experiment conditions, C2 significance via comparison to ordinary conditions, C3 reference through suitable tools). His success conditions AC1–AC3 concern appropriate experimental settings, appropriate real-world functional inference and sufficient instrumental precision; O1–O2 concern transportability to new domains and adaptability. The 'bug detector' vs 'fly detector' context warns against interpreting every provisional label as a successful new scientific referent.

**Direct MQR kill:** Introducing a named standard for 'concepts that enable future questions' is not new; Haueis already provides success/failure conditions including openness beyond the initial setting. MQR's earlier evidence-transfer ideas cannot simply relabel O1/O2.

**Still unexplained?** Funding choices for an exploratory project before its outcome can satisfy AC1–AC3; but this is ex ante decision theory and resource prioritization, already competing against Chang/Kitcher. Separate retrospective success from prospective justification.

**Status:** structured literature test, not completed mechanism or novel norm.
