The same spectral feature can be an asset for one question and a blind spot for another.

## Philosophical notes gained by this split
**(1) A measurement standard may be deliberately insensitive to a chemical variable.** That is not a weakness if its declared task is to probe photometric scale, but it cannot then identify that chemical variable. This is a strong positive example of MQR's task-indexed sufficient statistic.
**(2) Physical and numerical transformations form a partial, typed graph.** A wavelength resampling, a beam mixing, a chemical separation and a log transform are not generally interchangeable morphisms. Before lifting an error bound along the graph, show the required physical/contextual commutation conditions.
**(3) Reproduced source texts have separate provenance.** The 1983 compilation is a convenient acquisition point, not the originating author for each reprinted 1970s article or the issuer of a present-day revalidated certificate.
**(4) Good scientific design uses deliberate loss of irrelevant detail.** Such loss is allowable when the target claim is fixed and distinguishability remains adequate; it becomes an epistemic error only when transported without license to a finer question.
