## 1. Exact scientific question

When a normal-scientific practice adjusts measurement procedures or a chemical/physical model to reconcile surprising observations, **what independently constrained observations and feasible interventions distinguish a justified local measurement repair from a genuine change in the target explanation or concept?** A historical community's classification of a practice (normal science, anomaly, borderline knowledge) may shift independently of the measured response itself.

The original MQR question about whether UV–Vis can be reduced to logical circuits is a *founding measurement example*, not the permissible ontological domain. Subsequent MQR 1–25 already addressed translation loss, world resistance, calibration genealogy, state sufficiency, intervention constitution, dissent, model equivalence and the limits of a universal scalar progress index. We do not narrate philosophy as a late accidental detour.

## 2. Basic source-conditioned formalization (proposal, not an independently established law)

At time \(t\), for claimed target \(H\), admissible intervention design \(U\), documented calibration/source lineage \(C\), permitted nuisance family \(N\), and observation transformation \(Q\), let the *admissible raw-observation set* be

\[
\mathcal O_H(U;C,N)=\{\,y:\exists\theta_H\in\Theta_H,\ n\in N,\ u\in U,\ e\in E_C,\quad y=F_H(\theta_H,u,n)+e\,\}.
\]

Do **not** embed institutional trust directly as a coefficient in the optical model. \(C\) is a claim-specific record of calibrations, valid time and source identity that *restricts* possible \(F_H,N,E_C\); failures or uncertainty in the chain enlarge their admissible families rather than magically proving the rival false.

A strong **bounded deterministic separator** between rivals \(H_0,H_1\) for a specified intervention protocol \(U\) requires disjoint predicted sets (ideally positive separation under a declared metric), \(\mathcal O_{H_0}(U;C,N)\cap\mathcal O_{H_1}(U;C,N)=\varnothing\). If sets overlap, there are observational outcomes consistent with both and no *uniform error-free* discriminator across the declared families; overlapping sets do not prove the rivals equivalent globally or imply that no probabilistic evidence favors one.

**Caveat:** \(U\) may itself be institutionally and technologically constrained. \(C\), \(N\) and chosen targets can be endogenous to the research tradition; requiring a global oracle to certify independent provenance would beg the question. The frontier is **claim- and period-relative**, plural and revisable; it is *not* one-dimensional progress or a universal science/pseudoscience classifier.

## 3. Exact result inherited from P1I (not an empirical proof)

In the 3-channel synthetic optical case \(y=(M_0+\Delta M)x+q_0+\Delta q+\eta\), let \(\widehat x=M_0^{-1}(y-q_0)\), \(K=\|M_0^{-1}\|_\infty\), \(\|\Delta M\|_\infty\le\varepsilon\), \(\|\Delta q+\eta\|_\infty\le\delta\). If \(K\varepsilon<1\),

\[
\|x-\widehat x\|_\infty \le \frac{K}{1-K\varepsilon}
\bigl(\varepsilon\|\widehat x\|_\infty+\delta\bigr).
\]

This gives a **continuous** (not only 512-corner) conservative intensity enclosure under its premises; positive sample/reference intensities allow interval propagation to absorbance. See [the source + run P1I memo](mqr_4121_p1i_immutable_bootstrap_raw_authentication_and_continuous_matrix_bounds.md), [continuous enclosure code](../experiments/mqr-4.121/p1i_matrix_continuous_norm_certificate.py), and [successful Actions #39206409270](https://github.com/WhoSia/MQR/actions/runs/39206409270). **No measured uncertainty radius, external photon flux bound or chemical-species assay exists.** Large/unbounded pH-dependent extra-array light \(q\) restores global nonidentifiability. Do not infer a third chemical species or one particular molecular structure from a surviving synthetic spectral contrast.

## 4. Three rival explanations plus the mixed case

- **\(H_O\):** optical forward-map error, limited stray light, finite bandpass, detector/reference drift, wrong intensity-vs-log correction order. Change in observed spectra occurs without independently identified changed chemical state.
- **\(H_C\):** identifiable pH/T-dependent chemical structure or species response that survives independently sourced optical calibration; additional chemistry-specific experiments required.
- **\(H_M\):** both mechanisms coexist. Any binary 'optics OR chemistry' inference is incomplete.
- **\(H_Q\):** unbounded pH-dependent omitted optical source channel; can imitate chemical change by construction. Its *power to fit anything* must not count as empirical identification. Restrict \(q\) by an independent filter/source test before evaluating the claim.

Seek a **matched and predeclared** intervention design \(pH\times\) optical line-spread setting \(\times\) independent OOB filtering, with raw linear sample/reference/dark intensities and held-out chemical controls. A toy 2×2×3 cross-intervention test can constrain the measured response; it does not identify chemical ontology from a rank or residual count.

## 5. Four different levels of change (not a progress ladder)

| Type | Research-level claim and counter-test | Legitimate scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 normal-scientific instrumental repair | independent laser/standards calibration predicts *held-out* changes, before chemical-state claims | measurement procedure |
| D2 local explanatory extension | chemically informed model yields predeclared new results after tested nuisance controls | target model's domain |
| D3 alteration of operational target | existing state quotient or measurement category fails across credible interventions and histories | observable or kind individuation |
| D4 historical disciplinary change | manuscripts, education, community exemplars/standards change, independently sourced over time | historical normal-science matrix |

Harvest E (evidence), R (responsiveness to criticism), I (institutional status), M (measurement/explanatory coherence) are **analyst axes** without a validated global numerical metric; they can diverge. Kuhn's *Structure* and *Essential Tension*, Lakatos 1970, Laudan 1983, Chang 2004, Worrall 1989 are philosophical interlocutors and **not presumed to endorse this framework**. Need close primary-text and case-specific alternative reconstructions, including Aristotle/chymistry/quasicrystals/plate tectonics/borderlands as source access permits. No universal demarcation thesis is promoted.
