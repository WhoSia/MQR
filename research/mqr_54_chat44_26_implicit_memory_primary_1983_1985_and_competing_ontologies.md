## 2. A prospective real-literature contrast
**H1 (novel stable module):** implicit/explicit task dissociation tracks genuinely distinct, repeatable underlying memory mechanisms that generalize over tasks, modalities and conditions.
**H2 (task/process interplay):** different task cues, encoding and retrieval constraints generate dissociations without a unique two-module ontology.
**H3 (operational construct still unstable):** useful provisional 'implicit memory' category tracks an evolving family of tasks, not yet a uniquely established unitary kind; distinct experiments may diagnose non-identical processes.

All three can agree that some specific historical priming effects were real. Distinguishing them requires original study designs, convergent tests, causal manipulations, independent tasks and careful semantics of what 'memory system' means. **A dissociation alone does not logically select H1.**

MQR's novel value, if any, is not claiming that there is no implicit memory or that different memory systems have been disproved. It is applying the same **operational agreement / inferential divergence / ontological reserve** test protocol to Faraday's 1821 electromagnetism and 1980s cognitive psychology, and testing whether that cross-domain distinction predicts any independent documented research change.

## 3. Historical asymmetry and world contact
Faraday motor: physically circulating device, co-observed rotation, competing microscopic-current explanation.
Memory research: behavioral priming and recognition performance, whose interpretation involves task instructions, participant heterogeneity, neural/psychological latent variables and different external-validity constraints.
Neither case can be translated into the other by a literal three-state quotient; the commonality is logical structure of **experimental distinguishability under provisional explanatory inference**. Do not confuse the shared formal grammar with one substantive mechanistic theory.

## 5. Falsifiability demands
For a meaningful source-critical adjudication read primary original experiments and record (a) exact participant populations, (b) study/test contrast, (c) task controls, (d) actual observations and variance, (e) what authors did and did not infer, and (f) whether later independent studies discriminated H1-H3. Until then the appropriate status is PRIMARY PUBLISHING/BIBLIOGRAPHY PASS, ORIGINAL TABLE-LEVEL CLAIMS HOLD, ONTOLOGY HOLD. No exact causal or population effect estimate asserted.

**Research decision:** preserve positive experimental discovery and epistemic limitation simultaneously. The word 'implicit' may organize genuine inquiry without itself uniquely establishing a natural kind. This is precisely why MQR's question-genesis axis and its ontology-authorization axis must remain separated.
