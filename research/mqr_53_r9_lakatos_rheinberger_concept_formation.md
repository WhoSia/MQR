## Distinction to investigate

A mathematical proof can revise which axiomatic or topological conditions make a conjecture true; experimental systems can produce manipulable fractions before their molecular identities become determinate. Both may transform the object of investigation, but they face different material and logical constraints. Do not infer that scientific reality is constructed by mere decision, or that Lakatos's responses are universally productive.
