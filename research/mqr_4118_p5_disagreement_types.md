# MQR-4.118 P5 — Three Non-Equivalent Forms of Theoretical Disagreement

2026-10-17. ACTIVE 4.118. Primary philosophical sources reread in connected Research OS: Thomas S. Kuhn (1983) Drive 1Ep81ueXQGpa5lqeDZfKLj9na0jSYRHxc; Howard Sankey (1993) Drive 1u2h9Osxb57UAa0pGW3nnvOj6-bG1bC8U; Xinli Wang (2002) Drive 12SgyKtz8YpYr57YRLiaoC1-tb0Dhxwmd.

**Type I: shared-proposition conflict.** A and B can attach opposite truth values to a shared proposition. Disagreement is not automatically a translation breakdown.

**Type II: taxonomic translation challenge.** Some terms fail to map across scientific classificatory structures, as discussed by Kuhn 1983 and reconstructed differently by Wang 2002. Lack of an exact mapping need not eliminate interpretation, communication or comparison. The claim that a sentence lacks a truth value in another taxonomy is specifically Wang's reconstruction and needs his semantic assumptions.

**Type III: problem-selection conflict.** Participants disagree over which phenomena, evidential admissions, or questions deserve evaluation. This is a conceptual/normative conflict that neither Type I nor II necessarily resolves. MQR proposes this third axis; do not attribute it as a theorem from the three cited authors.

**Cross-source review:** Sankey 1993 distinguishes theories disagreeing in truth value over common statements from theories whose vocabularies differ in meaning, while allowing comparison in some latter cases. Kuhn 1983 separates local nontranslation from interpretation. Wang 2002 proposes a taxonomy/truth-value-gap account in criticism of untranslatability alone. This is a bounded argument map, NOT proof that three scholars agree.

**Falsifier:** A pair of theories with no shared direct kind-term translation but shared instrument readings and an intelligible comparison procedure would defeat the claim 'nontranslation implies no rational comparison'. Conversely, agreeing measurements do not certify equivalent questions or legitimate evidentiary authorities. No historically authenticated such pair is yet claimed.
