## Main argument verified from original

The paper distinguishes whether the raw data conflict from whether they are transformed into **evidence claims supporting contrary hypotheses**. It proposes discordance may arise because several lines of evidence adopt incompatible interpretations of the causal tracks by which the target phenomenon produces data. This unifies familiar contributors including random error, measurement noise, and systematic bias within an account of inference from data to phenomena. The paper directly extends an earlier lineage: Reijula & Blanco Sequeiros (2024), *Persistent Evidential Discordance*, DOI `10.1086/731543`, earlier complete manuscript held as Drive `1RnvBE9cjjTXJzEoC-RnYMh7KN5bnN6SC`; journal website still labels final item 'Just Accepted', so do **not** misstate a print volume/page.

Conceptual core in source-authored terms:
- Data can be identical or appear congruent while *evidential support* differs because competing claims and background causal assumptions differ.
- Discordance does not require Nature to have supplied two mutually contradictory events.
- Identifying the provenance and causal transformations from observed effects to evidential claims can help diagnose how a conflict arises, without guaranteeing a unique true theory in every case.
- The paper explicitly already discusses Breznau et al. (2022) and evidence-informed policy, so the MQR case of 'same data + differing interpretation' is strongly prior-art covered.

## Stress test against the 1956 tobacco study

Proposition A: radioactively labeled material was recovered from a specified virus fraction of **intact infected leaf disks**.
Proposition B: virus product labeling was **not** observed in the tested **infected cell-free extracts**, although other fractions labeled.
A and B **are not opposed evidence claims for the same protocol, sample or experimentally defined variable**. They form a contrast that gives investigators a lead about altered or missing processes after disrupted preparation. A purported contradiction between A and B would stem from conflating the evidential target 'TMV in intact leaves' with 'TMV in disrupted extract'. Tal's quantity individuation/Blanco Sequeiros's data-to-phenomenon inferences can distinguish this, alongside direct original historical author interpretations. A more substantive inference concerning a missing membrane structure, cellular compartment or donor may be plausible but **not established** from these observations alone.

Breznau/Engzell/Mathur: classification of statistical-significance categories, underlying effect-size magnitudes, causal estimands, and substantive societal inference may disagree even when the *same dataset* was shared. This is a direct use-case for the 2026 discordance theory; the present MQR project has made no new philosophical prediction.
