## What the new research has and has not established

**Established as exact synthetic fact:** Two local-variable models can have the same observations but different counterfactual guidance effects (R63). A PR-style set of valid contextwise joint distributions need not admit a classical global joint realization (R58). A global settings/outcomes-dependent postselection process can manufacture high CHSH values from completely local deterministic outcomes (R61). Both contextuality and causal confounding are heavily established prior art; this is an educational/analytical reconstruction, not a new discovery.

**Established with explicit scholarly evidence:** Anderson's account of his 1932 positron discovery does not support a naive 'Dirac theory instructed the experimentalist to discover it' chain (R62). Fry (2022) independently analyzes parallel Crick adaptor theory and Hoagland/Zamecnik tRNA research (R64). CHSH 1969 provides an explicit proposed experimental test following Bell's theorem, although interpretation of physical results still demands statistical/protocol assumptions. The cases exhibit different theory–experiment relationships.

**Not established:** Novel quantum interpretation; actual detection-loophole engineering, quantitative laboratory postselection; verified first-hand cause of every historical scientist's instrument choice; the direct execution of the precise committed Python bytes in CI; unique MQR ontology or a universal theory of scientific question birth.

## Immediate research priority

Select **one** published original (CHSH 1969 or 1972 original full methods) and independently recode the inference assumptions, while using **one** already held molecular-biological original to audit actual changes in the experimental question. Alternate these rather than taking six more nested CHSH toy cases. Original publication abstract ≠ figure-level independent result. Each case needs a dated predictions→design→observation→interpretation matrix, a negative or near-miss comparator, and an explicit prior-art parity judgment.
