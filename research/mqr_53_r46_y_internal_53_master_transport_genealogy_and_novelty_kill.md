## Exact historical gate, no fictional calibration parameter

Compare **1956 intact infected leaf disks** (reported labeled TMV recovered) with **infected cell-free extracts** (no labeled virus recovered though other protein fractions labeled):
- `T_intact` and `T_cellfree` are different physical preparation processes, not ordinary reprocessing of the same raw observed counts;
- `K` (detector and readout) reports label after different protein/fraction separation pathways. One cannot assume unchanged `K` and `D` simply because both use radioactive label;
- A scientifically justified transport would require an explicit biological state mapping `tau` linking functionally relevant intact-cell states to the extract's surviving states, and a plausible intervention mapping `omega` for experimental conditions. The papers do not demonstrate such maps as empirically verified. Therefore the equality of interventional distributions above is **NOT ESTABLISHED** for this case.
- The negative virus endpoint is positive historical information about the *limits* of the cell-free regime. It does **not** establish that the same abstract intervention exists in both regimes or that the failure can be causally attributed uniquely to one missing structure.
- The source paper contains useful partial comparisons (virus+normal-extract control; bacteria viability shifts; light and oxygen comparisons), but they do not identify `tau`, `omega`, all hidden contamination, or the exact required viral synthesis mechanism.
