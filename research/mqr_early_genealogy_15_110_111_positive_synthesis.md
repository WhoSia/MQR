2026-10-18. Historical rereading; not a new empirically verified finding or formal version transition. Following the contribution-pluralist doctrine, valuable synthesis does not require claiming an unprecedented theorem.

## 1.5 — Cross-level constraint is positive scientific content
Original Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3caef561cf928179af92ffdba7bb834b .
A typed bridge g:X_L -> X_H relates lower and higher regimes. Read the original Cross-Level Constraint Ledger: bridge, constitution, causal role, derivability, multiple realization, invariance, dependency, explanatory excess, defeat. Distinguish a redescription, constitutive compression, autonomous macro constraint and stronger emergent hypothesis. Explicitly retain the **non-duplication rule**: independent descriptions are not independent experiments. Causal manipulation is distinct from constitution. A reliable macroscopic constraint may warrant positive realism without postulating a second fundamental force.

## 1.10 — Compatible but non-identical reality
Original Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3caef561cf92817194baf843b1a7c41a .
A and B can both be real when they bear compatible, non-identical world-facing constraints. Two levels, two predicates and two evidential routes are different counting questions. Do not infer new substances from two scientific descriptions. The existence of a universal governing law, the simulation conjecture and unique ultimate substrate were left HOLD, not refuted.

## 1.11 — One-world comparison is an extra problem
Original Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3caef561cf92815da5f9f28974f3d143 and https://app.notion.com/p/3caef561cf928107b153e0f7e9a30537 .
Local signatures specify jurisdiction, variables, interventions, world constraints and provenance; inter-regime bridges have types and may themselves be partial/approximate. Pairwise compatibility does not entail joint satisfiability. A local-to-global gluing audit is a mathematical tool, but it still requires scientifically meaningful choices of variables and bridge observations; a formal global section is not physical truth without that bridge evidence. One-world compatibility does not require one privileged language.

## Specific constructive synthesis
Take a measurable microstate space X_L, a macro variable g:X_L -> X_H and a family of experimental contexts U. Define context-dependent predictions q_U. If a macro constraint holds for heterogeneous micro-realizers, and survives a prospectively vulnerable cross-context intervention, then the macro level can contribute **a useful autonomous projectible constraint** even when no new fundamental cause is identified. If another regime adds incompatible constraints, the appropriate response is to investigate g and inter-context bridges, not immediately label one level unreal.

Separate four claims:
(1) q_U(x)=q_U(y) conditional observational equivalence;
(2) g(x)=g(y) declared macro identity;
(3) joint existence of a global model satisfying the bridge constraints;
(4) world warrant that the particular bridge and constraint representation reflects relevant physical operations.

(1) does not imply (2); (2) does not imply (3); (3) does not imply (4) without original-world contact. These are **analysis boundaries**, not newly proven metaphysical laws.

## Connection to 4.42/4.54 and Harvest
4.42 warns currently generated rival families need not exhaust scientifically relevant rivals. 4.54 warns a mathematical invariance group must be licensed in physical scope and embedding context. The Historical Borderlands of Science Harvest supplies cases of distinct kinds of change: evidence defeat, type/measurement revision, institutional exit and concept survival. Those cases may illustrate this architecture; they are not yet formal instances proving the model.
