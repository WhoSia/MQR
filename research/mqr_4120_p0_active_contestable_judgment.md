## P0 problem
Let e be evidence, c a claim, r a rubric or rule-set, and a an authorizing review arrangement.
J1(e,c|r): conditional claim-level inference validity within r.
J2(r,a): legitimacy, scope, appeal, admissibility and revisability of r under a.
Neither J1 implies J2 nor a J2 objection by itself refutes e or c. The set of legitimate challenges need not be complete under the same r that it contests.

## P1 dual-ledger research contract
For each concrete dispute, create a typed J1 ledger (observations, source lineage, model, assumptions, inference, counterevidence) and a J2 ledger (institutional authority source, exclusions, appeal route, who can revise, dissent, scope). Allow PASS, FAIL, HOLD, and OUT_OF_SCOPE per claim with justification. Preserve the dissent and material alternative even under consensus. No global weighted legitimacy scalar. Distinguish independent external mandate from just another panel with shared authority ancestry.

## Falsification/adversarial criteria
1. Demonstrate a case where J1 and J2 do not diverge and the second audit adds no decision-relevant information; avoid forced difference.
2. Demonstrate a review procedure whose amendment path cannot hear its own exclusion critique; do not self-certify.
3. Show an empirical disagreement resolvable by new measurement, resisting inflation to irreducible normative conflict.
4. Test whether an enriched formal court can represent and repair the objection; do not assume tacit judgment intrinsically unformalizable.
5. Specify a concrete stopping rule for appellate recursion conditional on defensible independent mandate and claim scope, not universal closure.
