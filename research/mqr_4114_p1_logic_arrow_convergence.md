## Definition and epistemic limits

For a frozen finite set E of experiment descriptions and a versioned controlled vocabulary L of typed operation roles, let A be the directed bipartite binary method-to-operation relation. D(l) = sum over e in E of 1[(e,l) in A], while occurrence multiplicity W(l) needs a separately justified unit of repeated operation. Sum over l of D(l) = |A| is ordinary edge bookkeeping, not a new scientific theorem. Without a bounded corpus, source-admission criterion, operation identity, annotation resolution and distinction of edge roles, there is no well-defined count of ALL scientific-method arrows.

Four graphs should remain separate: physical material/interaction network; digitization/computational processes; statistical inference/decision; reference ancestry and independent falsification. A chromatographic separation, time arrest in rapid freeze-quench spectroscopy, photon detection, ADC and Boolean gates are not equivalent nodes merely because one circuit could simulate their finite input/output behavior.

Under a vocabulary refinement q from child roles to parent roles, recover parent coverage by the Boolean union of all child role supports. Provided coverage is preserved, parent coverage stays stable while raw child category degrees and ranked hubs do not. No observational mapping alone certifies physical identity, ontic primacy or independent standards.

## Executable finite counterexample: genuine CI, synthetic fixture

[Seven abstract analyst-coded templates](../experiments/mqr-4.114/fixtures/p1_abstract_graph.tsv), [native Rust analyzer and tests](../experiments/mqr-4.114/p1_abstract_graph_census.rs) and [read-only Actions CI #38200048166 SUCCESS](https://github.com/WhoSia/MQR/actions/runs/38200048166), tested commit c0f96322d5935da0d483f7876b4982575493c0f1. 10/10 tests PASS. Initial CI #38199962902 FAIL due to variable shadowing was repaired then retested at the specified commit.

This is deliberately NOT a corpus extracted from actual chemical/medical/physical instrument protocols. The seven synthetic templates A–G express 11 generic operation roles and precisely 51 method-to-role arrows. Five generic words (observe, boundary, normalize, encode, decide) occur in all seven templates, therefore the top five receive 35/51 = 68.63% of all arrows. The remaining degrees are infer 6, compare 5, calibrate 2, and partition/snapshot/iterate 1 each. Concentration is an artifact of this purposely shared vocabulary, not a discovery of a law in all experiments.

Refining only the universal role normalize into domain-specific subroles one-for-one leaves 51 arrows unchanged, changes the vocabulary size 11 to 17, and reduces all-seven-template hubs from 5 to 4. Duplicating the first abstract template raises the census from 51 to 58 without adding independent world evidence. Thus naive category-level high degree is representation-sensitive. A scale-free/Zipf fit from 11 constructed labels is inadmissible; a time-evolving preferential attachment mechanism is not established by a static distribution.

## Prior art and methodology rivals

- Soldatova and King (2006), An ontology of scientific experiments, DOI 10.1098/rsif.2006.0134 (EXPO already covers generic formal scientific experiment design).
- Bandrowski et al. (2016), The Ontology for Biomedical Investigations, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0154556 (OBI already integrates assays, equipment, biomaterials, data and analysis roles).
- Barabasi and Albert (1999), Emergence of Scaling in Random Networks, DOI 10.1126/science.286.5439.509 (preferential attachment requires a time-evolution and growth mechanism).
- Milo et al. (2002), Network Motifs: Simple Building Blocks of Complex Networks, DOI 10.1126/science.298.5594.824 (network motifs require typed topology and an appropriate null model).
- Clauset, Shalizi and Newman (2009), Power-Law Distributions in Empirical Data, DOI 10.1137/070710111 (selection, tail estimation, model comparisons and goodness-of-fit).
- Nami, Gast and Groenen (2016), Rapid Freeze-Quench EPR Spectroscopy: Improved Collection of Frozen Particles, DOI 10.1007/s00723-016-0783-7 (temporally arrested observation differs from the spectroscopic readout).
- Bruno and Svoronos (2012), Detectors for Gas Chromatography (NIST published comparative detector reference; do not invent a whole-book DOI).

Those sources make it untenable to market a generic experiment ontology, power-law histogram or optical-circuit reduction as new to MQR. Source-locked, independently coded experimental method graphs and specific physical-intervention predictions are the true remaining contribution target.
