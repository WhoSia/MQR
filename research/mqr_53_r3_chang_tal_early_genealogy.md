# MQR-5.3 R3 — Historical fixed points and genealogy

Research date: 2026-10-31. Primary source reading: Chang (2004) Inventing Temperature chapter 1, especially water boiling-point stabilization and historical superheating; Tal (2017) Calibration: Modelling the Measurement Process on predictive model calibration.

Chang reports Marcet's 1842 glass vessel conditioned by earlier sulfuric-acid use leading to water superheating beyond 105 C, and Donny's 1846 air-depleted water experiment reaching 137 C under special conditions. These are non-equilibrium superheating observations, not competing standard boiling-point definitions. The distinction between target fixed-point concept and physical realization of a standard matters.

Claim-relative instrument model R=a*T+b+noise, a>0: relative order (under stability and noise control), temperature differences (requires gain a), and absolute values (requires gain, offset and scale) demand different calibration evidence. This is a constructed teaching model, not a historical reconstruction.

Early MQR lineage read from original chat exports: MQR 1 (2026-08-26) UV-Vis physics to detector to ADC, MQR-0.1 observational equivalence, MQR 3 (MQR-1.69 adaptive audit/trace), MQR 7 (MQR-3.173 WRC/DG and REALITY != RESISTANCE). Hence the basic evidence-chain distinction predates MQR-5.3 and is not new priority.
