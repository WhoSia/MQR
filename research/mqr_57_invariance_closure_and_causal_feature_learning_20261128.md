## Source study
Kroeger, Xia & Bareinboim (2025), Learning Invariances for Causal Abstraction Inference, technical report R-129, https://causalai.net/r129.pdf. Read introduction, Defs 6–8, Thm 1, Corollaries, selected appendix A.2 proof and D.1 limitations. PDF screenshot attempts pages 3 and 5 cache miss; textual extraction successful. Structural invariance demands equality of downstream structural mechanisms for every exogenous state and other parental context, not equality of marginal outcome distributions. Equivalence closure of genuine invariances guarantees AIC; fewer known invariances yield finer safe clusters. The paper explicitly assumes such information is available; it is not inferred merely by matching finite noisy marginal distributions. Contrastive training is a learning approach conditional on these invariances. Technical report, not claimed peer-reviewed publication.

## Executed tests
Prospective decision research/data/mqr_57_invariance_learning_decision_20261128.json; executable language/real/experiments/approximate_invariance_closure_v57.mjs. Exact and sampled Bernoulli distributions p_i=(10+4i)/100, i=0..20, eps=.05. TV distance equals |p_i-p_j|. Exact single linkage connects all 21 states, true diameter .80. Exact max-diameter greedy sorted partition has 11 groups and maximum .04. Greedy is optimal in number for one-dimensional exact interval diameter constraint: leftmost uncovered point requires a cluster with maximum no larger than leftmost+eps, covering all points up to that endpoint cannot reduce feasibility of remaining points; induct. Not general multidimensional optimality. Under exact structural equality closure is valid; approximate closeness is nontransitive. For a path of m edges each <=eps, triangle inequality yields m*eps bound, not eps.
Finite sampling: fixed seed 20261128, 40 replications per n=250,2500,25000, independent Bernoulli draws per state. Three estimators: sorted adjacent single linkage; sorted greedy diameter of empirical probabilities; confidence-certified greedy diameter (singleton always allowed; otherwise empirical range+2r<=eps, r=sqrt(log(2*21/.05)/(2n))). Hoeffding plus union bound simultaneously covers p_i at >=.95 for IID sampling within each state, so on that event all selected nonsingleton diameters <=eps even after data-dependent grouping. No claim of structural AIC or general causal identification. Observed coverage 40/40 at each n (conservative, not proof of calibration).

The confidence method is unhelpfully conservative at these budgets: adjacent true gap .04, nominal observed gap .04 requires 2r<=.01; n>=log(840)/(2*.005^2) ~134669 per state merely at nominal point estimate, not a guarantee of compression. On the simultaneous event, sufficient worst-case gap condition is .04+4r<=.05, much more expensive. Adaptive targeted testing, variance-aware and equivalence tests with simultaneous error control are next methods to learn rather than asserting that absence of statistical rejection proves invariance.
## Separate exact witness: what cannot be learned from single-world interventions
SCM A Y=U, SCM B Y=X xor U, U~Bernoulli(.5) independent of X. All single-world Y marginals under do(X=0),do(X=1) are .5 in both; observational joint also equal for any common independent X distribution. But paired potential outcomes differ: P(Y_0 != Y_1)=0 in A, 1 in B. Thus distributional invariance permits merging X values for the declared single-world marginal query, while B has no pointwise mechanism invariance. To assert the stronger counterfactual property requires assumptions/coupled evidence; not obtained by increasing marginal sample size. Executed exhaustive U enumeration.
