# MQR44 — Malus mobile app and LEGO source, not experimental pair evidence

Two non-PDF objects arrived alongside PTB data, both related by subject and implementation to [Rivera-Ortega, Barcelata-Pinzon & Saldaña-Gonzalez (2025)](https://doi.org/10.3390/physics7040067). Original article is [held as a paper PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1opKbF2I9jO-56NR8R0slVLrSm_obm6fy/view). Its *Data Availability Statement* points to [Zenodo 17268611](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17268611), but no direct provider-file byte-equivalence test has been performed on these two uploads.

**[MIT App Inventor source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dmiuP_h92Qv0PMvoZKLLrkJjk-rmhkgh/view)** `.aia` is a ZIP container; its `youngandroidproject/project.properties` identifies project `graf_sens_copy`, App Inventor version 233 and the main `Screen1` path. Individual `.scm` screens define forms, light-intensity measurement and components, while `.bky` Blockly XML defines behaviors. This is editable *source code*, not the runtime phone environment and not a raw time series. SHA256 `7a3254198882403363c9f0236dc94180eb7669a73a4d1802ea317ec99b782494`.

**[LEGO Digital Designer CAD](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Bz6_KSRf5nzB4P34lmn134uIRoamOa2l/view)** `.lxf` is a ZIP container with `IMAGE100.LXFML` and PNG preview. XML reports `LEGO Digital Designer` 4.3, `Mindstorms` brickset and geometry. It is NOT an actual controller firmware, physical optical-bench calibration or precision angle time series. SHA256 `433bcda1de6432204f7c4e2eac374539e599133fb4d77cf502167ffac354ba9c`.

These sources permit reconstructing **which operations the authors conceived and implemented**, not the operational equivalence of physical preparations. For MQR's original AB/BA test, require independent photographic or encoder angle record, light-dark-bright reference, optical channel and physical polarizer order (not merely changing angle), device ID, timestamp, and randomized reverse-order repetitions. The 2025 published Malus angle-dependent table does not offer these 2-order same-bench paired readings. Physically unsupported assumptions remain explicit. Experiment design therefore stays **A3 PROSPECTIVE**, not “newly solved because app code acquired.”
