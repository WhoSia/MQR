## Candidate explanandum: not just knowledge about knowledge
Provisional working term 학문학 (studies of disciplinary genesis / general theory of inquiry). Distinguish (a) how an inquiry gains a new object, question, inferential move or operative capability, (b) how a productive research program becomes transmissible and self-renewing, (c) how it becomes a recognized institution, (d) how warrant for claims is acquired. These four can diverge. The word discipline may refer to a field, practice, profession, school, institutional department, or a family of programs; no universal rigid taxonomy yet.
Descriptive snapshot H_t=(Q,C,M,W,T,O): recognized questions and goals; conceptual/representational resources; available material and inferential means; claim-specific warrant/criticism norms; transmission of skills and results; organization/resource access. Not a new universal metalanguage, not an exhaustive ontology or a scalar score. Preserve history and agent-native repertoires. A field might have multiple overlapping histories; boundaries need not be partitions. Change of H is not automatically knowledge progress. A new academic name or department does not establish epistemic novelty; new inquiry can flourish before formal institutions. Distinguish the genesis of a phenomenon from the genesis of a way to inquire into it.
Bold provisional hypothesis: a durable epistemic research program often arises from a new productive feedback between questions, representations, capabilities, criticism and reproduction of competence, not from a single new method or theory alone. Successful results can become new problem-generating resources. A stable discipline may depend on external tools and externally assigned practical goals; "self-renewal" does not mean causal or institutional self-sufficiency, nor exclusive territory, nor a requirement that all scholars agree. Mere self-reproduction, authority or persistence is not epistemic legitimacy. Normative standards differ across inquiry: proof in mathematics, archival interpretation in history, explanation and experiment in natural sciences, justified practical performance in engineering, argument and criticism in philosophy. These are illustrative, not exclusive or necessary definitions.
Hypotheses worth attacking: tool-first, question-first, conceptual articulation, institutional reorganization, practical demand, cross-field recombination. Multiple paths can interact; do not assign universal birth order. Darden and Krauss disagree at a specific boundary: productive recombination of old resources versus novel method as necessary condition. If method includes any productive new arrangement, the latter risks definitional unfalsifiability; require prospective operational criteria for new method and alternative routes. Pinell and Stichweh separate stabilization from conceptual gain; Abbott challenges exclusive clusters and homogeneous validation. Existing MQR 1.54 already anticipated this separation, so no priority claim.
Research program has explanatory and design aspects: identify current bottlenecks that suppress tractable questions; propose plausible tool/representation/institution combinations; test whether new distinctions, new questions and independent uptake actually occur. A candidate hypothetical future discipline is not a prediction unless future criteria fixed independently and outcome observed; no manufactured examples of success. Reflexive test: MQR itself is a research program with artifacts and a user/AI collaboration, not an institutionalized new academic discipline. Research capacity and published durable public uptake remain distinct.

## Comparative case discriminators
* Darden/Maull genetics–cytology: old methods and initially different questions, bridge yields new relation; inspect dated Sutton/Boveri, Morgan and competing precursors before claiming new method unnecessary or a new discipline born. Compare a productive bridge that never becomes separate institution.
* Pinell's medicine: institutional reorganization, multiple knowledge spaces and practical professions. Compare a change in certification/organizational boundaries that leaves earlier valid knowledge intact; no automatic epistemic advancement from institutional creation.
* Abbott's sociology: an established discipline with heterogeneous, recurrent internal methodological divisions. Test whether recurring splits carry novel capabilities or just redescribe debate. For humanities use dated history/philology case, not a physics template. For mathematics distinguish proof-generating techniques and autonomous subject, e.g. already held Poincare/Smale genealogy; prove or disprove actual novel question recognition rather than hypothetical analyst expressibility.
* Compare deliberately failed/never institutionalized programs and research lines with different survival conditions. Freeze historical time, case selection and coding criteria before examining outcome; do not sample only Nobel successes. Need contemporaneous evidence of available methods, new questions, warrant, alternative explanations, community uptake and curricular/institutional events. Two observers independently code evidence; separate unresolved from absent. No full empirical classification yet.
Potential result beyond science: a multidimensional comparison of birth, differentiation, persistence and migration of inquiry, with typed warrants. The field can research its own formation without assuming its own standards are uniquely correct.
