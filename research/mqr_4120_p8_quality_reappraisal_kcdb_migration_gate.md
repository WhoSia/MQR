## Independent documentary rival found in the 2026-10-18 source check
BIPM, *CMC Approval Process* https://www.bipm.org/en/cipm-mra/cmc-approval-process : three pillars of CMC approval are participation in approved scientific comparison, appropriate approved quality management system, and international peer review of CMC claim. Comparison publication or degrees of equivalence are not identical to CMC authorization. Greyed-out/withdrawal and subsequent reinstatement processes have distinct paths.

BIPM, *Report to JCRB September 2019*, https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/43900424/KCDB_Report_2019_September.pdf/7499260c-ec44-8945-4b33-61866ba96490 , explains the expected move to KCDB 2.0 and integration of previously distinct CMC and comparison displays/review process. BIPM, *Report to JCRB March 2020*, https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/43900424/KCDB_Report_2020_March.pdf/1fa8d5b6-b726-eaa5-5252-721c76b12a5d , reports KCDB 2.0 actually implemented, with legacy data migration. This creates a **historical transport problem**: one cannot silently treat a present-day KCDB CMC record as an unchanged original 2015 review record, nor infer a 2018 comparison result caused a later CMC entry without an identified pre/post review trace. The migration alone also does NOT prove loss/corruption of original records.

BIPM KCDB introduction https://www.bipm.org/en/cipm-mra/kcdb distinguishes two record classes: comparison results and recognized CMCs. The first is not a single-step automatically propagating approval for the second.
