## 2. A counterexample to naive 'new operation means new object'
Suppose an experiment creates a stable operational distinction between two test protocols. It does NOT follow logically that there exists one unique latent physical or psychological kind naturally corresponding to the resulting classification. The same observed dissociation can be represented by rival causal/measurement models involving task-specific strategies, different processing components, motivation or measurement failure. The independent existence of 'implicit memory' as a unitary entity is not logically guaranteed by the mere ability to produce differential test results. This is not a claim that implicit memory is unreal; it is a methodological prohibition on prematurely promoting an operational classification into unique ontology.

Formally:
  NEW_TESTABLE_DISTINCTION(P_t→P_u) need not imply
  UNIQUE_LATENT_KIND_IDENTIFIED or SUCCESSFUL_ONTOLOGY_TRANSFER.
MQR R20's coarsest stable partition is a **representation-relative refinement**, not proof that a newly refined class is a new natural kind. This underdetermination survives mathematical stability proofs.

## 3. A historically interesting contrast
**Faraday rotation**: a material apparatus shows a real kinematic regularity, repeatedly demonstrated and transferred to Paris, but Ampère/Faraday disagree on what it proves about internal currents. **Implicit memory**: a protocol contrast makes a previously undelineated target researchable, but its individuation remains under revision. In both cases tests can **enable inquiry before stabilizing ontology**, yet these are not identical mechanisms; electromagnetic rotation is physical motion, priming/dissociation concerns operationalized psychological capacities and different confounds.

This suggests a three-stage research grammar, not a universal historical linear law:
1. **ENTRY**: a previously unavailable experimental or conceptual contrast becomes executable.
2. **STABILIZATION**: independent procedures, cross-context replication, rival probes, and material constraints narrow its interpretation.
3. **ONTOLOGICAL COMMITMENT**: a stronger claim about what entity/property exists is licensed only by independent tests of surviving alternatives.

Stages can overlap, reverse or fail; postulating them is not evidence that every science follows them. The proposed analytic distinction improves MQR's internal claim audit.

## 4. A different possible science with no physical new instrument
Feest's case supplies a challenge to 'instrument invention is necessary for concept creation'. New meaningful distinctions may emerge from reinterpreting **already available tasks or procedures**; new sensor construction is sufficient for some novel tests but not necessary for all concept development. Conversely, one can build a more discriminating apparatus without altering relevant scientific theory vocabulary.

This is a sharp counterexample pair to the one-directional 'instrument -> concept' slogan. Read with Nersessian's model-based reasoning and Kuhn's taxonomic loss, it reinforces that MQR's open horizon is about changes to **questions, skills, interpretation, and object individuation**, each independently assessed.

## 5. Discriminating research demand
To test the historical claim about 1980s implicit memory, obtain contemporaneous research papers establishing conscious recall vs priming dissociation, date operational definitions, and trace disagreement in successive tests. A Feest retrospective/2025 introduction does not itself independently prove original experimental chronology. Compare candidate latent models and a rival single memory-store-plus-retrieval-criterion model before deciding whether the revised concept individuates a novel entity.

No new physical experiment, no all-book read or original 1980s studies witnessed this turn.
