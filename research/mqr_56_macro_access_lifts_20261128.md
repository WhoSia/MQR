# MQR 5.6 — Observational macro autonomy versus intervention-relative autonomy
2026-11-28 KST · MQR 48. Exact finite constructed example; not empirical and not a novelty claim. Cross-level frontier independently reactivated after variable kinetic metric work.
Let micro states a,b,c, macro A={a,b}, B={c}. Weak model transition matrix P=(1/8)[[3,3,2],[1,1,6],[2,2,4]], strong model Q=(1/8)[[2,2,4],[2,2,4],[2,2,4]]. Whenever a macro history ends in A (after at least one transition), conditional distribution within A is uniform because columns a,b coincide. Initially start either uniform in A or at c. Induction gives the same full macro path law for P and Q: every next macro label is a fair coin, irrespective of past. Exact integer enumeration confirms 1,020 word/initial-condition checks through length 8. This is not an inference from only the finite enumeration; the induction proves all lengths.
Yet micro resets to a or b, both reported as A, produce P(B next)=1/4 or 3/4 under P, versus 1/2 for both under Q. Observationally exact macro autonomy does not establish intervention closure. Restriction to permitted lifts matters: if reset is always uniform on A the distinction cannot be observed by that intervention. Conversely micro-specific reset does not invalidate the baseline macro predictive law.
General fixed-step criterion: for transition P, macro indicator matrix C, and admitted micro distributions mu,nu with mu C=nu C, macro next-state prediction is well-defined exactly when (mu-nu)PC=0 for every admitted pair. Arbitrary pure-state lifts recover ordinary strong lumpability; restricted lifts can satisfy weaker conditions. This is elementary linear algebra/Markov lumpability, no claim of priority. A one-step condition for only initially accessible lifts need not certify longer horizons: five-state deterministic witness with a,b,c,d in A and e in B, a->c->e, b->d->d. Both initial and one-step macro labels agree but two-step labels disagree. For passive n-step marginal predictions require annihilation of (mu-nu)P^k C for requested k; full path distributions and composed interventions require the corresponding products with macro projectors/intervention kernels, not just marginals. No global sufficiency claim.
