## I. Moxham and Fyfe (2018)

Accepted manuscript in Drive 1WuCeSSXNTb1yRI41M8yK8Vv9JjApCIPp (89,893 extractable chars); DOI 10.1017/S0018246X17000334. The three institutional transitions explicitly analyzed are collective editorial management in 1752, expert written report practice formalized in 1832, and gatekeeping reform in 1896. Crucial negative criterion: the Royal Society's published 1752–1957 preface stated it did not certify a paper's factual certainty or reasoning on behalf of the corporate body, while significance/singularity/worthy treatment mattered. Late-18th-century consultations could be informal, not equivalent to systematic external anonymous review. So evidence that Wedgwood's 1784 calibration was printed does not entail validated high-temperature calibration. Need read printed 1784 Society 'advertisement' directly rather than rely indefinitely on Moxham/Fyfe secondary paraphrase.

## II. Baldwin (2018)

Published Isis 109(3):538–558, DOI 10.1086/700070, Drive 1I-ifooc4mT_4J6tDtg131UWaod3G3mfQ (96,776 extractable chars). Distinct shift: a rhetoric of scientific autonomy and public accountability, especially controversy over Cold War US federal research funding, made 'peer review' into a marker of scientific legitimacy. This is historically different from the nineteenth-century decision to get expert advice on a particular manuscript. NIH/NSF funding-adjudication and scholarly journal editing should not be silently collapsed. ## IV. Fyfe (2020), formal editor is not the same as functioning editorial management

Centaurus DOI 10.1111/1600-0498.12266, Drive 1EqOltH25pzzc3znSlG5-rEfNt3Up1klx. The 1752 Society eliminated individual editorship as the formal institutional arrangement, not human editorial work: secretaries and committee performed it. 1902 Henry Armstrong argued for a named editor to manage latency and coordination; circa 120 submissions each year, over 2,000 printed pages. At the Royal Society, distributed control and referee reports have historically been independent axes rather than a single indicator of quality.

## VI. Wedgwood falsification plan

1780 unpublished precursor, 1782 proposed clay pyrometer, 1784 scale-bridge, 1786 fabrication amendments are documentually distinct. A correspondence/print chain could establish priority and official circulation without guaranteeing that clay contraction at given final temperature is independent of batch, firing duration, and atmosphere. Rival explanations: research-worthy new operational domain (Chang/Feest), experimentally controlled independent indicators (Ohnesorge), institutional dissemination and status (Moxham/Fyfe and Secord). Do not assert that these rivals yield different predictions until each makes an explicit decision on a shared dated historical observation. Current Royal Society read date for 1784 May 13 and manuscript L&P/8/79/1 is catalog-supported. Individual 1784 referee record not verified. The three historical editorials are NOT a linear progress-to-truth story.
