# MQR 5.4 · MQR 44 · R29 — Prospective discriminators: does question-generation add explanatory power beyond known validation theories?

**2026-11-22 KST.** Research design / preregistration-style decision (NOT experiment already performed). This is intended as a direct answer to the risk of endless agreeable syntheses. MQR 5.4 OPEN.

## 1. Six competing explanations frozen in advance
We have historical Faraday–Ampère / Hachette 1821–22 and implicit/explicit memory psychology around 1980s as two candidate cases. The cross-disciplinary resemblance can be explained at several strengths:
- H0A: **Ordinary confirmation/replication** — one observed effect is replicated; the ensuing arguments are standard underdetermination.
- H0B: **Construct validation** — Feest 2010, Campbell–Fiske 1959, Messick 1995 and Kane 2013 already explain provisional categories, methods and claims-to-use without any extra framework.
- H0C: **Social transmission** — Secord 2004 / material-skill diffusion explain introduction of apparatus and publication; new question repertoire is an analyst's retrospective interpretation.
- H0D: **Selection and resources** — Zollman/Avin/Gross & Bergstrom explain research trajectories by networks and incentives; no needed 'science-bridge' primitive.
- H1Q: **Question-capability change** — independently dated adoption of an operation permits a new formerly unformulable/untestable contrast, and the previously adequate inference/translation contract becomes inadequate specifically under that enlarged task domain.
- H1O: **Ontological reorganization** — the new contrast leads eventually to a robust independently confirmed natural-kind/mechanism reclassification; this is stronger than H1Q and does not logically follow.

Claim scope: H1Q can be true while H1O remains false; H0A-C may still suffice even if H1Q describes events. Distinguish historical occurrence from *unique explanatory value*.

## 2. Exactly what would discriminate H1Q from a familiar relabeling
**Pre-contact frozen stage:** from dated sources before method diffusion, inventory actual admissible questions, apparatus, preparations, language and uncertainty (not the modern historian's labels). Independent historian should be able to cite this inventory, and absence of a term in a single letter is NOT proof that an idea was inaccessible.

**Intervention/change:** source-dated adoption of instrument/procedure **with actual execution**, not just sending a package or citing an article. Faraday/Hachette 19 Nov witness meets a limited physical reproduction condition but not yet the claim of newly formulated downstream science questions.

**Discriminating new contrast:** identify a question whose corresponding outcome differences could not be tested under the earlier operational repertoire, and later became testable. Prefer explicit actor statement and a subsequent negative control. The contrast must entail at least one different prospective outcome for H1Q vs H0A/B/C.

**Outcome:** record new question asked, independent apparatus or experimental design, rival explanations tested, and potential loss/reform of an old concept. Unsuccessful exploratory work is not zero by fiat and successful new instrument is not automatic ontological progress.

**Cross-domain check:** apply exactly the same protocol to a cognitive psychological construct (implicit memory), where concept categories may change without building a novel physical apparatus. If the protocol presupposes an entirely new machine, it fails to generalize and should be narrowed to engineering/method diffusion.

## 3. Negative controls that can actually defeat the ambitious claim
- **N0:** a new instrument is introduced but preserves all old operational distinctions and does not enable any new relevant question; old bridge remains sufficient. (R20's mathematics predicts this under fixed-state assumptions.)
- **N1:** the allegedly novel question was described or successfully attempted in earlier contemporaneous records; reject the chronology and novel-capability attribution.
- **N2:** post-diffusion theory divergence is fully resolved by shared calibration or ordinary prediction differences, with no revision of question/operation space.
- **N3:** a new psychological test contrasts tasks but once method factors are controlled the supposed new construct difference vanishes. This challenges object discovery (H1O) but may leave inquiry-enabling concept (H1Q) intact.
- **N4:** an apparent research institution 'innovation increase' is explained by selection and reward effects while independent long-term epistemic yield falls. (R22 exact conditional example; no empirical estimate.)
- **N5:** two reports prove no independent replication because they have the same ancestor; count correct evidence provenance.

## 4. Distinguish authority from productivity at each stage
Use four separate authority columns, not a composite scientific-progress scalar:
1. Method became **available**; dated implementation record (ABILITY).
2. Phenomenon/contrast is **reproducible**, independent evidence ancestry (REPLICATION).
3. Proposed inference **discriminates surviving rivals**, result-level predictive evidence (INFERENCE).
4. Claim about object/ontology **transports to relevant novel regimes** under stable measurement meaning and appropriate assumptions (ONTOLOGY).
Kane's use-validity and ethics constitute a *fifth* decision branch, not a mere ontological footnote, for selection cases; scientific measurement progress cannot by itself justify decisions about persons.

## 6. Acceptance and stopping rules
- Academic paper prospect: a concrete source-dated case with a discriminating test and an explicit rival-outcome table is promising as a careful comparative historical philosophy manuscript.
- Synthesis-only: if examples are compelling but H1Q cannot be separated from established frameworks, publish-worthy contribution may be a rigorous conceptual clarification and critical reconstruction without claiming theory novelty.
- HOLD: cannot reconstruct the pre-contact question set, or no independent evidence of post-contact question invention. Do not force agreement by retrospective category injection.
- BACKTRACK: if sources are not available, leave exact missing-register entries and pursue another frontier with held evidence instead of infinite retry of a paywalled edition.
