## What to learn
Cross-measurement coherence and added predictive information conditional on a prior measurement are different quantities. An index can give useful convergent evidence while adding little prediction when the other index already measures the same evolving phenomenon; alternatively redundant prediction can arise from common confounding and does not establish shared mechanism. Do not conflate the descriptive association with structural invariance. Next investigate multi-dimensional relation learning on gridded data, causal time-series methods (Runge et al. 2019 PCMCI), independent source vintage, season-dependent relations and block bootstrap uncertainty with explicit temporal assumptions. Distinguish expert-compressed index comparisons from genuinely learned concept formation.
