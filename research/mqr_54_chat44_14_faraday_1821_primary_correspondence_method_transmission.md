# MQR 5.4 · MQR 44 · Record 14 — Three Faraday letters: contemporary witnesses to concept revision, instrument refinement, and method transmission

**Date:** 2026-11-22 KST. Epistemic status: **PRIMARY LETTER TRANSCRIPTION DIRECTLY READ**, with manuscript provenance provided by editorial collection. Not direct paleographic verification of the physical manuscript; citations are scholarly digital transcriptions. Phase 5.4 OPEN.

## 1. Three new primary witnesses, now materially stronger than retrospective summaries
1. **12 September 1821, Michael Faraday to Charles-Gaspard De La Rive**, Ɛpsilon Faraday0148 https://epsilon.ac.uk/view/faraday/letters/Faraday0148, transcript from BPUG MS 2311 f.61–62. Faraday explicitly recasts apparent attractions/repulsions of needle and conductor as circular motions around a conducting wire; says the new motion can be realized with conductor or pole rotating; draws connections to coils and bar magnets. In the same letter he explicitly withholds certainty that currents exist inside an ordinary magnet despite analogies. The latter is a decisive negative control against the fallacy 'new instrument => full ontology validated'. The letter mentions De La Rive's apparatus and experiment, so single-inventor heroization is also inappropriate.
2. **18 October 1821, Faraday to Ludwig Wilhelm Gilbert**, Ɛpsilon Faraday0153 https://epsilon.ac.uk/view/faraday/letters/Faraday0153, editorial transcription attributed to Deutsches Postmuseum. Faraday reports attaching his experimental paper, reducing the apparatus to a small glass-tube assembly with simple hook support, and notes he is unable to follow important German-language science because he does not understand German. Apparatus redesign and language-limited knowledge transmission are *coeval* constraints, not an imagined modern analogy.
3. **16 November 1821, Faraday to De La Rive**, Ɛpsilon Faraday0158 https://epsilon.ac.uk/view/faraday/letters/Faraday0158, BPUG MS 2311 f.63. Faraday explicitly reports sending the paper **and a small physical rotation apparatus**, describes how to operate it and cautions that electrical contacts must be good. Thus material method transfer is witnessed in the source; recipient's actual use or a subsequent novel question is **not yet documented** by this letter.

## 2. Exact distinctions: what changed?
- `CONCEPT_REFORM` (Sep 12): attraction/repulsion vocabulary reinterpreted around a more encompassing circular motion; NOT that a unique correct modern electromagnetic ontology was already established.
- `APPARATUS_MODIFICATION` (Oct 18): reduced device support, size and setup; this witnesses engineering affordance change, but not quantified reliability or universal transfer success.
- `METHOD_DISTRIBUTION` (Nov 16): explicit sending of apparatus and operational instructions, beyond exchange of propositions. Subsequent user uptake remains unverified.
- `KNOWLEDGE_ACCESS_LIMIT` (Oct 18): native-language restriction; whether that specifically caused a scientific conceptual difference needs separate historical counterfactual evidence.
- `ONTOLOGY_WITHHELD` (Sep 12): doubt about Ampère's claim regarding currents inside conventional magnets survives even with observed rotation.

Chronology is supported by primary letters and by Steinle (2016, English translation of 2005 German book), chapter 6, held PDF `1-kviuErWDAEJMRlcbACBX7FbLR55hV1G`; compare Royal Institution https://www.rigb.org/explore-science/explore/blog/birth-electric-motion and David Gooding's prior work https://doi.org/10.1177/016224399001500202.

## 3. Strongest MQR case-specific proposition
**Proposition (historical witness, narrower than universal law):** Within ten weeks of a new electromagnetic effect, an identifiable investigator used the effect to reorganize interpretive categories, refine a reproducible apparatus, and send methodological means to another investigator while retaining explicit skepticism about a broader causal ontology. *This is directly supported by dated personal correspondence.* It gives a real sequence for MQR's `Q/I/E/semantic` axes rather than a synthetic proxy. The proposition does NOT establish that the recipient performed a test, discovered a new phenomenon or accepted Faraday's theory.

## 4. A severe conceptual competitor
Gooding (1990) had already analyzed the Faraday motor case as materially situated learning and the invention of communicable experimental procedures. Steinle (1995, 2016) also gave historically fine-grained explanations of the 'simple case' and representational changes. These are **substantial prior art**, so MQR cannot announce the procedure-communication thesis as unprecedented. The legitimate synthesis is to connect independently sourced primary events to explicit typed science-interface obligations, then test on DIFFERENT scientific traditions. The dated letters are a stronger evidence grade than modern secondary retellings, not a novelty claim.
