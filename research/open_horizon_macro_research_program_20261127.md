## Proposed macro architecture, deliberately revisable

Use a descriptive snapshot S_t=(L_t,M_t,A_t,Q_t,N_t) for language/mathematics, models, executable intervention repertoire, recognized questions, and epistemic norms. It is a projection of the richer earlier MQR state, not a canonical universal ontology. Evidence history, material/social resources, and actual agents must be retained in any executable case model. Relations between these components are typed; they are not nested sets or scalar progress. A practice may transform any component, and feedback may change the others. World resistance restricts proposed interpretations but does not uniquely determine one science. Reflexivity matters: even this descriptive vocabulary may be inadequate and must be revisable.

Four connected research explananda: (i) generation of new questions and methods, (ii) partial bridges between different scientific practices, (iii) world-facing selection and warranted retention/loss of structures, (iv) revision of significance and authority standards, including the meta-framework itself. The program should explain why the same operation may enlarge one capability while losing another, and how a bridge can become a new field, not merely assign audit labels.

## Prospective research discriminators

Keep formal expressibility, practical attainability, historical recognition, and evidential warrant apart; the first can hold without the other three. A proposed explanation of question genesis must identify a contemporaneous obstacle, a change in representational or material resources, a newly usable distinction, and an independent consequence. Rival explanations include mere renaming, preexisting executable capacity, hindsight embedding, improved precision, and institutional change without material discrimination. Temporal precedence alone does not establish historical causation; the necessity of a specific route may be unidentifiable.

Use distinct case families rather than forcing all into one template:
- mathematical bridge (Grosholz/de Rham): proof and structural transfer, no physical test required for mathematical conclusion;
- historical physical generation (Faraday/Maxwell, Bell): dated native questions, theoretical structure, experiment design and independent confirmation; avoid collapsing chronology into causation;
- cross-practice relation (X-ray/NMR, tRNA): distinguish shared target, changed regime, independent evidence and productive bridge;
- norm and significance changes (Kuhn, Friedman, Kitcher, Hacking): test whether changes can be justified under partially shifting standards without a privileged meta-language. Explicitly compare the strongest rival, not an invented weak rival. Existing 5.3 reset already documents these collisions.
