## 2. Pre-stated question and author-level limitations (1956)

1956 introduction: investigators explicitly sought to test protein-label incorporation in higher-plant fractions and to find a convenient system yielding a purified labeled protein for further study. Tobacco mosaic virus was an attractive candidate because of its relatively easy purification. The research program was therefore driven partly by **experimental tractability and target choice**, not solely a neutral search across all conceivable mechanisms.

1956 summary: incorporation into bulk proteins of intact leaf disks and isolated fractions was reported; oxygen and light had distinct effects in chloroplast-associated measurements; no viral synthesis was detected in these particular **cell-free fractions**, even though labeled virus occurred in intact infected cells. These findings have different adjudicative targets:
- bulk protein-label incorporation (positive under stated assay);
- relation to photosynthetic energy (conditional interpretation);
- synthesis of tobacco mosaic virus in that defined cell-free system (negative in the tested setup).

**Do not rewrite the last result as 'protein synthesis impossible in cell-free tobacco preparations'**, or first result as 'isolated TMV synthesis demonstrated.' Neither is supported.

## 3. 1958 does not retrospectively repair every 1956 negative

1958 article reports a rat-liver pH 5 RNA-associated amino-acid intermediate and conditional transfer to microsomal protein. Its distinct source of evidential strength is stage-specific RNA labeling and subsequent transfer under explicit conditions. That finding does not turn 1956's virus-production target into a retrospective success and does not prove that the 1956 preparation should have supported TMV synthesis.

At the same time, shared authors and broader macromolecular synthesis research constitute a defensible **genealogical connection**, not proof that the 1956 unsuccessful virus target caused the later pH 5 RNA experiment. Historical causal influence requires contemporaneous laboratory records or first-person citations documenting an actual transfer of methods, personnel or research questions. Source overlap alone is not that evidence.

## 4. Implication for rival philosophical accounts

A one-dimensional history 'old experiments failed → new concept invented → success' is false for these sources: positive and negative results coexist **within** one 1956 program, and later positive evidence concerns a **different** question. In particular:
- **Kitcher**: research significance and available questions may change without all local negative results becoming discoveries.
- **Brigandt/Feest/Nersessian/Rheinberger**: goals, operational targets, models and tools can coevolve; our source findings fit these accounts and do not defeat them.
- **Lari**: one can criticize a specific method relative to its existing aim without thereby settling whether the aim deserves pursuit in the first place.
- **MQR 4.53/4.56**: choosing/valuing an experiment and judging achieved evidence are typed, separate processes; already prior art in MQR.

**Novelty gate:** None of these strong theories is contradicted by the present facts. This is a source-level *negative result* for claims that MQR has already discovered a new universal problem-generative mechanism, but an improved concrete comparative history.

## 5. Prospective protocol, now stricter

- Time-slice the **1956** and **1958** documents separately; preserve author names, sample/tissue, operations, assay endpoint, immediate evidence, alternative explanations, question significance and contemporaneous confidence.
- Where a specific concept is historically new, require *dated actor text* of a new question or operation, rather than a later textbook label.
- Test whether later data were available at decision time; never import 1958 mechanisms into a 1956 ex-ante choice state.
- Include a genuine *nonadoption* control (Weyl/Brouwer; Kish Bar-On baseline) and an intervention/significance disagreement with fixed endpoints if available.
- Ask for an account that correctly separates **pursuit, local warrant, later uptake and conceptual retooling** without stipulating a universal success ranking.
