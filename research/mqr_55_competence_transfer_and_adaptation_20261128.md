Diagrams' representational conventions and roles diverged under different research tasks. The paper notes the original weak-coupling perturbative procedure did not directly work in strongly interacting contexts, yet diagrams were adapted for qualitative classification and other roles. Held Galison (2010), Trading with the Enemy, Drive 10OCF75Sqg__CgIVPmsQH3x3PYrGIS1dw, opening pp25–27 and pp43–44 selected read. Distinct experimental, theoretical and instrumental cultures and diagrammatic manipulation as a local symbolic language; explicitly cites Kaiser's work on piecemeal diagram transport. Prior art therefore already recognizes local translation, modified practice and partial overlap. No novelty for that generic proposition.
Historical contrast with Cantor 1872→1879: an analytical proof problem leads to operations on exceptional sets and autonomous questions concerning the sets. In the electron-gas case an existing problem obtains a modified technique, then the technique extends to related questions. In neither case is chronology alone sufficient to identify a unique causal pathway. In the Kaiser case, different modes of receiving a representation may transmit different capabilities. Distinguish reproducibility of the source use, successful adaptation in a recipient domain, independent recognition of new questions and institutional reproduction. Not a compulsory progression.

A prospective transfer evaluation could freeze a source task family A, a recipient family B with different constraints, and criteria for results appropriate to each domain; give recipients equivalent source materials, vary mentorship or opportunities for independent exploration under ethical and resource constraints, then separately evaluate source reproduction, recipient adaptation and unprompted, independently assessable new question production. Measure learning and prior expertise; include alternative methods, equal access and independent evaluators. A novel-looking question supplied by the experimenter is not evidence of participant-generated question recognition. A newly proposed question need not be original to the world, so record local novelty, field novelty and validity separately. Transfer of a correct result can occur without a change in capacity. Success on B is not evidence of unbounded generality. Text-only success is an admissible positive result, not an exception to exclude. No participants recruited, no experiment performed; this is a proposed evaluation, not evidence.
Specific comparison criteria for further historical cases: dated source use, dated recipient use, explicit rule or representational change, subsequent research question(s), independent uptake, and positive evidence of shared training or organizational structure where relevant. No genuine negative case of a technique never establishing an autonomous specialty verified here; Gell-Mann's extension shows adaptation within an established area, not absence across all institutional scales.
