## Crucial epistemic correction
'KCDB publication' is not synonymous with 'KCDB CMC approval or revision'. Official 2018 results show an institutional *public reporting* consequence of the revised evaluation. A further named-provider **service claim** change requires first-party CMC row ID, its approval history and documentary explicit relationship to K11.2/K12.2; publication dates alone do not identify this. Thus MQR 4.120 currently has a documented OAWG->published comparison chain but not OAWG->individual CMC change or real changed calibration practice.
