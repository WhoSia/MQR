## A stronger conditional research thesis, explicitly disputed

**Candidate (not yet original):** an experimental practice makes a previously unavailable scientific question 'materially askable' when an actor obtains a **repeatable manipulative contrast** whose outcome distinguishes some physically relevant rival explanations, and retains provenance/negative controls adequate for independent criticism. Such contrasts may precede a mature conceptual theory or arise from tentative theory-driven expectations. This is neither purely a language expansion nor a pure sensory observation count.

This phrasing must be tested against strong counterexamples:
1. **Apparatus artifact positive:** an apparent new transient vanishes on controlling contact or instrument; 'new question' may still remain as a question about apparatus, not a robust new physical kind.
2. **Theory-first scientific question:** a feasible question may be asked before its answering instrument exists (Crick's adaptor, Maxwell's models); requiring positive data before a question becomes *askable* may be too strong. Distinguish linguistically formulable, actionable, and scientifically answerable.
3. **Underdetermined world:** the same witness may fit genuinely different mechanism models; no universal convergence or explanatory identity follows.
4. **Failed exploratory variation:** Faraday's Sept 24 unsuccessful coils did not establish universal absence. The epistemically valuable negative result is source- and sensitivity-scoped.
5. **Community-mediated legitimacy:** material experiments can be executable but not adopted as worthwhile or trustworthy by a community; evidence rules and social organization matter (Masterman, Friedman, Longino, Star, Galison).

**Conclusion:** do not flatten linguistic conceivability, operational executability, credible evidence and communal salience into a stage score. Probe which of these changes and which may decline. This is a known philosophical research theme with robust prior work; only a discriminating historical or physical failure case can support an original MQR mechanism.
