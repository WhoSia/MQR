## 1. The mismatch that the research must not conceal

MQR 43 [records03–05](mqr_54_chat43_05_alignment_blind_active_query_learner.md) construct a conditional operational bridge: two native action alphabets are joined, a new mixed word reaches an old-lab-inaccessible outcome in one finite model, and an active learner locates an operational alignment class without being told the true alignment. Those are transparent *technical* achievements.

They still presuppose: an analyst's common state set, both laboratories' operators and initial preparations, a fixed class of six bridge hypotheses, an externally provided query interface and a simulator oracle. The learner optimizes **inside a prefabricated conceptual space**. Claiming that it created a scientific category or revised its notion of evidence would conflate search with the formation of a new question. It is not enough to note that the combined syntax contains words absent from either native syntax: that much is a formal grammar fact.

## 3. A substantive positive research program, not another mechanical checklist

The proper MQR 5.4 target is neither simply to recover the unknown bijection between two laboratories' hidden state labels nor to generate a mathematically new operation. Ask instead:

**When does a jointly designed intervention change what the investigators take the phenomenon, the admissible question, and the expected explanation to be, and how can that change become vulnerable to an independent physical outcome?**

A rigorous next research object is a **time-indexed inquiry sequence** rather than a frozen state machine. It must describe (a) each investigator's initial native problem and what would count as an answer; (b) the actual artifact or procedure that makes a previously unavailable experiment executable; (c) the unexpected native result and its live rival explanations; (d) an explicit revision to the investigators' own category, instrument, method, or explanatory target; and (e) a prospectively frozen new prediction that the revised research program enables and a rival would fail.

A *positive* conceptual-change case would not merely emit high instead of low. It would make a new operational distinction stable and useful across additional tasks, while documenting that its content was not already available as an unexercised word in the original lab's grammar. An unsuccessful change may still produce a worthwhile empirically constrained account of why a new category was not required.

A *negative* control is equally crucial: the same records can be re-explained by simply translating two old native categories or selecting one pre-existing output channel without changing any substantive question. If that rival accounts for the same independently tested consequences, the strong 'concept creation' verdict must remain HOLD.

## 4. Research-director decision and programme navigation

**KEEP** the MQR 43 native-action program as a transparent formal sub-result of the *possible sciences* branch. **BACKTRACK** from progressively longer permutations unless a physical or historical discriminant demands them. **EXPAND** the *paradigm genesis* branch through specific documented episodes where laboratory practices and conceptual targets co-developed, beginning with the held 2012 chapters and the 2026-02-05 founder's manuscript. A new open-ended question can be worthwhile without being an unprecedented mathematical theorem; faithful attribution and careful reconstruction are valid scholarship under [MQR prior-art doctrine](../doctrine/research-contribution-prior-art.md).

**Next DFS experiment:** with independently controlled native models and safe specimen handoffs, compare (i) passive crossed readout, (ii) cross action composition, and (iii) an alternative interpretation that requires no new conceptual primitive; preregister outcomes and validate that transfer preserves relevant preparations. An inexpensive classroom-safe token exercise may validate protocol logic but is *not* evidence of empirical alien-science contact or a world-discovered ontology. Actual physics-facing claims require real independently measured and calibrated instrumentation.

**Next BFS:** compare a few historically grounded investigative episodes with a causal question: what changed first—concept, apparatus, intervention, or explanatory aim—and which new physical tests subsequently made the revision useful? Do not silently upgrade a simulated held-out prediction into independent world contact.
