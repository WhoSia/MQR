# MQR-4.119 P3 — Contestable Adjudication and Preserving Dissent

2026-10-17; 4.119 ACTIVE. Predecessors P1/P2 audited before this contract. No human assessor or R0/R+/E outcome has been collected.

## The new objection
A benchmark designer who specifies anomalous cases and the 'right question' can silently make one epistemic style the definition of scientific understanding. Blinded adjudication helps but does not independently legitimate the ontology or the right to exclude alternative questions. The evaluative court must itself be contestable.

## Multi-perspective independent court
- A domain-method reviewer assesses instrument and analytical feasibility, without knowing which strategy generated the response.
- An inference reviewer assesses whether an additional observation actually discriminates rival explanations.
- A provenance reviewer checks whether claimed physical standards and certificates are authenticated, not merely named.
- A critical challenger is permitted to dispute the case formulation, available actions, and relevance criteria in original language.
These are **roles specified**, not reviewers recruited.

## Per-case non-scalar record
Preserve separately recognition, diagnosis, discriminating observation, question revision, confidence calibration, and provenance. Each role records verdict, source/observation supporting it, uncertainty, alternative explanation, and objection to the scoring framework. No majority rule automatically converts a normative contest to scientific truth. A dissenting interpretation is recorded even if other reviewers agree.

## Reciprocal adversarial cases
- A solution fits an expected spectral-overlap label but proposes a test that cannot discriminate overlap from baseline drift. Recognizing a keyword does not earn diagnosis/test credit.
- A solution requests authenticated stock custody and rejects the benchmark's forced numerical inference. It may be scientifically justified despite not offering the expected concentration.
- A formal R+ strategy offers a sound new test not among prewritten rubric choices. The court must evaluate it without secretly changing the model after results.
- An exemplar-based E strategy draws an inappropriate analogy: explicit calculation can defeat it.
These are constructed conceptual tests; they are not observed failures by participants.

## Stopping and HOLD semantics
A source deficiency blocks an empirical claim, not a debate about whether a question has been excluded. Separate TEST_UNEXECUTED, EVIDENCE_MISSING, DISPUTED_RELEVANCE, RULES_REVISED_AFTER_TEST, and UNSUPPORTED_INFERENCE. Never produce one global PASS or a scalar ranking of epistemic persons.

## P4 test
Implement a finite executable checker that ensures each judgement retains its grounds and dissent; compare a naive majority reduction against a non-collapsing record. A checker can enforce documentation but cannot establish the normative legitimacy of the court.
