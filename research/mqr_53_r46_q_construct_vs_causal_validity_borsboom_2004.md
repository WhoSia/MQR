## Methodological distinction

Construct-theoretic coherence, multitrait cross-method discrimination, and attribute→measurement causal influence are DIFFERENT claims. A system may pass several predictive/observational tests while the intended arrow 'measured attribute causes score' is false. Conversely a real causal measured attribute may be causally influential yet require careful assessment of interpretative generality and real-world test uses. This distinction does not settle social/ethical test-use validity in Messick's broader sense.

## Transparent toy witness, NOT new mathematics

Let U be independent fair Bernoulli(1/2). We observe triples (X,Y1,Y2). At baseline:

- H_A (proposed attribute is causally productive): X=U; Y1=X; Y2=X.
- H_B (attribute is a noncausal correlate): X=U; Y1=U; Y2=U. The common cause U drives both observed indicator scores and X; there is no X→Y arrow.

In ordinary observations, BOTH models yield only (0,0,0) with probability 1/2 and (1,1,1) with probability 1/2. Thus no purely observational statistic, including arbitrary sample size and matching MTMM-type correlations on these three variables, can discriminate H_A from H_B *without further assumptions*. This is a deliberately **degenerate** example with zero positivity and near-perfect observational measurement, for logic alone.

The `do(X=0)` intervention differs in the assumed structural models:
- H_A: (Y1,Y2)=(0,0) with probability 1.
- H_B: (Y1,Y2)=(U,U), so (0,0) and (1,1) each probability 1/2.

A selectively manipulable X and valid model assumptions are required for this distinguishing intervention. It may be impossible or conceptually incoherent to 'set' the psychological attribute while holding U fixed; do NOT claim the toy supplies an ethically or physically feasible experimental design.

## Adversarial decision

The simple observational-equivalence witness *reiterates existing causal measurement and identification ideas*, including Borsboom et al. (2004), NOT an original MQR theory. Source read reveals that R46's new terminology around identity of measurement targets, validity, and investigation was already pre-empted by Feest, Garner and Borsboom. No philosophical victory over the best competing accounts established.

A genuine MQR improvement needs an independently chosen empirical or archival case where all these strong accounts are **actually insufficient**, an explicit predictive difference with stated feasible interventions, and a contemporaneous rationale. Until then: **NOVELTY HOLD; MQR R46-Q subclaim 'observational convergence alone establishes target validity' REJECTED**.
