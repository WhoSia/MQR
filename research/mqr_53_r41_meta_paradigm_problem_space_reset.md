# MQR R41 — Return to the philosophy of scientific change itself

**2026-11-04. Research status: strategic thesis reconstruction and prior-art critique, NOT a newly established meta-theory.**

## 1. The recovered ambition (from actual MQR archives)

Targeted rereading of Drive *MQR 1.md* (Aug 2026) and *MQR 37.md* (Oct–Nov 2026), with a recent history check in *MQR 35.md*, recovers three warnings and a positive program:

1. **MQR 0.11: meta-language self-sealing.** A language for translating or judging competing scientific approaches can itself foreclose distinctions it is unable to express. That regulator must also be fallible and criticizable.
2. **MQR 0.13: maximal discrimination is not maximal scientific progress.** Idealization and productive coarse-graining can create understanding; reversibility matters but cannot simply be equated with greater factual content.
3. **MQR 5.0–5.3: formalisms and isolated measurement audits are supporting instruments, not the explanandum.** The overarching question concerns how scientific communities come to *be able to pose and rationally pursue questions not previously available to them*.
4. *MQR 37.md* explicitly asks how to recognize potential concepts not presently conceivable and emphasizes the historical evolution of science's possible questions rather than only thermometric calibration.

The prior working name **A Historical Theory of Scientific Problem-Space Evolution** is already present in archived MQR discussions, not a new R41 invention. The loop is material/social practice ↔ representational resources ↔ viable problem spaces ↔ outcomes and renewed practices, rather than a unidirectional stage theory.

## 2. The triple-level correction

**Level I — Theory revision**: change in scientific propositions, parameters, and causal models.
**Level II — Scientific matrix/style revision**: change in what scientists can legitimately ask, test, model, measure, classify, and count as a solution.
**Level III — Philosophy-of-science revision**: change in the account of what scientific rationality, objectivity, evidence, progress and change *are*.

Calling Level III a "paradigm of paradigms" is only a metaphor. Kuhn's paradigm is already not merely a theory; his exemplars, values, tools, training and disciplinary matrices span much of Level II. Merely positing a level above him is **not originality**.

## 3. Strongest philosophical rivals — directly checked

- **Kuhn (1962/1970, 1977)**: changing paradigms includes changing exemplars, methodological values and problem significance. Scientific revolution may involve Kuhn-loss rather than monotonic preservation. See [Stanford Encyclopedia 2025 revision](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/thomas-kuhn/). Kuhn's own postscript and *Essential Tension* belong to the next primary-reading packet. Do not strawman him as 'scientific theories replace one another'.
- **Michael Friedman (2001), *Dynamics of Reason***: directly retrieved full Drive [book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JAnxiRCtjaDEeTI_ZG1iLaCyLqKVmsNt/view) and read prefatory program, passages on constitutive principles, *Rationality, Revolution and the Community of Inquiry*, and instrumental versus communicative rationality. His relativized constitutive a priori already asks how framework changes can rationally be discussed, and gives philosophy a *distinctive meta-scientific role*. This heavily subsumes a generic MQR claim 'rationality's standards evolve'. [Publisher account](https://web.stanford.edu/group/cslipublications/cslipublications/site/1575862921.shtml).
- **Ian Hacking / A. Crombie, styles of reasoning**: styles historically constitute admissible objects, assertions, proof/measurement standards. Merely extending 'paradigm' to what questions can arise is not novel. **Martin Kusch (2010)** critiques Hacking for unclear individuation of styles and apparent relativism, DOI 10.1016/j.shpsa.2010.03.007. [Article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0039368110000191). This critique should be read before proposing a new meta-style.
- **Philip Kitcher (1993), *The Advancement of Science***: directly retrieved full Drive [book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-dqXnIObLoPTVXFyeRAKCG0Astd9oqM-/view), examined passages on Darwinian *individual practice* versus cross-generational *consensus practice*, significance of questions, and bounded rationality of finite inquirers. A four-stage 'question space evolves' mechanism without actual normative difference will be subsumed by his account of cognitive practice and progress.
- **Longino (1990), *Science as Social Knowledge***: [Drive holding](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OvZH1Y_fhcFWTFpCit80JELOdRxLrtCc/view) confirmed but Drive extraction yielded empty text, so **NOT directly read** in this research unit. External literature on transformative criticism is a challenge to treating social structure as passive context.
- **Chang (2004; 2020/21)**: epistemic iteration, complementary science, pragmatic pluralism and using history for alternative scientific possibilities. [Open 2021 essay](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10838-020-09512-8). The mere claim that forgotten problem paths can be re-opened is already Chang.
- **Pickering (1995)** material practice/agency/resistance, **Massimi (2022)** perspectival realism, and **Rheinberger (1997)** experimental systems also occupy interaction of material conditions and conceptual emergence; these require direct argument comparison in later units, not decorative citation.

## 4. Candidate macro question, intentionally adversarial

> **Under what historically describable conditions do inquiries alter not only their answers, but their own criteria for generating, recognizing, ranking and reopening legitimate questions?**

Different from the weaker: 'question spaces change over history', which is already Kuhn/Hacking/Kitcher/Friedman. What is additionally needed to earn paper status:

A. Describe **a concrete rival theory's verdict** for some change; it must not already be a Kuhnian paradigm revision, Friedman-style constitutive-rule negotiation, Hacking-style reasoning style, or Kitcher-style practice adjustment.
B. Show **at least one case of a previously excluded question becoming admissible** without simply a new discovery that the rival can easily absorb.
C. Show a **reverse case** where a new method or instrument expands the question space but does not improve scientific understanding; avoid inevitable-progress or maximal-question-count assumptions.
D. Examine how a community's *criteria for problem significance* change while the most powerful existing science remains successful: anomaly-trigger narratives alone are too narrow, but Kuhn/Kitcher may already account for this.
E. Demonstrate a **non-circular comparative standard** without smuggling a fixed higher 'meta-paradigm'. No demand for a complete value-neutral algorithm; contestable situated judgments may be the appropriate answer.

**Candidate status: OPEN QUESTION, NOT THEORY.** It could fail completely against Friedman–Kitcher–Hacking, and then the appropriate outcome is to re-read and refine the philosophical target.

## 5. Concrete opposition: the 'more questions' fallacy

Imagine an artificial research regime that permits every grammatical sentence as a funded research question. It has a strictly larger formal question space yet wastes all available resources on scientifically sterile inquiries. Conversely a successful approximation scheme may discard physically real differences while enabling discoveries impossible under a non-idealizing total description. Thus `question-space inclusion` is **not a monotone norm of progress**; explicit goal sensitivity and path-dependence are indispensable. These are *constructed objections*, not original counterexamples with priority claim. The point follows already from MQR-0.13 and literature on idealization and epistemic significance.

A plausible guiding target is to explain **how criteria of significance and methods of admissibility become contestable within ongoing science**, rather than to maximize possible questions.

## 6. Research program and controls

- **R42A** Kuhn *The Essential Tension* (primary) vs Friedman *Dynamics of Reason* Part III and discussion on constitutive principles. What counts as justified transformative revision for each?
- **R42B** Kitcher chapters on significance/progress and Hacking/Kusch individuation debate. What exactly counts as a change in a practice, as opposed to a new style or matrix?
- **R42C** choose *two different scientific histories* (e.g. nineteenth-century electrodynamics, genetics and the origin of statistics as method); specify their own contemporary questions and their losses. Avoid a one-case grand theory.
- **R42D** test a hard negative case: larger experimental freedom with reduced understanding, or maintained mathematical technique across major taxonomic rupture. Show which rival predicts which answer.
- **Novelty kill gate:** if best existing rival or a simple combination explains the cases and the meta-criteria, formally retire a grand-theory claim. Preserve the comparative philosophical paper as a genuine research result, not as forced novelty.
