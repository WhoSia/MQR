Date 2026-11-05. This is a **synthetic methodological counterexample**; it does NOT reproduce a Feest psychological experiment nor make a claim about identifiable latent kinds in real data. Its main effect is to demonstrate that merely observing convergent indicators is logically insufficient.

## Explicit algebraic toy construction

Let U be a fair Bernoulli(1/2) variable and let q and v be binary *designed probes*. In the **observational baseline** q=v=0, three distinct hypothetical structural models give precisely the same joint observable distribution:
- H1, common stable target: Y1=U, Y2=U.
- H2, context-dependent operational target: Y1=U, Y2=U XOR q.
- H3, shared readout artifact: Y1=U, Y2=U XOR v.

In all cases `P(Y1=0,Y2=0)=P(Y1=1,Y2=1)=1/2` at baseline, and all other pairs have probability zero. Consequently any statistic of baseline observational (Y1,Y2) has the **same distribution** under all H1/H2/H3; more observations of the same baseline alone cannot discriminate the models.

Under probe (q=1,v=0): H1 and H3 yield equal readouts; H2 yields opposite readouts.
Under probe (q=0,v=1): H1 and H2 yield equal readouts; H3 yields opposite readouts.
These signatures are distinct across the two probes, **conditional on the toy equations and effective selective interventions**. The probes do not claim a realistic biological intervention or independently validate the concepts 'target', 'context', or 'artifact'. With unknown confounding, partial compliance, measurement error, or ill-defined intervention targets, the identification result does not transfer automatically.

## Novelty kill

The toy demonstrates only a *logical underdetermination* and an elementary way to break it with designed interventions. The need for multiple discriminating methods and competing experimental explanations was known in the 1950s and is treated directly by Feest (2005, 2025) and Campbell/Fiske (1959). It is not an original result and provides **no incremental explanatory advantage** for MQR.

A genuinely new MQR contribution requires a historically or empirically grounded case where a strong alternative (Feest's converging operations, Campbell–Fiske MTMM, contextualism/measurement theory) fails a precommitted discrimination task, while a new method succeeds. Without that, stop extending the MQR framework and treat the strong previous theories as the correct explanation.
