## Proposed MQR interpretive test (not an original historical discovery)
Separate (a) mathematical expressibility, (b) identification of a prediction difference, (c) availability of a physically implementable instrument/protocol, (d) witnessed contemporaneous recognition, (e) independently corroborated claim. A theory can pass (b) while failing (c). A mathematical inequality can instantiate a *new experimentally discriminating estimand* without inventing the underlying observable. This is not equivalent to the strong historical claim that no scientist could previously formulate the question.

**Adversarial rival:** The entire content can already be explained by standard Bell-test methodology, Maxwell historiography, and Abramsky–Brandenburger (2011). The MQR-specific contribution, if any, must be a new cross-domain contrast with independent historical or empirical evaluation, not the mere existence of these examples.
