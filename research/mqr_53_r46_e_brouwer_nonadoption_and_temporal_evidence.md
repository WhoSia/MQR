# MQR-5.3 R46-E — Non-adoption is not failed inquiry: Brouwer as a rival historical case

Date: 2026-11-05. Research authority: primary-text reconnaissance plus historiographical comparison. Verdict: NON-ADOPTION DOCUMENTED IN SECONDARY SOURCE / EX-ANTE COMPARATIVE RATIONALITY HOLD / THEORY NOVELTY HOLD.

## Research target and explicit contrast

R46-D reconstructed laboratory-level variations in the same 1954 protein-incorporation program. Low readout within one condition is **not** evidence of failed research, let alone an irrational program. R46-E looks for a *non-adopted reform program*, with Brouwer's intuitionism in foundations of mathematics as a strong candidate. This is not a matched comparison: laboratory procedures and mathematical foundations have different evidence, counterfactuals and success criteria.

The MQR explanatory target is three distinct questions: whether pursuit was reasonable at the time; whether a proposed framework was warranted by its own mathematical/methodological arguments; and whether the community later adopted it. Neither institutional adoption nor failure of adoption can be substituted for the first two.

## Evidence hierarchy

A. **Reported historical facts**: Brouwer proposed a deep reformation of the foundations of mathematics; intuitionism did not displace classical mathematics as the dominant general mathematical practice. Kish Bar-On explicitly recognizes continued research and subsequent contributions after failure to prevail.

B. **Interpretation already in prior art**: Kish Bar-On analyzes why the failed adoption and individual deliberations of Weyl/Fraenkel matter to Friedman's account of paradigm/framework transitions. A MQR claim 'failed revolutions need explanation' is PRIOR-ART SUBSUMED.

C. **Not established by this examination**: the specific moment when Weyl or Fraenkel decided to endorse or depart, the precise reasons in their original dated words, a cost-benefit ranking of options, or a historically genuine head-to-head contradiction among Friedman, Chang and Kitcher. Later anthologies and biographies cannot fill these gaps without a dated passage ledger.

## A non-tautological prospective evaluation design

Freeze decision point *t*; represent the information available to actor *a* at t as E(a,t), a set of live alternatives A(a,t), and relevant practical norms N(a,t). Keep distinct:
- PERMISSIBLE PURSUIT: whether the proposed program had reasonable expected intellectual value under E and N.
- PRIORITY: whether it deserved relatively more scarce effort than the alternatives in A.
- ACCEPTANCE: whether claims were warranted.
- UPTAKE: whether a community institutionalized or widely used the program.
- CONTRIBUTION: whether it produced useful future results, even without replacing the dominant framework.

Do not infer E, A or N from later uptake U. Do not infer rationality from the successes of later intuitionistic mathematics.

## Three explanatory challenges

(1) **Selection bias**: an inquiry surviving in historiography may be atypical; successful and failed routes must be sampled using a pre-stated criterion rather than selected only because famous.
(2) **Temporal leakage**: the later refined version of intuitionism, intuitionistic logic, or secondary historical explanation cannot simply be placed into a 1920 actor's decision state.
(3) **Domain mismatch**: an experimental assay in 1954 and a mathematical foundational reform in the 1920s have no common scalar output. At most they share a formal *schema* for separating pursue / accept / adopt / contribute. This is an organizing device, not an established universal theory.

## Negative result and novelty decision

We have NOT shown a first-person contemporaneous case of an investigator choosing a knowingly unsuccessful program, nor a case in which a stronger extant philosophical theory gives a false verdict. The historical material instead strengthens the prior-art constraint: Kish Bar-On already addresses failed adoption within a sophisticated model of rational transitions.

**Verdict: NEGATIVE ON PRESENT NOVELTY HYPOTHESIS.** An MQR-level theory is still HOLD. R46-F should not make a new universal rule; it should pin an original dated Weyl and Fraenkel decision document (with original context and text), then test whether the ex-ante legitimacy and ultimate uptake judgments are separable in this actual actor decision.
