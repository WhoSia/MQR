## 2. Proposed central programme (not an established theory)
**Dynamic question-and-bridge ecology (DQBE), provisional descriptor only:**

A scientific practice is a historically situated configuration of observational languages, operational skills, known explanatory alternatives, admissible questions, social exchange, and evaluation institutions. Its evolution includes:
- obtaining new evidence under existing interfaces;
- creating new measurement or intervention opportunities;
- revising categories in response to their outcomes;
- changing which previously adequate partial translations remain sufficient;
- transmitting apparatus and know-how between researchers;
- selectively funding, rewarding or suppressing projects and communities.

No single scalar 'scientific flourishing' is defended. At minimum track:
(1) reproducible external-world constraints;
(2) newly *executable* discriminating questions and new method transfer;
(3) independent evidence ancestries;
(4) retained and lost distinctions, question reforms;
(5) task-relative inter-practice mapping/operation conservation;
(6) institutionally mediated opportunity and epistemic externalities.

This is a synthesis of established bodies of work: Kuhn/Masterman/Friedman/Galison/Steinle/Nersessian, statistical and causal inference, Blackwell comparison, model checking/congruence, Hacking/Secord, Zollman/Avin/Gross/Bergstrom. MQR's epistemic contribution requires clear source-conditioned predictions, not renaming established theorems.

## 3. One provisional, falsifiable MQR thesis
**T:** The practical value of a historical cross-science bridge can change after *newly accessible operations* appear, even when old passive observations remain jointly reproducible; this can produce a requirement to refine concepts, instruments, translations or the targeted explanatory task.

Formal positive witness: R20 stable partition refinement. Formal negative witness: an operation that maps each old observational class uniformly requires NO refinement. Historical candidate: Faraday–Ampère common rotation reproduction but divergent standards of theory inference in 1821–22; no literal map from historical states to our finite transition system yet justified.

**Critically important alternate thesis:** the historical divergence may be fully explained by different prior assumptions, evidential thresholds, target explanatory reductions or apparatus choices while preserving a shared operational language. If that wins, the value of MQR is careful separation of interface/evidence/inference, NOT a unique discovery of 'dynamic incommensurability'.

## 4. Discriminating protocol for the next source-driven research unit
- **Freeze source dates:** Ampère 1820/1821 originals, Faraday 1821 paper and notebook, Hachette 19 Nov, 12 Dec, Faraday 1822 reply; verify French translation and edited notes.
- **Freeze 1821 operational repertoire:** which precise actions, preparations, materials and geometries were feasible at each location; do not smuggle later Maxwell theory into 1821.
- **Specify claim contracts:** which observation did each theory expect, and what additional observation did each author explicitly require to decide magnetic micro-currents versus circulation-as-primitive?
- **Hold-out counterexamples:** find an experiment both parties interpret the same; find a dispute where the divergence vanishes with shared instrument calibration rather than a new concept.
- **Trace social method transfer:** Paris repetition already witnessed; can we find an ensuing novel independent experiment due to the transmitted apparatus rather than a renamed old test?
- **Extend across domains:** choose an unrelated historical case in mathematics or chemistry with different instrument/experiment semantics. If the thesis fails there, constrain it rather than rescoring it as automatic support.
