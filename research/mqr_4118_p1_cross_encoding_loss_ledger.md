# MQR-4.118 P1 — Cross-Encoding Loss Ledger

2026-10-17. Analytic pre-registration, no laboratory case falsely claimed.

Consider UV–Vis concentration determination. L contains logical prerequisites: blank, wavelength, absorbance, standards, fit and units. P contains practices: calibration stock custody, operator competency, failed-run retention, admission/challenge rights and instrument handling.

| Assertion / question | L alone | P alone | Additional witness to bridge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unit conversion correct | Formally checkable when units are specified | Not assured | Original units and calculation |
| Calibration reference physically traceable | Not assured by a reference node | May document chain, not automatically authenticate | Contemporaneous stock labels, certificate versions, transfer records |
| Failed measurements were excluded selectively | Not recoverable from final-fit graph | Auditable if retained-run register exists | Full eligible-run ledger including omissions |
| Evidence-admission rules can be contested | Outside bare inference graph | May identify gatekeeper and appeals | Historical SOP revisions and testimony |
| Claimed concentration correct in measured sample | Neither encoding alone suffices | Neither encoding alone suffices | Raw spectral signals, validated uncertainty model, independent physical check |

**Disagreement types:** (A) unencoded / no field; (B) encoded but data missing; (C) encoded with conflicting observations; (D) normative conflict over who may define the encoding. These categories are provisional and open to objections; not a universal classification.

**Translation test:** A map L→P that carries a calibration-node label into a supposed custody witness fabricates information unless an independently checked provenance record is supplied. A map P→L that drops admission authority cannot faithfully preserve contestability assertions. Thus translations are generally partial, but this does not prove historical incommensurability. Both have bounded strength and different gaps.

**Adversarial pair:** Same numerical UV–Vis final report and same L-graph, different eligible-run custody → P distinguishes. Same P-custody and admission practice but erroneous unit conversion → L distinguishes. Hypothetical, not real-world findings.
