## P0: two-level legitimacy
J1: within a specified evidential protocol, does a judgment follow from inspected observations and warranted inference?
J2: is the protocol's scope, inclusion policy, correction route and authority to revise itself defensible when challenged?
A J1 PASS is conditional on the protocol and does not imply J2. A J2 objection need not invalidate a physical measurement or logical proof. Evidence needed differs by claim.

## Constructed adversarial examples
- Three reviewers demand a numeric UV-Vis answer and a fourth requests missing standard-custody records: the majority's compliance verdict does not establish traceable concentration.
- Reviewers disagree on an anomalous absorbance peak: this may be an empirical-data gap remediable by discriminating measurement, not necessarily a legitimacy conflict.
- An excluded challenge asks why SOP-listed questions alone count: the panel's own procedures require a separate contestable authority review. An unorthodox challenge is not automatically valid.
