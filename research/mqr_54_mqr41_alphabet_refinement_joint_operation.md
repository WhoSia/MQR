## Exact three-action counterexample

Let the observable action alphabet be {a,b,c}. The restricted language consists of all repetition-free words of length at most two. The permissive language extends it with all six permutations of abc. Both are finite prefix-closed trace languages. Every two-action projection is identical in the two languages. For the rejected trace abc, accepted traces ab, ac and bc witness its three local projections individually. The product of the three pairwise views therefore admits all six length-three words. Its 16 traces contain six false positives relative to the restricted language of 10 traces. Because each symbol occurs in a component and repeats are locally forbidden, the finite enumeration covers the entire product.

[Executable Python example](../experiments/mqr-5.4/mqr41_pairwise_projection_limit.py). Run `python experiments/mqr-5.4/mqr41_pairwise_projection_limit.py` from repository root. This is a compact instance of Henry et al.'s product-observation criterion, not a new decomposition theorem. It extends MQR's previous compositional sensor example by exhibiting an ambiguity even when *every* pairwise observation agrees. In this example a single controlled test of abc distinguishes the two candidate languages. It does not recover the underlying mechanism.

## From decomposition to a shared experimental question

Compare [Feest (2025), Introduction pp. 5–7](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mHLsGGz88TDiXy9suW-Kizyd2h1eqbNl/view) on provisional measurement operations and their material presuppositions with [Galison (1999), Trading Zone, Schwinger and microwave networks](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSJUgdSg7RX1a8AFGUmf5IzhaNcxB7T-/view). In Galison's account, equivalent circuits made selected field-calculation results usable in an engineering practice. His account also treats the creation of the shared representation as historical work. Henry et al. give a precise way to revise a partition-like representation once the actions and feedback are available. The two cases motivate asking how a shared operation and its agreed readout become available in the first place. Galison is the historical interpretation used here, with primary engineering comparison still open.

For MQR's UV–Vis question, the immediate empirical task is to find or acquire records in which preparation, illumination and detector configuration were varied jointly. A proposed comparison would choose a narrow reported quantity, define the preparation and admissible instrument settings, then withhold at least one jointly varied condition from both calibration and model fitting. Compare a representation based on pairwise effects with one that includes a joint effect, using the same held-out readings and uncertainty model. If the discrepancy disappears after controlling drift or sample preparation, that favors an apparatus explanation for this case. If it survives the controls, it can justify a new joint test within that apparatus. The synthetic language does not predict that either outcome will occur. The Real Language bridge can record a validated correspondence after such evidence exists. It currently takes the witness declarations as inputs.
