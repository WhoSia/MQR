## Drake witness

The Drake equation is admitted only as a typed inquiry scaffold:
- decomposition of a vague SETI question into factors;
- question generation;
- measurement/research agenda;
- coordination of heterogeneous inquiry.

No numerical confidence or literal-world authority follows merely from those roles.

## Toy-model boundary

Exploratory and idealized models may earn:
- modal;
- regulative;
- question-generating;
- baseline;
- decomposition roles.

These roles do not automatically establish how-actually authority.
