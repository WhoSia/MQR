## Purpose

`REALSTRATEGY 0.24` serializes a **strategic discovery-ecology constitution** around a live WCEPR.

It treats challenge supply, reported cost, target framing, proxy choice, agent multiplicity and institutional feedback as potentially endogenous to incentives.

It does not assume that truthful reporting, strategic equilibrium or institutional stability imply epistemic adequacy.

## Local strategic robustness

A packet can earn local strategic robustness only if:
- agents/roles are explicit;
- private-information surfaces are explicit;
- incentive maps are audited;
- challenge-supply provenance is audited;
- reported cost is reconciled with realized cost;
- target and proxy choice provenance pass;
- performative feedback is represented;
- strategic ancestry is audited;
- nominal multiplicity is compressed through anti-Sybil discipline;
- a mechanism counterfactual passes;
- an exterior reserve remains active;
- equilibrium is explicitly prevented from self-authorizing epistemic adequacy;
- mechanism-induced capture can be attributed;
- reopening is active;
- mechanism scope is local and declared;
- WCEPR dependence is explicit;
- no universal-mechanism claim is made.
