# REALACQUIRE 0.29 — Prospective Evidence-Acquisition Constitution

Status: **CANDIDATE / PRE-REVEAL**

MQR-4.62 extends the MQR live surface from dynamic promotion-history receipts to prospective selection among evidence-generating actions.

- **EASR** — Evidence-Acquisition Selection Receipt
- **RAER** — Relation-Anticipation Envelope Receipt

- **ARR** — Adaptive Reopening Reserve
- **NEDL** — Nonmyopic Exploration-Debt Ledger

## Constitutional rule

Evidence-generating actions are not ranked by one scalar by default.

A candidate action may be locally compared through typed consequences for:
- current claim/relation discrimination;
- local identifiability;
- option creation or destruction;
- evidence ancestry / common mode;
- intervention semantics;
- baseline preservation;
- reopening capacity;
- debt;
- scope/horizon;
- declared cost/resource contract.

The constitution may return:
- **LOCAL_ADMISSIBLE**
- **OPTION_PRESERVE**
- **REOPEN_REQUIRED**
- **DEBT_CARRY**
- **HOLD_INCOMPARABLE**
- **BOUNDED_EXTERIOR_PROBE**
- **FORBIDDEN_BY_DESTRUCTIVE_CONTRACT**

None is a universal experiment ranking.

## Authority ceiling

v0.29 is not:
- a universal active-learning rule;
- a universal Bayesian design utility;
- a universal random-experiment rule;
- a complete causal-discovery policy;
- a proof that greedy choice fails generally;
- a proof that randomization is always best;
- a utility over all scientific futures;
- a final discovery algorithm.

It is a typed, reopenable acquisition constitution whose authority remains local to its declared action set, rival/frontier state, evidence ancestry, intervention semantics, scope, horizon and reopening conditions.
