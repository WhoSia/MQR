## Why v0.20 exists

MQR-4.52 showed that fixed event count is not a representation-invariant naturalistic stopping coordinate.

MQR-4.53 asks the upstream question:

> does scientific inquiry possess any representation-invariant notion of progress that can help constitute “enough to stop”?

The first 4.53 theory court rejects a universal scalar ruler while admitting local invariant geometries, partial orders and preorders.

The live candidate is therefore an **atlas**, not a ruler.

## World-Contact Progress Atlas

A local progress chart is represented abstractly as:

~~~text
C = (
  S,
  G_inert,
  Q = S / G_inert,
  K,
  R,
  P
)
~~~

where:
- S is an inquiry-state space;
- G_inert is the declared class of scientifically inert representation transformations;
- Q is the quotient state space;
- K is the claim/use/decision contract;
- R is world-contact and reopening transition structure;
- P is a locally earned metric, partial order, preorder or admissible-region structure.

The atlas supplies the meta-constitution governing charts. It does not force their numerical magnitudes into one global scale.

## Inert quotient

A chart seeking full 4.53 atlas admissibility must quotient:
- inert checkpoint refinement/coarsening;
- information-preserving evidence batching;
- admissible parameter recoding;
- obligation-carrier split/merge that preserves burden ancestry;
- inert loops with no durable world-contact consequence.

These transformations are not universally inert. The declaration is load-bearing.

If a transformation changes a scientific distinction, intervention possibility, use contract, burden ancestry or reopening state, it is MATERIAL and must not be quotiented.

## Path order

Noncommuting world-facing paths must survive.

~~~text
CALIBRATE ; INTERVENE
may differ from
INTERVENE ; CALIBRATE
~~~

A quotient that erases a materially order-sensitive path is invalid even if endpoints share superficial labels.

## Local geometry is permitted

v0.20 explicitly rejects the stronger no-go claim that invariant geometry is impossible.

Examples of admissible local structures include:
- Fisher-type metric geometry inside a statistical model chart;
- Blackwell-type partial order over experiments;
- burden-ancestry discharge preorder;
- causal identifiability order;
- contract-relative admissible regions.

No local success establishes a universal progress metric.

## Correction-sensitive progress

Scientific progress may remove an over-authorized claim.

Therefore progress cannot be defined as monotone growth of authority.

v0.20 distinguishes at the constitutional level:
- positive acquisition;
- corrective progress;
- null representational motion.

The current grammar does not pretend to supply a complete quantitative calculus over these types.

## Progress and stopping

A progress chart alone does not license a stop.

The MQR-4.53 successor architecture separates:

### WCQP
World-Contact Quotient Preorder / local progress structure.

### CVE
Continuation-Value Envelope over currently registered admissible next actions.

### PAR
Progress Admissible Region under a declared claim/use contract.

A candidate stop has the form:

~~~text
CURRENT STATE IN PAR
+ LIVE MATERIAL OBLIGATION DEBT EMPTY
+ NO REGISTERED CONTINUATION ACTION
  HAS WARRANTED MARGINAL VALUE
  ABOVE THE DECLARED OPPORTUNITY-COST BOUND
+ REOPENING RESERVE ACTIVE
~~~

This is not a claim about unconceived future probes.

## Current 4.53 claim ceiling

Candidate earned by the executable countermodel family:
- raw event count is not representation-invariant;
- obligation carrier count is not burden invariant;
- equal descriptive information can coexist with unequal intervention reach;
- inert loops defeat raw authority-path length;
- corrective progress can lower claim authority;
- independently rescaled axes can reverse scalar rankings;
- crossing profiles can remain Pareto-incomparable;
- identical achieved progress states can require opposite continuation actions;
- structural cross-domain transport can survive while information/cost magnitudes do not.

Positive donors:
- local statistical geometry may be reparameterization invariant;
- experiment informativeness may form a partial order rather than a scalar total order.

Not earned:
- one global scientific progress metric;
- universal cross-domain magnitude;
- final progress ontology;
- scalar progress threshold as a universal stop rule;
- final truth distance.
