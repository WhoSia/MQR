# MQR Research Contribution and Prior-Art Doctrine — 2026-10-18

**This amendment overrules earlier assistant-imposed requirements that a completely new phenomenon, unprecedented theorem, or independently new research object must be found before research has value or a new formal stage may be considered.** The user has repeatedly rejected novelty obsession, and prior assistant treatment was erroneous.

## Legitimate scholarly contributions — equal standing
1. **Synthesis:** accurately combine cited theories into a productive framework, resolving tensions and specifying scopes.
2. **Rigorous reconstruction:** establish assumptions, derive or repair proofs, compare representations, make concrete counterexamples.
3. **Transfer and application:** use existing mathematics, science or philosophy in an informative new domain, with grounded limits.
4. **Replication and reanalysis:** reproduce, independently check, identify data/provenance issues and contribute auditable research objects.
5. **Conceptual differentiation:** make inherited distinctions useful, with explicit donor acknowledgment.
6. **Novel formal or empirical discovery:** welcome when evidenced, but is not the exclusive valid contribution type.

## Attribution instead of novelty anxiety
Cite original authors for results, concepts, evidence, and technical methods. Say what MQR borrows, revises or integrates. Using and being influenced by prior work with correct attribution is normal scholarship, not plagiarism. Existing work can refute a claim of exclusive invention without invalidating the value of careful synthesis, application or philosophical development. Neither fabricate novelty nor treat overlap as a reason to stop.

## Formal generation and promotion
A formal stage/title can be justified by a sustained meaningful shift in central question, rigorous clarification, explanatory reach, synthesis or domain transfer, not only by an unprecedented scientific discovery or independently observed institutional impact. Never claim empirical verification for conceptual work. Specify contribution TYPE, evidence level, inherited uncertainties and which prior work is cited. The absence of a particular J2 empirical witness must not become a universal gate on the entire MQR intellectual program. Existing HOLDs remain correctly typed.

## Default research process
Revisit earliest MQR questions and contemporaneous archival records; read other labs and literature; build positive scientific-philosophical inquiry, not just procedural HOLD registries. Preserve real falsifiers without turning novelty evaluation into the core scientific goal. Avoid repeated redundant micro-notes. Distinguish real-world warrant from theoretical insight without denying either's value.
