# MQR Origin-Return Realism — live through MQR-3.172

This file keeps MQR's founding philosophical question visible while technical and empirical courts proliferate.

## Founding question

**What can science justifiably call real when every scientific description, ontology, measurement system and vocabulary remains revisable?**

MQR does not identify reality with one ontology, one privileged grain, one formalism, one consensus, or the currently best predictive model.

Its realist target is **world-resistant constraint**: a claim earns scoped realist authority when alternatives are excluded in ways that survive hostile reformulation and materially noncommon contact, while remaining reopenable.

- `CURRENTLY RULED OUT != WORLD-IMPOSSIBLE`
- `ACCESS FAILURE != WORLD REFUSAL`
- `GLOBAL FIT != GLOBAL LAW`
- `UNIFICATION != ULTIMATE METAPHYSICS`
- `CONSENSUS != REALITY`

## World-Possibility Cut

For a scientific claim `C`, let `W_C` denote the currently articulated worlds compatible with the claim. The claim's modal content can be represented by the worlds it excludes:

`Cut(C) = W \\ W_C`.

This is not a metaphysical definition of reality. It is an authority device for asking whether excluded alternatives fail because of the world or merely because the current ontology, measurement system, intervention grammar, translation map, governance regime, or resource envelope cannot express or reach them.

## World-Resistance Realism

A constraint gains realist authority when its exclusions survive materially noncommon contact routes, hostile reformulation in rival ontologies, and prospective challenge while remaining capable of losing authority when the possibility space, access map, translation family, or dependence genealogy expands.

The project separates epistemic exclusion, model-induced exclusion, access-induced exclusion, translation-induced exclusion, and world-resistant exclusion. Only the last supports realist promotion, and only at declared scope.

## Modal access and boundary evidence

Human non-performability is not world-contact impossibility.

- `NO SURGICAL INTERVENTION != NO WORLD CONTACT`
- `FAILURE TO ACCESS != EVIDENCE OF IMPOSSIBILITY`
- `UNREACHABLE TARGET != UNCONTACTABLE MODAL BOUNDARY`

A **Modal Boundary Trace** is a reachable consequence generated near or because of an inaccessible boundary, including finite-resource scaling, reachable no-go inequalities, natural neighboring regimes, and independently validated source-to-target transport.

A boundary trace has realist force only to the extent that the route by which it is predicted, measured, calibrated, transported and interpreted can disagree with the boundary claim it supports.

- `DERIVATIONAL ROBUSTNESS != EMPIRICAL INDEPENDENCE`
- `MANY TRACES != MANY INDEPENDENT WORLD RESISTANCES`
- `ASYMPTOTIC AGREEMENT != ENDPOINT REALIZATION`

> **A boundary trace is evidence only if it can become a wound.**

## Dependence and finite independence

Independence audits are fallible because ancestry graphs can be incomplete. A newly discovered common ancestor revises authority rather than erasing the observation.

- `JOINT FAILURE != COMMON-CAUSE PROOF`
- `DEPENDENCE DISCOVERY != RETROACTIVE DATA INVALIDATION`
- `NEW COMMON ANCESTOR -> AUTHORITY RECOMPUTATION, NOT HISTORY ERASURE`

Scientific realism does not require proving that no hidden common ancestor remains. It requires enough **Finite Route Separation** that the major known and plausible common-failure modes have been made capable of diverging.

- `STRONG INDEPENDENCE WARRANT != PROOF OF NO HIDDEN COMMON CAUSE`
- `FALLIBILISM != PERMANENT WEAK WARRANT`
- `STRONG WARRANT != FINAL WARRANT`

## Route saturation and inquiry allocation

Additional evidence need not increase realist authority once currently reachable failure geometry is saturated.

- `PRECISION GAIN != AUTHORITY GAIN`
- `MORE RECEIPTS != MORE WORLD-RESISTANCE ROUTES`
- `ROUTE COUNT != EXTERIORITY`
- `SATURATION != FINALITY`

> **Scientific authority grows when inquiry creates new ways for reality to disagree, not merely when it accumulates more agreement.**

Operational precision and realist-authority value are distinct. Mature inquiry should preserve enough portfolio freedom that, when familiar routes stop creating new ways to be wrong, resources can still buy a genuinely different encounter with reality.

## Pluralism, lineage and translation

Rival theories and methods matter because they can expose failures unavailable inside an incumbent framework.

`RIVAL VALUE = NEW REACHABLE WAYS FOR THE WORLD TO SAY NO`.

Succession, branching, recombination and translation may transport or compare warrant, but they cannot manufacture new world-contact by genealogy or vocabulary alone.

- `FORK COUNT != EVIDENCE COUNT`
- `MERGER AGREEMENT != NEW WORLD-CONTACT`
- `SHARED VOCABULARY != NEUTRAL VOCABULARY`
- `QUOTIENTED DISAGREEMENT != RESOLVED DISAGREEMENT`
- `MAXIMUM AGREEMENT != MAXIMUM REALIST WARRANT`

## MQR-3.172 — World-Resistance Invariance candidate

MQR-3.172 forces a return to the philosophical center. When several scientifically defensible translations between rival ontologies preserve different overlaps and disagreements, realist authority cannot depend on whichever dictionary is selected.

The candidate synthesis is:

> **Reality is not whatever representation remains textually or mathematically identical across perspectives. Realist authority attaches to constraints whose capacity to defeat alternatives survives admissible changes of description, measurement, translation and intervention.**

This is deliberately not identical to classical representation-invariance or structural realism. MQR does not identify reality with an invariant symbol, equation, category or mathematical structure. Its candidate realist carrier is **defeat geometry**:

- which possibilities remain excluded;
- which interventions or observations could reopen them;
- which failures remain available to the world;
- whether those failure routes survive changes of description;
- whether materially noncommon contact continues to support the constraint.

`REPRESENTATION INVARIANCE != REALITY`.

`DEFEAT-GEOMETRY INVARIANCE -> CANDIDATE REALIST CARRIER`.

Deep compression:

> **Reality is not the invariant representation; reality is what keeps being able to resist us when representations change.**

This is a synthesis candidate, not a historical novelty claim. Neo-Kantian invariance traditions, structural realism, perspectival realism and theoretical-equivalence literature are serious near-rivals.

## Real without reduction

Two levels or non-isomorphic ontologies may both be real when each carries compatible but nonredundant world-resistant exclusions.

- `NONREDUCIBILITY != ANTI-REALITY`
- `MULTIPLE DESCRIPTIONS != MULTIPLE REALITIES`

The point is not that all descriptions are equally real. The point is that realist authority is indexed to the constraints and defeat routes a description exposes, not to its vocabulary alone.

## Higher-Law Non-Vacuity Gate

A higher-law candidate must earn more than extensional fit. It should provide cross-domain generation, modal consequences not fixed by the construction record, reachable or independently grounded defeat routes, resistance to lookup-table reconstruction, and humility about governing-law versus Humean versus deeper-realizer metaphysics.

`LAW AUTHORITY = MODAL EXCESS + REOPENABLE DEFEAT + NONVACUITY`.

## Reality / authority firewall

MQR governs scientific authority, not reality itself. Increasing authority for a claim does not make the claim metaphysically final.

`NO FINITE SCIENTIFIC CONSTITUTION CERTIFIES ITS OWN POSSIBILITY-SPACE, TRANSLATION-FAMILY OR DEPENDENCE-GRAPH COMPLETENESS`.
