# MQR Origin-Return Realism — live through MQR-3.162

This file exists to keep MQR's founding philosophical question visible while the project continues to use technical and empirical courts.

## Core thesis

MQR does not identify reality with one ontology, one privileged grain, one formalism, or the currently best predictive model.

Its realist target is **world-resistant constraint**: a claim earns scoped realist authority when alternatives are excluded in ways that survive hostile reformulation and materially noncommon contact, while remaining reopenable.

- `CURRENTLY RULED OUT != WORLD-IMPOSSIBLE`
- `ACCESS FAILURE != WORLD REFUSAL`
- `GLOBAL FIT != GLOBAL LAW`
- `UNIFICATION != ULTIMATE METAPHYSICS`

## World-Possibility Cut

For a scientific claim `C`, let `W_C` denote the currently articulated worlds compatible with the claim. The claim's modal content can be represented by the worlds it excludes:

`Cut(C) = W \\ W_C`.

This is not a metaphysical definition of reality. It is an authority device for asking whether the excluded alternatives fail because of the world or merely because the current ontology, measurement system, method, intervention grammar, governance regime, or resource envelope cannot express or reach them.

## World-Resistance Realism

A constraint gains realist authority when its exclusions survive materially noncommon contact routes, hostile reformulation in rival ontologies, and prospective challenge while remaining capable of losing authority when the possibility space, access map, or dependence genealogy expands.

The project separates epistemic exclusion, model-induced exclusion, access-induced exclusion, and world-resistant exclusion. Only the last supports realist promotion, and only at declared scope.

## Modal access and boundary evidence

MQR-3.157 requires every load-bearing modal exclusion to carry a typed access profile. Human non-performability is not world-contact impossibility.

- `NO SURGICAL INTERVENTION != NO WORLD CONTACT`
- `FAILURE TO ACCESS != EVIDENCE OF IMPOSSIBILITY`
- `UNREACHABLE TARGET != UNCONTACTABLE MODAL BOUNDARY`

A **Modal Boundary Trace** is a reachable consequence generated near or because of an inaccessible boundary, including finite-resource scaling, reachable no-go inequalities, natural neighboring regimes, and independently validated source-to-target transport.

## MQR-3.158 — boundary-evidence independence

Boundary evidence must not become a theory echo. A trace has realist force only to the extent that the route by which it is predicted, measured, calibrated, transported and interpreted can disagree with the boundary claim it is meant to support.

- `DERIVATIONAL ROBUSTNESS != EMPIRICAL INDEPENDENCE`
- `MANY TRACES != MANY INDEPENDENT WORLD RESISTANCES`
- `ASYMPTOTIC AGREEMENT != ENDPOINT REALIZATION`
- `TRACE PREDICTED BY LAW + TRACE INTERPRETED THROUGH LAW != INDEPENDENT SUPPORT FOR LAW`

Deep compression:

> **A boundary trace is evidence only if it can become a wound.**

## MQR-3.159 — latent boundary dependence

Independence audits are themselves fallible because the relevant ancestry graph can be incomplete. Two traces honestly treated as noncommon may later fail together and reveal a previously unknown theoretical, calibration, software, material, selection or inferential ancestor.

- `JOINT FAILURE != COMMON-CAUSE PROOF`
- `DEPENDENCE DISCOVERY != RETROACTIVE DATA INVALIDATION`
- `NEW COMMON ANCESTOR -> AUTHORITY RECOMPUTATION, NOT HISTORY ERASURE`
- `MORE PRECISE != MORE INDEPENDENT`
- `PLURAL FORM != PLURAL FAILURE ANCESTRY`

Deep philosophical compression:

> **Evidence can remain real while its independence status changes.**

## MQR-3.160 — finite independence without genealogical completeness

Scientific realism does not require proving that no hidden common ancestor remains. It requires enough **Finite Route Separation (FRS)** that the major known and plausible common-failure modes have been actively made capable of diverging.

A claim may therefore earn `WORLD-RESISTANT@SCOPE` while retaining a **Residual Dependence Reserve (RDR)**.

- `STRONG INDEPENDENCE WARRANT != PROOF OF NO HIDDEN COMMON CAUSE`
- `FALLIBILISM != PERMANENT WEAK WARRANT`
- `STRONG WARRANT != FINAL WARRANT`

## MQR-3.161 — route saturation

Additional evidence need not continue increasing realist authority once currently reachable failure geometry is saturated.

A claim reaches **Route Saturation** over a declared scope and failure family when new receipts from available routes add no materially new defeat surface.

- `PRECISION GAIN != AUTHORITY GAIN`
- `MORE RECEIPTS != MORE WORLD-RESISTANCE ROUTES`
- `ROUTE COUNT != EXTERIORITY`
- `SATURATION != FINALITY`

A nominally new route is authority-incrementing only when it creates a new marginal defeat opportunity.

Deep compression:

> **Scientific authority grows when inquiry creates new ways for reality to disagree, not merely when it accumulates more agreement.**

## MQR-3.162 — post-saturation inquiry allocation

Once route families saturate, scientific resource allocation must separate at least two kinds of value:

- **operational / precision utility**: narrower uncertainty, improved calibration, prediction, monitoring, reliability and throughput;
- **realist-authority utility**: creation of a materially new opportunity for the world to defeat a load-bearing claim.

`OPERATIONAL VALUE != REALIST-AUTHORITY VALUE`.

A saturated replication may remain scientifically worth funding while being authority-neutral.

### Marginal Defeat Value

A candidate route has high **Marginal Defeat Value (MDV)** when, conditional on the current evidence family, it plausibly opens a failure surface that incumbent routes cannot expose: for example by breaking shared ancestry, reaching a new regime or observable, implementing a rival-sensitive intervention, or enabling one route to fail while another survives.

`NEW METHOD != NEW DISAGREEMENT GEOMETRY`.

### Exteriority Reserve

A mature programme may maintain an **Exteriority Reserve (ER)**: protected capacity to fund some routes whose main value lies in opening noncommon defeat rather than maximizing incumbent performance metrics.

ER is not a universal budget percentage. It is justified conditionally by claim importance, route saturation, ancestry concentration, identifiable blind spots, and the presence of plausible exterior candidates.

- `EXPENSIVE EXTERIORITY != AUTOMATIC PRIORITY`
- `CHEAP PRECISION != AUTOMATIC EPISTEMIC EFFICIENCY`
- `INCUMBENT PERFORMANCE METRIC != NEUTRAL EXTERIORITY METRIC`

The allocation constitution therefore distinguishes precision spending from authority expansion and protects a separate hearing for failure-surface contribution.

Exploration can itself saturate; MQR requires capacity to reopen, not permanent novelty production.

Deep compression:

> **A mature science should preserve enough portfolio freedom that, when familiar routes stop creating new ways to be wrong, resources can still buy a genuinely different encounter with reality.**

## No-go theorem firewall

A mathematical no-go result does not by itself settle ontology. Its premises need independent world-contact support, and at least some reachable consequence must be capable of reopening the claim.

`NO-GO THEOREM != UNIQUE ONTOLOGICAL VERDICT`.

## Real without reduction

Two levels or non-isomorphic ontologies may both be real when each carries compatible but nonredundant world-resistant exclusions.

- `NONREDUCIBILITY != ANTI-REALITY`
- `MULTIPLE DESCRIPTIONS != MULTIPLE REALITIES`

## Higher-Law Non-Vacuity Gate

A higher-law candidate must earn more than extensional fit. It should provide cross-domain generation, modal consequences not fixed by the construction record, reachable or independently grounded boundary-trace defeat routes, resistance to lookup-table reconstruction, and humility about governing-law versus Humean versus deeper-realizer metaphysics.

`LAW AUTHORITY = MODAL EXCESS + REOPENABLE DEFEAT + NONVACUITY`.

## Feyerabend return

The strongest reason to preserve rival theories and methods is that they can create new ways for the world to say no to an incumbent framework.

`RIVAL VALUE = NEW REACHABLE WAYS FOR THE WORLD TO SAY NO`.

MQR-3.162 adds an institutional corollary: incumbent metrics must not have sole jurisdiction over whether a challenge route deserves the resources needed to become testable.

## Reality / authority firewall

MQR governs scientific authority, not reality itself. Increasing authority for a claim does not make the claim metaphysically final.

`NO FINITE SCIENTIFIC CONSTITUTION CERTIFIES ITS OWN POSSIBILITY-SPACE OR DEPENDENCE-GRAPH COMPLETENESS`.

## Origin-return cycle

`ORIGIN QUESTION -> FORMAL VULNERABILITY -> WORLD CONTACT -> ORIGIN RETURN`.

Formal machinery that does not sharpen a founding ontic or epistemic question, expose a new defeat route, or change a philosophical verdict should be compressed as notation debt.
