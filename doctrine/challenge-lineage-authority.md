# MQR Challenge-Lineage Authority Doctrine — live through MQR-3.169

This file compresses the reusable doctrine produced by MQR-3.163–3.169. Detailed genealogy, literature pressure, and stage verdicts remain in Notion.

## 1. Exterior challenges must not be assimilated before they become testable

- `METHOD-LABEL DIVERSITY != FAILURE-SURFACE DIVERSITY`
- `VISIBLE PLURALITY != LIVE PLURALISM`
- `TRANSLATABILITY FOR REVIEW != NEUTRALITY OF REVIEW LANGUAGE`
- `NO INCUMBENT STANDARD != NO RIGOR`
- `UNTRANSLATED != UNRIGOROUS`

Pre-standard rigor is assessed through explicitness, consequence discipline, reachable vulnerability, ancestry disclosure, translation-loss declaration, and a revision rule—not by forcing a rival ontology to satisfy the incumbent's success criteria in advance.

## 2. Protection from assimilation does not imply immunity from maturation

- `PROTECTION FROM PREMATURE ASSIMILATION != PROTECTION FROM MATURATION`
- `DEFERRED VULNERABILITY != CANCELLED VULNERABILITY`
- `MORE ARTICULATION WITHOUT MORE DEFEAT EXPOSURE != MATURATION`
- `PURSUITWORTHY != CONFIRMED`

A protected challenge must accumulate sharper defeat exposure over time. Translation shelter may delay contact, but it cannot erase the obligation to become vulnerable.

## 3. Challenge identity is carried by inherited target and inherited defeat burden

MQR does not require an immutable Lakatosian hard core. A challenge may revise deep commitments while remaining the same lineage when it preserves traceable target inheritance and at least one substantive defeat obligation.

- `SAME NAME + CONTINUOUS AUTHORS != SAME CHALLENGE`
- `SHARED TARGET WITHOUT SHARED DEFEAT BURDEN != FULL IDENTITY`
- `SUCCESSOR CONTINUITY != AUTHORITY CONTINUITY`
- `OBLIGATION SATISFIED != OBLIGATION REDEFINED`

When both load-bearing target and defeat topology are replaced, launch a successor challenge rather than retroactively declaring maturation success.

## 4. Credit transport is typed and must carry relevant failure transport

Possible transported credits include diagnostic, contact, infrastructure, predictive, and realist credit. They do not share one transfer rule.

- `LINEAGE CONTINUITY != CREDIT CONTINUITY`
- `RETAINED NAME != RETAINED WARRANT`
- `SELECTIVE RETENTION != SELECTIVE AMNESIA`
- `DIAGNOSTIC VINDICATION != MECHANISM VINDICATION`
- `STRUCTURAL RETENTION != ONTOLOGICAL RETENTION`
- `CREDIT TRANSPORT != TRUTH TRANSPORT`
- `OLD WORLD CONTACT != NEW ONTOLOGY RECEIPT`

A successor may inherit only the authority supported by the retained claim–receipt relation. If the load-bearing retained claim and defeat route survive, relevant negative receipts survive too.

## 5. Lineage memory is bidirectional

A lineage that claims ancestral success must preserve the still-relevant ancestral ways of being wrong.

- `LINEAGE MEMORY = POSITIVE RECEIPTS + RELEVANT NEGATIVE RECEIPTS`
- `ARCHIVED FAILURE != LIVE FAILURE MEMORY`
- `NEGATIVE RECEIPT RETIRED -> RETIRE MATCHED POSITIVE CREDIT OR SHOW INDEPENDENT DISCHARGE`
- `DISCONTINUITY THAT RELEASES FAILURE ALSO RELEASES MATCHED CREDIT`
- `GROSS SUCCESS ACCUMULATION != NET REALIST AUTHORITY`

Flag successor rebranding when continuity is asserted to inherit success but denied to escape failure without a claim-level reason.

## 6. Forked lineages conserve ancestral receipt identity

A single historical world-contact does not become several independent contacts because a research lineage branches.

- `ONE WORLD-CONTACT -> ONE ANCESTRAL RECEIPT ID`
- `FORK COUNT != EVIDENCE COUNT`
- `SHARED ANCESTRAL RECEIPT != MULTIPLE WORLD RESISTANCES`
- `LOCAL REUSE != GLOBAL MULTIPLICATION`
- `SEPARATE PROGRAMMES != SEPARATE RECEIPTS`
- `SHARED USE != SHARED MULTIPLICATION`

Every successor may locally cite a shared ancestral receipt when it still bears on retained content. But joint or portfolio authority is computed over the **union of unique live receipts plus their dependence graph**, not the sum of branch ledgers.

## 7. Forking earns new authority only through post-fork exteriority

Genealogical branching can create hypotheses and future opportunities for defeat, but not new past evidence.

- `GENEALOGICAL DIVERGENCE != EVIDENTIAL EXTERIORITY`
- `GENEALOGICAL DIVERGENCE + NONCOMMON POST-FORK CONTACT -> POSSIBLE NEW AUTHORITY`

New authority requires branch-specific noncommon contact: distinct intervention or measurement routes, separable calibration chains, divergent frozen predictions, distinct regimes, or other ways branches can independently fail.

## Deep compression

> **Science may inherit what survived contact with the world, but neither succession nor branching is allowed to manufacture additional world-contact from the same historical receipt.**

The same rule applies symmetrically to positive and negative lineage memory: ancestry may transport warrant, but it cannot duplicate warrant, erase liabilities, or turn historical prestige into fresh realist authority.
